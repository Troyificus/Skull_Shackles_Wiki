# [[Player Characters]]

The founding officers and legendary lords/lady of the [[Blood Wake]].

---

## [[Lanny]] (aka [[Captain Skull Crusher]])
*   **Class/Ancestry:** male Islander Goliath Barbarian / Druid [Things to remember.txt, 18, 42]
*   **Traits:** Stoic, honorable, childlike curiosity, massive physical strength [17, 29, 44].
*   **Key Abilities:** Primal [[Rage]] [17, 35], [[Wild Shape]] (transforms into a shark-skinned "Shark-Bear") [52, 55], permanent telepathic bond with [[Rat Damon]] [34].
*   **Achievements:** Crushed the skull of [[Braden the Brawler Brimstone]] [23], ripped [[Gunter Plugg]]'s arm off with raw strength [23], successfully drank putrid fish slime at [[The Hagfish]] [32], awarded the noble title of Lord of [[Little Upara]] [44].

## [[Torrin Horrendous]] (aka [[Lady Blackheart]])
*   **Class/Ancestry:** Female human Wizard [Things to remember.txt, 1]
*   **Traits:** Pragmatic, ruthless, calculating, traumatized past (survived torture by [[Blackheart]] and carries guilt over a fire that killed her brother) [33, 34, 38].
*   **Deific Shift:** Formerly dedicated to [[Myrkul]] (Lord of Bones) [48, 55], she renounced him after feeling abandoned during a spectral attack, disintegrating her skull necklace and accepting [[Besmara]]'s patronage [55, 56]. This shifted her lightning magic from dark black to brilliant white [56, 57].
*   **Key Spells:** [[Animate Dead]] (maintains a small cohort of skeletons/zombies) [30, 45, 56], [[Lightning Bolt]] [47, 55], [[Misty Step]] [26, 47].
*   **Familiar:** A bat named [[Zima]] [29, 36].

## [[Devi McRae]] (aka [[Crimson Cray]])
*   **Class/Ancestry:** Male human Rogue [Things to remember.txt, 1]
*   **Traits:** Flamboyant, strategic, highly protective of his twin brother [[Desmond]] and the crew's legal standing [17, 34]. Wears a striking feathered hat and custom crimson dragon-hide studded leather [44, 46].
*   **Key Abilities:** [[Insightful Fighting]], sneak-attack master [36, 59]. Left-handed [54].
*   **Weapons:** Wields the magical rapier [[Brine's Sting]] [59, 60].
*   **Family:** Son of estranged smuggler admiral [[Gavin McRae]] of [[Tidewater Rock]] [21, 34].

## [[Desmond McRae]] (aka [[Fist]])
*   **Class/Ancestry:** Male human Monk / Champion of [[Besmara]] [Things to remember.txt, 1, 28]
*   **Traits:** Bare-knuckle brawler, deeply superstitious, quick-tempered, loyal [1, 23, 27]. Bald with a broken nose [1].
*   **Key Abilities:** [[Flurry of Blows]], [[Eldritch Blast]] (granted by [[Besmara]]'s pact) [28, 29], [[Step of the Wind]] [27].
*   **Achievements:** Defeated [[The Pugilist]] in [[Blood Cove]] [24], struck by divine lightning and resurrected as the chosen champion of [[Besmara]] [27, 28].
