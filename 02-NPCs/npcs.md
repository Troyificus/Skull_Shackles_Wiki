# [[NPCs and Key Figures]]

Allies, enemies, and notable individuals met during Golarion's high-seas campaign.

---

## [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]]
*   **Role:** Dread Captain of the [[Wormwood]] [24]
*   **Traits:** Brutal, vengeful, physically imposing, wearing battle scars and a metal eyepatch [1, 21]. Wiped [[Albert]]'s mind as punishment for burning his previous ship [21].

## [[Mr. Plugg]] (Gunter Plugg)
*   **Role:** First Mate of the Wormwood / Captain of the [[Man's Promise]] [51, 201]
*   **Traits:** Tyrannical, abusive, wearing Devin's stolen purple coat [16, 217]. After being bound in the bilges [18], he had his arm torn off by [[Lanny]] [23], was tortured by [[Torrin]] [23], and was ultimately stuffed in a barrel and raised as [[Zombie Plugg]] [23, 30].

## [[Master Scourge]]
*   **Role:** Boatswain/Whip-master of the Wormwood [10, 208]
*   **Traits:** Sadistic, cruel, carried a gold-toothed smile [17, 306]. He was beaten down by [[Lanny]]'s mace/fists and burned alive in the mutiny [17, 299].

## [[Sandara Quinn]]
*   **Role:** Cleric of [[Besmara]] / Ship's Healer [2, 10]
*   **Traits:** Compassionate, dedicated, wearing thigh-high boots and a tricorn hat [1, 44]. She became an invaluable officer and spiritual guide for [[Desmond]] [28, 34].

## [[Rosie Cusswell]]
*   **Role:** Muscular Halfling / Ship's Fiddler [1, 15]
*   **Traits:** Aggressive, swearing, highly protective of her musical instrument [1, 15]. She tragically lost her leg to the Grindylow Queen [15], and was later fitted with a custom wooden peg leg carved by [[Lanny]] [19].

## [[Albert]] (aka [[Owlbear Albert]])
*   **Role:** First Mate of the [[Blood Wake]] [32, 590]
*   **Traits:** A bumbling, mind-wiped giant pacified by Lanny's honey-smelling magic [17]. Formerly a proud pirate captain blinded and mind-wiped by Harrigan [21]. He was fully restored by [[Father Torbin]] in Goat's Head and returned to his highly intelligent, loyal self [31, 32].

## [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]]
*   **Role:** Captain of the [[Pinion]] [19, 20]
*   **Traits:** Warm-smelling, honorable, peg-legged pirate commodore in a dapper gray-blue coat [19, 20]. He operates four ships and serves as a valuable ally and advisor to the crew [20, 21].

## [[Whalebone Pilk]]
*   **Role:** Cursed Ghost Captain of the [[Death Knell]] [37, 47]
*   **Traits:** A terrifying undead abomination that drains the life-essence of living crewmen with a spectral green hand [47, 958]. His spirit was temporarily bound to the ship's magical bell, which was eventually split and cast into the sea [47, 55].

## [[Lady Olandra]]
*   **Role:** Duchess and Governor of [[Little Upara]] [43, 44]
*   **Traits:** Elegant, wealthy, easily frustrated by her wild pirate sister [[Petrina]] [44]. She awarded the party their dragon bounties and honorary noble titles [44].
