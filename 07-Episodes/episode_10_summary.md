# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 10: Electrocuting Friends and Fighting Grindylows]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 16 (During the journey of the [[Man's Promise]])

## Plot Summary
The journey of the [[Man's Promise]] continues, but the weather quickly deteriorates into rough seas and heavy rain. During a difficult sailing maneuver, [[Desmond]] slips on the rigging, failing to secure a rope, which results in a massive wave broadsiding the vessel. Enraged, [[Mr. Plugg]] orders that both [[Desmond]] and [[Devi McRae]] be immediately disciplined on the deck.

To prevent [[Master Scourge]] from brutally whipping the twins, [[Torrin Horrendous]] boldly intervenes. She proposes administering "shock therapy" using her lightning magic as a more efficient and painful punishment, which [[Mr. Plugg]] permits. [[Torrin Horrendous]] casts [[Shocking Grasp]] on both [[Desmond]] and [[Devi McRae]]. The twins play along with theatrical screams, thrashing about the deck in mock agony, which thoroughly delights and satisfies [[Mr. Plugg]].

As night falls, the weather escalates into a full-on tropical storm. With the ship in danger of breaching, [[Torrin Horrendous]] runs to the front of the ship and casts [[Light]] on the figurehead/prow, illuminating the path with 40 feet of light to help [[Mr. Plugg]] navigate through the treacherous reefs. During the frantic maneuvers, [[Desmond]] hobbles to [[Torrin Horrendous]] and [[Lanny]], whispering that this chaotic storm might be the perfect opportunity to strike and launch their mutiny. [[Torrin Horrendous]] strongly advises against a mutiny during a storm, insisting they prioritize keeping the ship afloat.

The argument is cut short when the [[Man's Promise]] is suddenly ambushed by a swarm of tentacled, goblin-like octopoid creatures known as [[Grindylows]]. The beasts swarm the deck, attacking the crew. Using [[Thormaturgy]] to amplify her voice, [[Torrin Horrendous]] shouts a warning across the entire ship for all able men to take up arms.

During the combat, a grindylow uses its tentacles to trip [[Lanny]], knocking him prone. On his turn, [[Lanny]] stands up, enters a rage, and delivers a brutal overhand swipe, driving his weapon through a grindylow and pinning it to the deck for 14 points of damage. [[Torrin Horrendous]] battles the creatures using [[Chromatic Orb]] (black lightning) and [[Toll the Dead]], while [[Devi McRae]] stabs and skewers another with his [[Rapier]].

In the middle of the melee, a loud shuddering crash echoes through the ship as the [[Man's Promise]] collides with a hidden reef. Despite the collision, the party successfully slays several of the beasts, and the remaining [[Grindylows]] retreat and flee back into the dark ocean. [[Mr. Plugg]] remains at the wheel, frantically ordering the crew back to their posts to save the ship.

With the battle over, the party counts heads and makes a terrifying discovery: both [[Rosie Cusswell]] and [[Sandara Quinn]] are missing. They have been swept overboard during the chaotic struggle and are nowhere to be found.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Mr. Plugg]]**: The furious captain of the [[Man's Promise]] who demands the twins be disciplined and struggles to steer the ship through the storm.
*   **[[Torrin Horrendous]]**: The party's wizard who uses [[Shocking Grasp]] to spare the twins a whipping and later uses [[Light]] to help navigate the storm.
*   **[[Desmond]]**: The monk who slips on the rigging, leading to [[Mr. Plugg]]'s disciplinary action.
*   **[[Devi McRae]]**: The rogue who plays along with [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s mock punishment.
*   **[[Lanny]]**: The barbarian who is tripped by a grindylow but rages and smashes it into the deck.
*   **[[Grindylows]]**: The tentacled, goblin-like aquatic creatures that ambush the ship.
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: The cleric who is discovered missing and presumed swept overboard.
*   **[[Rosie Cusswell]]**: The halfling who is discovered missing and presumed swept overboard.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Man's Promise]]**: The vessel currently navigating a violent tropical storm and a reef system.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **The Grindylow Ambush**: The crew defends the deck against a sudden swarm of [[Grindylows]] climbing aboard during the storm.
*   **Reef Collision**: The [[Man's Promise]] runs aground on a hidden reef during the combat.

## Loot & Rewards
