# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 12: Posh Ghouls and the Hanging Man]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 5 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The morning aboard the [[Man's Promise]] begins with the party dealing with the aftermath of a devastating storm. [[Mr. Plugg]] reveals to [[Torrin Horrendous]] his ulterior motives for sending the twins, [[Devi McRae]] and [[Desmond]], to explore a nearby island: he hopes to run them into the ground so they return exhausted and easy to manage, planning to let out his frustrations on [[Desmond]] specifically. 

The party, consisting of [[Devi McRae]], [[Desmond]], [[Lanny]], and [[Torrin Horrendous]], sets off in a [[Rowboat]] to search for fresh water. Upon landing, they discover an [[Abandoned Village]] and follow a path into a [[Swampy Jungle]]. They encounter a [[Boggy Mire]] with only the posts of a crumbled bridge to cross. There, they are ambushed by a pair of [[Giant Toads]]. After defeating the toads, they continue deeper into the jungle until they reach a clearing containing a large, chaotic tent hung around a massive tree.

Entering the tent, they find humanoid remains smeared with blood and swarming with insects. Suddenly, three undead [[Ghouls]] in rotting, high-society [[Courtiers' Outfits]] awaken to attack. A brief, chaotic combat ensues: [[Desmond]] disengages and runs, pursued by a ghoul. [[Devi McRae]] sneaks around to execute a sneak attack with his [[Rapier]] for major damage, while [[Lanny]] enters a [[Rage]] and obliterates a ghoul with his [[Maul]]. After some close calls, including [[Devi McRae]] resisting a paralyzing cold touch from a ghoul, the undead are destroyed.

Upon searching the tent, the party recovers two chests containing valuable [[Gems]] worth several hundred gold pieces, along with rotted clothing and skeletal remains. Proceeding cautiously around the coast, [[Devi McRae]] takes the lead. He senses a rumbling beneath the ground before Lanny is suddenly clamped in the mandibles of a [[Large Insect-like Creature]] (an [[Ankheg]]). The creature spews a line of acid, coating [[Lanny]] and [[Torrin Horrendous]] for substantial damage. [[Lanny]] breaks the grapple, raging and retaliating with his [[Maul]], while [[Torrin Horrendous]] unleashes a [[Chromatic Orb]] (lightning) and [[Devi McRae]] throws his last [[Dagger]] to finally slay the beast. The party then takes a short rest to recuperate.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Mr. Plugg]]**: The cruel first mate who orchestrates the island expedition to exhaust and weaken the party.
*   **[[Ghouls]]**: Three flesh-eating undead dressed in rotted high-society garments who ambush the party inside their tent.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Man's Promise]]**: The captured merchant vessel currently anchored off the coast.
*   **[[Abandoned Village]]**: A ghost town on the shore of the island.
*   **[[Swampy Jungle]]**: The dense, boggy interior of the island.
*   **[[Boggy Mire]]**: A swampy crossing with crumbled bridge posts where the toad ambush occurs.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[Giant Toads]] Ambush**: A combat encounter at the swamp crossing where the party fends off two giant, hungry amphibians.
*   **Tent [[Ghouls]]**: A fight inside the bloody tent against three surprisingly fast, rotting undead.
*   **[[Large Insect-like Creature]] (An [[Ankheg]])**: A deadly encounter with an underground insect that clamps [[Lanny]] and spews acid before being slain by the party's combined efforts.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Gems]]**: A collection of valuable gemstones recovered from two chests inside the ghouls' tent, worth at least a couple of hundred gold pieces.
