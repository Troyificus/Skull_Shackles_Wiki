# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 20: Weaponized Honesty and the Wizard Rodeo]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 19 at [[Rickety's Squibs]] (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The tense standoff at the shipyard docks is resolved as the party engages in an amicable parley with the newly arrived pirate captain, [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]]. Handing his greatsword to a crewman, [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]] approaches unarmed with a warm smile. [[Lanny]] (acting as [[Captain Skull Crusher]]) and [[Torrin Horrendous]] (acting as [[Miss Blackheart]]) raise their hands in peace. [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]] reveals that he commands a fleet of four ships, with his flagship being the [[Pinion]]. He explains that [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] is "absolutely no friend" of his and is highly amused to learn that the party mutinied against and disposed of [[Gunter Plugg]] (referred to as [[Mr. Plugg]] or [[Gunter Plug]]) to steal the ship. He invites the party to visit his home port at [[Goat's Head]] on the [[Fire Grass Isles]] and offers to establish mutual trust.

To aid [[Rickety Hake]] and ensure the safety of the shipyard, the party agrees to investigate the nearby flag tower, which has failed to raise its warning flag. [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]] decides to join them on the hike up the ridge. 

While navigating the dense canopy trail, the group is ambushed by a pair of wild boars. A frantic combat ensues. Amidst the chaos, a boar of immense size—a raging "mama pig"—charges out of the shadows and bombards directly into [[Desmond]], inflicting a deep, gashing chest wound that knocks him unconscious. Showing incredible bravado, [[Torrin Horrendous]] teleports herself directly onto the thrashing beast's back, holding on for dear life while administering severe electrical shocks using [[Shocking Grasp]] that blacken and crisp the creature's fur. [[Lanny]] goes into a furious rage and pile-drives his heavy [[Maul]] directly onto the giant pig's forehead, crushing its skull and ending the threat. 

After [[Desmond]] is stabilized and healed, the group presses on to the stilt-mounted wooden tower. There, they discover the lifeless body of [[Lyle the Lookout]] (the lookout), whose neck is marked by a grotesque, swollen wound—likely the work of the giant wasps. Among the debris in the tower, they find a poorly treated, plucked [[Parrot]] in a sorry state, which takes a liking to [[Desmond]] and sits on his hand. The party wraps [[Lyle the Lookout]]'s body in a hammock to carry back for a proper burial. As they begin their trek back to the shipyard, dragging the pig carcasses, [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]] begins to regale the party with the tragic backstory of [[Owlbear Albert]], ending the session on a cliffhanger.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]]**: A peg-legged pirate captain commanding four ships, including the [[Pinion]]. He forms a tentative alliance with the party and joins their excursion.
*   **[[Lyle the Lookout]]**: The shipyard lookout found dead of wasp stings in the flag tower.
*   **[[Parrot]]**: A plucked, mistreated bird found in the tower that bonds with [[Desmond]].
*   **[[Rickety Hake]]**: Shipyard owner who requested the flag tower investigation.
*   **[[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]]**: Mentioned as a common enemy of both [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]] and the party.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Rickety's Squibs]]**: The main shipyard docks where the party parleys with [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]].
*   **[[The Flag Tower]]**: A stilt-mounted lookout hut situated on a high ridge overlooking the sea.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[The Boar Backwoods Fray]]**: The party defeats a pair of wild boars and an enormous "mama pig" on the ridge trail. [[Desmond]] is knocked unconscious by a charge, [[Torrin Horrendous]] rides the beast's back while casting [[Shocking Grasp]], and [[Lanny]] delivers the killing blow.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Plucked Parrot]]**: Rescued from the flag tower by [[Desmond]].
*   **[[Lyle's Body]]**: Retrieved for a proper burial.
*   **[[Boar Carcasses]]**: Harvested for meat to feed the shipyard commune.
