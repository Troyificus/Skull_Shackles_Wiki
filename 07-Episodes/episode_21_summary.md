# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 21: Mind Wipes and Mercenary Parrots]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 15 to Day 18 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
On the trek back from the flag tower to the shipyard, [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]] of the [[Pinion]] shares the tragic backstory of [[Owlbear Albert]] (originally known simply as [[Albert]] or [[The Albert]]). 

Years ago, [[Albert]] and [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] were young rival pirate captains on the docks of [[Port Peril]]. Their rivalry came to a head at [[The Slippery Whale]] tavern, culminating in a legendary bar brawl where [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] smashed a bottle over [[Albert]]'s face, blinding him in one eye. In retaliation, [[Albert]] burned [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]]'s ship. Enraged by the destruction, [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] paid off three other captains to ambush [[Albert]], executed his entire crew before his eyes, and had his [[Sorceress]] cast a powerful mind-wipe spell to turn [[Albert]] into the bumbling, simple-minded giant he is today. [[Torrin Horrendous]] consults with [[Sandara Quinn]] about whether restorative magic could reverse this condition, but both agree it is far beyond their current magical capabilities.

[[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]] also advises the party on how to officially become [[Free Captains]] of the [[Shackles]], explaining that they must gather enough [[Infamy]] to warrant an audience with the [[Hurricane King Karch Bonefist]] at [[Port Peril]]. 

The group returns to [[Rickety Hake]]'s dockyard, delivering the body of the lookout [[Lyle the Lookout]] wrapped in a hammock. [[Rickety Hake]] provides a refund on their rental of the horse and cart and prepares a proper burial for [[Lyle the Lookout]]. That evening, a grand celebratory feast is held. [[Devi McRae]] tells an embellished story of the party's victory in the [[Grindylow Cave]] (rolling a high [[Performance]] check with advantage), successfully recruiting several of [[Rosie Cusswell]]'s halfling companions to join their crew. 

Meanwhile, [[Lanny]] casts [[Speak with Animals]] to converse with [[Desmond]]'s newly acquired, plucked [[Parrot]]. The parrot, who is highly aggressive but quickly pacified by food, bonds with the party.

The party sets sail south for [[Blood Cove]] on their ship, the [[Sea Breeze]] (formerly the [[Man's Promise]]). On the voyage, [[Ambrose Kroop]] (aka [[Fishguts]]) remains remarkably sober and warns the crew that [[Blood Cove]] is run strictly on mercantile business; they must behave themselves to avoid drawing the wrath of the local militia. 

Upon arrival, the party negotiates with [[Dock Master Tesha Ambertine]], paying harbor fees of [[8 gold pieces]] per day upfront for a week's stay. They register the vessel under the name [[Sea Breeze]], officially listing [[Captain Skull Crusher]] (Lanny) as captain and [[Dev McRae]] (Devi McRae) as first mate. To prevent a panic in the streets of [[Blood Cove]], they decide to transport the bound and unconscious [[Gunter Plugg]] below deck inside a wooden [[Crate]] carried by the colossal [[Lanny]].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]]**: The peg-legged captain of the Chelish galleon [[Pinion]], who shares [[Albert]]'s tragic backstory and acts as an amicable pirate advisor.
*   **[[Owlbear Albert]]**: The party's simple-minded giant companion, revealed to have once been a proud rival pirate captain named [[Albert]].
*   **[[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]]**: The cruel captain of the [[Wormwood]] who executed [[Albert]]'s crew and had his mind wiped.
*   **[[Rickety Hake]]**: The master of the squibbing yard at [[Rickety's Squibs]] who concludes business with the party.
*   **[[Lyle the Lookout]]**: The deceased lookout of [[Rickety's Squibs]], whose body is returned to the yard.
*   **[[Ambrose Kroop]]**: The ship's cook who provides sober and practical advice about [[Blood Cove]].
*   **[[Dock Master Tesha Ambertine]]**: The strict bureaucrat who registers the [[Sea Breeze]] upon arrival in [[Blood Cove]].
*   **[[Plucked Parrot]]**: A highly aggressive, mostly featherless bird rescued from [[Lyle the Lookout]]'s tower who bonds with [[Desmond]].

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Rickety's Squibs]]**: The coastal dry dock and estuary where the ship's refitting is completed.
*   **[[The Slippery Whale]]**: A tavern in [[Port Peril]] mentioned as the location of [[Harrigan]] and [[Albert]]'s legendary bar brawl.
*   **[[Port Peril]]**: The pirate capital of the [[Shackles]].
*   **[[Blood Cove]]**: A sprawling, wealthy port town outside the [[Shackles]] built on massive mangrove roots.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **Recruitment Drive**: [[Devi McRae]] performs an elaborate retelling of the [[Grindylow]] battle, converting a crowd of halflings and dockworkers into loyal crew members.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Plucked Parrot]]**: Rescued from the watchtower and kept as a pet.
*   **[[Pig Carcasses]]**: Harvested from the ridge to provide a week of fresh pork feast for the crew.
