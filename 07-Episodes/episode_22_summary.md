# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 22: The Silent Howl and the Cursed Greatsword]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 19 and Day 20 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
After docking the [[Sea Breeze]] at [[Blood Cove]], the party ventures ashore to explore the sprawling port town. Twin brothers [[Devi McRae]] and [[Desmond]] immediately seek out local entertainment and head to a local gambling house owned by [[Shadook]] (or [[Shidduch]]). 

At the gaming tables, the twins play poker and [[Dice]], winning a few silver pieces. They inquire about purchasing a sleeker, faster ship and [[Shadook]] points them in the direction of [[Captain Glade Fletcher]], a retired sea dog who possesses a highly nimble ship he might be willing to part with. [[Shadook]] also tells them about a bare-knuckle boxing event scheduled for the following evening. Desmond, possessing a keen interest in fighting, secures a test bout for later that night against [[Shadook]]'s cousin, [[Tank]].

Meanwhile, [[Torrin Horrendous]] and [[Lanny]] browse the shops along the elevated wooden gangways of [[Blood Cove]]. They visit the [[Umbertine Arms and Armor]] store, where [[Torrin Horrendous]] identifies some magical armor, and [[Lanny]]'s attention is strangely captivated by an incredibly heavy, cursed [[Greatsword]].

Later, the party reunites at [[The Blood Root]] tavern to meet with [[Captain Glade Fletcher]]. The limping old captain agrees that he might be willing to sell his ship, [[The Silent Howl]], but the transaction requires a significant favor: the party must retrieve a buried cache of treasure located at a once-idyllic cove that has mysteriously transformed into a dangerous, haunted [[Death Trap]] surrounded by shipwrecks. [[Captain Glade Fletcher]] also promises to use his local contacts to find a buyer for the party's current ship, the [[Sea Breeze]].

That evening, the crew returns to [[Shadook]]'s gambling house for [[Desmond]]'s test fight. Inside a makeshift ring, [[Desmond]] easily beats [[Tank]] to a pulp, earning a spot in the main event against the reigning local champion, [[The Pugilist]]. [[Shadook]] is so impressed that he agrees to let [[Lanny]] participate in a fight the following night as well. The episode ends as the party returns to the [[Sea Breeze]] to rest before the big fight night.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Shadook]]**: The proprietor of a local [[Blood Cove]] gambling den who organizes the bare-knuckle fights and guides the party to [[Captain Glade Fletcher]].
*   **[[Captain Glade Fletcher]]**: A friendly, limping retired captain who offers to sell [[The Silent Howl]] in exchange for a treasure retrieval favor.
*   **[[Tank]]**: [[Shadook]]'s brawler cousin who is soundly defeated by [[Desmond]] in a test bout.
*   **[[The Pugilist]]**: The local bare-knuckle boxing champion scheduled to fight [[Desmond]].

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Blood Cove]]**: The grand trade port built into the side of a colossal mangrove tree.
*   **[[Umbertine Arms and Armor]]**: A local armory containing dwarven iron furnishings and unique armaments.
*   **[[The Blood Root]]**: A local tavern where the party parleys with [[Captain Glade Fletcher]].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[Desmond]] vs. [[Tank]]**: A brief test bout in [[Shadook]]'s fighting ring. [[Desmond]] easily outmaneuvers and defeats [[Tank]] without taking any significant damage.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **Silver Coins**: Won by [[Desmond]] at the gambling tables.
