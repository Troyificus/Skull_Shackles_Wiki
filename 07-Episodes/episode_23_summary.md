# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 23: Buying Loyalty and Ripping Off Arms]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 21 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The morning begins aboard the [[Sea Breeze]]. [[Ambrose Kroop]] requests funds to purchase fresh kitchen supplies, and the party happily provides gold from their ship's kitty. [[Lanny]] hands out gold payments to the crew, including the [[Rahadoumi]] sailors. 

During the payments, [[Halle]] (or [[Hella]]) makes it known that she wishes to leave the ship and return to her homeland. Having been treated well despite her initial capture, [[Halle]] and the party part on good terms, with [[Devi McRae]] releasing her hand with a warm smile. She and her two male companions (including [[Sargava]]) depart the ship, leaving the party with only [[Owlbear Albert]] and [[Conchobhar Shortstone]] on board.

With the ship cleared, the party decides that [[Gunter Plugg]] has outstayed his welcome. Fearing that [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] can magically track [[Gunter Plugg]]'s physical form or possessions, they decide to permanently incapacitate him.

Lanny, Torrin, Devi, and Desmond descend into the damp bilges. [[Lanny]] rages, puts his foot against [[Gunter Plugg]]'s chest for leverage, and takes hold of his left arm. With a brutal twist and a massive pull, [[Lanny]] tears [[Gunter Plugg]]'s left arm completely off at the shoulder joint. To prevent him from dying, [[Torrin Horrendous]] immediately casts [[Cure Wounds]] to seal the bleeding stump, waking the former first mate back to consciousness. 

[[Torrin Horrendous]] then interrogates [[Gunter Plugg]]. Showing zero remorse for his past actions, [[Gunter Plugg]] is subjected to brutal torture. [[Torrin Horrendous]] channels multiple casts of [[Shocking Grasp]], repeatedly knocking him unconscious and shocking him back to wakefulness. They stuff Gunter's smoldering body head-first into a wooden [[Barrel]] along with his severed arm. The party then heads back up to deck to enjoy a peaceful breakfast of bacon, fried eggs, and blood pudding. 

During the day, [[Torrin Horrendous]] copies spells into her [[Spellbook]] while [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] prepare for the fights. That evening, the party heads to [[Shadook]]'s gambling house. The room is packed to standing room only, and the odds are posted on massive chalkboards. [[Devi McRae]] bets heavily on his companions.

In the first fight, [[Lanny]] (fighting as [[Captain Skull Crusher]]) faces the dwarven brawler [[Braden the Brawler Brimstone]]. [[Lanny]] fights recklessly and easily pummels [[Braden the Brawler Brimstone]] into submission, winning [[50 gold pieces]] for his victory. 

In the main event, [[Desmond]] enters the ring against [[The Pugilist]]. They trade fast, brutal blows, but [[Desmond]] struggles to connect his signature [[Flurry of Blows]]. The episode ends on a high-stakes cliffhanger mid-fight as both combatants trade heavy strikes in the center of the ring.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Ambrose Kroop]]**: The ship's cook who receives funds to gather fresh kitchen supplies.
*   **[[Halle]]**: A female [[Rahadoumi]] sailor who is granted permission to leave the crew and return to her homeland.
*   **[[Gunter Plugg]]**: The bound captive who is brutally dismembered by [[Lanny]] and tortured by [[Torrin Horrendous]] in the bilges.
*   **[[Owlbear Albert]]**: The party's simple-minded companion who is kept safe aboard the ship.
*   **[[Conchobhar Shortstone]]**: The gnome dandy assigned to guard the ship's gangplank during the fight night.
*   **[[Braden the Brawler Brimstone]]**: A dwarven brawler defeated by [[Lanny]] in the undercard match.
*   **[[The Pugilist]]**: The local champion who engages in a brutal, even-matched brawl with [[Desmond]].
*   **[[Shadook]]**: The fight organizer who coordinates the bets and matches.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Blood Cove]]**: The grand trade port where the fights are held.
*   **[[Sea Breeze]]**: The party's ship, specifically the dark and flooded bilges.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **Dismemberment of Plugg**: [[Lanny]] tears [[Gunter Plugg]]'s left arm off in the bilges while [[Torrin Horrendous]] uses [[Shocking Grasp]] to torture him.
*   **[[Lanny]] vs. [[Braden the Brawler Brimstone]]**: An undercard match where [[Lanny]] dominates [[Braden]] with raw power and reckless strikes.
*   **[[Desmond]] vs. [[The Pugilist]]**: The main event of [[Blood Cove]]'s fight night, featuring a highly technical, brutal fistfight.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Gunter Plugg's Left Arm]]**: Kept in the barrel as a gruesome trophy/item.
*   **[[50 gold pieces]]**: Won by [[Lanny]] for winning his boxing match.
