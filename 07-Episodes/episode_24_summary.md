# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 24: Owlbear Handshakes and Unread Vampire Contracts]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 16 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Following the epic, hard-fought victory at [[Chidux's Gambling Den]] (also known as [[Shadook's Gambling Den]]), the crowd erupts into deafening cheers. [[Desmond]] stands in the center of the ring, victorious after knocking out the local champion, [[The Pugilist]], with a devastating uppercut. [[Torrin Horrendous]] steps forward and heals [[Desmond]]'s dislocated shoulder and severely bruised jaw using a Level 3 [[Cure Wounds]] spell.

Carried by the towering goliath brawler [[Lanny]] (who also won his undercard match against the dwarven brawler [[Braden the Brawler Brimstone]]), a drunken and battered [[Desmond]] is escorted back to their ship, the [[Sea Breeze]]. On the way, the party stops by a street vendor's cart to buy hot pies to celebrate with the rest of the crew.

At the ship, [[Owlbear Albert]] (who has finally been cleaned of his tar and feathers) stands guard alongside [[Sandara Quinn]] and [[Rosie Cusswell]]. The next morning, [[Captain Glade Fletcher]] arrives at the dock with a potential buyer for the [[Sea Breeze]]: [[Mr. Girdan Danan]], an elegant representative of the wealthy [[House Carthagen]] (secretly run by vampires).

[[Mr. Girdan Danan]] conducts a thorough inspection of the ship, examining the Captain's quarters (including [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s newly acquired magical [[Orca-Jaw Harp]] and inscribed [[Jawbone]] spell scrolls), the cargo hold, and the bilges where [[Gunter Plugg]] had recently been executed. 

[[Mr. Girdan Danan]] offers [[4,000 gold pieces]] (to be paid in [[400 platinum pieces]]) for the vessel. The party successfully barters and coordinates the transfer. [[Captain Glade Fletcher]] brings his nimble, fast ship, the [[Silent Howl]], alongside the [[Sea Breeze]] by lunchtime. The crew immediately begins transferring all their plundered cargo, magical items, and personal gear to their new vessel.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Captain Glade Fletcher]]**: The friendly captain who arranged the sale of the [[Sea Breeze]] and traded his nimble vessel, the [[Silent Howl]].
*   **[[Mr. Girdan Danan]]**: The sophisticated buyer representing [[House Carthagen]].
*   **[[House Carthagen]]**: A highly influential and wealthy merchant house in [[Blood Cove]], secretly run by vampires.
*   **[[The Pugilist]]**: The formidable local boxing champion defeated by [[Desmond]].
*   **[[Owlbear Albert]]**: The reformed, mind-wiped goliath companion of the party.
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]] & [[Rosie Cusswell]]**: Handled guard duties on the ship.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Blood Cove]]**: The lawless, thriving trade port where the transaction takes place.
*   **[[Chidux's Gambling Den]]** / **[[Shadook's Gambling Den]]**: The location of the bare-knuckle fight night.
*   **[[The Sea Breeze]]**: The party's refitted merchant ship (formerly the [[Man's Promise]]), officially sold to [[House Carthagen]].
*   **[[The Silent Howl]]**: The party's newly acquired, exceptionally fast ship.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[Desmond]] vs. [[The Pugilist]]**: Concludes with [[Desmond]] landing a glorious uppercut to knock out the champion.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[4,000 gold pieces]]** (paid as **[[400 platinum pieces]]** by [[Mr. Girdan Danan]] for the [[Sea Breeze]]).
*   **[[The Silent Howl]]**: Their sleek new ship.
