# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 26: Botched Heist Spirals Into Zombie Horror]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Days 18-20 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The high-stakes chase with [[The Dominator]] reaches a boiling point. [[Torrin Horrendous]] uses [[Misty Step]] to sneak onto a decorative overhang on the hull of [[The Dominator]] to sabotage it, but rolls another **natural 1** on her stealth check, getting immediately spotted.

Cornered by Chelish officers, [[Torrin Horrendous]] defends herself by casting [[Mirror Image]]. An officer attacks her, destroying her duplicates. She retaliates by unleashing a devastating Level 3 [[Dragon's Breath]] (fire damage) to burn the officers and set fire to the ship's rigging. She then uses [[Misty Step]] to leap 20 feet out of a cabin window into the dark ocean below.

The [[Silent Howl]] picks up speed and sails directly into the line of fire to rescue [[Torrin Horrendous]] and [[Devi McRae]] (who was hanging over the side). [[Lanny]] and [[Desmond]] struggle to haul them up but manage to pull them safely aboard.

Enraged, the crew of [[The Dominator]] unleashes a volley of heavy crossbow bolts and fires their rear catapult. A massive projectile strikes the hull of the [[Silent Howl]] above the water line, causing repairable damage. [[Captain Glade Fletcher]] is struck by two crossbow bolts, including a critical hit to the clavicle, and collapses. [[Sandara Quinn]] bravely rushes to the stern castle to take the wheel and keep the ship on course. Due to the severe fire damage on [[The Dominator]]'s rigging and rudder ropes, the Chelish warship is left floundering in flames as the [[Silent Howl]] escapes into the night.

Over the next two days of sailing, the party recovers during a long rest. They pass the [[Fire Grass Isles]] and [[Goat's Head]] before reaching the [[Ongongta Overlook]] on the mainland, where [[Captain Glade Fletcher]] has his treasure buried.

To avoid the "graveyard of wrecks" clogging the cove, they trek through a dense, humid jungle to reach the top of the cliff. Everyone except [[Torrin Horrendous]] rolls terribly on their Constitution checks and gains a point of [[Exhaustion]] from the grueling climb.

Using the [[Steadfast Grapple]] and extra rope, they abseil down onto the sandy beach. [[Torrin Horrendous]] casts [[Detect Magic]] and senses a magical aura buried next to a rock. As they begin to dig, the ocean waves roll in, and a shambling horde of bloated, rotting [[Brine Zombies]] carrying rusted blades emerges from the surf to attack them. The session ends on a terrifying cliffhanger.
