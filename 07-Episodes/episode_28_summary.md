# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 28: Dez's Lightning Pact and the Alien Dagger]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 16 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The episode begins with [[Desmond]] in a state of death-like unconsciousness on the beach of the [[Ongongta Overlook]]. While unconscious, his soul is transported to a divine realm where he stands on the deck of an ancient, mysterious ship suspended within an immense sphere of ocean water. He is greeted by [[Besmara]] herself, the Pirate Queen. [[Besmara]] reveals that she has placed a heavy bet on the fate of the [[Shackles]], which is threatened by a looming, poisonous danger. She offers [[Desmond]] a pact: to become her champion, grow strong, and gather his companions to defend her domain. [[Desmond]] accepts the deal and is instantly revived back on the beach, his skin now glowing with a fresh, crackling electrical energy and a new skull-and-crossbones tattoo emblazoned on his skin.

With [[Desmond]] back on his feet and boasting his newfound powers, the party escapes the beach and climbs back up the cliff using the [[Steadfast Grapple]], leaving the remaining [[Brine Zombies]] behind. Upon reaching the shoreline, [[Desmond]] tests his divine connection by placing [[Sandara Quinn's Tricorn Hat]] upside down in the surf. Willing it to change, the magical hat successfully transforms into a functional rowing boat with an anchor, providing a safe transport back to their ship.

Reboarding the [[Silent Howl]], the party is warmly greeted by [[Captain Glade Fletcher]], who is ecstatic to see his [[Chest of Gold]] safely recovered. When they describe the terrifying zombie ambush, [[Captain Glade Fletcher]] is thoroughly bewildered, swearing there were no shipwrecks or undead on the beach when he buried his cache twenty years ago. 

Before handing the chest over, [[Devi McRae]] orchestrates a ruse, asking [[Torrin Horrendous]] to use her magical senses to identify the items inside so they might pocket some of the loot. [[Torrin Horhendous]] performs a ritual of [[Identify]] on the locked chest. As [[Captain Glade Fletcher]] unlocks the chest with the key from his neck, they find a massive hoard of gold. However, [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s magical senses immediately zero in on a highly unusual, ominous item at the bottom of the chest: a mysterious, golden, twin-plated dagger that seems to hum with ancient and foreign magic.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Besmara]]**: The Pirate Queen of the [[Shackles]], who makes a divine pact with [[Desmond]] and appoints him as her champion.
*   **[[Captain Glade Fletcher]]**: The retired owner of the [[Silent Howl]], who opens his recovered chest of treasure.
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: The party's cleric ally whose magical tricorn hat transforms into a boat.
*   **[[Zima]]**: [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s bat familiar, summoned to watch over the party.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Ongongta Overlook]]**: The cliffside and haunted beach where the party recovers the chest.
*   **[[Silent Howl]]**: The party's fast new ship where they reunite with [[Captain Glade Fletcher]].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **The Divine Communion**: [[Desmond]]'s near-death vision and pact-making with the goddess [[Besmara]].

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Chest of Gold]]**: Recovered from the beach, containing [[4,000 gold pieces]] and magical items.
*   **[[Golden Twin-Plated Dagger]]**: A mysterious, potentially cursed magical weapon discovered at the bottom of [[Captain Glade Fletcher]]'s chest.
