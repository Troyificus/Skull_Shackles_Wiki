# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 29: Taser Bats on the Ghost Ship]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 17 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Aboard the [[Silent Howl]], the party examines the mysterious [[Golden Twin-Plated Dagger]] found in [[Captain Glade Fletcher]]'s chest. To prevent the captain from throwing it away out of fear, [[Torrin Horrendous]] casts a "protective spell" over it—a successful ruse backed by [[Devi McRae]]'s dirty 20 deception check. Believing the danger is contained, [[Captain Glade Fletcher]] locks the chest and requests to be dropped off at [[Goat's Head]] on the [[Fire Grass Isles]] to retire, leaving the ship fully in the party's possession. In private, [[Torrin Horrendous]] informs [[Devi McRae]] that the dagger is heavily enchanted to draw blood and would make an excellent, high-risk weapon for [[Desmond]].

As they sail, [[Desmond]] enthusiastically tests his new divine powers, launching crackling bolts of [[Eldritch Blast]] into the sky, which [[Lanny]] watches with childlike wonder. While [[Sandara Quinn]] celebrates [[Desmond]]'s blessing from [[Besmara]], [[Torrin Horrendous]] remains deeply skeptical, harboring dark suspicions about [[Desmond]]'s powers and privately praying to her own dark patron, [[Myrkul]].

The next day, they spot a crippled, waterlogged fishing boat drifting off-course. Upon closer inspection, the vessel is breached, and they are ambushed by four aggressive [[Sahuagin]] (fish-like humanoids) wielding crude spears. [[Lanny]] and [[Devi McRae]] dive into the ocean to engage them. Entering a rage, [[Lanny]] delivers a devastating reckless swing with his [[Maul]], pulverizing one Sahuagin and heavily wounding another. [[Torrin Horrendous]] attempts to launch her familiar [[Zima]] to deliver a [[Shocking Grasp]], but she misjudges the distance, flying past the target. [[Desmond]] uses his sentinel abilities to punch the remaining foes, and [[Lanny]] crushes the skull of the final escaping creature.

With the deck clear, [[Zima]] spots two more [[Sahuagin]] fleeing into the depths. [[Devi McRae]] and [[Lanny]] dive into the flooded hold of the fishing boat. They discover the fresh, dead bodies of the human crew. [[Devi McRae]] takes a vial of blood from one of the corpses and salvages a strange [[Dull Gray Metal Necklace]] that glints strangely in the light. 

Returning to the [[Silent Howl]], [[Desmond]] ties a rope around his waist and jumps overboard, letting [[Lanny]] drag him through the ship's wake to inspect the bottom of their hull. Confirmed clear of any sea monsters, they set sail once more toward [[Goat's Head]].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Captain Glade Fletcher]]**: The retiring captain who agrees to hand over the [[Silent Howl]] once they reach [[Goat's Head]].
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: The cleric who interprets [[Desmond]]'s new powers as a true blessing.
*   **[[Zima]]**: [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s bat familiar used to deliver touch spells and scout the enemy deck.
*   **[[Sahuagin]]**: Feral, fish-like sea monsters that ambushed the crippled fishing boat.
*   **[[Myrkul]]**: The dark god of the dead to whom [[Torrin Horrendous]] prays.
*   **[[Besmara]]**: The pirate goddess whose power now flows through [[Desmond]].

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Silent Howl]]**: The party's main ship, currently navigating the [[Slithering Coast]].
*   **[[Crippled Fishing Boat]]**: A waterlogged wreck adrift on the sea, used by [[Sahuagin]] as an ambush site.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[Sahuagin]] Ambush**: A fast-paced ocean combat against four fish-men guarding their waterlogged prize.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Dull Gray Metal Necklace]]**: Salvaged from the corpse of a dead fisherman in the flooded hold, emitting a strange magical glint.
*   **[[Vial of Blood]]**: Collected by [[Devi McRae]] from a fresh corpse for [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s future experiments.
