# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 32: Dressing Up Zombies and Drinking Fish Slime]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 20 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Back aboard the [[Silent Howl]], [[Devi McRae]] and [[Torrin Horrendous]] dress [[Zombie Plugg]] in chainmail and a hooded cloak to disguise his rotting frame. Suddenly, the door to the captain's quarters opens, revealing [[Captain Glade Fletcher]]. Horrified by the sight of the zombie, the captain fumbles and draws his short sword. [[Torrin Horrendous]] quickly rolls a 22 on a persuasion check, successfully spinning a ruse: she claims the zombie is a temporary curse caused by the magical [[Golden Twin-Plated Dagger]] they recovered in the chest. She bluffs that they must keep the zombie locked in the cabin until they set sail, at which point throwing the dagger into the ocean will break the spell. A relieved Fletcher agrees, changes his shirt, and leaves, promising to return at sunset to finalize the ship's sale.

Meanwhile, [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] continue their "pretend-drunk" pub crawl to search for syndicate leads. They head to [[The Hagfish]], where [[Lanny]] enters a famous local dare: drinking a mug of [[Nora]]'s slimy juices—the putrid mucus secreted from a live hagfish's skin. [[Lanny]] incredibly manages to keep the vile, reprocessed-flesh-tasting slime down. For winning the dare, [[Lanny]] is awarded a pouch containing [[37 gold pieces]], and [[Nora]] carves his name, "[[Captain Skull Crusher]]", into the bar's wall above the tank.

[[Torrin Horrendous]] visits [[Hannah]] (number 6), a local herbalist on the bottom-left of the map, purchasing a variety of pungent herbs and potpourri. She stuffs these into [[Zombie Plugg]]'s clothing and orifices to successfully mask his rotting stench.

At sunset, [[Father Torbin]] and a red-haired female cleric arrive at the [[Silent Howl]] to perform the restoration ritual on [[Owlbear Albert]]. As the procedure begins, [[Albert]] becomes highly nervous, but [[Sandara Quinn]] uses magic to force him to hold still. The spell is completed successfully. [[Albert]]'s eyes open, dazed but fully restored to his true, intelligent self. He tearfully recounts his tragic backstory, remembering that [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] slaughtered his crew, burned his ship, and had his sorceress wipe his mind after [[Albert]] blinded [[Harrigan]] in one eye during a bar fight. The party welcomes [[Albert]] as a full ally, promising to help him seek revenge.

Moments later, the bartender from [[The Fat Man's Feed Bag]] appears at the gangplank, informing [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] that he has found a potential employer. He leads them to the town square, where they are ambushed by a stereotypical pirate captain wearing a green swashbuckler's cloak and eyepatch, flanked by the two bruised half-orcs they beat up in Episode 31. The pirate captain, furious that his crew was accosted, draws his sword. A chaotic battle erupts in the square as [[Lanny]] instantly rages and Reckless Attacks with his [[Maul]], [[Desmond]] activates [[Patient Defense]], and [[Torrin Horrendous]] and [[Devi McRae]] sprint from the ship to join the fray. The episode ends on a high-stakes combat cliffhanger.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Captain Glade Fletcher]]**: The retiring captain of the [[Silent Howl]] who is tricked into believing [[Zombie Plugg]] is a temporary curse.
*   **[[Owlbear Albert]]** (aka **[[Albert]]**): Fully restored to his true, intelligent self by [[Father Torbin]], swearing vengeance against [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]].
*   **[[Father Torbin]]**: The cleric of [[Besmara]] who successfully casts the restoration spell on [[Albert]].
*   **[[Nora]]**: The bartender of [[The Hagfish]] who administers the slime dare.
*   **[[Hannah]]**: The local herbalist who sells [[Torrin Horrendous]] the potpourri.
*   **Pirate Captain**: The unnamed, green-cloaked captain who ambushes [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] in the town square.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Silent Howl]]**: The party's main ship, moored at the docks.
*   **[[The Hagfish]]**: A dockside tavern famous for its slimy hagfish challenge.
*   **[[Hannah]]'s Clinic** (number 6): A small herbalist shop in [[Goat's Head]].
*   **The Town Square**: The site of the climactic pirate ambush.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **Town Square Ambush**: A sudden, high-stakes street combat against the green-cloaked pirate captain and his half-orc bodyguards.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[37 gold pieces]]**: Won by [[Lanny]] for successfully drinking [[Nora]]'s slimy juices at [[The Hagfish]].
*   **[[A Carved Name]]**: [[Lanny]]'s moniker, "[[Captain Skull Crusher]]", is carved into the wall of [[The Hagfish]].
*   **[[Albert's Sanity]]**: [[Albert]]'s mind is fully restored, granting the party a powerful, intelligent ally.
