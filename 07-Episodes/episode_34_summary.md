# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 34: Coconuts, Corpses and the Council of Secrets]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 20 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The episode begins in the pre-dawn darkness as [[Devi McRae]] quietly slips back aboard the ship. Carrying a large, blood-stained sack behind him, he approaches his companions with a peace offering to ease the tension of the previous night’s volatile argument. For his brother [[Desmond]], he presents a fine bottle of rum; for [[Lanny]], a sack filled with fresh coconuts; and for [[Torrin Horrendous]], a fresh dead body to serve as a new subject for her necromantic experiments. 

[[Devi McRae]] offers a sincere apology to the group, explaining his emotional outburst. As a sociopath beginning to genuinely care for his crewmates, his anger stemmed from a fear of starting a self-destructive gang war with [[Goat's Head]]'s powerful local syndicate. 

Before assembling the crew, the four heroes convene a private meeting—dubbed the **[[Council of Secrets]]**—to establish absolute transparency and share their deepest, darkest backstories:
*   **[[Torrin Horrendous]]'s Backstory**: She reveals that she spent years imprisoned and brutally tortured by a sadistic man named [[Blackheart]]. Upon escaping, she adopted his name as her own moniker, [[Lady Blackheart]], to project absolute menace.
*   **[[Lanny]]'s Backstory**: He shares that he was banished from his goliath tribe in the far west. A mysterious, unnatural [[Mist]] rolled over his island; when his chieftain went to investigate, he returned crazed, blood-drenched, and violent. [[Lanny]] was forced to kill his own chieftain in self-defense, resulting in his permanent exile.
*   **The McRae Twins' Backstory**: [[Devi McRae]] and [[Desmond]] reveal that they are on a high-stakes quest for vengeance against their father, [[Gavin McRae]]. Gavin, a narcissistic captain who grandiosely calls himself an admiral, murdered their mother. He currently operates out of [[Tidewater Rock]], a heavily fortified stronghold secured through his marriage to a wealthy [[Chelaxian]] aristocrat. The twins plan to gather [[Infamy]] in the [[Shackles]], gain an audience with [[Hurricane King Karch Bonefist]], and ultimately execute their father, even if it costs them their lives.

With their bonds reinforced, the group officially renames the [[Silent Howl]] to the **[[Blood Wake]]** (or **[[Bloodwake]]**). 

Later that day, [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] explore the town to gather supplies. They visit [[Bottled Solutions]], a pungent local alchemy shop owned by a mysterious [[Alchemist]]. [[Desmond]] purchases specialized brewing ingredients to experiment with while out on the high seas, while [[Lanny]] buys some "experimental" potion samples. 

Having finalized their preparations and paid their dock fees, the crew of the [[Blood Wake]] sets sail from the harbor of [[Goat's Head]], steering southwest out into the treacherous waters of the [[Fever Sea]].

To celebrate their official emergence as independent free captains, the party is bestowed with unique mid-level boons:
*   **[[Devi McRae]]** gains absolute proficiency with the [[Tidewater Cutlass]].
*   **[[Lanny]]** establishes a permanent telepathic bond with his resident rat, [[Rat Damon]] (also known as [[Ratty]] or [[Mr. Rat]]), allowing him to use [[Speak with Animals]] on him at will without expending spell slots.
*   **[[Desmond]]** gains a divine [[Commune]] ability, allowing him to ask his patron [[Besmara]] a single, vague yes-or-no question once per week.
*   **[[Torrin Horrendous]]** receives [[Grave Sense]], allowing her to instinctively detect the presence of any nearby undead.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Alchemist]]**: The eccentric proprietor of the [[Bottled Solutions]] alchemy shop in [[Goat's Head]].
*   **[[Gavin McRae]]**: The narcissistic pirate captain, self-styled admiral, and estranged father of the McRae twins, based at [[Tidewater Rock]].
*   **[[Rat Damon]]** (aka **[[Ratty]]** or **[[Mr. Rat]]**): [[Lanny]]'s loyal shipboard rat companion.
*   **[[Blackheart]]**: The cruel torturer from [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s past whose name she adopted.
*   **[[Ambrose Kroop]]** (aka **[[Fishguts]]**): The loyal ship's cook aboard the [[Blood Wake]].
*   **[[Besmara]]**: The Pirate Queen deity whose divine mark now flows through [[Desmond]].

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Goat's Head]]**: The lawless island haven where the party stocks up on supplies before departure.
*   **[[Bottled Solutions]]**: A heavily scented alchemy shop specializing in bizarre potions and brewing ingredients.
*   **[[Fever Sea]]**: The vast, open ocean southwest of [[Goat's Head]] where merchant ships operate.
*   **[[Tidewater Rock]]**: The heavily fortified estate of [[Gavin McRae]].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **The [[Council of Secrets]]**: An emotional, pivotal roleplay encounter where the party shares their tragic pasts and aligns their long-term campaign goals.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[White Cap Nightcap]]**: Bizarre, experimental brewing ingredients purchased by [[Desmond]] from [[Bottled Solutions]].
*   **[[Cloak of Protection]]**: Salvaged from their previous excursions and officially identified by [[Torrin Horrendous]].
*   **[[Tidewater Cutlass]] Proficiency**: Awarded to [[Devi McRae]] as a specialized combat boon.
