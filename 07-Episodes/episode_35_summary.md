# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 35: Catapulting Zombies and Punching Sharks]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 21 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The crew of the [[Blood Wake]] enjoys their first full day of sailing southwest into the [[Fever Sea]]. During the afternoon, [[Devi McRae]] presents the newly identified [[Cloak of Protection]] to [[Torrin Horrendous]], who puts it on. She retreats to the [[Captain's Quarters]] to study the magical [[Jawbone]] scrolls, attempting to copy [[Enlarge/Reduce]] into her spellbook. However, she rolls a failure on her Arcana check, and the complex magical runes fade permanently from the bone, leaving her highly frustrated. Seeking a distraction, she begins conducting anatomical experiments on the fresh corpse [[Devi]] brought her, cutting off its fingers to test how the blood drains. 

By evening, the ship anchors in open water. While [[Desmond]] and [[Devi McRae]] sleep below deck and [[Torrin Horrendous]] rests with her undead servants in the cabin, [[Lanny]] drapes his heavy poncho over his shoulders and sleeps in the open air at the prow of the ship. 

In the dead of night, [[Lanny]] is abruptly woken by a wet, clambering sound. He spots a feral [[Sahuagin]] (sea orc) stepping onto the deck, followed by a second scaling the port rail. [[Lanny]] attempts to sneakily grab his [[Maul]], but rolls an 11 on his Stealth check, alerting the intruders. Instantly, a third [[Sahuagin]] climbs up, followed by a massive, battle-scarred, four-armed [[Sahuagin Baron]] wielding a heavy, double-headed trident. 

[[Lanny]] lets out a roaring battlecry, entering a rage to engage them. The [[Sahuagin Baron]] strikes [[Lanny]] with a devastating critical hit (natural 20) with his trident, but [[Lanny]] uses [[Stone's Endurance]] and his barbarian rage to mitigate the damage. 

The commotion wakes the rest of the ship. [[Torrin Horrendous]] exits her quarters with [[Zombie Plugg]] and [[Zombie Villahelm]] in tow, immediately locking the hatch to below deck to keep the rest of the vulnerable crew safe. [[Desmond]] and [[Devi McRae]] sprint up from the lower holds just in time to join the fray. 

[[Desmond]] casts [[Hex]] on the [[Sahuagin Baron]]. [[Torrin Horrendous]] channels two powerful casts of [[Toll the Dead]], felling one of the smaller [[Sahuagin]] whose head violently implodes as black blood spews from his ears. Seeing his vanguard decimated, the [[Sahuagin Baron]] decides that retreat is wise and leaps overboard into the dark ocean. 

Determined not to let his prey escape, [[Desmond]] leaps overboard into the pitch-black water after him. Under the surface, [[Desmond]]'s eyes adjust to reveal a horrifying sight: the [[Sahuagin Baron]] has mounted a massive, predatory [[Hammerhead Shark]]. The Baron thrusts his coral-tipped trident deep into [[Desmond]]'s chest. The wound is severe, and [[Desmond]]'s skin immediately begins to crackle, stiffen, and turn to stone under the weapon's magical petrifying venom. 

Gravely wounded but stubborn, [[Desmond]] fires two crackling bolts of [[Eldritch Blast]] into the gloomy depths. Above him, [[Lanny]] wild shapes into a massive, shark-skinned bipedal "shark-bear" (using the statistics of a brown bear with sharkskin) and dives into the ocean. [[Devi McRae]] activates his magical [[Magic Ring of Swimming]] and plunges into the water alongside him. 

The trio swims close together in the deep, inky blackness of the [[Fever Sea]], illuminated only by [[Lanny]]'s magically glowing weapon. As they scan the darkness, three fast-moving shapes—a shiver of predatory [[Sharks]]—suddenly emerge from the gloom, swimming directly towards them to feed. The episode concludes on a high-stakes, underwater cliffhanger.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Sahuagin Baron]]**: A massive, four-armed sea orc leader wielding a double-headed coral trident; rides a [[Hammerhead Shark]].
*   **[[Sahuagin]]**: Ferocious, fish-like sea humanoids (referred to as "sea orcs" by the crew) who launch a coordinated midnight raid on the ship.
*   **[[Zombie Plugg]]**: [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s one-armed undead servant, used as a bodyguard during the deck brawl.
*   **[[Zombie Villahelm]]**: [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s newly raised zombie, dressed in chain mail, who assists in the fight.
*   **[[Sharks]]**: A shiver of three hostile, harnessed sharks that surround the party in the deep water.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Blood Wake]]**: The party's newly christened ship, anchoring in the open sea.
*   **[[Fever Sea]]**: The deep, treacherous shipping lanes southwest of [[Goat's Head]].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **Midnight [[Sahuagin]] Boarding**: A frantic deck battle where the party defends the [[Blood Wake]] against a midnight ambush.
*   **Deep Water Chase**: An underwater skirmish where [[Desmond]] pursues the mounted [[Sahuagin Baron]] into the depths, leading to the party being surrounded by sharks.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Cloak of Protection]]**: Formally equipped by [[Torrin Horrendous]] after identification.
