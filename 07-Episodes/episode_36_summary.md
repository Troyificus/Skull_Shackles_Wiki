# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 36: Shark Ambush, Sahuagin Skins, and Lightning Bolts]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 19 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The session opens in the freezing, pitch-black depths of the [[Fever Sea]], where the submerged trio of [[Devi McRae]], [[Desmond]], and [[Lanny]] find themselves immediately surrounded by three hostile [[Sharks]]. Realising his brother [[Desmond]] is gravely wounded from his previous encounter with the [[Sahuagin Baron]], [[Devi McRae]] swims over to hand him two [[Potions of Greater Healing]] before stealthily hiding behind [[Lanny]] to position himself for a sneak attack. 

[[Lanny]] rages and leads the underwater assault, swinging his glowing [[Maul]] through the water. Together, the trio defeats the sharks. [[Lanny]] then points upwards, and the group swims back to the surface of the [[Blood Wake]].

Upon breaking the surface, [[Torrin Horrendous]] has [[Owlbear Albert]] throw down a rope to haul the exhausted fighters back on deck. [[Sandara Quinn]] immediately tends to [[Desmond]]'s wounds. [[Owlbear Albert]] is highly frustrated at being locked below deck during the combat, but the party reassures him of his value to the crew. 

To turn their victory into a psychological advantage, [[Devi McRae]] decides to harvest the Sahuagin blood into barrels and skin the dead creatures to display their hides on the ship's rails as a warning. [[Cogwood]] eagerly assists with the skinning. To assist them in the dark, [[Torrin Horrendous]] transfers her [[Light]] spell to her bat familiar, [[Zima]], who flies above the deck as a tiny, winged beacon.

The following day, the crew sails into calmer waters. During watch, [[Cogwood]] spots a ship on the horizon—the [[True Wind]], a merchant vessel. Realising the [[Blood Wake]] is significantly faster, [[Lanny]] steers the ship to pursue. [[Torrin Horrendous]] commands [[Sandara Quinn]] to pray for the ship, boosting their speed and allowing them to close the distance to 100 feet.

A boarding action is initiated. [[Lanny]] leaps across the gap onto the deck of the [[True Wind]], instantly entering a rage and recklessly smashing his [[Maul]] into a [[Dragonborn Bodyguard]]'s face, dealing massive damage. [[Torrin Horrendous]] casts [[Misty Step]] to teleport onto the deck behind a surrendering guard, holding her action to cast [[Shocking Grasp]] if anyone resists. 

Seeing their bodyguard fall, the crew of the [[True Wind]] surrenders. The captain of the vessel begs for mercy and asks [[Torrin Horrendous]] to spare a critically wounded female crew member. [[Torrin Horrendous]] complies, casting [[Spare the Dying]] on the woman. The crew of the [[Blood Wake]] plunders the ship's cargo, seizing a wealth of tools, farming equipment, textiles, and metals.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[True Wind Crew]]**: The merchant sailors aboard the captured vessel who quickly surrender to the party.
*   **[[Dragonborn Bodyguard]]**: A formidable defender of the merchant vessel who is brutally beaten down by [[Lanny]].
*   **[[Owlbear Albert]]**: The party's powerhouse ally who assists in throwing ropes and guarding the ship.
*   **[[Zima]]**: [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s bat familiar used to provide light on deck.
*   **[[Cogwood]]**: The crew's rigger who assists [[Devi McRae]] in skinning the dead Sahuagin.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Fever Sea]]**: The open ocean where the party battles underwater sharks and sails in search of prey.
*   **[[True Wind]]**: The captured merchant vessel carrying manufactured goods.
*   **[[Blood Wake]]**: The party's main ship.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[The Underwater Shark Fray]]**: A tense, murky battle against three trained sharks in the depths of the Fever Sea.
*   **[[Boarding of the True Wind]]**: A fast-paced boarding action that culminates in the complete surrender of the merchant crew.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[True Wind Cargo]]**: A substantial hoard of manufactured tools, copper, salt, clothing, and farming equipment.
*   **[[Sahuagin Hides]]**: Harvested by [[Devi McRae]] and [[Cogwood]] to decorate the ship's rails.
