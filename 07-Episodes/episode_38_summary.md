# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 38: Coconut Moonshine and Punching Dragons]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 22-23 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The episode begins with the crew of the [[Blood Wake]] sailing frantically through the night to outrun the spectral [[Death Knell]]. Inside the captain's quarters, [[Desmond]] confronts [[Torrin Horrendous]] about an offhand comment he made about his twin brother, [[Devi McRae]], which deeply upset her. [[Torrin Horrendous]] opens up about her past, revealing that she accidentally started a fire that killed her own brother—a heavy guilt she carries to this day.

The next day, [[Torrin Horrendous]] attempts to copy another spell and fails again, completely running out of paper. Meanwhile, [[Desmond]] attempts to brew a new batch of moonshine but spoils the ingredients. [[Devi McRae]], with the help of [[Cogwood]], successfully skins the Sahuagin bodies harvested from their previous encounter.

On the second night of their flight, [[Devi McRae]] is on watch and hears the raspy bell of the [[Death Knell]] ringing closer than before, accompanied by the eerie green glow. The crew is thrown into a panic. To restore order, [[Lanny]] delivers a highly inspiring and concise speech to the crew from the stern castle, successfully calming their nerves.

The following afternoon, the exhausted crew finally makes port at [[Little Upara]], a talden colony in the Shackles. Due to sailing non-stop for two days, [[Devi McRae]], [[Desmond]], and [[Lanny]] all suffer from a level of exhaustion. After paying the port fees to the [[Dock Master]], the party splits up:

*   [[Sandara Quinn]], [[Rosie Cusswell]], and [[Johnny]] head into town to gather rumors and information from the locals on how to defeat [[Whalebone Pilk]] and his undead crew.
*   [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] visit [[The Arena]] (number 16) to investigate fighting opportunities.
*   On the local notice board, they discover a lucrative bounty: **5,000 gold pieces** offered for the extermination of a winged menace plaguing the island's hunting grounds, known to the locals as [[Black Fang]].

Realising they need coin and a way to build their infamy to gain an audience with the [[Hurricane King Karch Bonefist]], the party decides to take on the quest to hunt down [[Black Fang]].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Little Upara Dock Master]]**: The port official who registers the [[Blood Wake]] and collects the harbor fees.
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: The cleric who leads the investigation into [[Whalebone Pilk]]'s weaknesses in town.
*   **[[Rosie Cusswell]]**: Accompanies [[Sandara Quinn]] on her search for lore.
*   **[[Black Fang]]**: A terrifying winged creature/dragon plaguing [[Little Upara]], carrying a 5,000 gold piece bounty.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Little Upara]]**: A beautifully organized Taldan colony on the eastern edge of the Shackles.
*   **[[The Arena]]**: A grand gladiatorial amphitheatre in [[Little Upara]] where champions fight.
*   **[[Blood Wake]]**: The party's vessel, currently docked safely in the harbor.

## Battles & Encounters
*   None (this is a transitional session focused on travel, roleplay, and quest acquisition).

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Black Fang Bounty Notice]]**: A lead for a high-paying beast-hunting contract.
