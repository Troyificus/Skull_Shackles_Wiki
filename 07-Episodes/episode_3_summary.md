# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 3: Cheating the Owlbear and Captain Harrigan’s Punch]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 2 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The third day aboard the [[Wormwood]] begins with the party facing their mandatory morning tasks. Expecting a harsh altercation with [[Mr. Plugg]] following [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s public defiance the previous evening, [[Devi McRae]] is instead assigned to the galley to assist [[Ambrose Kroop]] (aka [[Fishguts]]), who is hopelessly incapacitated by drink. Tasked with preparing the day's main meal, [[Devi McRae]] wrangles together a turtle recipe, successfully preparing a decent turtle soup with the help of [[Desmond]], who assists from his high vantage point up in the rigging. 

Meanwhile, [[Lanny]] and [[Torrin Horrendous]] are assigned to rat-catching duty in the lower holds. They easily manage the chore, and [[Lanny]] spends his time bizarrely trying to "interrogate" a bewildered rat.

After the evening meal, tensions boil over on deck. It is revealed that every threat and insult directed at [[Mr. Plugg]] by the party has been reported to [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]]. Rather than lying low, [[Devi McRae]] flagrantly repeats his insubordinate comments directly in front of the captain and the assembled crew. Enraged by the disrespect, [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] delivers a single, devastating punch that knocks [[Devi McRae]] unconscious. [[Desmond]] instinctively steps forward to defend his twin brother, but [[Torrin Horrendous]] holds him back, convincing him to show restraint as the officers draw their weapons—including a heavy crossbow. [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] declares that any threat to his officers is a personal threat to him, deeming the matter closed before retiring to his quarters.

Attempting to defuse the palpable tension on deck, [[Mr. Plugg]] suggests some sport and offers a purse of gold to anyone who can defeat his personal champion, the lumbering, simple-minded goliath-sized gerridae [[Owlbear Albert]] (aka [[Albert]]), in a boxing match. Still furious over his brother's injury, [[Desmond]] eagerly accepts the challenge.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]]**: The ferocious captain of the [[Wormwood]] who demands complete obedience and knocks [[Devi McRae]] out for his insubordination.
*   **[[Mr. Plugg]]**: Cruel first mate of the [[Wormwood]] who reports insubordination to the captain and wagers a gold purse on a boxing match to mock the party.
*   **[[Owlbear Albert]]** (aka **[[Albert]]**): [[Mr. Plugg]]'s simple-minded, physically imposing champion who fights [[Desmond]].
*   **[[Ambrose Kroop]]** (aka **[[Fishguts]]**): The drunken ship's cook whose inebriation forces [[Devi McRae]] to take over the galley.
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: Cleric of [[Besmara]] who watches over the party's antics and assists in caring for the wounded.
*   **[[Corki]]**: The young cabin girl who arrives to collect and taste-test the captain's food.

## Locations Visited
*   The **[[Wormwood]]**: The pirate vessel currently sailing the open seas.
    *   **[[The Galley]]**: Where [[Devi McRae]] cooks the turtle meal.
    *   **[[The Bilges/Lower Deck]]**: Where [[Lanny]] and [[Torrin Horrendous]] catch rats.
    *   **[[The Main Deck]]**: The location of the morning roll call, [[Devi McRae]]'s confrontation, and [[Desmond]]'s boxing match.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] vs. [[Devi McRae]]**: The captain summarily knocks [[Devi McRae]] out with a single punch to enforce discipline.
*   **[[Desmond]] vs. [[Owlbear Albert]]**: A brutal boxing match on the main deck. [[Albert]] strikes first and hard, but [[Desmond]]'s superior speed allows him to counter. During the bout, [[Torrin Horrendous]] cheats by secretly casting [[Blindness/Deafness]] on [[Albert]]. Blinded, the gerridae swings wildly at the air. Just as the spell wears off, [[Desmond]] executes a final, decisive combination, finishing with a heavy blow to the back of [[Albert]]'s head to knock the giant out cold.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Purse of Gold]]**: A bounty offered by [[Mr. Plugg]], won by [[Desmond]] after knocking out [[Owlbear Albert]].
