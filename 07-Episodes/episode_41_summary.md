# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 41: Double Dragon]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 24 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Setting off on foot at first light, the party tracks the dragon's path deeper into the dense, ancient jungle [766, 777]. Following the coordinates on their map, [[Devi McRae]] spots an old, moss-covered stone statue marking the entrance to a dark, subterranean cave system [716, 767, 777]. Creeping inside, they find themselves exploring an [[Abandoned Temple]] [777]. As they navigate the twisting, damp tunnels, they discover ancient bones, mysterious runes carved over a massive sealed stone door, and an obelisk depicting graphic acts of torture [771, 777]. They are briefly ambushed by a [[Giant Spider]], which [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s zombies and the brawler [[Desmond]] quickly dispatch [777, 778].

Ascending a steep, slippery stone ridge at the end of a winding passage, they peer into a massive, hollow cavern illuminated by a shaft of natural sunlight from a collapsed ceiling [777]. There, they make two shocking discoveries: first, the badly burned, semi-dissolved corpse of [[Dark Wolf]] (Copeland), who clearly failed his solo hunt; and second, the massive, sleeping form of the black dragon [[Black Fang]] [777]. 

Adopting a clever strategy, the party commands [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s chain-mail-clad [[Zombies]] to advance first, creating a formidable meat shield [777, 801]. As the dragon wakes and roars, the battle erupts [801]. [[Lanny]] rages and advances with his heavy [[Maul]], while [[Desmond]] uses his monk agility to flank [801]. [[Devi McRae]] spots a glowing, magical arrow dropped by the dead Copeland, quickly scoops it up, and fires it from his shortbow—though he misses [801].

The battle takes a catastrophic turn when the dragon unleashes a devastating spray of acidic, flesh-melting breath across the cavern [801]. Before they can recover, a second, equally ferocious black dragon emerges from the dark corners of the cave [802]. The sibling dragon spews another jet of acid that leaves [[Lanny]] virtually skinless and knocks him unconscious [802].

Acting quickly under pressure, [[Devi McRae]] maneuvers behind the first dragon and delivers a critical strike with his [[Rapier]] directly between its ribs, felling the beast [802]. To deal with the remaining dragon, [[Torrin Horrendous]] unleashes her devastating [[Chromatic Orb]] (lightning), while [[Devi McRae]] and [[Sandara Quinn]] desperately channel healing magic (including [[Cure Wounds]] and [[Spare the Dying]]) to revive [[Lanny]] [780, 781, 802]. [[Desmond]] retrieves the magical arrow from the cavern floor and leaps onto the dragon's back, brutally jamming the arrow directly down between its eyes [802]. A massive surge of crackling, thunderous energy erupts through the dragon's body, killing it instantly [802].

Morale restored, the party loots the dragons' modest hoard and hacks off the two massive [[Dragon Heads]] as trophies [782, 783, 788, 791, 802]. They load their spoils onto the cart and return to [[Little Upara]] [792, 797, 803]. After parading the heads through the cheering streets to build their [[Infamy]], they look down at the docks and spot a familiar blue-gray military coat fanning in the wind: [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]] is waving to them from the deck of his grand galleon, the [[Pinion]] [798, 799, 800, 803].
