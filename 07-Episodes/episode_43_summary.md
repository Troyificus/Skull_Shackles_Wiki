# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 43: Scribing Spells and the Colosseum Clash]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 20-21

## Plot Summary
The morning after their return, the party plans their next moves. With the looming threat of the spectral captain [[Whalebone Pilk]] and his ghost ship, the [[Death Knell]], chasing them, they debate seeking an alliance or researching a way to put him down permanently. [[Torrin Horrendous]] decides to utilize her downtime to research necromancy and [[Whalebone Pilk]] at local arcane shops.

Before heading into town, [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] deliver the two dragon heads to [[White Hall]], the headquarters of the [[Town Guard]]. They meet with [[Captain Haley]], who is thoroughly impressed by their proof. He coordinates with [[Lady Olandra]]'s estate to secure their payment of [[5,000 gold pieces]]. While at [[White Hall]], the boys chat with the guards about the [[Venom Troll]]. The guards reveal a vital tactical clue: the troll possesses highly acidic, poisonous blood that sprays violently out of its wounds whenever it is struck in melee. 

Armed with this knowledge, [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] visit [[The Copper Chalice]] to buy exceptionally fine, aristocratic clothing for their upcoming dinner with the duchess. Meanwhile, [[Torrin Horrendous]] and [[Desmond]] visit [[Chorga's Quill]], the local spell scribe. [[Torrin Horrendous]] sells the massive, engraved [[Ivory Walrus Tusk]] / [[Whalebone]] they salvaged from the grindylow caves, which contains [[Alter Self]], [[Blur]], and [[Color Spray]] spells, to fund her purchase of spell scrolls. [[Lanny]] and [[Torrin Horrendous]] also visit [[Ellie the Blacksmith]] at the [[Red Dog Smithy]] to discuss forging a colossal greatsword out of predatory fish parts.

That evening, the main event arrives. The [[Blood Wake Crew]] enters [[The Arena]] to a deafening roar as the crowd chants "[[Skull Crusher]]!". They face off against the massive, slimy [[Venom Troll]]. The battle is brutal; every time [[Lanny]] and [[Desmond]] strike the beast, a spray of corrosive [[Troll Venom]] bursts from its flesh, burning [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] for [[7 poison damage]]. [[Desmond]] strategically casts [[Hex]] to weaken the troll's strength. Unleashing his new druidic powers, [[Lanny]] goes into a primal rage, transforming his hands into savage [[Claws]] to deliver devastating strikes. The episode ends as the party closes in for the kill amidst the roaring cheers of the colosseum.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Captain Haley]]**: The captain of the [[Town Guard]] at [[White Hall]] who verifies the dragon heads.
*   **[[Ellie the Blacksmith]]**: The skilled dwarf blacksmith at [[Red Dog Smithy]] who discusses future weapon crafting with [[Lanny]].
*   **[[Stephanie Garrison]]**: The colosseum master who coordinates the grand fight.
*   **[[Venom Troll]]**: The massive, acid-bleeding colosseum beast.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[White Hall]]**: The stone headquarters of the [[Little Upara]] [[Town Guard]].
*   **[[The Copper Chalice]]**: A dapper half-timbered boutique selling fine, high-end clothing.
*   **[[Chorga's Quill]]**: An arcane shop and spell scribe in the town's small magical quarter.
*   **[[Red Dog Smithy]]**: An open-walled smithy guarded by three large dogs.
*   **[[The Arena]]**: The grand colosseum of [[Little Upara]].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[The Venom Troll Battle]]**: A high-stakes, televised-style colosseum combat where the party must bypass the troll's acidic blood sprays.
