# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 45: Crimson Cray and Besmara's Warning]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 25 - Day 29

## Plot Summary
Following their elegant dinner with [[Lady Olandra]], the party departs her estate carrying a heavy wooden chest containing [[5,000 gold pieces]] [876, 878]. As they exit the gates into the cool night air, [[Lanny]] sheepishly admits to the twins that he never actually wanted the duchess's peacock as a pet—he simply wanted to roast and eat it [878]. The party heads back to the [[Blood Wake]] to count and divvy up their massive dragon-slaying haul, celebrating their largest financial score to date [879].

With their pockets lined with platinum and gold, the party spends the following day on a lavish shopping spree in [[Little Upara]] [876, 880]. [[Devi McRae]] visits [[Fox's Armors and Shields]] to place a custom order for a suit of dapper studded leather armor made from their harvested black dragon hide [880]. He leaves a hefty [[500 gold pieces]] deposit for the custom gear, which is designed to hold both "smoldering" and "mariner's" magical properties [880]. In a generous gesture of friendship, [[Devi McRae]] also takes [[Sandara Quinn]] and [[Rosie Cusswell]] to [[The Copper Chalice]] fine clothing boutique, instructing the shopkeeper to let the ladies pick out any fine outfits they desire on his tab [880]. Meanwhile, [[Torrin Horrendous]] attempts to purchase a second-level spell scroll of [[Knock]] from the magic shop [[Gigi's Tinctures]] [883]. She rolls a disappointing 14 (failing the 15 DC check) and is instead sold a useless scroll that disintegrates into a sticky substance resembling "pva glue" and congealed spider webs [883, 884].

Looking ahead, the party plans a return expedition to the northern [[Abandoned Temple]] where they fought the black dragons to fully harvest their carcasses for bone decorations, scales, and hide [881, 884]. However, [[Torrin Horrendous]] faces a major necromantic logistical problem: she has two active zombies, [[Zombie Plug]] and [[Zombie Villahelm]], who require her to reassert control over them every 24 hours [885]. Unable to leave them unattended on the ship without risk of them going rogue, [[Lanny]] and the twins help [[Torrin Horrendous]] pack the manacled zombies into large wooden barrels to smuggle them along in their rented horse-and-cart [885, 886]. 

During the journey north, [[Sandara Quinn]] sits on the front bench of the cart next to [[Torrin Horrendous]] [887]. Fascinated and only mildly unsettled by the cargo, [[Sandara Quinn]] strikes up a theological conversation about necromancy, asking [[Torrin Horrendous]] about the exact limits of the magic and "how dead" a corpse must be for animate spells to function [887]. They spend the night camped in the wilderness before re-entering the temple the next morning [888].

Inside the cavern lair, [[Lanny]] and [[Devi McRae]] set to work skinning the dragon remains, collecting scales and removing the massive leathery wings to fashion a custom cloak for [[Torrin Horrendous]] [891]. Meanwhile, [[Torrin Horrendous]] explores the spider-infested side tunnels [888]. She discovers the rotted skeleton of a canine-like [[Gnoll]] and performs a minute-long ritual to raise it as her third skeletal thrall [888, 889]. [[Sandara Quinn]] watches the process and admits that clean skeletons are far less creepy and a much easier sell to the crew than rotting zombies [889, 890]. The party also burns away residual flesh from the dragon bones using acid to prep them as ship trophies [890]. Before departing, the group senses an ominous, powerful vibration emanating from the mysterious closed door in the cavern [892]. Acting on a bad vibe, [[Desmond]] urges the party to leave immediately, and [[Torrin Horrendous]] reluctantly backs out of the temple with her newly expanded undead entourage in tow [892].

*   **[[Rosie Cusswell]]**: Treated to a fine clothing shopping spree by [[Devi McRae]] [880].
*   **[[Lady Olandra]]**: The duchess who paid the party their bounty [876].
