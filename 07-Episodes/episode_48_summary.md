# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 48: Weaponizing Rats and Hiding Skeletons]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Not explicitly specified, but takes place shortly after departing the colony of [[Little Upara]].

## Plot Summary
The session begins in the immediate aftermath of the battle against the ghost ship. [[Torrin Horrendous]] (also known as [[Miss Blackheart]]) retires to the captain's quarters on the [[Blood Wake]] with the magical [[Bell of the Death Knell]]. To neutralize its unnatural necromantic power, she detaches its [[Clapper]] and wraps the pieces in thick cloth, hiding them in her cabin.

While inside her quarters, [[Torrin Horrendous]] realizes that the mysterious [[Golden Twin-Plated Dagger]] she had been keeping in a drawer is missing. She immediately confronts her companions, suspecting a theft. The tension is defused when it is revealed that [[Devi McRae]] (now calling himself [[Crimson Cray]]) has the dagger safely tucked away in his pocket. They share a brief, serious conversation regarding the nature of [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s dark deity, [[Myrkul]].

Meanwhile, [[Lanny]] (known to the crew as [[Captain Skull Crusher]]) descends into the cargo hold of the [[Blood Wake]] to check on the ship's resident rats. Using his druidic magical ability [[Speak with Animals]], [[Lanny]] holds a formal meeting with [[Rat Damon]] (also referred to as [[Damien]]) and the rest of the rat population. [[Lanny]] successfully negotiates a pact with the rodents: in exchange for protection and food, the rats agree to serve as an onboard security force and defend the [[Blood Wake]] if the vessel is ever boarded by hostile forces.

After [[Lanny]] feeds his new allies with bread and crackers, [[Torrin Horrendous]] reassures the rest of her skeleton crew on deck, and the party officially sets sail southwest into the open ocean, steering directly into the shipping lanes.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Rat Damon]]**: The intelligent, leading resident rat of the [[Blood Wake]] who swears eternal loyalty to [[Lanny]]'s crew in exchange for food and safety.
*   **[[Myrkul]]**: The dark god of the dead whom [[Torrin Horrendous]] serves and prays to for guidance.
*   **[[Whalebone Pilk]]**: The spectral captain of the ghost ship mentioned during the recap.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Blood Wake]]**: The party's primary ship, currently sailing southwest into the shipping lanes.

## Battles & Encounters
*   None. This session focused on roleplay, tactical preparations, and character monikers (including [[Devi McRae]] adopting the name [[Crimson Cray]] and [[Desmond]] calling himself [[Fist]]).

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Golden Twin-Plated Dagger]]**: Retained by [[Devi McRae]].
*   **[[Bell of the Death Knell]]**: A cursed, magical bell salvaged from the ghost ship, safely neutralized by detaching its [[Clapper]].
