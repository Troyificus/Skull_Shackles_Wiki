# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 49: Shattered Masts and a Dead Cabin Boy]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 1 following the previous session's events.

## Plot Summary
The day begins with immediate internal crew drama. The goliath enforcer [[Albert]] catches the young cabin boy [[Dutch]] stealing food from [[Ambrose Kroop]]'s kitchen store. [[Albert]] drags [[Dutch]] onto the deck of the [[Blood Wake]] and throws him before [[Lanny]] (acting as [[Captain Skull Crusher]]) for judgment. After a brief deliberation with [[Torrin Horrendous]] and [[Devi McRae]], [[Lanny]] decides to let the terrified boy off with a stern warning: if [[Dutch]] is caught stealing again, the party will cut off one of his fingers as punishment. [[Albert]] is deeply unsatisfied with this lenient sentence, grumbling that on his old pirate ship, the boy would have received three lashes at the mast.

With the internal dispute settled, the [[Blood Wake]] continues its voyage. In the late afternoon, [[Desmond]] (going by [[Fist]]) spots a [[Rahadoumi Shooner]] on the horizon. Believing it to be a rich merchant vessel, [[Lanny]] orders the crew to give chase. Using a combination of tactical sails coordination, prayers from [[Sandara Quinn]], and [[Desmond]]'s acrobatic maneuvers in the rigging, the [[Blood Wake]] swiftly closes the distance to a mere 100 feet.

Sensing the imminent boarding, the [[Rahadoumi]] crew opens a large metal cage on their deck and releases two massive, red-coloured [[Manticores]]. The winged beasts take flight and immediately attack the [[Blood Wake]]. A fierce battle commences. [[Desmond]] unleashes crackling beams of [[Eldritch Blast]] at the creatures, while the [[Manticores]] retaliate by firing toxic spikes that strike [[Lanny]].

As the ships draw alongside each other, [[Torrin Horrendous]] uses her magical ability [[Misty Step]] to teleport directly onto the enemy deck. She materializes behind a guard and casts a critical [[Shocking Grasp]], instantly cooking him from the inside out. [[Lanny]] follows her, leaping onto the enemy ship and unleashing two devastating swings of his [[Maul]] to slaughter one of the [[Manticores]]. [[Torrin Horrendous]] then channels a powerful [[Lightning Bolt]] that tears through the deck, vaporizing two more guards and forcing [[Torrin]] to demand that the remaining crew "kneel or die."

Completely overwhelmed, the remaining [[Rahadoumi]] guards and their female captain surrender. The [[Blood Wake Crew]] begins boarding the vessel to secure the cargo and prisoners. However, the celebration is cut short when [[Devi McRae]] discovers [[Dutch]] sitting lifelessly in the shadows under the stairs with a [[Manticore Spike]] driven directly through his neck. [[Sandara Quinn]] attempts to heal him, but it is too late—[[Dutch]] has already passed away.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Dutch]]**: The young cabin boy of the [[Blood Wake]] who is pardoned for kitchen theft but is tragically killed by an errant [[Manticore Spike]].
*   **[[Albert]]** / **[[Owlbear Albert]]**: The imposing goliath enforcer who advocates for harsher discipline but respects the captain's final ruling.
*   **[[Ambrose Kroop]]**: The ship's cook whose kitchen stores were raided by [[Dutch]].
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: The ship's cleric who tragically arrives too late to heal [[Dutch]].
*   **[[Rahadoumi]]**: The faction of merchant sailors and guards defending the targeted shooner.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Blood Wake]]**: The party's main ship.
*   **[[Rahadoumi Shooner]]**: The captured merchant vessel boarded and occupied by the party.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[The Battle of the Rahadoumi Shooner]]**: A brutal high-seas confrontation against two flying [[Manticores]] and [[Rahadoumi]] defenders, ending in a hard-won victory.

## Loot & Rewards
