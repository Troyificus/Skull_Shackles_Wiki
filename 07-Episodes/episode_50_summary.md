# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 50: Burying Friends and Raising Enemies]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 1 (continuation immediately following the battle).

## Plot Summary
Following the tragic discovery of [[Dutch]]'s death, [[Devi McRae]] uses his [[Message]] spell to quietly inform [[Lanny]] and [[Desmond]]. [[Lanny]] orders the crew to begin looting and securing the captured [[Rahadoumi Shooner]]. Below deck, [[Tally]] and the crew discover three points of [[Plunder]] consisting of cloth, copper, and salt, along with a disassembled [[Light Ballista]] in the cargo hold.

On deck, [[Devi McRae]] has [[Sargava]] translate to the 20 cowering [[Rahadoumi]] sailors that the ship is now under new ownership. When a defiant defender spits on the deck in front of [[Sargava]], [[Torrin Horrendous]] immediately makes a brutal example of him, casting [[Toll the Dead]] to deal 17 points of necrotic damage, causing him to bleed out of his eyes and collapse dead on the floor.

The crew gathers on deck to hold a solemn pirate's funeral for [[Dutch]]. [[Sandara Quinn]] administers the blessings of [[Besmara]], and the crew respectfully slips his wrapped body over the side of the ship. Each crew member tosses a gold coin into the water after him.

Once the funeral is finished, [[Torrin Horrendous]] pitches a bold and controversial plan to the crew. She argues that because their enemies use monsters (like manticores) against them, they should utilize monsters of their own to protect the living crew. She then uses [[Animate Dead]] (amplified by [[Thormaturgy]]) to summon her four [[Skeletons]] out of the captain's quarters.

The crew initially panics and draws their weapons, but [[Devi McRae]] and [[Lanny]] step forward to demonstrate that the skeletons are completely harmless, obedient, and under control, with [[Devi McRae]] putting his arm around one of them. [[Torrin Horrendous]] explains that the undead will serve as "bony shields" in future boarding actions so that the living crew doesn't have to die. [[Sandara Quinn]] and [[Lanny]] support her explanation, though [[Conchobhar Shortstone]] admits he finds them creepy.

The party ties the captured shooner behind the [[Blood Wake]] and sets sail northeast toward the [[Ushinawa Isles]]. Around 3:00 AM, the detached [[Bell of the Death Knell]] in the captain's quarters mysteriously begins to ring and vibrate despite being wrapped in cloth. [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] rush to investigate and spot a thick bank of fog rolling in from the east. Suddenly, two grappling hooks slam into the rails of the ship out of the fog, signaling a terrifying spectral ambush!

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: The cleric who conducts [[Dutch]]'s pirate funeral and helps calm the crew during the skeleton reveal.
*   **[[Tally]]**: The navigator who coordinates the search of the hold and reports the captured plunder.
*   **[[Sargava]]**: The translator who facilitates communication with the cowering [[Rahadoumi]] crew.
*   **[[Conchobhar Shortstone]]**: The rigger who openly expresses his unease with [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s undead servants.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Blood Wake]]**: The party's main ship.
*   **[[Rahadoumi Shooner]]**: The captured merchant vessel being towed behind the party's ship.
*   **[[Ushinawa Isles]]**: The plotted northeast destination.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[The Defiant Sailor's Execution]]**: [[Torrin Horrendous]] executes a captured defender to deter mutiny.
*   **[[The Midnight Fog Ambush]]**: A sudden, terrifying cliffhanger as grappling hooks latch onto the ship out of the spectral fog.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[3 Points of Plunder]]**: Salvaged cloth, copper, and salt.
*   **[[Light Ballista]]**: Disassembled pieces of a deck weapon salvaged from the hold.
