# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 52: Sea Shanties and the Shark Bear]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 27

## Plot Summary
As the morning mist parts, a **[[Rahadoumi Slavers]]** merchant schooner becomes visible in the distance. Eager to pursue, **[[Torrin Horrendous]]** casts **[[Animate Dead]]** to raise four of the slain feral invaders as **[[Zombies]]**—including **[[Zombie Drunk]]** and **[[Zombie Pirate]]**—to help crew the vessel. They begin chasing the fleeing ship.

To prepare for boarding, **[[Torrin]]** uses her **[[Driftwood Wand]]** to cast **[[Mirror Image]]** on herself and commands her **[[Skeletons]]** to rain down shortbow arrows. **[[Lanny]]** utilizes his new **[[Wild Shape]]** ability to transform into a massive, shark-skinned "shark-bear" and leaps across to the enemy deck.

**[[Dez]]** casts **[[Hex]]** on the defenders and utilizes **[[Tipsy Sway]]** to make the enemies attack their own allies. **[[Torrin]]** casts a massive **[[Tidal Wave]]** across the enemy deck, knocking multiple defenders prone. Though the shark-bear takes heavy damage and Lanny reverts to his goliath form, the crew successfully subdues the defenders, including the dragonborn bodyguard **[[Kiliaks]]** and **[[Lady Zyra Viniloa]]**. Searching the vessel, **[[Dez]]** descends into the lower hold and is shocked to discover a hidden cargo area packed with young children and teenagers forced to man the ship's oars.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Rahadoumi Slavers]]**: Faction of the captured vessel.
*   **[[Kiliaks]]**: The noble dragonborn bodyguard who defends **[[Lady Zyra Viniloa]]**.
*   **[[Lady Zyra Viniloa]]**: The captain/proprietor of the captured schooner.
*   **[[Zombies]]**: Raised by **[[Torrin]]** to bolster the boarding crew.

## Locations Visited

*   **[[Rahadoumi Schooner]]**: The captured slave vessel.
