# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 53: Adopting Cultists and Skinning the Captain]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Days 28 to 30

## Plot Summary
Standing in the dark hold, **[[Dez]]** confronts the terrified captives while the injured slaver captain tries to attack him, prompting **[[Dez]]** to grapple and non-lethally subdue him. As **[[Torrin Horrendous]]** joins them, the teenagers make a hand gesture and call her "**[[Mother]]**" in gurgling grunts. Guided by a newly discovered conscience, the party decides to rescue the 20 to 30 children rather than abandoning them. They plan to tow the captured ship to **[[Genzai]]** in the **[[Ushinawa Isles]]** to deliver them safely to a local monastery.

To sail both vessels, they split their crew: **[[Ambrose Kroop]]** (aka **[[Croup]]**), **[[Rosie Cusswell]]**, **[[Balak]]**, **[[Dez]]**, and two of the **[[Fiska]]** brothers remain aboard the ***[[Blood Wake]]***, while **[[Lanny]]**, **[[Torrin]]**, **[[Devi McRae]]**, and **[[Sandara Quinn]]** pilot the towed prize ship with the help of the **[[Zombies]]** pulling the rigging. Before setting sail, they harvest the tattooed skin map from the dead female spellcaster to locate **[[Mancatcher Cove]]**, and **[[Sandara]]** performs a respectful pirate burial for the deceased enemies, throwing gold coins into the sea. **[[Torrin]]** uses **[[Detect Magic]]** to sweep the vessel for hidden arcane dangers.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Bazoo]]**: The teenage spokesperson for the captured children.
*   **[[Ambrose Kroop]]** (aka **[[Croup]]**): The cook left on the **[[Blood Wake]]**.
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: Cleric who assists with the burials and the children.
*   **[[Balak]]**: The dwarf carpenter assisting on the **[[Blood Wake]]**.
*   **[[Crawl Fiska]]** & **[[Frank Fiska]]**: The rigger brothers remaining on the **[[Blood Wake]]**.
