# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 55: The Shark Bear and Torrin’s Divine Betrayal]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 31 - Day 32 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The session picks up directly with the terrifying manifestation of [[Whalebone Pilk]] on the deck of the ship. Erupting from the cracked [[Bell of the Death Knell]], his spectral form expands as a tornado of green ghostly spirits and trapped souls swirls around him [[1130]]. 

[[Pilk]] unleashes a "Dreadful Visage," a terrifying wave of horror across the deck [[1131]]. While [[Devi McRae]] and [[Lanny]] successfully resist the fear, [[Torrin Horrendous]] and [[Desmond]] are completely paralyzed with terror [[1131]]. Because [[Torrin Horrendous]] is paralyzed, her undead skeletons and zombies are unable to receive orders and stand motionless on deck [[1132]]. 

[[Devi McRae]] fires a magical arrow from his longbow at [[Pilk]], which successfully damages the spectral captain despite his ghostly form [[1133]]. [[Lanny]] casts [[Entangle]] using his newly gained druid magic, sending spectral vines to wrap around the ghost captain, but [[Pilk]] manages to break free [[1133]]. [[Pilk]] retaliates by throwing his spectral harpoon at [[Lanny]]. The harpoon pierces [[Lanny]]'s side, dealing a massive combination of piercing and necrotic damage (34 points total), leaving the goliath heavily wounded [[1134]]. 

At the end of his turn, [[Torrin Horrendous]] is finally free of her paralysis [[1134, 1135]]. [[Sandara Quinn]] emerges from the below-deck hatch, successfully braves the dreadful presence (taking only minor damage), and rushes to [[Lanny]]'s side to cast a healing spell [[1138, 1140]]. Reinvigorated, [[Lanny]] activates his [[Wild Shape]] and transforms into a massive, shark-skinned "shark bear" to claw and tear at the ghost captain [[1137, 1140]]. 

[[Pilk]] attacks [[Lanny]] again with his spectral harpoon, dealing heavy damage and forcing the goliath out of his shark-bear form, leaving him on the brink of death at only 1 hit point [[1140, 1141, 1144]]. [[Torrin Horrendous]] draws the magical [[Diamond]] from her pocket and channels her arcane power to cast a powerful spell [[1140]], while [[Devi McRae]] lets loose a critical arrow from his bow, dealing 23 damage and nearly tearing [[Pilk]]'s spectral body apart [[1141]]. [[Devi McRae]] yells at the party to "destroy that fucking Bell!" [[1141]] 

[[Lanny]] reaches deep into the chest cavity of [[Pilk]] and rips out his essence, causing a massive, blinding explosion of green light [[1142]]. With [[Pilk]] destroyed, the tattered souls he had trapped for centuries are finally freed, drifting up into the sky and whispering "thank you" as a soul named "Murray" is released [[1143]]. The only things left of the ghost captain are tattered ash and the cracked [[Bell of the Death Knell]], now split completely into two pieces [[1143]]. 

[[Desmond]] uses his monk abilities to perform a final banishing rite over the cracked bell, casting [[Besmara]]'s name to banish [[Pilk]]'s soul to the deep bilges of the sea wraith forever [[1145]]. To break the tension, [[Lanny]] playfully uppercuts [[Devi McRae]] for his "good shot with the arrows" and plants a kiss on his bald head [[1146]]. The crew emerges from the hatch, celebrating their survival [[1147]]. 

In a massive roleplay breakthrough, [[Torrin Horrendous]] stands on deck staring at the [[Myrkul]] skull necklace around her neck. Feeling betrayed by the god of death who did not help her, she casts [[Shocking Grasp]] on the skull, completely disintegrating it into dust and throwing it into the sea, officially denouncing her devotion to [[Myrkul]] [[1148, 1149]]. 

In an act of complete defiance, [[Torrin Horrendous]] unhooks the cursed [[Golden Twin-Plated Dagger]] from her belt and hurls it into the ocean [[1150]]. Instantly, she is struck by a wave of excruciating psychic damage (9 points, followed by 6 points), causing her to bleed from her ears and weep blood as the dagger's curse retaliates [[1150, 1151, 1152]]. [[Lanny]] dives into the ocean with his 40-foot swim speed, retrieves the cursed dagger, and stabs it into the wooden deck in front of [[Torrin Horrendous]], telling her she must keep it for now [[1151, 1152]]. Torrin takes another 10 points of psychic damage [[1153]]. 

[[Desmond]] kneels in front of her, urging her to call upon [[Besmara]] for help, promising that the pirate goddess can protect her from the curse [[1154]]. Supported by her friends, a weeping, bleeding [[Torrin Horrendous]] finally whispers: "help me please," requesting [[Besmara]]'s aid before passing out [[1155]].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Whalebone Pilk]]**: The terrifying spectral whaler captain who is finally destroyed for good.
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: The cleric who provides vital healing to [[Lanny]] during the fight.
*   **[[Myrkul]]**: The god of death whom [[Torrin Horrendous]] denounces.
*   **[[Besmara]]**: The pirate goddess whose aid [[Torrin Horrendous]] begs for at her lowest moment.
