# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 56: Divine Divorce and the Rat Jesus Incident]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Days 32 to 35 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The session opens as [[Torrin Horrendous]] lies unconscious on deck [[1155, 1158]]. In her sleep, she experiences a vivid, terrifying vision of [[Myrkul]] standing above her in smoking black robes. However, she actively turns her back on the god of death and accepts the warm, guiding light of [[Besmara]] [[1158]]. [[Torrin Horrendous]] wakes up fully recovered and free of the psychic pain [[1158]]. Wrapping her hand securely in her cloak, she pulls the cursed [[Golden Twin-Plated Dagger]] out of the deck and has [[Lanny]] hurl it as far as he can into the sky over the ocean [[1159]]. In mid-air, she unleashes a brilliant white orb of lightning, completely vaporizing the cursed dagger [[1159, 1176, 1177]]. The magical energy has transformed from a creepy, necrotic black into a brilliant, pure white. 

The party holds a massive discussion about their deific experiences. They realize that [[Myrkul]] may have been the very threat [[Besmara]] sent [[Desmond]] back to fight, or that the pirate queen strategically poached [[Torrin Horrendous]] to weaken her divine rival [[1160]]. [[Torrin Horrendous]] formally aligns with [[Besmara]], noting that while she is not her slave, she will happily live by the tenets of piracy and take what she wants [[1161, 1162]]. The crew jokes about getting matching [[Besmara]] tattoos to commemorate their divine connections [[1163]]. 

[[Torrin Horrendous]] casts [[Animate Dead]] to reassert her control over her 4 [[Skeletons]] and 2 surviving [[Zombies]] (which are safely stowed away in the [[Captain's Quarters]] of the new ship) [[1163, 1164]]. [[Desmond]] throws one half of the cracked [[Bell of the Death Knell]] into the sea to ensure it is lost forever, keeping the other half [[1164]]. 

[[Lanny]] heads to the hold of the [[Blood Wake]] and ritually casts [[Speak with Animals]] to commune with his tiny friend [[Rat Damon]], feeding the ship's rats cheese and bread [[1165, 1166, 1167]]. 

They set sail northeast, arriving at [[Genzai]] on the [[Ushinawa Isles]] in the late afternoon [[1169, 1172]]. At [[Genzai]], the party conducts major business: they sell their captured Rahadoumi merchant ship and their accumulated cargo of plunder, giving them a massive payout of gold [[1171, 1172]]. Accompanied by [[Sandara Quinn]], [[Devi McRae]] and [[Torrin Horrendous]] visit the local temple/monastery and successfully offload the 20 to 30 rescued slave children [[1170, 1177]], paying the monks a generous donation of [[5 platinum pieces]] and [[68 gold pieces]] to ensure they receive a safe upbringing and education [[1185]]. They pay off the [[Blood Wake Crew]] and commission full repairs to the [[Blood Wake]], choosing to keep the smaller, nimbler vessel over the massive, slow merchant ship [[1171, 1172]]. 

[[Lanny]] studies [[Cyrus Wolfe's Skin Map]] with a local cartographer, who identifies the island cluster as [[Mancatcher Cove]], located directly north of their current position [[1177, 1187]]. 

[[Desmond]] finishes brewing his experimental beverage and eagerly drinks it [[1175, 1177, 1178]]. The brew has a scaly, glittery taste, and [[Desmond]] wakes up the next day realizing he has grown shiny, protective scales on the inside of his throat [[1178]]. 

The next morning, [[Sandara Quinn]] runs onto deck and alerts [[Lanny]] that their newest, overly cheerful crew member, [[Johnny]], is missing and hasn't been seen since they docked [[1188]]. [[Torrin Horrendous]] casts a [[Sending]] spell to contact [[Johnny]] [[1189]]. [[Johnny]] replies that she has decided to stay on the island and seek placement on another ship [[1189]]. [[Devi McRae]] runs around the docks for 10 minutes trying to say goodbye, but no local captains have heard of her [[1190, 1191]]. The party decides she left of her own free will and prepares to set sail for [[Mancatcher Cove]] [[1191]].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Johnny]]** (aka **[[Janny]]**): The cheerful crew member who unexpectedly departs the crew at [[Genzai]].
*   **[[Rat Damon]]**: [[Lanny]]'s rat friend below deck.
*   **[[Myrkul]]** & **[[Besmara]]**: The competing deities whose conflict has directly shaped the party's destiny.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Genzai]]**: The peaceful, wooden-built monastery town in the [[Ushinawa Isles]].
*   **[[Mancatcher Cove]]**: The northern treasure site identified on [[Cyrus Wolfe's Skin Map]].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[Parley with the Monks]]**: A social encounter where the party successfully negotiates a safe haven for the rescued children in exchange for a massive gold donation.
