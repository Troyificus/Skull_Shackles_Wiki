# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 60: Punching Sharks and One-Shotting the Boss]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 30 to Day 32 (Season 1 Finale)

## Plot Summary
The epic season finale begins deep within the flooded caverns of **[[Mancatcher Cove]]**. The four heroes, accompanied by **[[Sandara Quinn]]**, examine the **[[Shackled Prisoner]]** they discovered at the end of the previous episode. The prisoner is revealed to be a vivid orange-and-blue **[[Locathah]]**—an aquatic humanoid with large round eyes and fat lips, who has been brutally whipped and left for dead by the **[[Sahuagin]]** [1226]. To help collect any items, **[[Lanny]]** (aka **[[Captain Skull Crusher]]**) uses his **[[Cloak of Patches]]**, detaching the **[[Sack Patch]]** which instantly transforms into a real canvas sack [1227].

### The Feeding Frenzy
As the party advances, **[[Lanny]]** leads them into a massive central chamber where a "shiver" of **[[Sahuagin]]** are engaged in a wild **[[Feeding Frenzy]]** on raw meat [1228]. **[[Torrin Horrendous]]** (aka **[[Lady Blackheart]]**) unleashes a massive, high-level life-draining spell (similar to *Life Transference* or a necrotic area siphon), drawing dark energy from the feeding crowd to deal **[[9 necrotic damage]]** to the combatants while instantly healing **[[Lanny]]** back to full health [1229, 1230, 1231]. 

A chaotic melee breaks out. **[[Desmond]]** (aka **[[Dez]]** or **[[Fist]]**) leaps forward, punching several Sahuagin with **[[Flurry of Blows]]** [1232]. An older, bloated **[[Sahuagin]]** priest wielding a **[[Coral Staff]]** emerges from behind a seaweed curtain and casts **[[Mass Healing Word]]** to revive the fallen defenders [1232, 1233], allowing a revived warrior to stab **[[Desmond]]** with a spear for **[[10 piercing damage]]** [1234]. Enraged, **[[Lanny]]** enters a primal rage and recklesly slams his **[[Maul]]** into the bloated priest, dealing a staggering **[[31 points of damage]]** and crushing the spellcaster against the cavern wall [1235]. **[[Desmond]]** and **[[Devi McRae]]** (aka **[[Crimson Cray]]**) quickly clean up the remaining combatants, clearing the chamber [1236, 1237].

### Clearing the Barracks & The Shark Shiver
While **[[Desmond]]** takes a quick breather in a nearby air pocket, **[[Devi McRae]]** uses his rapier to intimidate the cowering non-combatants, ensuring they do not interfere [1238]. Exploring the adjacent private quarters, **[[Torrin]]** finds a **[[String of Pearls]]** and a valuable **[[Soapstone Statuette]]** [1239]. They proceed through a **[[Barracks]]** room containing rusted weapons and set their **[[Zombies]]** to guard the rear tunnels [1240, 1241] before swimming into a deep pool where a "shiver" of three **[[Hammerhead Sharks]]** awaits them [1242].

**[[Torrin]]** kicks off the battle by casting **[[Chromatic Orb]]** (lightning), dealing **[[11 lightning damage]]** [1243]. One of her loyal **[[Skeletons]]** is torn apart by a shark's teeth [1243, 1244, 1245]. **[[Desmond]]** swims in and lands a stunning blow on one shark [1244], while **[[Devi]]** scores a critical strike with his magical rapier, **[[Brine's Sting]]**, threading it directly through the eyes of a shark to kill it instantly [1248]. **[[Lanny]]** smashes the final shark to pieces [1247].

### The Final Showdown
Following a long tunnel to the exit of the cave system, **[[Lanny]]** discovers the throne room where the gargantuan **[[Sahuagin Baron]]** (the boss) stands on a raised altar wielding a massive double-ended trident [1250]. The Baron activates a lair action, causing thick tendrils of **[[Seaweed]]** to erupt from the floor to restrain **[[Lanny]]**, but the goliath barbarians easily kicks them away [1250, 1251]. A Sahuagin minion scores a critical spear strike on **[[Lanny]]** for **[[13 piercing damage]]** [1251].

**[[Desmond]]** hexes the Sahuagin Baron [1252], and **[[Devi]]** follows up with an incredibly spectacular critical hit using **[[Brine's Sting]]**, dealing a massive **[[49 points of damage]]** in a single thrust [1253]. **[[Torrin]]** summons her **[[Storm Sphere]]** to zap the boss with **[[16 lightning damage]]** [1254], and her **[[Zombies]]** slam him for another **[[6 bludgeoning damage]]** [1256]. The Baron desperately stabs **[[Devi]]** for 19 damage, but **[[Desmond]]** steps in and delivers a final, devastating punch to the back of the Baron's head, crushing his skull and ending the Sahuagin threat once and for all [1257, 1259].

### The Old King's Hoard
Inside the Sahuagin Baron's private chambers behind the throne, the party discovers a **[[Stone Chest]]** and a pristine, magical wooden **[[Chest of Holding]]** (which is far larger on the inside) [1260, 1261]. Inside the chests, they uncover **[[Cyrus Wolfe's Old King's Hoard]]** [1261]:
*   A magical **[[Bone Figurehead]]** (scrimshawed bones designed to be mounted as a ship figurehead) [1261].
*   **[[Magical Footwear]]** [1261].
*   An ornate **[[Deep Platinum Crown]]** worth **[[5,000 gold pieces]]** [1261, 1262].
*   A small, exquisite **[[Music Box]]** inscribed: *"For my darling Isabella"* [1262].
*   **[[10 Points of Plunder]]** consisting of fine silks, exotic furs, spices, and raw gold/silver ore [1262].
*   Assorted coins worth a massive **[[7,000 gold pieces]]** [1262].

With their hold overflowing with plunder, their pockets loaded with gold, and their names whispered in awe throughout the ports, the crew of the **[[Blood Wake]]** sets their course back to **[[Goat's Head]]** to celebrate their incredible success, concluding **[[Season 1]]** of their legendary high-seas adventures [1262, 1263].
