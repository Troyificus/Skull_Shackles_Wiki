# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 6: Botched Drills and a Bilge Murder]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 6 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Following their messy encounter with [[Reefclaws]] on the reef, the crew of the [[Wormwood]] returns to the ship with their catch. [[Mr. Plugg]] remains utterly unimpressed by the chaotic state of the landing party, ordering [[Fedor]] to carry the captured [[Reefclaws]] down to the galley. There, [[Devi McRae]] assists [[Ambrose Kroop]] (aka [[Fishguts]]) in butchering the creatures, noting that [[Reefclaw]] meat is exceptionally succulent and highly prized as a delicacy.

In a rare move of positive reinforcement, [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] decides to reward the party for bringing back such a succulent treat. He overrides [[Mr. Plugg]]'s objections and allows each member of the party to reclaim one personal item from [[Quartermaster Grook]]'s store. [[Devi McRae]] happily retrieves his fine [[Rapier]], [[Desmond]] recovers his first set of [[Knuckle Dusters]], [[Lanny]] reclaims his [[Satchel]] (containing his [[Poncho]] and personal rations), and [[Torrin Horrendous]] retrieves her personal [[Dagger]]. The party gathers below deck to celebrate with an unusually satisfying meal of boiled crabs and [[Reefclaw]] prepared by [[Fishguts]].

The next morning, the brief respite ends. During daily inspections, [[Mr. Plugg]] introduces [[Rhiaris]], a fierce, peg-legged officer sporting a gold septum nose ring, who has been tasked with putting the new recruits through rigorous boarding drills. The party, along with [[Conchobhar Shortstone]], [[Sandara Quinn]], and [[Jack Scrimshaw]], is forced to paddle a [[Jolly Boat]] out to a target vessel, throw [[Grappling Hooks]], and climb up the hull. To simulate combat chaos, the resting crew pelt them with rotten food, garbage, and empty bottles. 

While [[Lanny]] effortlessly scales the rope using raw strength, [[Devi McRae]] and [[Torrin Horrendous]] suffer embarrassing failures. [[Torrin Horrendous]] completely loses her footing on the slick wooden rail and falls directly into the water below, landing in a thick, putrid slick of fresh pig blood thrown overboard to attract sharks. [[Devi McRae]] is heavily mocked by [[Rhiaris]], who deems him "completely useless" after he slips into the sea multiple times.

The boarding drills are cut short when a large ship sits spotted on the horizon. Although [[Mr. Plugg]] attempts to warn the captain of mysterious and unresolved "trouble below" deck (implying the uninvestigated death of [[Jape]]), [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] angrily silences him. The captain bellows that there is no time for domestic disputes, ordering [[Mr. Plugg]] to immediately prepare the boarding teams.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]]**: The brutal captain of the [[Wormwood]] who rewards the party with their gear but prioritizes chasing down the sighted ship over crew problems.
*   **[[Mr. Plugg]]**: The first mate whose domestic warnings about the bilge are ignored by the captain.
*   **[[Rhiaris]]**: A fierce, peg-legged officer with a gold septum ring who oversees the brutal boarding and climbing drills.
*   **[[Grook]]**: The half-orc [[Quartermaster]] who reluctantly returns the party's confiscated gear.
*   **[[Ambrose Kroop]]** (aka **[[Fishguts]]**): The ship's cook who enthusiastically prepares the [[Reefclaw]] feast.
*   **[[Fedor]]**: [[Mr. Plugg]]'s lackey who carries the crab pots down to the galley.
*   **[[Jape]]**: A crewman whose corpse is discovered in the hold, raising unanswered questions.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Wormwood]]**: Specifically the [[Main Deck]], [[Galley]], [[Quartermaster Store]], and [[Sleeping Quarters]].
*   **[[The Reef]]**: The site where the landing party finished securing their crab pots.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[Boarding and Climbing Drills]]**: A non-lethal but physically exhausting training exercise led by [[Rhiaris]]. The party faces high climbing DCs while being bombarded with garbage, resulting in [[Torrin Horrendous]] falling into bloody, shark-infested waters.
