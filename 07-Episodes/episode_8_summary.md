# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 8: Melting Eyes and the Unloaded Ballista]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 7 (Evening)

## Plot Summary
The battle aboard the [[Rahadoumi Merchant Ship]] (revealed to be named [[Man's Promise]]) reaches its climax on the [[Stern Castle]]. The party must act swiftly to stop three merchant sailors from escaping in a [[Jolly Boat]]. [[Lanny]] makes a daring, reckless leap directly into the suspended boat, while [[Devi McRae]] takes up his position at the ship's wheel to secure their primary objective.

A formidable female [[Rahadoumi]] officer wielding a magical [[Boarding Pike of Repelling]] steps forward to defend the escape craft. She thrusts her pike with deadly precision, delivering a devastating, critical blow to [[Desmond]] that knocks him unconscious and leaves him bleeding out on the deck. Seeing his companion fall, [[Lanny]] rages, leaping across the gap to pile-drive his massive [[Maul]] directly onto the officer's head, instantly crushing her skull and ending the escape attempt.

[[Devi McRae]] abandons the ship's wheel and rushes to his twin brother's side, administering first aid and casting [[Cure Wounds]] to stabilize [[Desmond]] and bring him back from the brink of death. Meanwhile, [[Torrin Horrendous]] uses her newly acquired [[Diamond]] focus to cast [[Chromatic Orb]] and [[Toll the Dead]], decimating the remaining sailors alongside [[Lanny]]'s brutal hammer strikes.

With the [[Stern Castle]] fully secured, the rest of the [[Wormwood]] crew overwhelms the remaining defenders. [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] climbs onto the railing and commands his crew to plunder the ship's cargo hold. The crew spends hours hauling over valuable crates of furs, oils, and exotic nuts, completely filling the [[Wormwood]]'s hold. 

The victory is somber for the party as they discover that [[Badger]], the friendly barber who had cut [[Desmond]]'s hair on their first day, was killed in the fray. [[Rosie Cusswell]] is seen tearfully sewing his body into an old piece of sail alongside [[Jape]] (who is falsely believed to have died of battle wounds) for a pirate's burial at sea.

Following the plunder, [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] and [[Mr. Plugg]] distribute the spoils. Each member of the party is rewarded with a hefty pouch of **300 gold pieces** and a standard **[[Potion of Healing]]**. For her assistance during the battle, [[Torrin Horrendous]] is officially allowed to keep her **50 gold [[Diamond]]**, while [[Devi McRae]] receives an additional **50 gold pieces** for his efforts. 

To cope with the trauma of their first true boarding action, [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] begin brewing a strong batch of "dwarven courage" moonshine in a spare barrel below deck. The session ends with [[Desmond]] performing a dark, private ritual: he cuts his hand with his dagger, covers a gold coin in his blood, and offers it as a prayer to his mysterious patron, [[Myrkul]], pledging his soul in exchange for power.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]]**: The captain of the [[Wormwood]] who declares a grand victory and distributes the plunder.
*   **[[Mr. Plugg]]**: The first mate who helps coordinate the distribution of gold and potions.
*   **[[Master Scourge]]**: The boatswain who oversees the capture of the surviving [[Rahadoumi]] sailors.
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: The cleric who defends the [[Wormwood]] during the boarding and later assists the wounded.
*   **[[Rosie Cusswell]]**: The halfling rigger who prepares [[Badger]]'s body for a burial at sea.
*   **[[Badger]]**: A friendly crewman and barber who tragically dies during the boarding action.
*   **[[Rahadoumi Officer]]**: A skilled warrior who knocks [[Desmond]] unconscious with her magical pike before being slain by [[Lanny]].

## Locations Visited
*   **[[The Man's Promise]]**: The captured [[Rahadoumi]] merchant vessel, specifically the [[Stern Castle]] and [[Jolly Boat]] stations.
*   **[[Wormwood]]**: The ship's deck and sleeping quarters where the crew celebrates.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[Clash at the Jollyboat]]**: A desperate skirmish to prevent the merchant crew from escaping. [[Desmond]] is nearly killed by the [[Rahadoumi Officer]]'s pike, but [[Lanny]] and [[Torrin Horrendous]] turn the tide, wiping out the remaining defenders.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **300 Gold Pieces**: Awarded to each party member.
*   **[[Potion of Healing]]**: Given to each party member.
*   [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s **[[Diamond]]**: Officially awarded (50 gold value).
*   [[Devi McRae]]'s **50 Gold Pieces**: Additional reward for exemplary service.
*   **[[Boarding Pike of Repelling]]**: A magical, extending plus-one pike salvaged from the dead [[Rahadoumi Officer]] (later identified by the party).
