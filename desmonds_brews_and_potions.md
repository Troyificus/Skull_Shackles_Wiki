# [[Desmond]]'s Alchemical Ledger: Brews & Potions of the [[Blood Wake]]

This ledger catalogs every experimental alcoholic beverage, spirit, and potion brewed by the **[[Blood Wake Crew]]**'s resident monk and brawler, **[[Desmond]]** (aka **[[Dez]]** or **[[Fist]]**), using his **[[Brewor's Supplies]]** [649, 1318]. 

Every successful batch is standardized for future production, while the failures serve as costly lessons in high-seas mixology.

---

## 🍺 Successful Brews & Potions

### 1. [[Boar's Blood Brew]] (aka "**[[Red Bull]]**")
*   **Episode Created:** Episode 31 [453, 454]
*   **Ingredients:** 
    *   **[[Boar's Blood]]** (harvested from wild boars) [74, 454]
    *   **[[Cereal Grains]]** and **[[Yeast]]** [454, 455]
*   **Brewing Details:** Desmond successfully boiled the mash and fermented the mixture with a high roll of **19** and **21** [455, 467].
*   **Effects & Flavor Profile:**
    *   Smells surprisingly pleasant and sweet [455].
    *   Grants the drinker **[[Temporary Hit Points]]** equal to **[[2d4 + 4]]** (lasting until their next long rest) [456, 457].
    *   The drinker counts as **[[Huge]]** size for the purposes of determining carrying capacity and the maximum weight they can push, drag, or lift [456].
    *   Nicknamed "**[[Red Bull]]**" (or "Red Ball") by the crew because *"it gives you wings"* [461].

### 2. [[Troll Venom Brew]] (aka "**[[Snake Bite]]**")
*   **Episode Created:** Episode 43 & 44 [853]
*   **Ingredients:** 
    *   **[[Venom Troll]]** Acidic Venom (harvested following the colosseum clash in Little Upara) [44, 853]
    *   **[[Alchemical Spirits]]** [853]
*   **Brewing Details:** Desmond successfully distilled the toxic sludge, leaving a thick tar-like waste at the bottom and a purified dark-colored liquid in the collection bulb (rolling a **19** and a **23** to complete) [854, 899].
*   **Effects & Flavor Profile:**
    *   A dark, transparent liquor with a distinct green tinge and tiny sparkling flakes floating inside [899].
    *   Tastes of an intense, acrid peat with a sharp, burning acidic aftertaste [900, 1115].
    *   Increases the drinker's **[[Wisdom]]** score by **[[+2]]** for one minute [901]. For a Monk like Desmond, this temporarily boosts their **[[Armor Class]]**, **[[Wisdom Saving Throws]]**, and **[[Stunning Strike DC]]** [901].

### 3. [[Blue Dartwing Spirit]] (aka "**[[Deep Dive]]**")
*   **Episode Created:** Episode 43 & 44 [775]
*   **Ingredients:** 
    *   **[[Blue Dartwing]]** (rare dragonfly/insect wings) [775]
    *   **[[Neutral Spirits]]** [775, 776]
*   **Brewing Details:** Meticulously prepared and distilled to avoid any mistakes, resulting in a successful **21** roll [776, 814].
*   **Effects & Flavor Profile:**
    *   A beautiful, shimmering blue spirit with a pearlescent film on top and a sweet, floral aroma, though it tastes completely neutral [815].
    *   **[[Double Spell Effect]]**: The next time the drinker is targeted by *any* spell, the effects of that spell are completely **[[doubled]]** [829]. (During the Venom Troll fight, Desmond drank this and was subsequently targeted by a *Greater Healing Potion*, doubling its healing output to a massive **[[28 hit points]]**!) [829].

### 4. [[Death Bell Wine]] (aka "**[[Unsafety Dance]]**")
*   **Episode Created:** Episode 46 [948]
*   **Ingredients:** 
    *   **[[Death Bell]]** (a rare, deep purple jungle flower) [948]
    *   **[[Wine Yeast]]** and **[[Cereal Grains]]** [948]
*   **Brewing Details:** Successfully boiled and fermented with a roll of **17** [948].
*   **Effects & Flavor Profile:**
    *   A smooth, pleasant, and deeply floral wine that goes down exceptionally easy [950].
    *   **[[Manic Dancing Curse]]**: The drinker is instantly filled with a frantic, restless physical energy and is forced to dance a jaunty jig uncontrollably until they collapse from complete physical exhaustion [950, 954].
    *   The manic dancing cannot be cured by poison-neutralizing magic or **[[Lesser Restoration]]** [953]. However, the drinker can be temporarily anchored in place using a **[[Hold Person]]** spell [953].

### 5. [[Coconut Moonshine]]
*   **Episode Created:** Episode 37 [649]
*   **Ingredients:** 
    *   **[[Coconuts]]** (milk and flesh) [649]
    *   **[[Alchemical Yeast]]** [649]
*   **Brewing Details:** Brewed over several days on the open sea by puncturing and fermenting coconuts using apparatus from his poisoner's kit [649, 650].
*   **Effects & Flavor Profile:**
    *   A highly volatile, rough-cut **[[80% proof moonshine]]** that is extremely intoxicating [421, 649].
    *   Smells strongly of raw alcohol and coconut husk [421].

## 🤢 Failed Batches & Disasters

### 1. [[Ghoul's Blood Brew]] (aka "**[[Gold's Blood]]**")
*   **Ingredients:** Watered-down **[[Ghoul's Blood]]** harvested from the island ghouls [26, 73, 272].
*   **Result:** The blood curdled and congealed in the mash, failing to ferment correctly and creating a foul, clotted sludge [73]. Desmond had to discreetly dump the entire batch into the sea [73, 80].

### 2. [[Frothy Green Liquid Brew]]
*   **Ingredients:** Frothy green liquid harvested from a water-naga-like creature [74].
*   **Result:** Failed batch [80].

### 3. [[Spider Egg Liqueur]]
*   **Ingredients:** **[[Spider Egg]]** and **[[Giant Spider]]** web [905].
*   **Result:** Failed the DC 15 check with a roll of **14** [905, 906]. The spider silks and egg whites congealed into a thick, sticky, and completely foul-smelling substance resembling "PVA glue" [906].
