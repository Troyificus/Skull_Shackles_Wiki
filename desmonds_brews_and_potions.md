# [Desmond](/desmond)'s Alchemical Ledger: Brews & Potions of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake)

This ledger catalogs every experimental alcoholic beverage, spirit, and potion brewed by the **[Blood Wake Crew](/blood-wake-crew)**'s resident monk and brawler, **[Desmond](/desmond)** (aka **[Dez](/dez)** or **[Fist](/fist)**), using his **[Brewor's Supplies](/brewors-supplies)** [649, 1318]. 

Every successful batch is standardized for future production, while the failures serve as costly lessons in high-seas mixology.

---

## 🍺 Successful Brews & Potions

### 1. [Boar's Blood Brew](/boars-blood-brew) (aka "**[Red Bull](/red-bull)**")
*   **Episode Created:** Episode 31 [453, 454]
*   **Ingredients:** 
    *   **[Boar's Blood](/boars-blood)** (harvested from wild boars) [74, 454]
    *   **[Cereal Grains](/cereal-grains)** and **[Yeast](/yeast)** [454, 455]
*   **Brewing Details:** Desmond successfully boiled the mash and fermented the mixture with a high roll of **19** and **21** [455, 467].
*   **Effects & Flavor Profile:**
    *   Smells surprisingly pleasant and sweet [455].
    *   Grants the drinker **[Temporary Hit Points](/temporary-hit-points)** equal to **[2d4 + 4](/2d4-4)** (lasting until their next long rest) [456, 457].
    *   The drinker counts as **[Huge](/huge)** size for the purposes of determining carrying capacity and the maximum weight they can push, drag, or lift [456].
    *   Nicknamed "**[Red Bull](/red-bull)**" (or "Red Ball") by the crew because *"it gives you wings"* [461].

### 2. [Troll Venom Brew](/troll-venom-brew) (aka "**[Snake Bite](/snake-bite)**")
*   **Episode Created:** Episode 43 & 44 [853]
*   **Ingredients:** 
    *   **[Venom Troll](/venom-troll)** Acidic Venom (harvested following the colosseum clash in Little Upara) [44, 853]
    *   **[Alchemical Spirits](/alchemical-spirits)** [853]
*   **Brewing Details:** Desmond successfully distilled the toxic sludge, leaving a thick tar-like waste at the bottom and a purified dark-colored liquid in the collection bulb (rolling a **19** and a **23** to complete) [854, 899].
*   **Effects & Flavor Profile:**
    *   A dark, transparent liquor with a distinct green tinge and tiny sparkling flakes floating inside [899].
    *   Tastes of an intense, acrid peat with a sharp, burning acidic aftertaste [900, 1115].
    *   Increases the drinker's **[Wisdom](/wisdom)** score by **[+2](/2)** for one minute [901]. For a Monk like Desmond, this temporarily boosts their **[Armor Class](/armor-class)**, **[Wisdom Saving Throws](/wisdom-saving-throws)**, and **[Stunning Strike DC](/stunning-strike-dc)** [901].

### 3. [Blue Dartwing Spirit](/blue-dartwing-spirit) (aka "**[Deep Dive](/deep-dive)**")
*   **Episode Created:** Episode 43 & 44 [775]
*   **Ingredients:** 
    *   **[Blue Dartwing](/blue-dartwing)** (rare dragonfly/insect wings) [775]
    *   **[Neutral Spirits](/neutral-spirits)** [775, 776]
*   **Brewing Details:** Meticulously prepared and distilled to avoid any mistakes, resulting in a successful **21** roll [776, 814].
*   **Effects & Flavor Profile:**
    *   A beautiful, shimmering blue spirit with a pearlescent film on top and a sweet, floral aroma, though it tastes completely neutral [815].
    *   **[Double Spell Effect](/double-spell-effect)**: The next time the drinker is targeted by *any* spell, the effects of that spell are completely **[doubled](/doubled)** [829]. (During the Venom Troll fight, Desmond drank this and was subsequently targeted by a *Greater Healing Potion*, doubling its healing output to a massive **[28 hit points](/28-hit-points)**!) [829].

### 4. [Death Bell Wine](/death-bell-wine) (aka "**[Unsafety Dance](/unsafety-dance)**")
*   **Episode Created:** Episode 46 [948]
*   **Ingredients:** 
    *   **[Death Bell](/death-bell)** (a rare, deep purple jungle flower) [948]
    *   **[Wine Yeast](/wine-yeast)** and **[Cereal Grains](/cereal-grains)** [948]
*   **Brewing Details:** Successfully boiled and fermented with a roll of **17** [948].
*   **Effects & Flavor Profile:**
    *   A smooth, pleasant, and deeply floral wine that goes down exceptionally easy [950].
    *   **[Manic Dancing Curse](/manic-dancing-curse)**: The drinker is instantly filled with a frantic, restless physical energy and is forced to dance a jaunty jig uncontrollably until they collapse from complete physical exhaustion [950, 954].
    *   The manic dancing cannot be cured by poison-neutralizing magic or **[Lesser Restoration](/lesser-restoration)** [953]. However, the drinker can be temporarily anchored in place using a **[Hold Person](/hold-person)** spell [953].

### 5. [Coconut Moonshine](/coconut-moonshine)
*   **Episode Created:** Episode 37 [649]
*   **Ingredients:** 
    *   **[Coconuts](/coconuts)** (milk and flesh) [649]
    *   **[Alchemical Yeast](/alchemical-yeast)** [649]
*   **Brewing Details:** Brewed over several days on the open sea by puncturing and fermenting coconuts using apparatus from his poisoner's kit [649, 650].
*   **Effects & Flavor Profile:**
    *   A highly volatile, rough-cut **[80% proof moonshine](/80-proof-moonshine)** that is extremely intoxicating [421, 649].
    *   Smells strongly of raw alcohol and coconut husk [421].

## 🤢 Failed Batches & Disasters

### 1. [Ghoul's Blood Brew](/ghouls-blood-brew) (aka "**[Gold's Blood](/golds-blood)**")
*   **Ingredients:** Watered-down **[Ghoul's Blood](/ghouls-blood)** harvested from the island ghouls [26, 73, 272].
*   **Result:** The blood curdled and congealed in the mash, failing to ferment correctly and creating a foul, clotted sludge [73]. Desmond had to discreetly dump the entire batch into the sea [73, 80].

### 2. [Frothy Green Liquid Brew](/frothy-green-liquid-brew)
*   **Ingredients:** Frothy green liquid harvested from a water-naga-like creature [74].
*   **Result:** Failed batch [80].

### 3. [Spider Egg Liqueur](/spider-egg-liqueur)
*   **Ingredients:** **[Spider Egg](/spider-egg)** and **[Giant Spider](/giant-spider)** web [905].
*   **Result:** Failed the DC 15 check with a roll of **14** [905, 906]. The spider silks and egg whites congealed into a thick, sticky, and completely foul-smelling substance resembling "PVA glue" [906].
