# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 13: The Hanging Ghoul and the Grindylow Cave]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 5 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Following their encounter with the insectoid beast, the party ascends the cliffside path. At the crest, they find a wooden [[Stockade]] built beside a fresh water stream. Approaching the stockade, they find a large tree growing next to a spring and a wooden hut. Inside, they discover the rotted corpse of the former occupant, who apparently hung himself.

While [[Lanny]] and [[Devi McRae]] search the hut, [[Desmond]] and [[Torrin Horrendous]] investigate a [[Spyglass]] mounted outside. Peering through, [[Desmond]] spots three [[Grindylows]] frolicking on the rocks at the water's edge. To his horror, he realizes one of them is wearing [[Sandara Quinn]]'s distinct tricorn hat. Inside the hut, the hanging corpse suddenly wrenches itself into life (a [[Ghoul]]) and attacks [[Lanny]]. [[Lanny]] blocks the attack with his [[Maul]], and [[Desmond]] rushes in, hitting the undead with his quarterstaff and a [[Flurry of Blows]]. [[Devi McRae]] uses insightful tactics and stabs it with his [[Rapier]], and [[Torrin Horrendous]] casts [[Toll the Dead]] to finish the creature.

Searching the cabin's cupboard, the party discovers seven or eight finely tailored [[Courtiers' Outfits]] in surprisingly good condition (worth 20 gold pieces each). In one of the pockets, [[Devi McRae]] finds a [[Silver Ring]]. 

Determined to rescue [[Sandara Quinn]] and [[Rosie Cusswell]], the party creeps south toward the beach. [[Devi McRae]] takes point, spotting the half-submerged skeleton of a wrecked ship off the coast. They locate the entrance to a narrow, five-foot-wide [[Grindylow Cave]] system guarded by four [[Grindylows]]. Initiating a surprise attack, they ambush the sea goblins. 

A fierce, underwater skirmish breaks out in the cold, deep water. [[Devi McRae]] is severely injured by a spear to the gut, taking a critical hit for 10 piercing damage and dropping to 7 hit points. [[Lanny]] uses [[Sentinel]] to protect him, and [[Desmond]] cracks the creatures with his quarterstaff. [[Torrin Horrendous]] unleashes a double [[Toll the Dead]] spell, causing black blood to pour from the creatures' eyes and ears, destroying the remaining guards. Recognizing that they are badly beaten and the cave is heavily occupied, the party retreats to the cliff's edge to take a short rest, tend to [[Devi McRae]]'s wounds with a [[Potion of Healing]], and plan their next move.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Hanging Man]] (Ghoul)**: The undead corpse of the stockade's former owner, who attacks the party when they explore his hut.
*   **[[Grindylows]]**: Cruel, octopus-like sea goblins who have taken over the caves and captured [[Sandara Quinn]] and [[Rosie Cusswell]].

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Stockade]]**: A fortified outpost on the cliff containing a fresh water spring and a wooden hut.
*   **[[Grindylow Cave]]**: A wet, claustrophobic cavern network at the base of the cliffs.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[Hanging Man]]**: A sudden cabin fight against a suspended ghoul.
*   **[[Grindylow Cave]] Entrance**: An ambush and wet skirmish against four grindylow guards, which leaves [[Devi McRae]] critically wounded before [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s magic sweeps the field.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Courtiers' Outfits]]**: Seven or eight high-quality fancy outfits worth 20 gold pieces each.
*   **[[Silver Ring]]**: A simple silver ring found in a pocket of the courtiers' outfits.
*   **[[Potion of Healing]]**: Used immediately to restore [[Devi McRae]]'s health.
