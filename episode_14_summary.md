# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 14: Lanny’s Cannonball and the Whale Toad]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 5 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
As the party rests on the cliff's edge, a lone [[Grindylow]] emerges from the cave, spots its dead kin, and locks eyes with [[Lanny]]. [[Lanny]] immediately launches himself off the 60-foot cliff, falling like a cannonball to smush the creature with his [[Maul]] (rolling a natural 20 on initiative). The party re-enters the caves. [[Devi McRae]] uses his newly discovered [[Magic Ring of Swimming]] (which grants him a swim speed and the ability to breathe underwater) to scout ahead. 

[[Devi McRae]] hears chattering and spots two [[Grindylows]] in a heated argument. He signals back to the group, but as they move into a bottleneck pool, they are ambushed. After a quick skirmish, they push deeper. [[Devi McRae]] swims ahead and discovers the [[Grindylow Queen]] (a spellcasting matriarch) alongside her guards and a massive, swollen [[Whale-Toad]] (a grotesque, giant grindelow). Stringing from the ceiling are [[Sandara Quinn]] and a pale, unconscious [[Rosie Cusswell]], whose leg has been brutally severed below the knee.

The party coordinates a rescue plan. [[Devi McRae]] swims stealthily under the water (rolling a 14) and cuts [[Sandara Quinn]]'s binds, handing her a dagger as she floats to the surface. However, the combat spirals out of control: [[Lanny]] is bitten and swallowed whole by the massive [[Whale-Toad]], taking acidic stomach damage. [[Desmond]] dives in to punch the beast with disadvantage, breaking off pieces of its rubbery skin. [[Torrin Horrendous]] protects the swallowed barbarian by casting [[Shield of Faith]] on [[Lanny]], and repeatedly uses [[Shocking Grasp]] to shock the grndylows.

The [[Grindylow Queen]] launches her magical harpoon at [[Desmond]], who deftly deflects it. During the chaos, [[Sandara Quinn]] cuts [[Rosie Cusswell]]'s bonds and drags her to safety. The Queen stabs [[Devi McRae]], knocking him unconscious at the water's surface. With their spell slots spent and resources depleted, [[Torrin Horrendous]] steps up. She unleashes a level 2 [[Chromatic Orb]] (lightning), dealing 21 lightning damage to the Queen. As [[Devi McRae]] floats unconsciously, [[Lanny]] slices his way out of the [[Whale-Toad]]'s stomach. [[Torrin Horrendous]] finishes off the [[Grindylow Queen]] with a final level 1 [[Chromatic Orb]], electrocuting her from the inside out and laughing maniacally as the matriarch collapses.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Grindylow Queen]]**: The spellcasting matriarch of the caves who holds [[Sandara Quinn]] and [[Rosie Cusswell]] captive.
*   **[[Whale-Toad]]**: A grotesque, giant grindelow of swollen proportions that swallows [[Lanny]] whole during the battle.
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: Captured cleric of [[Besmara]] rescued from the ceiling of the cavern.
*   **[[Rosie Cusswell]]**: Captured halfling rigger rescued by the party, suffering from a severed leg.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Grindylow Cave]]**: The flooded, dark cavern system of the sea goblins.
*   **The Queen's Cavern**: The main chamber of the grindylow tribe where prisoners are kept.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **The Cliff Plunge**: [[Lanny]] jumps off the cliff to crush a lone grindylow.
*   **The Queen's Chamber Battle**: A high-stakes, underwater boss fight where [[Lanny]] is swallowed by a [[Whale-Toad]], [[Devi McRae]] is knocked unconscious, and [[Torrin Horrendous]] ultimately electrocutes the [[Grindylow Queen]] to secure a desperate victory.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Magic Ring of Swimming]]**: A ring that grants the wearer aquatic mobility and water breathing (discovered earlier and tested by [[Devi McRae]] this session).
