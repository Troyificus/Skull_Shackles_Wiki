# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 15: Tactical Gore and the Hat Boat]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 12-13 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Following the chaotic battle in the depths of the [[Grindylow Cave]], the session begins with the party securing their victory. [[Desmond]] rushes to retrieve the limp body of his twin brother, [[Devi McRae]], who is sinking beneath the water's surface [18]. [[Desmond]] brings [[Devi McRae]] to the surface and calls out for [[Sandara Quinn]]'s aid [18]. Having survived her own ordeal, [[Sandara Quinn]] channels her remaining level 1 spell slot to cast [[Cure Wounds]] on [[Devi McRae]], restoring him to consciousness [18].

The party then turns their attention to the cavern, checking on a pale and exhausted [[Rosie Cusswell]], who has tragically lost her leg from the knee down [18, 144]. Under the supervision of [[Sandara Quinn]] and the party, [[Rosie Cusswell]] is kept stable [27]. The heroes proceed to loot the [[Grindylow Queen]]'s cavern, retrieving several valuable items including a [[Silver Dagger]], a [[Silver Sickle]], and an ornate [[Bone Blade]] with a scrimshawed handle [20]. They also discover [[Sandara's Tricorn Hat]], a massive [[Shark's Jawbone]] engraved with magical runes representing spell scrolls, and four [[Magical Crossbow Bolts]] [21, 22].

Recognizing that the afternoon is drawing to a close, [[Lanny]] helps carry the injured [[Rosie Cusswell]] while the party collects their substantial spoils [23, 25]. They scale the cliffs using their grappling hook and return to the safety of the fortified [[Stockade]] at the top [25].

To prepare for their eventual return to the [[Man's Promise]], the party devises a cunning ruse to deceive [[Mr. Plugg]]. They cut down the rotting corpse of the stockade's former occupant (the hung man) and burn it outside [26]. Before doing so, [[Devi McRae]], with [[Lanny]]'s assistance, harvests the thick, dark ghoul blood from the corpse to create a gruesome "bloody mary" cocktail mixed with water [26, 27]. The twins plan to smear this thin, visceral liquid over themselves to fake being in a severely weakened and beaten state, claiming that [[Desmond]] was "eaten by a giant toad" or otherwise mauled, to make [[Mr. Plugg]] believe they are too exhausted to mount any resistance [28, 30].

Meanwhile, [[Sandara Quinn]] reveals a critical secret to [[Desmond]]: her tricorn hat is actually a magical item passed down from her father [34]. Upon reading [[Besmara's Code]] embroidered along the inside brim, [[Desmond]] learns that the hat can magically transform into a functional rowing boat when placed in water [34, 445]. This provides the party with the perfect vehicle to execute a midnight mutiny. The plan is finalized: [[Torrin Horrendous]], [[Devi McRae]], and [[Lanny]] will return to the ship by day with the fresh water, faking the tragic news of [[Desmond]]'s demise [30]. At night, [[Desmond]], [[Sandara Quinn]], and [[Rosie Cusswell]] will use the magical tricorn hat boat to sneak onto the opposite side of the ship, allowing the party to launch a surprise attack on [[Mr. Plugg]] and his loyalist officers [30, 31].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: The devout cleric of [[Besmara]] who heals the party, shares the secret of her magical tricorn hat, and stands ready to support the mutiny against [[Mr. Plugg]] [18, 30, 34].
*   **[[Rosie Cusswell]]**: The tough halfling rigger who is rescued from the [[Grindylows]], though she is left severely weakened after losing her leg from the knee down [18, 36, 144].
*   **[[Mr. Plugg]]**: The cruel first mate of the [[Man's Promise]] whose impending plot to "thin out" the party's ranks has forced the group to plan an immediate, high-stakes mutiny [28].
*   **[[Master Scourge]]**: Mentioned as a loyalist and chief threat aboard the ship who must be dealt with during the mutiny [29, 31].
*   **[[Fedor]]**: Mentioned as a potential wild card; the party wonders if he can be swayed to their side due to his previous interaction with [[Lanny]] [29].
*   **[[Owlbear Albert]]**: The chained goliath-sized champion who might be turned to the party's side or used as a chaotic distraction if released [29, 32].

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Grindylow Cave]]**: The damp, sea-swept cavern where the party rescued their friends, looted the Queen's hoard, and recovered the magical [[Shark's Jawbone]] [16, 21].
*   **[[Stockade]]**: The fortified wooden camp at the top of the island cliff where the party holes up to rest, heal their wounds, and coordinate their mutiny plan [25, 33].

*   **[[Silver Sickle]]**: Found on the [[Grindylow Queen]]'s person [20].

*   **[[Ghoul Blood Cocktail]]**: A thin, viscid mixture of water and congealed undead blood crafted by [[Devi McRae]] to simulate severe wounds [26, 27].
