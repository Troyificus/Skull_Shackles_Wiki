# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 17: Pulling Gold Teeth and Chaining Gunter]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 14-15 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Picking up immediately after the savage, chaotic midnight mutiny aboard the [[Man's Promise]], the deck is silent save for the groans of the wounded [297]. [[Devi McRae]] rushes to the side of [[Fedor]] to cast [[Spare the Dying]], but discovers the loyalist thug is already dead (having rolled a natural 1 on his death saving throws) [299]. [[Devi McRae]] retrieves his beloved purple jacket from [[Fedor]]'s corpse [300]. Nearby, the burning, smoldering remains of [[Master Scourge]] are extinguished by [[Torrin Horrendous]], who casts [[Control Flames]] [300].

Meanwhile, [[Desmond]] is furious with [[Owlbear Albert]], who nearly ruined the ambush due to his rattling chains [301]. [[Desmond]] demands to execute the goliath-sized beast, but [[Torrin Horrendous]] fiercely defends him, promising that [[Owlbear Albert]] will be her personal responsibility [301]. Utilizing her magical prowess, [[Torrin Horrendous]] casts [[Thormaturgy]] to manifest the sweet, soothing aroma of fresh honey [315]. Combined with her calm words, she rolls a spectacular natural 20 to soothe the panicked beast, permanently winning his trust [314, 315].

From below decks, [[Sargava]] stumbles up the ladder clutching a bloody cutlass, announcing that the [[Rahadoumi]] captives handily won the fight below [302]. The other [[Rahadoumi]] sailors are wounded but alive, while [[Sly]] and the unnamed dwarf lie dead [302, 303]. The creepy [[Cogwood]] is found lying on the floor, laughing hysterically next to the corpses [303].

With [[Mr. Plugg]] bound and chained to the main mast, [[Desmond]] and [[Torrin Horrendous]] begin a brutal interrogation [305, 310]. [[Lanny]] steps up and uses his dagger to shear off [[Mr. Plugg]]'s prized ponytail [306]. [[Torrin Horrendous]] kneels before [[Mr. Plugg]], who snarls and calls her a "treacherous bitch" [307]. Undeterred, [[Torrin Horrendous]] twists her fingers into his manacles, executing a devastating [[Intimidation]] check (rolling a 24) to draw blood and force a confession [307, 311]. The defeated first mate finally whimpers his true name: [[Gunter Plug]] [311]. [[Devi McRae]] places a cold blade against [[Gunter Plug]]'s neck, telling him how much he loathes insignificant bullies, before leaving him to stew [312, 313].

The party systematically loots the fallen loyalists:
*   On [[Master Scourge]], they find his leather armor, a [[Whip]] (cat-of-nine-tails), a [[Punching Dagger]], two unique dark glass vials, 29 gold coins, a silver wedding ring, and a diamond-studded leather snuff box [316, 317].
*   On [[Fedor]], they retrieve a gold earring (worth 15 gold pieces), 10 gold coins, and a lucky rabbit's foot [317].
*   On [[Arita]], they recover a gold earring, a purple gemstone, and a small silver tankard [320].
*   [[Desmond]] also uses a chisel to pry out five remaining gold teeth from [[Master Scourge]]'s mouth [319].

[[Torrin Horrendous]] casts [[Detect Magic]] and discovers that the manacles holding [[Owlbear Albert]] are actually the magical [[Shackles of Compliance]], which she promptly claims [320]. Her spell also pings a magical aura coming from [[Mr. Plugg]]'s hammock [320]. 

At dawn, [[Sandara Quinn]] leads a solemn pirate burial at sea for the fallen crew members, throwing their bodies over the side while the crew tosses gold coins into the water as tribute to [[Besmara]] [321, 1044].

The next morning, the crew is summoned to the deck by the ship's bell [331]. [[Ambrose Kroop]] (aka [[Fishguts]]) is brought up by the twins, completely hungover but absolutely ecstatic that the party has successfully overthrown [[Mr. Plugg]] [331-334]. [[Torrin Horrendous]] uses [[Thormaturgy]] to address the assembled crew, officially announcing the change in leadership and welcoming their new era of freedom [334]. [[Gunter Plug]] is stripped of his dignity and sent to the bilges to pump water alongside the crew [335].

Consulting with [[Tally]], the helmsman, the party plots a course north toward [[Rickety's Squibs]], a hidden dry dock where they can safely repair and remodel the ship to evade [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]]'s wrath [340, 342]. [[Tally]] also warns them about the legendary vampire captain who commands the ghost ship [[Drachia]] (or [[Dreidshire]]), which is constantly shrouded in an unnatural, creeping fog [339]. The session closes with the crew united, preparing a celebratory lunch in the galley as they set sail under their own banner [336, 342].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Gunter Plug]]** (formerly [[Mr. Plugg]]): The defeated first mate, now bound in chains, stripped of his ponytail, and sent to work the bilges [306, 311, 335].
*   **[[Owlbear Albert]]**: Calmed and befriended by [[Torrin Horrendous]]; he is officially freed from his shackles and welcomed as a crew ally [301, 315].
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: Conducted the sacred burial at sea and supported the party's transition to command [321].
*   **[[Ambrose Kroop]]** (aka [[Fishguts]]): The ship's cook who happily celebrates the mutiny and prepares a celebratory lunch [331, 333, 336].
*   **[[Sargava]]**: The leader of the [[Rahadoumi]] sailors who helped secure the lower decks during the mutiny [302].
*   **[[Tally]]**: The half-elf helmsman who provides vital navigational advice and warns the party about the dread vampire ship [339, 340].
*   **[[Cogwood]]**: Found laughing maniacally among the dead; remains on the ship [303].
