# [Skull and Shackles Episode 17: Pulling Gold Teeth and Chaining Gunter](/skull-and-shackles-episode-17-pulling-gold-teeth-and-chaining-gunter)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 14-15 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Picking up immediately after the savage, chaotic midnight mutiny aboard the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise), the deck is silent save for the groans of the wounded [297]. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) rushes to the side of [Fedor](/fedor) to cast [Spare the Dying](/spare-the-dying), but discovers the loyalist thug is already dead (having rolled a natural 1 on his death saving throws) [299]. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) retrieves his beloved purple jacket from [Fedor](/fedor)'s corpse [300]. Nearby, the burning, smoldering remains of [Master Scourge](/master-scourge) are extinguished by [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous), who casts [Control Flames](/control-flames) [300].

Meanwhile, [Desmond](/desmond) is furious with [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert), who nearly ruined the ambush due to his rattling chains [301]. [Desmond](/desmond) demands to execute the goliath-sized beast, but [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) fiercely defends him, promising that [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert) will be her personal responsibility [301]. Utilizing her magical prowess, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) casts [Thormaturgy](/thormaturgy) to manifest the sweet, soothing aroma of fresh honey [315]. Combined with her calm words, she rolls a spectacular natural 20 to soothe the panicked beast, permanently winning his trust [314, 315].

From below decks, [Sargava](/sargava) stumbles up the ladder clutching a bloody cutlass, announcing that the [Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi) captives handily won the fight below [302]. The other [Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi) sailors are wounded but alive, while [Sly](/sly) and the unnamed dwarf lie dead [302, 303]. The creepy [Cogwood](/cogwood) is found lying on the floor, laughing hysterically next to the corpses [303].

With [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) bound and chained to the main mast, [Desmond](/desmond) and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) begin a brutal interrogation [305, 310]. [Lanny](/lanny) steps up and uses his dagger to shear off [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)'s prized ponytail [306]. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) kneels before [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg), who snarls and calls her a "treacherous bitch" [307]. Undeterred, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) twists her fingers into his manacles, executing a devastating [Intimidation](/intimidation) check (rolling a 24) to draw blood and force a confession [307, 311]. The defeated first mate finally whimpers his true name: [Gunter Plug](/gunter-plug) [311]. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) places a cold blade against [Gunter Plug](/gunter-plug)'s neck, telling him how much he loathes insignificant bullies, before leaving him to stew [312, 313].

The party systematically loots the fallen loyalists:
*   On [Master Scourge](/master-scourge), they find his leather armor, a [Whip](/whip) (cat-of-nine-tails), a [Punching Dagger](/punching-dagger), two unique dark glass vials, 29 gold coins, a silver wedding ring, and a diamond-studded leather snuff box [316, 317].
*   On [Fedor](/fedor), they retrieve a gold earring (worth 15 gold pieces), 10 gold coins, and a lucky rabbit's foot [317].
*   On [Arita](/arita), they recover a gold earring, a purple gemstone, and a small silver tankard [320].
*   [Desmond](/desmond) also uses a chisel to pry out five remaining gold teeth from [Master Scourge](/master-scourge)'s mouth [319].

[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) casts [Detect Magic](/detect-magic) and discovers that the manacles holding [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert) are actually the magical [Shackles of Compliance](/shackles-of-compliance), which she promptly claims [320]. Her spell also pings a magical aura coming from [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)'s hammock [320]. 

At dawn, [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) leads a solemn pirate burial at sea for the fallen crew members, throwing their bodies over the side while the crew tosses gold coins into the water as tribute to [Besmara](/besmara) [321, 1044].

The next morning, the crew is summoned to the deck by the ship's bell [331]. [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) (aka [Fishguts](/fishguts)) is brought up by the twins, completely hungover but absolutely ecstatic that the party has successfully overthrown [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) [331-334]. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) uses [Thormaturgy](/thormaturgy) to address the assembled crew, officially announcing the change in leadership and welcoming their new era of freedom [334]. [Gunter Plug](/gunter-plug) is stripped of his dignity and sent to the bilges to pump water alongside the crew [335].

Consulting with [Tally](/tally), the helmsman, the party plots a course north toward [Rickety's Squibs](/ricketys-squibs), a hidden dry dock where they can safely repair and remodel the ship to evade [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan)'s wrath [340, 342]. [Tally](/tally) also warns them about the legendary vampire captain who commands the ghost ship [Drachia](/drachia) (or [Dreidshire](/dreidshire)), which is constantly shrouded in an unnatural, creeping fog [339]. The session closes with the crew united, preparing a celebratory lunch in the galley as they set sail under their own banner [336, 342].

*   **[Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert)**: Calmed and befriended by [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous); he is officially freed from his shackles and welcomed as a crew ally [301, 315].
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: Conducted the sacred burial at sea and supported the party's transition to command [321].
*   **[Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)** (aka [Fishguts](/fishguts)): The ship's cook who happily celebrates the mutiny and prepares a celebratory lunch [331, 333, 336].
*   **[Sargava](/sargava)**: The leader of the [Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi) sailors who helped secure the lower decks during the mutiny [302].
*   **[Tally](/tally)**: The half-elf helmsman who provides vital navigational advice and warns the party about the dread vampire ship [339, 340].
*   **[Cogwood](/cogwood)**: Found laughing maniacally among the dead; remains on the ship [303].
