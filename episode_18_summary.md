# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 18: Mutiny Logistics and the Blood Wake]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 12 to Day 15 aboard the [[Man's Promise]] (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Following their successful midnight mutiny, the party must deal with the logistics of managing the [[Man's Promise]] and securing their victory. [[Ambrose Kroop]] (aka [[Fishguts]]) joins the party on deck and warns them that [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] might possess magical means of tracking [[Gunter Plugg]] (formerly [[Mr. Plugg]]), who is currently bound and imprisoned in the bilges under the guard of their [[Rahadoumi]] allies. To sever any potential magical link and prevent [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] from hunting them down, they discuss Plugg's fate, ultimately deciding to keep him locked away in the dark, wet hold for the time being.

The party turns their attention to renaming the vessel to ensure it is unrecognizable on the high seas. [[Lanny]] suggests [[Skull Smasher]], but they decide to wait until they reach [[Rickety's Squibs]] to select a permanent, inconspicuous name—eventually considering mundane options like [[Dog Rose]] to avoid drawing unwanted attention. During this transition, [[Torrin Horrendous]] identifies their magical loot using [[Identify]], including the [[Steadfast Grapple]] and the [[Shackles of Compliance]]. 

Later, [[Torrin Horrendous]] and [[Devi McRae]] descend into the dark bilges to interrogate [[Gunter Plugg]], with the scene fading to black as they roll up their sleeves. 

Over the next few days of sailing north, the weather remains pleasant but the work is strenuous due to their skeleton crew. In the privacy of her quarters, [[Torrin Horrendous]] establishes a small shrine to her dark patron [[Myrkul]], decorating it with gold coins, a skull necklace, and her bone dagger. Meanwhile, [[Desmond]] spends his nights meditating and praying to [[Besmara]], acutely aware that he was only able to land a blow on [[Gunter Plugg]] after invoking the pirate goddess's name. [[Lanny]] grows increasingly confident in his seafaring abilities, stoically steering the ship at the wheel with a rat perched on his shoulder.

The ship eventually reaches the hidden estuary of [[Rickety's Squibs]]. Thanks to a successful perception check and help from [[Tally]], [[Lanny]] spots a well-camouflaged wooden lookout tower hidden in the trees. They slowly approach and dock at the yard run by [[Rickety Hake]] (referred to as [[Rickety]]), who agrees to "squib" (refit and disguise) the vessel to make it completely unrecognizable.

To pay for the refitting, which costs [[2500 gold pieces]], the party barters with [[Rickety Hake]]. They sell a collection of valuable items salvaged from their voyages, including three carved skulls (one with a hammerhead and one with octopuses) for [[500 gold pieces]], a scrimshawed ivory walrus tusk map of the [[Shackles]] for [[200 gold pieces]], and eight silver ingots for [[100 gold pieces]]. This brings the total value of their trade-ins to [[1710 gold pieces]] (including other small items). Rather than spending their valuable [[Plunder]] points, the party barters [[Rickety Hake]] down, splits the remaining balance of [[790 gold pieces]], and contributes [[200 gold pieces]] each of their personal gold to cover the cost, keeping their [[Plunder]] intact. [[Rickety Hake]]'s workers immediately go to work sawing and hammering on the ship.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Gunter Plugg]]** (formerly [[Mr. Plugg]]): Bound in the bilges, stripped of his authority, and interrogated by [[Torrin Horrendous]] and [[Devi McRae]].
*   **[[Ambrose Kroop]]** (aka [[Fishguts]]): The ship's cook who advises the party on mutiny logistics and [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]]'s tracking abilities.
*   **[[Rickety Hake]]**: The proprietor of [[Rickety's Squibs]] who agrees to refit and disguise the party's stolen ship for a steep price.
*   **[[Tally]]**: A rigger who assists [[Lanny]] in spotting the lookout tower at the squib yard.
*   **[[Myrkul]]**: The dark deity of death whom [[Torrin Horrendous]] actively worships and builds a shrine for.
*   **[[Besmara]]**: The pirate goddess whom [[Desmond]] begins to pray to after their victory.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Man's Promise]]**: The party's captured ship, currently undergoing refitting.
*   **[[Rickety's Squibs]]**: A highly secretive, camouflaged shipyard hidden in an estuary north of the [[Shackles]], specializing in refitting stolen vessels.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **None**: This session focuses on the logistical aftermath of the mutiny, sailing, and bartering at the squib yard.

*   **[[Shackles of Compliance]]**: Magical manacles identified by [[Torrin Horrendous]] that grant advantage on intimidation checks against the wearer and allow the keyholder to cast [[Command]] three times per day.
*   **[[500 gold pieces]]**: Earned from selling three carved skulls to [[Rickety Hake]].
*   **[[200 gold pieces]]**: Earned from selling the ivory walrus tusk map.
*   **[[100 gold pieces]]**: Earned from selling eight silver ingots.
*   **[[Silver Wedding Ring]]**: Owned by [[Grooks]], sold for [[20 gold pieces]].
*   **[[Gold Earring]]**: Worth [[15 gold pieces]].
*   **[[2 Plunder Points]]**: Successfully retained by the party after paying for their ship's refitting in personal gold.
