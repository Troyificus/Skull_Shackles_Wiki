# [Skull and Shackles Episode 19: Naga Lightning and the Stolen Chelish Galleon](/skull-and-shackles-episode-19-naga-lightning-and-the-stolen-chelish-galleon)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Days 16 to 18 at [Rickety's Squibs](/ricketys-squibs) (approximate)

## Plot Summary
As work begins on refitting the ship at [Rickety's Squibs](/ricketys-squibs), the party uses their downtime to recuperate, but peace is short-lived. A terrifying, magical creature emerges from the nearby muddy waters to attack the shipyard. The beast is identified as a [Naga](/naga)—a massive snake with a lady's face that has poisonous abilities and can shoot powerful lightning bolts. [Lanny](/lanny) takes a full thunderbolt to the chest, leaving him physically smoking and singed. He goes into a rage and swings his heavy [Maul](/maul), delivering two devastating blows that crush the Naga's skull and send her lifeless body sinking back into the mud. [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) emerges from the common house and is thoroughly disconcerted by the bizarre creature's face.

Following the battle, [Lanny](/lanny) finishes a personal project: crafting a custom wooden peg leg for [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) using a curved design. Rosie is absolutely delighted with the gift, and although she is wobbly at first, she successfully walks around without her crutch.

While the crew is celebrating her recovery, a low, distant buzzing sound begins to echo from the sky. A swarm of [Giant Wasps](/giant-wasps) (wasp-like insects) swoops down on the dockyard, snapping and stinging at the terrified dock workers. The party leaps into action to defend those unable to find cover. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) casts [Mage Armor](/mage-armor) to bolster her defenses, while [Lanny](/lanny) (operating under his moniker [Captain Skull Crusher](/captain-skull-crusher)) rages and smashes a wasp to pieces with his [Maul](/maul). [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) then unleashes a level 2 [Chromatic Orb](/chromatic-orb) (black lightning) to fry a wasp that had been skewered on [Lanny](/lanny)'s pike, and [Lanny](/lanny) uses his momentum to smash the smoking carcass into the deck. [Desmond](/desmond) is badly stung by a wasp for 8 piercing damage but manages to shrug off the poison. 

After the wasp swarm is eradicated, [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) administers healing to the wounded party members. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) climbs onto the roof of the shipyard to keep watch and spots a massive ship approaching the harbor. She and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) identify the vessel as a heavily armed [Chelish Galleon](/chelish-galleon) of [Chelaxian](/chelaxian) design, but they quickly realize it is under the control of pirates. 

The shipyard workers panic and arm themselves as a [Jolly Boat](/jolly-boat) carrying six or seven armed pirates leaves the galleon and heads toward the pier. The final figure to emerge from the boat is an imposing pirate captain with brown hair tied in a ponytail, a peg leg, and a grand gray-blue military great coat with gold embroidery. The captain's men form a tight, defensive circle around him as they step onto the dock under the gaze of the tense shipyard. The episode ends on a high-stakes cliffhanger as [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) commands the crew to hold their fire and wait for orders.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: Helps identify the slain [Naga](/naga) and provides healing after the wasp attack.
*   **[Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)**: Receives a custom-carved wooden peg leg from [Lanny](/lanny) and is able to walk again.
*   **[Rickety Hake](/rickety-hake)**: His dock workers are saved from the wasp swarm by the party.
*   **[Unidentified Pirate Captain](/unidentified-pirate-captain)** (later revealed as [Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth)): A peg-legged captain wearing a decorated [Chelaxian](/chelaxian) military great coat who arrives in a jolly boat.
*   **[Chelaxian Empire](/chelaxian-empire)** / **[Cheliad](/cheliad)**: Mentioned as the origin of the captured galleon.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Rickety's Squibs](/ricketys-squibs)**: The shipyard where the party is staying while their ship is "squibbed."

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[The Naga Ambush](/the-naga-ambush)**: A swamp-dwelling [Naga](/naga) ambushes the shipyard, shooting lightning before [Lanny](/lanny) crushes her skull with his [Maul](/maul).
*   **[The Wasp Swarm Attack](/the-wasp-swarm-attack)**: A sudden infestation of [Giant Wasps](/giant-wasps) attacks the dock workers. The party successfully defeats the swarm using a combination of [Lanny](/lanny)'s [Maul](/maul) and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s level 2 [Chromatic Orb](/chromatic-orb).

## Loot & Rewards

*   **[Rosie's Peg Leg](/rosies-peg-leg)**: A beautifully crafted wooden peg leg gifted to [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell).
