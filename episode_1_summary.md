# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 1: Press-Ganged, Stripped, and Facing a Keelhaul]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 1 of captivity on the [[Wormwood]] [3, 5].

## Plot Summary
The campaign, hosted by [[Dungeon Master]] [[Sean Bear]] of the [[T.N.T Nerds]], begins in the capital of the [[Shackles]], [[Port Peril]] [1, 3]. Three adventurers—the twin brothers [[Devi McRae]] and [[Desmond]], along with the wizard [[Torrin Horrendous]]—wake up in pitch-black, bound by their wrists inside the damp hold of a ship [2, 4, 6]. They realize they have been drugged and press-ganged after a night of heavy drinking at the [[Formidably Maid]] tavern [4, 7]. Bound next to them is [[Sandara Quinn]], who explains their situation [7]. A fourth captive, the massive goliath [[Lanny]], also wakes up nearby, having been held in chains for several weeks [4, 7].

The hold hatch opens and [[Master Scourge]], a gold-toothed pirate whom [[Devi]] and [[Desmond]] recognize from the tavern, releases their wall bindings and forces them onto the deck [8, 9, 10]. Stepping into the blinding sunlight, they realize they are stripped of their gear—including [[Devi]]'s beloved top hat, gloves, and [[White-Dragon-Skin Dagger]] [13, 14], [[Torrin]]'s [[Black Leather-Bound Magic Book]] [12, 13], and [[Lanny]]'s main equipment (though [[Lanny]] and [[Desmond]] successfully hid small weapons) [13, 15].

On deck, they join other captives in a line: [[Sandara Quinn]] [16], [[Rosie Cusswell]] (a highly muscular halfling swearing about her missing fiddle) [16, 21], a crying [[Gnome in Purple Suit]] [17], and an unnamed man in normal clothes with a long blue bandanna [17]. They are confronted by [[Mr. Plugg]], a bald pirate with a braided beard who is wearing [[Devi]]'s stolen purple jacket [18, 19, 24]. When [[Rosie Cusswell]] demands her fiddle, [[Mr. Plugg]] brutally cuts her cheek with a knife as a threat [21, 22].

The towering [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] emerges from his quarters and welcomes the captives to the [[Wormwood]] [22, 23]. He declares his absolute rule: no thievery is allowed, and they are forbidden from speaking to him directly [23, 24]. He then offers a race up the ship's rigging to the [[Crow's Nest]] to fill two coveted rigger positions [25].

The race begins as [[Master Scourge]] unlocks their manacles [25]. When [[Devi]] hesitates, [[Master Scourge]] whips him [27]. [[Desmond]] uses his monk training ([[Step of the Wind]]) to scale the ropes incredibly fast, securing first place to loud cheers from the crew [30, 31, 33, 34]. The [[Gnome in Purple Suit]] climbs well and takes the second spot, narrowly evading a punch from [[Desmond]], who tried to slow him down to help his twin [34, 35, 36].

As they climb down, [[Torrin]] casts [[Blindness/Deafness]] on the [[Gnome in Purple Suit]] using a verbal component in [[Undercommon]] [37, 38]. The blinded gnome slips, but [[Lanny]] catches him by his shirt, dangling him 20 feet above the deck and saving his life [40, 41]. [[Devi]] and [[Desmond]] notice [[Torrin]]'s use of [[Undercommon]] and realize she can serve as a secret ally [42, 43].

[[Mr. Plugg]] assigns the crew's jobs:
*   **Riggers:** [[Desmond]] and the [[Gnome in Purple Suit]] [43].
*   **Cook's Assistant:** [[Devi]], who is sent below to the [[Galley]] [43, 44].
*   **Deck Swabs:** [[Lanny]], [[Torrin]], and the remaining captives [44].

In the [[Galley]], [[Devi]] meets the perpetually drunk cook, [[Ambrose Kroop]] (also known as [[Fishguts]]) [56, 59]. Taking advantage of [[Ambrose Kroop]]'s inebriated state, [[Devi]] rolls a natural 20 on a Sleight of Hand check to steal a kitchen knife to replace his dagger [63, 64]. He also learns of the female half-orc [[Quartermaster]], [[Grook]], who keeps the ship's locked equipment store [66, 67, 68]. [[Ambrose Kroop]] warns him that while [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] is fair but firm, [[Mr. Plugg]] is a sadist who whips crewmen for pleasure [69, 70].

Meanwhile on deck, [[Lanny]] and [[Torrin]] are taught how to scrub the deck with heavy stones by a tiny [[Gnome Girl]] [46]. [[Torrin]] uses her time to scout the ship and listen to conversations, which draws suspicious attention from [[Mr. Plugg]], who writes her name in his notebook [53, 54].

At the end of the day, the bell rings and the crew gathers for a meal [70]. In a quiet corner, [[Torrin]], [[Desmond]], and [[Devi]] converse in [[Undercommon]], cementing an alliance to retrieve their stolen items and potentially plan a mutiny [72-74]. They discuss recruiting [[Lanny]] to their cause [75].

Suddenly, [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] comes on deck, silencing the crew [77]. [[Master Scourge]] and [[Mr. Plugg]] drag out [[Japes Magpie]], a crew member accused of stealing from the [[Quartermaster Store]] [78]. [[Japes Magpie]] pleads guilty and is sentenced to [[Keelhauling]] [78]. He is tied to a rope, thrown overboard, and dragged rapidly under the ship's barnacle-covered hull [78-80]. He is pulled up dead, his body shredded, and is cast into the sea [80, 81]. 

[[Mr. Plugg]] then steps forward and reports that there is one more punishment to be meted out for failure to perform duties adequately. He consults his notebook, points directly at [[Torrin]], and the session ends on a dramatic cliffhanger [81].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]]**: The brutal, imposing commander of the [[Wormwood]] [23]. He enforces absolute discipline and demands that no one speak to him directly [23].
*   **[[Mr. Plugg]]**: The cruel first mate of the [[Wormwood]] who wears [[Devi]]'s stolen purple coat [18, 19, 24]. He is described as a sadist who enjoys watching others suffer and actively notes down failures [54, 70].
*   **[[Master Scourge]]**: The gold-toothed boatswain who press-ganged the party and uses a bullwhip to enforce labor [9, 11, 27].
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: A red-haired cleric also press-ganged into service who offers advice and forms an early connection with the party [7, 16].
*   **[[Rosie Cusswell]]**: A highly muscled halfling captive whose beloved fiddle was stolen and who was brutally assaulted by [[Mr. Plugg]] for complaining [16, 21, 22].
*   **[[Ambrose Kroop]]** (aka **[[Fishguts]]**): The friendly, heavily drinking ship's cook who advises [[Devi]] on how to survive under the crew's leadership [59, 69, 70].
*   **[[Grook]]**: The slender, scarred female half-orc [[Quartermaster]] who manages the ship's locked equipment storeroom [66, 68].
*   **[[Gnome in Purple Suit]]** (aka [[Conchobhar Shortstone]]): A stylish, sobbing gnome captive who wins a rigger position but is temporarily blinded by [[Torrin]]'s spell, nearly falling to his death [17, 26, 38, 40].
*   **[[Gnome Girl]]**: A small, energetic deckhand who teaches [[Lanny]] and [[Torrin]] how to clean the deck [46].
*   **[[Japes Magpie]]**: A crewman caught stealing from the [[Quartermaster Store]] who is brutally executed via [[Keelhauling]] [78, 80].
*   **[[Shackles Pirates]]**: The wider faction of outlaw sailors operating under the [[Hurricane King]] [[Kerdak Bonefist]] in the capital city of [[Port Peril]] [3].

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Port Peril]]**: The capital city of the [[Shackles]], where the party was originally drugged [3, 4].
*   **[[Formidably Maid]]**: The notorious tavern in [[Port Peril]] where [[Devi]], [[Desmond]], and [[Torrin]] were spiked and abducted [4].
*   **The [[Wormwood]]**: The pirate vessel on which the characters are now trapped, including:
    *   The **[[Hold]] / [[Bilges]]**: The dark, damp lower compartment where the party initially wakes up bound [6, 24].
    *   The **[[Deck]]**: The open-air main level where the rigging race, cleaning duties, and executions occur [11, 44, 77].
    *   The **[[Crow's Nest]]**: The high lookout platform at the top of the rigging [25, 34].
    *   The **[[Galley]]**: The messy kitchen below deck run by [[Ambrose Kroop]] [56].
    *   The **[[Quartermaster Store]]**: The locked room where the party's equipment is heavily implied to be kept [66, 67, 68].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[The Rigging Race]]**: A competitive obstacle encounter where the captives race to climb 40 feet of rope to the [[Crow's Nest]] [25, 33]. [[Desmond]] wins first place using [[Step of the Wind]] [30, 34]. [[Devi]] takes slashing damage from [[Master Scourge]]'s whip [27].
*   **[[The Gnome's Fall]]**: A non-combat hazard encounter. After [[Torrin]] casts [[Blindness/Deafness]] on the [[Gnome in Purple Suit]] [37, 38], he loses his footing [40]. [[Lanny]] performs a successful grab check to save him from a 20-foot drop [40, 41].

*   **[[Lanny's Hidden Punching Daggers]]**: Two punching daggers successfully concealed within [[Lanny]]'s loincloth during his capture [15].
