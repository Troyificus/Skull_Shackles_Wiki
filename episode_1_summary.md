# [Skull and Shackles Episode 1: Press-Ganged, Stripped, and Facing a Keelhaul](/skull-and-shackles-episode-1-press-ganged-stripped-and-facing-a-keelhaul)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 1 of captivity on the [Wormwood](/wormwood) [3, 5].

## Plot Summary
The campaign, hosted by [Dungeon Master](/dungeon-master) [Sean Bear](/sean-bear) of the [T.N.T Nerds](/tnt-nerds), begins in the capital of the [Shackles](/shackles), [Port Peril](/port-peril) [1, 3]. Three adventurers—the twin brothers [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) and [Desmond](/desmond), along with the wizard [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)—wake up in pitch-black, bound by their wrists inside the damp hold of a ship [2, 4, 6]. They realize they have been drugged and press-ganged after a night of heavy drinking at the [Formidably Maid](/formidably-maid) tavern [4, 7]. Bound next to them is [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn), who explains their situation [7]. A fourth captive, the massive goliath [Lanny](/lanny), also wakes up nearby, having been held in chains for several weeks [4, 7].

The hold hatch opens and [Master Scourge](/master-scourge), a gold-toothed pirate whom [Devi](/devi) and [Desmond](/desmond) recognize from the tavern, releases their wall bindings and forces them onto the deck [8, 9, 10]. Stepping into the blinding sunlight, they realize they are stripped of their gear—including [Devi](/devi)'s beloved top hat, gloves, and [White-Dragon-Skin Dagger](/white-dragon-skin-dagger) [13, 14], [Torrin](/torrin)'s [Black Leather-Bound Magic Book](/black-leather-bound-magic-book) [12, 13], and [Lanny](/lanny)'s main equipment (though [Lanny](/lanny) and [Desmond](/desmond) successfully hid small weapons) [13, 15].

On deck, they join other captives in a line: [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) [16], [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) (a highly muscular halfling swearing about her missing fiddle) [16, 21], a crying [Gnome in Purple Suit](/gnome-in-purple-suit) [17], and an unnamed man in normal clothes with a long blue bandanna [17]. They are confronted by [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg), a bald pirate with a braided beard who is wearing [Devi](/devi)'s stolen purple jacket [18, 19, 24]. When [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) demands her fiddle, [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) brutally cuts her cheek with a knife as a threat [21, 22].

The towering [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) emerges from his quarters and welcomes the captives to the [Wormwood](/wormwood) [22, 23]. He declares his absolute rule: no thievery is allowed, and they are forbidden from speaking to him directly [23, 24]. He then offers a race up the ship's rigging to the [Crow's Nest](/crows-nest) to fill two coveted rigger positions [25].

The race begins as [Master Scourge](/master-scourge) unlocks their manacles [25]. When [Devi](/devi) hesitates, [Master Scourge](/master-scourge) whips him [27]. [Desmond](/desmond) uses his monk training ([Step of the Wind](/step-of-the-wind)) to scale the ropes incredibly fast, securing first place to loud cheers from the crew [30, 31, 33, 34]. The [Gnome in Purple Suit](/gnome-in-purple-suit) climbs well and takes the second spot, narrowly evading a punch from [Desmond](/desmond), who tried to slow him down to help his twin [34, 35, 36].

As they climb down, [Torrin](/torrin) casts [Blindness/Deafness](/blindness/deafness) on the [Gnome in Purple Suit](/gnome-in-purple-suit) using a verbal component in [Undercommon](/undercommon) [37, 38]. The blinded gnome slips, but [Lanny](/lanny) catches him by his shirt, dangling him 20 feet above the deck and saving his life [40, 41]. [Devi](/devi) and [Desmond](/desmond) notice [Torrin](/torrin)'s use of [Undercommon](/undercommon) and realize she can serve as a secret ally [42, 43].

[Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) assigns the crew's jobs:
*   **Riggers:** [Desmond](/desmond) and the [Gnome in Purple Suit](/gnome-in-purple-suit) [43].
*   **Cook's Assistant:** [Devi](/devi), who is sent below to the [Galley](/galley) [43, 44].
*   **Deck Swabs:** [Lanny](/lanny), [Torrin](/torrin), and the remaining captives [44].

In the [Galley](/galley), [Devi](/devi) meets the perpetually drunk cook, [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) (also known as [Fishguts](/fishguts)) [56, 59]. Taking advantage of [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)'s inebriated state, [Devi](/devi) rolls a natural 20 on a Sleight of Hand check to steal a kitchen knife to replace his dagger [63, 64]. He also learns of the female half-orc [Quartermaster](/quartermaster), [Grook](/grook), who keeps the ship's locked equipment store [66, 67, 68]. [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) warns him that while [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) is fair but firm, [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) is a sadist who whips crewmen for pleasure [69, 70].

Meanwhile on deck, [Lanny](/lanny) and [Torrin](/torrin) are taught how to scrub the deck with heavy stones by a tiny [Gnome Girl](/gnome-girl) [46]. [Torrin](/torrin) uses her time to scout the ship and listen to conversations, which draws suspicious attention from [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg), who writes her name in his notebook [53, 54].

At the end of the day, the bell rings and the crew gathers for a meal [70]. In a quiet corner, [Torrin](/torrin), [Desmond](/desmond), and [Devi](/devi) converse in [Undercommon](/undercommon), cementing an alliance to retrieve their stolen items and potentially plan a mutiny [72-74]. They discuss recruiting [Lanny](/lanny) to their cause [75].

Suddenly, [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) comes on deck, silencing the crew [77]. [Master Scourge](/master-scourge) and [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) drag out [Japes Magpie](/japes-magpie), a crew member accused of stealing from the [Quartermaster Store](/quartermaster-store) [78]. [Japes Magpie](/japes-magpie) pleads guilty and is sentenced to [Keelhauling](/keelhauling) [78]. He is tied to a rope, thrown overboard, and dragged rapidly under the ship's barnacle-covered hull [78-80]. He is pulled up dead, his body shredded, and is cast into the sea [80, 81]. 

[Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) then steps forward and reports that there is one more punishment to be meted out for failure to perform duties adequately. He consults his notebook, points directly at [Torrin](/torrin), and the session ends on a dramatic cliffhanger [81].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan)**: The brutal, imposing commander of the [Wormwood](/wormwood) [23]. He enforces absolute discipline and demands that no one speak to him directly [23].
*   **[Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)**: The cruel first mate of the [Wormwood](/wormwood) who wears [Devi](/devi)'s stolen purple coat [18, 19, 24]. He is described as a sadist who enjoys watching others suffer and actively notes down failures [54, 70].
*   **[Master Scourge](/master-scourge)**: The gold-toothed boatswain who press-ganged the party and uses a bullwhip to enforce labor [9, 11, 27].
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: A red-haired cleric also press-ganged into service who offers advice and forms an early connection with the party [7, 16].
*   **[Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)**: A highly muscled halfling captive whose beloved fiddle was stolen and who was brutally assaulted by [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) for complaining [16, 21, 22].
*   **[Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)** (aka **[Fishguts](/fishguts)**): The friendly, heavily drinking ship's cook who advises [Devi](/devi) on how to survive under the crew's leadership [59, 69, 70].
*   **[Grook](/grook)**: The slender, scarred female half-orc [Quartermaster](/quartermaster) who manages the ship's locked equipment storeroom [66, 68].
*   **[Gnome in Purple Suit](/gnome-in-purple-suit)** (aka [Conchobhar Shortstone](/conchobhar-shortstone)): A stylish, sobbing gnome captive who wins a rigger position but is temporarily blinded by [Torrin](/torrin)'s spell, nearly falling to his death [17, 26, 38, 40].
*   **[Gnome Girl](/gnome-girl)**: A small, energetic deckhand who teaches [Lanny](/lanny) and [Torrin](/torrin) how to clean the deck [46].
*   **[Japes Magpie](/japes-magpie)**: A crewman caught stealing from the [Quartermaster Store](/quartermaster-store) who is brutally executed via [Keelhauling](/keelhauling) [78, 80].
*   **[Shackles Pirates](/shackles-pirates)**: The wider faction of outlaw sailors operating under the [Hurricane King](/hurricane-king) [Kerdak Bonefist](/kerdak-bonefist) in the capital city of [Port Peril](/port-peril) [3].

## Locations Visited
*   **[Port Peril](/port-peril)**: The capital city of the [Shackles](/shackles), where the party was originally drugged [3, 4].
*   **[Formidably Maid](/formidably-maid)**: The notorious tavern in [Port Peril](/port-peril) where [Devi](/devi), [Desmond](/desmond), and [Torrin](/torrin) were spiked and abducted [4].
*   **The [Wormwood](/wormwood)**: The pirate vessel on which the characters are now trapped, including:
    *   The **[Hold](/hold) / [Bilges](/bilges)**: The dark, damp lower compartment where the party initially wakes up bound [6, 24].
    *   The **[Deck](/deck)**: The open-air main level where the rigging race, cleaning duties, and executions occur [11, 44, 77].
    *   The **[Crow's Nest](/crows-nest)**: The high lookout platform at the top of the rigging [25, 34].
    *   The **[Galley](/galley)**: The messy kitchen below deck run by [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) [56].
    *   The **[Quartermaster Store](/quartermaster-store)**: The locked room where the party's equipment is heavily implied to be kept [66, 67, 68].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[The Rigging Race](/the-rigging-race)**: A competitive obstacle encounter where the captives race to climb 40 feet of rope to the [Crow's Nest](/crows-nest) [25, 33]. [Desmond](/desmond) wins first place using [Step of the Wind](/step-of-the-wind) [30, 34]. [Devi](/devi) takes slashing damage from [Master Scourge](/master-scourge)'s whip [27].
*   **[The Gnome's Fall](/the-gnomes-fall)**: A non-combat hazard encounter. After [Torrin](/torrin) casts [Blindness/Deafness](/blindness/deafness) on the [Gnome in Purple Suit](/gnome-in-purple-suit) [37, 38], he loses his footing [40]. [Lanny](/lanny) performs a successful grab check to save him from a 20-foot drop [40, 41].

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Stolen Dagger](/stolen-dagger)**: A basic kitchen knife surreptitiously stolen from the [Galley](/galley) by [Devi](/devi) during his shift with the inebriated [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) [63, 64].
*   **[Lanny's Hidden Punching Daggers](/lannys-hidden-punching-daggers)**: Two punching daggers successfully concealed within [Lanny](/lanny)'s loincloth during his capture [15].
*   **[Desmond's Hidden Chisel](/desmonds-hidden-chisel)**: A sturdy chisel successfully hidden in [Desmond](/desmond)'s boot [14].
