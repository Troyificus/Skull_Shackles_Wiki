# [Skull and Shackles Episode 23: Buying Loyalty and Ripping Off Arms](/skull-and-shackles-episode-23-buying-loyalty-and-ripping-off-arms)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 21 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The morning begins aboard the [Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze). [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) requests funds to purchase fresh kitchen supplies, and the party happily provides gold from their ship's kitty. [Lanny](/lanny) hands out gold payments to the crew, including the [Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi) sailors. 

During the payments, [Halle](/halle) (or [Hella](/hella)) makes it known that she wishes to leave the ship and return to her homeland. Having been treated well despite her initial capture, [Halle](/halle) and the party part on good terms, with [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) releasing her hand with a warm smile. She and her two male companions (including [Sargava](/sargava)) depart the ship, leaving the party with only [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert) and [Conchobhar Shortstone](/conchobhar-shortstone) on board.

With the ship cleared, the party decides that [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg) has outstayed his welcome. Fearing that [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) can magically track [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg)'s physical form or possessions, they decide to permanently incapacitate him.

Lanny, Torrin, Devi, and Desmond descend into the damp bilges. [Lanny](/lanny) rages, puts his foot against [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg)'s chest for leverage, and takes hold of his left arm. With a brutal twist and a massive pull, [Lanny](/lanny) tears [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg)'s left arm completely off at the shoulder joint. To prevent him from dying, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) immediately casts [Cure Wounds](/cure-wounds) to seal the bleeding stump, waking the former first mate back to consciousness. 

[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) then interrogates [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg). Showing zero remorse for his past actions, [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg) is subjected to brutal torture. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) channels multiple casts of [Shocking Grasp](/shocking-grasp), repeatedly knocking him unconscious and shocking him back to wakefulness. They stuff Gunter's smoldering body head-first into a wooden [Barrel](/barrel) along with his severed arm. The party then heads back up to deck to enjoy a peaceful breakfast of bacon, fried eggs, and blood pudding. 

During the day, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) copies spells into her [Spellbook](/spellbook) while [Desmond](/desmond) and [Lanny](/lanny) prepare for the fights. That evening, the party heads to [Shadook](/shadook)'s gambling house. The room is packed to standing room only, and the odds are posted on massive chalkboards. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) bets heavily on his companions.

In the first fight, [Lanny](/lanny) (fighting as [Captain Skull Crusher](/captain-skull-crusher)) faces the dwarven brawler [Braden the Brawler Brimstone](/braden-the-brawler-brimstone). [Lanny](/lanny) fights recklessly and easily pummels [Braden the Brawler Brimstone](/braden-the-brawler-brimstone) into submission, winning [50 gold pieces](/50-gold-pieces) for his victory. 

In the main event, [Desmond](/desmond) enters the ring against [The Pugilist](/the-pugilist). They trade fast, brutal blows, but [Desmond](/desmond) struggles to connect his signature [Flurry of Blows](/flurry-of-blows). The episode ends on a high-stakes cliffhanger mid-fight as both combatants trade heavy strikes in the center of the ring.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)**: The ship's cook who receives funds to gather fresh kitchen supplies.
*   **[Halle](/halle)**: A female [Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi) sailor who is granted permission to leave the crew and return to her homeland.
*   **[Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg)**: The bound captive who is brutally dismembered by [Lanny](/lanny) and tortured by [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) in the bilges.
*   **[Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert)**: The party's simple-minded companion who is kept safe aboard the ship.
*   **[Conchobhar Shortstone](/conchobhar-shortstone)**: The gnome dandy assigned to guard the ship's gangplank during the fight night.
*   **[Braden the Brawler Brimstone](/braden-the-brawler-brimstone)**: A dwarven brawler defeated by [Lanny](/lanny) in the undercard match.
*   **[The Pugilist](/the-pugilist)**: The local champion who engages in a brutal, even-matched brawl with [Desmond](/desmond).
*   **[Shadook](/shadook)**: The fight organizer who coordinates the bets and matches.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Blood Cove](/blood-cove)**: The grand trade port where the fights are held.
*   **[Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze)**: The party's ship, specifically the dark and flooded bilges.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **Dismemberment of Plugg**: [Lanny](/lanny) tears [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg)'s left arm off in the bilges while [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) uses [Shocking Grasp](/shocking-grasp) to torture him.
*   **[Lanny](/lanny) vs. [Braden the Brawler Brimstone](/braden-the-brawler-brimstone)**: An undercard match where [Lanny](/lanny) dominates [Braden](/braden) with raw power and reckless strikes.
*   **[Desmond](/desmond) vs. [The Pugilist](/the-pugilist)**: The main event of [Blood Cove](/blood-cove)'s fight night, featuring a highly technical, brutal fistfight.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Gunter Plugg's Left Arm](/gunter-pluggs-left-arm)**: Kept in the barrel as a gruesome trophy/item.
*   **[50 gold pieces](/50-gold-pieces)**: Won by [Lanny](/lanny) for winning his boxing match.
