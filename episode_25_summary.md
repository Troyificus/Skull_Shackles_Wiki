# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 25: Torrin's Natural One Alerts the Dominator]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 17 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The session begins with the party dividing their massive earnings from the ship sale and fight bets. To celebrate, [[Lanny]] purchases [[200 pipes of beer]] for the locals in the crowded tavern.

The next morning, the party conducts final shopping in [[Blood Cove]] before departure. [[Lanny]] goes clothes shopping for a colder climate, spending [[2 gold pieces]] on practical hand-made [[baggy trousers]] and a massive [[poncho]] with a hood. [[Torrin Horrendous]], [[Desmond]], and [[Devi McRae]] buy several [[Potions of Healing]] (three regular potions each at [[50 gold pieces]] each, totaling [[150 gold pieces]] for the twins and 150g for Torrin). [[Torrin Horrendous]] also receives a spare set of [[Leather Armor]].

In the evening, [[Mr. Girdan Danan]] returns with four hired guards to hand over the [[400 platinum pieces]] and take official possession of the [[Sea Breeze]].

Eager to hit the open sea, [[Captain Glade Fletcher]] unmoors the [[Silent Howl]] and sails out of the estuary into the [[Slithering Coast]] under the cover of night. He allows [[Lanny]] to pilot the ship to test its superior speed and handling.

While sailing northwest, they spot the towering silhouette of [[The Dominator]], a massive naval galleon belonging to the hostile [[Chelaxian Navy]]. Realizing the warship carries dozens of marines and heavy ballistas, they attempt to stealthily slip past it.

To gain an advantage, [[Torrin Horrendous]] attempts to sneak closer (or cast a spell to obscure them/disable the warship's lookouts), but rolls a catastrophic **natural 1** on her stealth check. This critical failure immediately alerts the crew of [[The Dominator]], turning their quiet escape into a high-stakes naval chase as the warship begins tracking the [[Silent Howl]].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Captain Glade Fletcher]]**: Piloted the [[Silent Howl]] out of [[Blood Cove]] and oversaw the escape.
*   **[[Chelaxian Navy]]** / **[[Chelish Navy]]**: The elite, highly anti-pirate military force patrolling the coast.
*   **[[The Dominator]]**: The massive Chelish galleon that intercepts the party.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Blood Cove]]**: Where they finalized their shopping and departed.
*   **[[The Slithering Coast]]**: The treacherous coastal waters they navigate.
*   **[[The Silent Howl]]**: Their new vessel.

## Battles & Encounters
*   A high-tension stealth encounter trying to slip past [[The Dominator]] which goes horribly wrong due to [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s critical failure.
