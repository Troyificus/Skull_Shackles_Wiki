# [Skull and Shackles Episode 25: Torrin's Natural One Alerts the Dominator](/skull-and-shackles-episode-25-torrins-natural-one-alerts-the-dominator)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 17 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The session begins with the party dividing their massive earnings from the ship sale and fight bets. To celebrate, [Lanny](/lanny) purchases [200 pipes of beer](/200-pipes-of-beer) for the locals in the crowded tavern.

The next morning, the party conducts final shopping in [Blood Cove](/blood-cove) before departure. [Lanny](/lanny) goes clothes shopping for a colder climate, spending [2 gold pieces](/2-gold-pieces) on practical hand-made [baggy trousers](/baggy-trousers) and a massive [poncho](/poncho) with a hood. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous), [Desmond](/desmond), and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) buy several [Potions of Healing](/potions-of-healing) (three regular potions each at [50 gold pieces](/50-gold-pieces) each, totaling [150 gold pieces](/150-gold-pieces) for the twins and 150g for Torrin). [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) also receives a spare set of [Leather Armor](/leather-armor).

In the evening, [Mr. Girdan Danan](/mr-girdan-danan) returns with four hired guards to hand over the [400 platinum pieces](/400-platinum-pieces) and take official possession of the [Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze).

Eager to hit the open sea, [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher) unmoors the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl) and sails out of the estuary into the [Slithering Coast](/slithering-coast) under the cover of night. He allows [Lanny](/lanny) to pilot the ship to test its superior speed and handling.

While sailing northwest, they spot the towering silhouette of [The Dominator](/the-dominator), a massive naval galleon belonging to the hostile [Chelaxian Navy](/chelaxian-navy). Realizing the warship carries dozens of marines and heavy ballistas, they attempt to stealthily slip past it.

To gain an advantage, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) attempts to sneak closer (or cast a spell to obscure them/disable the warship's lookouts), but rolls a catastrophic **natural 1** on her stealth check. This critical failure immediately alerts the crew of [The Dominator](/the-dominator), turning their quiet escape into a high-stakes naval chase as the warship begins tracking the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl).

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher)**: Piloted the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl) out of [Blood Cove](/blood-cove) and oversaw the escape.
*   **[Chelaxian Navy](/chelaxian-navy)** / **[Chelish Navy](/chelish-navy)**: The elite, highly anti-pirate military force patrolling the coast.
*   **[The Dominator](/the-dominator)**: The massive Chelish galleon that intercepts the party.

## Locations Visited

*   **[The Slithering Coast](/the-slithering-coast)**: The treacherous coastal waters they navigate.
