# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 27: Struck by Lightning for Cursing Besmara]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 15 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
On the eerie beach of the [[Ongongta Overlook]], the party's search for [[Captain Glade Fletcher]]'s buried [[Chest of Gold]] is rudely interrupted as three rotting, bloated [[Brine Zombies]] emerge from the ocean surf. A chaotic struggle begins as [[Devi McRae]] takes point, stabbing one of the creatures with his rapier and delivering a heavy kick that shatters its bones, though the resilient undead remains standing. 

[[Desmond]] maneuvers to protect [[Torrin Horrendous]] as the zombies slowly march forward and swing their rusted cutlasses. [[Lanny]] enters a furious rage, bringing his heavy [[Maul]] down in a devastating strike that completely pulverizes the head of one of the zombies. 

To speed up the excavation of [[Captain Glade Fletcher]]'s treasure, [[Devi McRae]] quickly hands a kitchen [[Shovel]] to [[Lanny]]. Using his colossal goliath strength, [[Lanny]] rolls a natural 20 (a total of 28 on Athletics) to furiously dig a three-foot-deep hole in the wet sand, finally making visual contact with the chest's lid. [[Desmond]] uses [[Step of the Wind]] to disengage from the combat, retreats to the hole, and ties a secure knot with a rope around the chest's handle.

As more [[Brine Zombies]] crawl from the waves, [[Torrin Horhendous]] steps up and unleashes a massive, crackling bolt of lightning across the beach. During the magical storm and chaotic crossfire, [[Desmond]]—who had recently expressed deep skepticism and irreverence toward the pirate goddess [[Besmara]]—is suddenly struck directly by a natural bolt of lightning from the heavens. The devastating shock knocks [[Desmond]] completely unconscious, leaving him at death's door as the episode ends on a shocking cliffhanger.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Captain Glade Fletcher]]**: The retired pirate captain whose buried cache on the beach is the object of the party's excavation.
*   **[[Besmara]]**: The pirate goddess of the [[Shackles]], whose name [[Desmond]] cursed, resulting in a sudden deific strike of lightning.
*   **[[Myrkul]]**: The god of the dead to whom [[Torrin Horrendous]] is devoted.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Ongongta Overlook]]**: The sheer, dangerous cliff and haunted beach where [[Captain Glade Fletcher]]'s treasure is buried amidst a literal graveyard of shipwrecks.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[Brine Zombies]] Encounter**: A grueling combat on the beach against slow but incredibly hardy undead that emerge continuously from the surf.
*   **The Lightning Strike**: [[Desmond]] is struck by a deific bolt of lightning from [[Besmara]]'s storm.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Chest of Gold]]**: Located and partially excavated by [[Lanny]] using a [[Shovel]], then tied off by [[Desmond]].
