# [Skull and Shackles Episode 28: Dez's Lightning Pact and the Alien Dagger](/skull-and-shackles-episode-28-dezs-lightning-pact-and-the-alien-dagger)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 16 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The episode begins with [Desmond](/desmond) in a state of death-like unconsciousness on the beach of the [Ongongta Overlook](/ongongta-overlook). While unconscious, his soul is transported to a divine realm where he stands on the deck of an ancient, mysterious ship suspended within an immense sphere of ocean water. He is greeted by [Besmara](/besmara) herself, the Pirate Queen. [Besmara](/besmara) reveals that she has placed a heavy bet on the fate of the [Shackles](/shackles), which is threatened by a looming, poisonous danger. She offers [Desmond](/desmond) a pact: to become her champion, grow strong, and gather his companions to defend her domain. [Desmond](/desmond) accepts the deal and is instantly revived back on the beach, his skin now glowing with a fresh, crackling electrical energy and a new skull-and-crossbones tattoo emblazoned on his skin.

With [Desmond](/desmond) back on his feet and boasting his newfound powers, the party escapes the beach and climbs back up the cliff using the [Steadfast Grapple](/steadfast-grapple), leaving the remaining [Brine Zombies](/brine-zombies) behind. Upon reaching the shoreline, [Desmond](/desmond) tests his divine connection by placing [Sandara Quinn's Tricorn Hat](/sandara-quinns-tricorn-hat) upside down in the surf. Willing it to change, the magical hat successfully transforms into a functional rowing boat with an anchor, providing a safe transport back to their ship.

Reboarding the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl), the party is warmly greeted by [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher), who is ecstatic to see his [Chest of Gold](/chest-of-gold) safely recovered. When they describe the terrifying zombie ambush, [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher) is thoroughly bewildered, swearing there were no shipwrecks or undead on the beach when he buried his cache twenty years ago. 

Before handing the chest over, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) orchestrates a ruse, asking [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) to use her magical senses to identify the items inside so they might pocket some of the loot. [Torrin Horhendous](/torrin-horhendous) performs a ritual of [Identify](/identify) on the locked chest. As [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher) unlocks the chest with the key from his neck, they find a massive hoard of gold. However, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s magical senses immediately zero in on a highly unusual, ominous item at the bottom of the chest: a mysterious, golden, twin-plated dagger that seems to hum with ancient and foreign magic.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Besmara](/besmara)**: The Pirate Queen of the [Shackles](/shackles), who makes a divine pact with [Desmond](/desmond) and appoints him as her champion.
*   **[Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher)**: The retired owner of the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl), who opens his recovered chest of treasure.
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: The party's cleric ally whose magical tricorn hat transforms into a boat.
*   **[Zima](/zima)**: [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s bat familiar, summoned to watch over the party.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Ongongta Overlook](/ongongta-overlook)**: The cliffside and haunted beach where the party recovers the chest.
*   **[Silent Howl](/silent-howl)**: The party's fast new ship where they reunite with [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher).

## Battles & Encounters
*   **The Divine Communion**: [Desmond](/desmond)'s near-death vision and pact-making with the goddess [Besmara](/besmara).

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Chest of Gold](/chest-of-gold)**: Recovered from the beach, containing [4,000 gold pieces](/4000-gold-pieces) and magical items.
*   **[Golden Twin-Plated Dagger](/golden-twin-plated-dagger)**: A mysterious, potentially cursed magical weapon discovered at the bottom of [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher)'s chest.
