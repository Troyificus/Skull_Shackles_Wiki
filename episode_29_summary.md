# [Skull and Shackles Episode 29: Taser Bats on the Ghost Ship](/skull-and-shackles-episode-29-taser-bats-on-the-ghost-ship)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 17 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Aboard the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl), the party examines the mysterious [Golden Twin-Plated Dagger](/golden-twin-plated-dagger) found in [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher)'s chest. To prevent the captain from throwing it away out of fear, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) casts a "protective spell" over it—a successful ruse backed by [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)'s dirty 20 deception check. Believing the danger is contained, [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher) locks the chest and requests to be dropped off at [Goat's Head](/goats-head) on the [Fire Grass Isles](/fire-grass-isles) to retire, leaving the ship fully in the party's possession. In private, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) informs [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) that the dagger is heavily enchanted to draw blood and would make an excellent, high-risk weapon for [Desmond](/desmond).

As they sail, [Desmond](/desmond) enthusiastically tests his new divine powers, launching crackling bolts of [Eldritch Blast](/eldritch-blast) into the sky, which [Lanny](/lanny) watches with childlike wonder. While [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) celebrates [Desmond](/desmond)'s blessing from [Besmara](/besmara), [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) remains deeply skeptical, harboring dark suspicions about [Desmond](/desmond)'s powers and privately praying to her own dark patron, [Myrkul](/myrkul).

The next day, they spot a crippled, waterlogged fishing boat drifting off-course. Upon closer inspection, the vessel is breached, and they are ambushed by four aggressive [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) (fish-like humanoids) wielding crude spears. [Lanny](/lanny) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) dive into the ocean to engage them. Entering a rage, [Lanny](/lanny) delivers a devastating reckless swing with his [Maul](/maul), pulverizing one Sahuagin and heavily wounding another. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) attempts to launch her familiar [Zima](/zima) to deliver a [Shocking Grasp](/shocking-grasp), but she misjudges the distance, flying past the target. [Desmond](/desmond) uses his sentinel abilities to punch the remaining foes, and [Lanny](/lanny) crushes the skull of the final escaping creature.

With the deck clear, [Zima](/zima) spots two more [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) fleeing into the depths. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) and [Lanny](/lanny) dive into the flooded hold of the fishing boat. They discover the fresh, dead bodies of the human crew. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) takes a vial of blood from one of the corpses and salvages a strange [Dull Gray Metal Necklace](/dull-gray-metal-necklace) that glints strangely in the light. 

Returning to the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl), [Desmond](/desmond) ties a rope around his waist and jumps overboard, letting [Lanny](/lanny) drag him through the ship's wake to inspect the bottom of their hull. Confirmed clear of any sea monsters, they set sail once more toward [Goat's Head](/goats-head).

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher)**: The retiring captain who agrees to hand over the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl) once they reach [Goat's Head](/goats-head).
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: The cleric who interprets [Desmond](/desmond)'s new powers as a true blessing.
*   **[Zima](/zima)**: [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s bat familiar used to deliver touch spells and scout the enemy deck.
*   **[Sahuagin](/sahuagin)**: Feral, fish-like sea monsters that ambushed the crippled fishing boat.
*   **[Myrkul](/myrkul)**: The dark god of the dead to whom [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) prays.
*   **[Besmara](/besmara)**: The pirate goddess whose power now flows through [Desmond](/desmond).

## Locations Visited
*   **[Silent Howl](/silent-howl)**: The party's main ship, currently navigating the [Slithering Coast](/slithering-coast).
*   **[Crippled Fishing Boat](/crippled-fishing-boat)**: A waterlogged wreck adrift on the sea, used by [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) as an ambush site.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[Sahuagin](/sahuagin) Ambush**: A fast-paced ocean combat against four fish-men guarding their waterlogged prize.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Dull Gray Metal Necklace](/dull-gray-metal-necklace)**: Salvaged from the corpse of a dead fisherman in the flooded hold, emitting a strange magical glint.
*   **[Vial of Blood](/vial-of-blood)**: Collected by [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) from a fresh corpse for [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s future experiments.
