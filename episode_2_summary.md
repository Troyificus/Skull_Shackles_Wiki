# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 2: Defying Plugg and Regaining the Spellbook]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 1 to Day 2 (Morning)

## Plot Summary
Following the brutal [[Keelhauling]] and death of crewman [[Japes Magpie]] on the previous night [1], [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] asks [[Mr. Plugg]] if there are any other punishments for the day [1]. [[Mr. Plugg]] reads from his notebook, nominating the wizard [[Torrin]] for failing to adequately perform her swabbing duties [1]. [[Devi McRae]] attempts to intervene, boldly stepping forward to argue that [[Mr. Plugg]] is being unfair and suggests the punishment is motivated by [[Mr. Plugg]]'s rejected "amorous intentions" toward [[Torrin]] [2]. [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] dismisses the defense, threatening to put [[Devi McRae]] on the whipping post himself, forcing [[Devi McRae]] to step back [2, 3].

[[Torrin]] is secured to the main mast by [[Master Scourge]] using rope loops [3, 4]. During the whipping, [[Torrin]] considers casting a spell but is unable to perform somatic components due to her bound hands [4, 5]. [[Master Scourge]] administers three lashes, dealing 12 points of slashing damage total [5, 6]. Undeterred, [[Torrin]] smiles and spits directly into [[Mr. Plugg]]'s face [6]. Enraged, [[Mr. Plugg]] seizes the whip and strikes [[Torrin]] a fourth time for 4 points of slashing damage before [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] halts the punishment [6, 7]. [[Torrin]] is released and silently retrieves her coat [7].

During the subsequent free time on deck, the crew relaxes with drinks, dice, and arm wrestling [8]. [[Desmond]] sits down with [[Jack Scrimshaw]], who teaches him the art of [[Scrimshaw]] [10, 16]. Using his [[Mason's Tools]] proficiency, [[Desmond]] successfully carves a square wave pattern into a piece of bone, which [[Jack Scrimshaw]] allows him to keep [11-13]. Meanwhile, [[Lanny]] is challenged to an arm-wrestling match by a mustachioed human named [[Fedor]] [17, 18, 75]. Caught off-guard, [[Lanny]] loses the match, and [[Fedor]] mockingly refuses a rematch due to [[Lanny]]'s lack of coin [19].

[[Devi McRae]] approaches [[Lanny]] at the bow of the ship to discuss plans for a future mutiny [21, 22]. [[Lanny]] eagerly agrees to join the conspiracy, citing his need to escape the vessel [22]. [[Devi McRae]] informs [[Lanny]] that his twin brother, [[Desmond]], is also part of the plan, and they agree to target [[Mr. Plugg]]'s lackeys first [22, 23]. 

[[Sandara Quinn]] approaches [[Torrin]] and leads her down to the crew's sleeping quarters below the main hold [25]. There, [[Sandara Quinn]] reveals that she retrieved [[Torrin]]'s stolen spellbook from the superstitious quartermaster, [[Quartermaster Grook]], by claiming it and her own holy symbol were sacred to [[Besmara]] [27, 28]. [[Sandara Quinn]] then casts a healing spell to restore 10 hit points to [[Torrin]]'s back, and the two establish a pact to watch each other's backs [28, 29]. Left alone, [[Torrin]] conjures her fruit bat familiar, [[Zema]], who hides inside her coat [32]. 

On deck, [[Desmond]] plays a game of poker [[Dice]] with an elven rigger named [[Tally]] [33, 34]. Lacking gold, [[Desmond]] wagers a future favor against [[Tally]]'s three silver pieces [34, 37]. [[Desmond]] wins the match with a three-of-a-kind, successfully winning the three silver pieces [38, 39]. Later, the party converges at the bow to pool information [40]. [[Lanny]] reveals he has two [[Punching Daggers]] concealed in his waistband, and [[Torrin]] demonstrates her magical abilities by sparking lightning between her fingers [43]. [[Lanny]] explains that he is from a [[Goliath]] clan on the island of [[Bogoponda]] and was captured in chains after drifting in a small boat [47, 48].

At nightfall, [[Master Scourge]] orders the crew to the sleeping quarters [54]. [[Torrin]] locks her spellbook in a locker chest and pockets the key [58]. The next morning, the ship's bell rings [58]. As the crew prepares to head up, [[Lanny]] notices [[Fedor]], the half-orc arm wrestler, a dwarf, and a dark-haired female pirate loitering near the stairs [59, 60]. [[Fedor]] approaches [[Torrin]], claiming to have something for her from [[Mr. Plugg]], and suddenly swings a punch at her face [61, 62].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]]**: The cruel commander of [[The Wormwood]]. He oversees the morning's punishments but halts [[Mr. Plugg]]'s excessive whipping of [[Torrin]] [7].
*   **[[Mr. Plugg]]**: The first mate of [[The Wormwood]]. Motivated by spite after [[Torrin]]'s defiance, he takes the whip to lashing her himself [6, 7]. He later orders [[Fedor]] to ambush [[Torrin]] below decks [74].
*   **[[Master Scourge]]**: The brutal boatswain who administers the lashes to [[Torrin]] and orders the crew below at night [3, 54].
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: A captive cleric of [[Besmara]] who successfully tricks [[Quartermaster Grook]] to recover [[Torrin]]'s spellbook, heals [[Torrin]]'s wounds, and forms an alliance with the party [27-29].
*   **[[Jack Scrimshaw]]**: A friendly member of the crew who carves bone art and teaches [[Desmond]] the basics of [[Scrimshaw]] [10, 16].
*   **[[Tally]]**: An elven rigger who plays poker [[Dice]] with [[Desmond]] and wagers silver against a favor [33, 34].
*   **[[Fedor]]**: A mustachioed human pirate and lackey of [[Mr. Plugg]] [74, 75]. He defeats [[Lanny]] in arm wrestling, but is later cowed by the party after launching a failed ambush on [[Torrin]] [19, 61, 74, 75].
*   **[[Quartermaster Grook]]**: The superstitious half-orc quartermaster of [[The Wormwood]] who returns confiscated goods out of fear of angering [[Besmara]] [27].
*   **[[Ambrose Kroop]]** (aka **[[Fishguts]]**): The ship's cook, found completely incapacitated and snoring drunk behind a barrel in the galley [80, 81].
*   **[[Conchobhar Shortstone]]**: A gnome captive who expresses his gratitude to [[Lanny]] with a gift of silver [55, 56].

## Locations Visited
*   **[[The Wormwood]]**: The pirate vessel where the party remains trapped under press-ganged service [1].
    *   **The Main Deck & Mast**: The location of the whipping post where [[Torrin]] receives her lashes [3, 4].
    *   **The Sleeping Quarters**: The hammock-filled lower deck where the crew sleeps, and where [[Torrin]] and [[Sandara Quinn]] form their alliance [25, 26].
    *   **The Galley**: The ship's kitchen where [[Devi McRae]] finds [[Ambrose Kroop]] passed out drunk [80, 81].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **The Lower Deck Ambush**:
    *   **Opponents**: [[Fedor]] and his dark-haired female pirate accomplice [59, 61].
    *   **Notable Tactical Moments**: [[Fedor]] attempts to sucker-punch [[Torrin]] under the guise of delivering an item [61, 62]. [[Torrin]] reacts instantly by casting [[Shield]], blocking the strike [62, 63]. 
    *   **The Skirmish**: 
        *   [[Torrin]] retaliates with [[Shocking Grasp]], delivering 5 points of lightning damage to [[Fedor]]'s gut [64]. 
        *   [[Lanny]] enters a rage and lands an unarmed strike to [[Fedor]]'s face for 10 damage, knocking him off-balance [66, 68].
        *   [[Devi McRae]] attempts to strike [[Fedor]] with a [[Stolen Dagger]] from the galley but misses [67, 68].
        *   [[Desmond]] intercepts [[Fedor]]'s shadow-like female accomplice, using a key point to execute a [[Flurry of Blows]] that completely knocks her unconscious [69, 70].
        *   The half-orc wrestler grapples [[Devi McRae]] in a bear hug [71], but [[Fedor]] quickly calls a halt to the combat and disengages [72, 73].
    *   **Consequences**: The half-orc releases [[Devi McRae]] [73]. Surrounded by [[Lanny]] and [[Devi McRae]], [[Fedor]] is thoroughly intimidated by [[Desmond]] [74]. [[Fedor]] confesses that [[Mr. Plugg]] ordered the attack as revenge for the spitting incident [74]. [[Desmond]] kicks [[Fedor]] in the groin, cowing him completely, and warns him to tell [[Mr. Plugg]] to fight them himself on deck [75]. [[Fedor]] and his allies flee upstairs [76].

*   **Three Silver Pieces**: Gifted to [[Lanny]] by [[Conchobhar Shortstone]] as a reward for saving him from the rigging fall in the previous episode [55, 56].
