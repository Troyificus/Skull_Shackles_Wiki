# [Skull and Shackles Episode 30: Justifying Necromancy as Recycling Meat](/skull-and-shackles-episode-30-justifying-necromancy-as-recycling-meat)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 18 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Following their successful navigation of the [Slithering Coast](/slithering-coast), the crew of the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl) arrives at [Goat's Head](/goats-head), a lawless pirate haven situated on the [Fire Grass Isles](/fire-grass-isles). Before disembarking, [Desmond](/desmond) consults [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) about the strange [Dull Gray Metal Necklace](/dull-gray-metal-necklace) he recovered from the dead sahuagin's prize boat, expressing concern that putting it on might turn him into a fish person. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) promises to identify it once they return from shore.

[Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher) briefs the party on the local layout of [Goat's Head](/goats-head), pointing out [Dock 20](/dock-20) where shipyard repairs are conducted, and warning them that while the town has no formal laws or guards, they must remain on their guard as street justice is swift. He highly recommends [The Rusty Kraken](/the-rusty-kraken) at [Building 22](/building-22) for quality lodging and drinks. Investigating the local bulletin board, the party finds it entirely bare except for a single piece of parchment nailed to the wood with a dagger. The message scrawled on it reads: *"No laws nor lords, everyone is his own master. We are the last bastion of freedom."*

The group explores the town, noting locations like [Free Trade Square](/free-trade-square) (number 4) for offloading cargo and [Shadook's Nine Pins](/shadooks-nine-pins) (number 5) for gambling. They eventually settle at [The Rusty Kraken](/the-rusty-kraken), a tavern marked by a large kraken sign constructed from flat sheets of rusted iron. There, they are served by a very attractive black-haired young barmaid with distinct white streaks on each side of her hair parting and almond-shaped eyes. [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher) also mentions the town's [Cathedral](/cathedral) on the north side, which serves as a communal sanctuary housing altars to all major deities, including [Besmara](/besmara).

Returning to the ship at night, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) decides to test her magical studies. She descends into the bilges, retrieves the rotting corpse of [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg) from his barrel, and performs her first necromantic ritual, casting [Animate Dead](/animate-dead). [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg) awkwardly twitches, rises, and stands before them as [Zombie Plugg](/zombie-plugg)—still manacled, missing his left arm (which was torn off by [Lanny](/lanny) in [Skull and Shackles Episode 23](/skull-and-shackles-episode-23)), and lacking eyes. 

Reactions at the table are highly split: [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) is ecstatic with her success, and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) is deeply intrigued. However, [Desmond](/desmond) is horrified and deeply concerned, voicing extreme skepticism about keeping an undead monstrosity in their quarters. [Lanny](/lanny) remains completely indifferent and falls asleep in his hammock. A massive moral and practical debate ensues. [Desmond](/desmond) fears that [Zombie Plugg](/zombie-plugg) will turn on them or spark a crew mutiny, but [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) reassures him that the [Manacles of Compliance](/manacles-of-compliance) she placed on [Zombie Plugg](/zombie-plugg)'s remaining wrist grant her absolute control and the ability to cast [Command](/command) on him at will. Seeking to soothe [Desmond](/desmond)'s disgust, she jokes about stuffing potpourri up the zombie's rear to mask his rotting stench. [Desmond](/desmond) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) later converse privately on the gangplank, expressing growing concerns that [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) is becoming power-mad and wondering if she might be the very "evil growing in the Shackles" that [Besmara](/besmara) warned [Desmond](/desmond) about during his lightning-induced vision.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher)**: The retiring captain who acts as the party's local guide to [Goat's Head](/goats-head).
*   **[Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg)** (aka **[Zombie Plugg](/zombie-plugg)**): The former first mate of the [Wormwood](/wormwood), raised as an undead thrall by [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous).
*   **[Besmara](/besmara)**: The pirate goddess whose power resides in the region.
*   **[Myrkul](/myrkul)**: The dark god of the dead to whom [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s magic is tied.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Goat's Head](/goats-head)**: A lawless pirate settlement on the [Fire Grass Isles](/fire-grass-isles).
*   **[Silent Howl](/silent-howl)**: The party's fast-sailing ship.
*   **[The Rusty Kraken](/the-rusty-kraken)** (number 22): A well-kept tavern and lodge in [Goat's Head](/goats-head).
*   **[Free Trade Square](/free-trade-square)** (number 4): The central marketplace of [Goat's Head](/goats-head).
*   **[Shadook's Nine Pins](/shadooks-nine-pins)** (number 5): A prominent gambling den.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **The Ritual of Animation**: [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) successfully casts [Animate Dead](/animate-dead) to raise [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg)'s rotting corpse as a zombie in the captain's quarters.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Zombie Plugg](/zombie-plugg)**: Raised as an undead servant under [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s absolute control.
