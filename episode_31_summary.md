# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 31: Boar's Blood and a Zombie Toy]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 19 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The morning after the necromantic debate, the party prepares for their second day in [[Goat's Head]]. [[Torrin Horrendous]] sleeps on the cabin floor of the [[Silent Howl]], keeping [[Zombie Plugg]] covered under a sheet in the corner, while [[Lanny]] snores in his tight hammock.

[[Sandara Quinn]] and [[Devi McRae]] coordinate a plan to visit [[The Cathedral]] on the north side of town. Seeking to undo [[Owlbear Albert]]'s tragic mental condition, they decide to take [[Albert]] with them to find a specialist in restorative magic. [[Lanny]] delegates duties for the day, granting shore leave to [[Rosie Cusswell]] and [[Sandara Quinn]], while ordering [[Conchobhar Shortstone]], [[Cogwood]], and [[Tally]] to guard the ship.

[[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] head out first, seeking trouble in the lower quarters. They enter [[The Fat Man's Feed Bag]], a run-down tavern with straw on the floor and mismatched furniture. Hoping to lure out a representative of the local crime syndicate, they act loudly and brashly. While no syndicate member approaches, they draw the ire of two hostile half-orcs. A brawl quickly erupts. [[Lanny]] launches a devastating reckless attack with his [[Maul]] (without raging), completely shattering the lower half-orc, while [[Desmond]] punches out the second, leaving both half-orcs bloody and unconscious. The bartender, [[Craig]], is delighted by the gold coin [[Desmond]] throws him for the mess and shares local rumors.

Meanwhile, [[Devi McRae]], [[Torrin Horrendous]], [[Sandara Quinn]], and [[Owlbear Albert]] walk north. On the way, they pass [[Building 21]] (a local theater) and [[The Cracked Tooth Tavern]] (number 3), both of which are closed. They observe the [[Red Dog Smithy]] (number 10), an open-walled forge where a tall, red-haired blacksmith works under the guard of three massive, short-haired dogs. [[Torrin Horrendous]] asks [[Devi McRae]] to case the smithy for a future break-in. 

They also discuss [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s necromancy. [[Devi McRae]] argues that "recycling meat" is bad for business in a pirate haven like [[Goat's Head]], and they shouldn't zombify local armorers because they still need them to make gear. 

The group reaches [[The Cathedral]] (number 15), a stilted stone courtyard building with an open ceiling. [[Sandara Quinn]] is awed by the grand shrine to [[Besmara]] and leaves an offering. In the central courtyard, they meet [[Father Torbin]], a cleric dressed in blue robes, alongside a red-haired female cleric. They explain [[Albert]]'s condition, and [[Father Torbin]] agrees to visit the [[Silent Howl]] later that evening to attempt a restorative ritual.

On their return journey, [[Torrin Horrendous]] and [[Devi McRae]] stop at [[The Buttered Ram]] (number 23), a busy dwarven forge. They are warmly greeted by a young, clean-shaven dwarf wearing spectacles and a welder's mask, who is highly cooperative. [[Lanny]] and [[Desmond]] join them there, and [[Lanny]] pitches a creative idea to the dwarf about forging a massive sword using the jaws of predatory fish.

To wrap up their shopping, [[Torrin Horrendous]] and [[Devi McRae]] visit a basic clothing shop. [[Torrin Horrendous]] purchases two heavy, hooded waterproof cloaks for 4 silver pieces each to disguise [[Zombie Plugg]]. [[Devi McRae]] spends 3 gold pieces on two fresh outfits and boots to wash his old garments. The session ends as they coordinate their plans to meet [[Father Torbin]] back at the ship.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Father Torbin]]**: A cooperative cleric of the [[Cathedral]] who agrees to perform restorative magic on [[Owlbear Albert]].
*   **[[Owlbear Albert]]** (aka **[[Albert]]**): The mind-wiped goliath-sized pirate, brought to [[The Cathedral]] for help.
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: The cleric who accompanies the group to [[The Cathedral]] to pray to [[Besmara]].
*   **[[Craig]]** (aka **[[Greg]]**): The bartender at [[The Fat Man's Feed Bag]].
*   **Clean-Shaven Dwarf**: The friendly young smith at [[The Buttered Ram]].

## Locations Visited
*   **[[The Fat Man's Feed Bag]]**: A dilapidated, mismatched dive bar in [[Goat's Head]].
*   **[[The Cathedral]]** (number 15): A generic stone temple housing shrines to multiple deities on the north side of town.
*   **[[Red Dog Smithy]]** (number 10): A closed-off forge guarded by three massive dogs.
*   **[[The Buttered Ram]]** (number 23): A busy dwarven forge with a ram-headed anvil.
*   **[[Goat's Head]]**: The pirate haven on the [[Fire Grass Isles]].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[The Fat Man's Feed Bag]] Brawl**: [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] engage and quickly knock out two aggressive half-orcs inside the tavern.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Disguise Cloaks]]**: Two heavy, hooded waterproof cloaks purchased by [[Torrin Horrendous]] to hide [[Zombie Plugg]].
*   **[[Fresh Outfits]]**: Two sets of clean clothing and boots purchased by [[Devi McRae]].
