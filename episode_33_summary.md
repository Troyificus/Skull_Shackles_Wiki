# [Skull and Shackles Episode 33: Necromancy, Paranoia, and a Bloody Sack](/skull-and-shackles-episode-33-necromancy-paranoia-and-a-bloody-sack)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 19 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The session opens immediately following the chaotic street brawl in the town square of [Goat's Head](/goats-head) on the [Fire Grass Isles](/fire-grass-isles). Having handily defeated the hostile pirate crew, the party sets about securing their immediate perimeter and looting the fallen. 

[Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) thoroughly loots the unconscious green-cloaked captain, [Captain Paul](/captain-paul), reclaiming a fancy hat, a fine cloak, [Studded Leather](/studded-leather) armor, a [Rapier](/rapier), a [Whip](/whip), and a coin purse filled with [200 gold pieces](/200-gold-pieces). Meanwhile, [Lanny](/lanny) and [Desmond](/desmond) search the other fallen pirates, recovering an additional [100 gold pieces](/100-gold-pieces) and several mundane weapons, which they hide under some dockside matting to retrieve later. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) (operating under the name [Miss Blackheart](/miss-blackheart)) intervenes to ensure [Captain Paul](/captain-paul) and another badly injured pirate do not die, casting [Spare the Dying](/spare-the-dying) on both of them to preserve them for future use. 

As the local onlookers and tavern patrons begin to pick the dead pirates' bodies dry of remaining clothes and boots, the party hoists the two stabilized, unconscious prisoners over their shoulders and carries them back to the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl). Once aboard, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) chains the two captives to a support pole in the [Captain's Quarters](/captains-quarters), directly alongside [Zombie Plugg](/zombie-plugg). 

Below deck, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) visits [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) (aka [Fishguts](/fishguts)) and the fully restored [Albert](/albert) (formerly [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert)) in the galley. [Fishguts](/fishguts) expresses his relief that the tyrannical [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg) is dead and notes that things have been much sunnier since the party took over. 

Meanwhile, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous), [Desmond](/desmond), and [Lanny](/lanny) return to the streets to deal with the duplicitous barman from the [Fat Man's Feed Bag](/fat-mans-feed-bag) who lured them into the ambush. Before they can enter, they are intercepted by [Gibral](/gibral), the local safety enforcer who is responsible for the protection of local businesses. [Gibral](/gibral) makes it clear that while he was content to watch them slaughter [Captain Paul](/captain-paul)'s crew, he will not tolerate them disrupting or threatening local establishments. A tense standoff ensued, culminating in [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) delivering a chilling threat directly to [Gibral](/gibral): "I will make sure your death is as insignificant as your life." 

The party returns to the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl) to finalize their vessel purchase with [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher). They combine their funds to meet the [5,000 gold pieces](/5000-gold-pieces) price tag—using the [4,000 gold pieces](/4000-gold-pieces) from the sale of the [Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze) (formerly the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise)) and contributing [250 gold pieces](/250-gold-pieces) each of their personal wealth. 

With [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher) waiting on deck to conclude the deal, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) slips into the [Captain's Quarters](/captains-quarters) alone. Using her final spell slots, she unceremoniously slits the throats of [Captain Paul](/captain-paul) and the other captive, casting [Animate Dead](/animate-dead) to raise [Captain Paul](/captain-paul)'s fresh corpse as [Zombie Villahelm](/zombie-villahelm) (also referred to as [Zombie Wilhelm](/zombie-wilhelm)). She dresses the new zombie in chain mail and a hooded cloak to disguise his undead nature. 

After completing the transaction, [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher) briefly enters the cabin to retrieve his personal shirts. He is horrified to find [Zombie Plugg](/zombie-plugg) and the newly raised [Zombie Villahelm](/zombie-villahelm) huddled in the corner. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) successfully bluffs him (rolling a dirty 22 on her Deception check), claiming that the undead are a temporary containment solution for the cursed beach dagger and that they will dispose of them once they reach the open sea. Terrified but appeased, [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher) bids them a hasty farewell and departs for the [Rusty Kraken](/rusty-kraken), leaving the ship officially in the party's hands.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Captain Paul](/captain-paul)**: The green-cloaked pirate captain whom [Lanny](/lanny) and [Desmond](/desmond) battered in the town square; later executed and raised by [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) as [Zombie Villahelm](/zombie-villahelm).
*   **[Gibral](/gibral)** (also spelt **[Jabrel](/jabrel)**): The cold, professional local enforcer responsible for business protection in [Goat's Head](/goats-head).
*   **[Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)** (aka **[Fishguts](/fishguts)**): The ship's cook who expresses deep loyalty to the new management.
*   **[Albert](/albert)** (formerly **[Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert)**): The highly intelligent goliath brawler whose mind was restored in the previous session.
*   **[Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher)**: The retiring owner of the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl) who completes the ship's sale and departs the crew.
*   **[Zombie Plugg](/zombie-plugg)**: The animate, one-armed corpse of [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg), kept as [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s servant.
*   **[Zombie Villahelm](/zombie-villahelm)** (also referred to as **[Zombie Wilhelm](/zombie-wilhelm)**): The newly raised corpse of [Captain Paul](/captain-paul), outfitted in chain mail and a hooded robe.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Goat's Head](/goats-head)**: The lawless, rowdy pirate haven on the [Fire Grass Isles](/fire-grass-isles).
*   **[Silent Howl](/silent-howl)**: The party's newly acquired, highly maneuverable vessel.
*   **[Captain's Quarters](/captains-quarters)**: The main cabin of the ship, now serving as a makeshift laboratory for [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s necromantic experiments.
*   **[Rusty Kraken](/rusty-kraken)**: The local tavern where [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher) retires.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **The Aftermath of the Square Brawl**: The party loots and cleanses the battlefield following their victory over [Captain Paul](/captain-paul)'s crew.
*   **Confrontation with [Gibral](/gibral)**: A tense verbal standoff in the streets of [Goat's Head](/goats-head) where [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) threatens the local enforcer.

*   **[Captain's Hat and Cloak](/captains-hat-and-cloak)**: Salvaged by [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae).
*   **[300 gold pieces](/300-gold-pieces)**: Total gold looted from the fallen pirates (including [200 gold pieces](/200-gold-pieces) from [Captain Paul](/captain-paul)'s purse and [100 gold pieces](/100-gold-pieces) from his crew).
*   **[Silent Howl](/silent-howl)**: Full ownership of the ship is officially transferred to the party.
