# [Skull and Shackles Episode 34: Coconuts, Corpses and the Council of Secrets](/skull-and-shackles-episode-34-coconuts-corpses-and-the-council-of-secrets)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 20 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The episode begins in the pre-dawn darkness as [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) quietly slips back aboard the ship. Carrying a large, blood-stained sack behind him, he approaches his companions with a peace offering to ease the tension of the previous night’s volatile argument. For his brother [Desmond](/desmond), he presents a fine bottle of rum; for [Lanny](/lanny), a sack filled with fresh coconuts; and for [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous), a fresh dead body to serve as a new subject for her necromantic experiments. 

[Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) offers a sincere apology to the group, explaining his emotional outburst. As a sociopath beginning to genuinely care for his crewmates, his anger stemmed from a fear of starting a self-destructive gang war with [Goat's Head](/goats-head)'s powerful local syndicate. 

Before assembling the crew, the four heroes convene a private meeting—dubbed the **[Council of Secrets](/council-of-secrets)**—to establish absolute transparency and share their deepest, darkest backstories:
*   **[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s Backstory**: She reveals that she spent years imprisoned and brutally tortured by a sadistic man named [Blackheart](/blackheart). Upon escaping, she adopted his name as her own moniker, [Lady Blackheart](/lady-blackheart), to project absolute menace.
*   **[Lanny](/lanny)'s Backstory**: He shares that he was banished from his goliath tribe in the far west. A mysterious, unnatural [Mist](/mist) rolled over his island; when his chieftain went to investigate, he returned crazed, blood-drenched, and violent. [Lanny](/lanny) was forced to kill his own chieftain in self-defense, resulting in his permanent exile.
*   **The McRae Twins' Backstory**: [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) and [Desmond](/desmond) reveal that they are on a high-stakes quest for vengeance against their father, [Gavin McRae](/gavin-mcrae). Gavin, a narcissistic captain who grandiosely calls himself an admiral, murdered their mother. He currently operates out of [Tidewater Rock](/tidewater-rock), a heavily fortified stronghold secured through his marriage to a wealthy [Chelaxian](/chelaxian) aristocrat. The twins plan to gather [Infamy](/infamy) in the [Shackles](/shackles), gain an audience with [Hurricane King Karch Bonefist](/hurricane-king-karch-bonefist), and ultimately execute their father, even if it costs them their lives.

With their bonds reinforced, the group officially renames the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl) to the **[Blood Wake](/blood-wake)** (or **[Bloodwake](/bloodwake)**). 

Later that day, [Desmond](/desmond) and [Lanny](/lanny) explore the town to gather supplies. They visit [Bottled Solutions](/bottled-solutions), a pungent local alchemy shop owned by a mysterious [Alchemist](/alchemist). [Desmond](/desmond) purchases specialized brewing ingredients to experiment with while out on the high seas, while [Lanny](/lanny) buys some "experimental" potion samples. 

Having finalized their preparations and paid their dock fees, the crew of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) sets sail from the harbor of [Goat's Head](/goats-head), steering southwest out into the treacherous waters of the [Fever Sea](/fever-sea).

To celebrate their official emergence as independent free captains, the party is bestowed with unique mid-level boons:
*   **[Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)** gains absolute proficiency with the [Tidewater Cutlass](/tidewater-cutlass).
*   **[Lanny](/lanny)** establishes a permanent telepathic bond with his resident rat, [Rat Damon](/rat-damon) (also known as [Ratty](/ratty) or [Mr. Rat](/mr-rat)), allowing him to use [Speak with Animals](/speak-with-animals) on him at will without expending spell slots.
*   **[Desmond](/desmond)** gains a divine [Commune](/commune) ability, allowing him to ask his patron [Besmara](/besmara) a single, vague yes-or-no question once per week.
*   **[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)** receives [Grave Sense](/grave-sense), allowing her to instinctively detect the presence of any nearby undead.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Alchemist](/alchemist)**: The eccentric proprietor of the [Bottled Solutions](/bottled-solutions) alchemy shop in [Goat's Head](/goats-head).
*   **[Gavin McRae](/gavin-mcrae)**: The narcissistic pirate captain, self-styled admiral, and estranged father of the McRae twins, based at [Tidewater Rock](/tidewater-rock).
*   **[Rat Damon](/rat-damon)** (aka **[Ratty](/ratty)** or **[Mr. Rat](/mr-rat)**): [Lanny](/lanny)'s loyal shipboard rat companion.
*   **[Blackheart](/blackheart)**: The cruel torturer from [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s past whose name she adopted.
*   **[Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)** (aka **[Fishguts](/fishguts)**): The loyal ship's cook aboard the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake).
*   **[Besmara](/besmara)**: The Pirate Queen deity whose divine mark now flows through [Desmond](/desmond).

## Locations Visited
*   **[Goat's Head](/goats-head)**: The lawless island haven where the party stocks up on supplies before departure.
*   **[Bottled Solutions](/bottled-solutions)**: A heavily scented alchemy shop specializing in bizarre potions and brewing ingredients.
*   **[Fever Sea](/fever-sea)**: The vast, open ocean southwest of [Goat's Head](/goats-head) where merchant ships operate.
*   **[Tidewater Rock](/tidewater-rock)**: The heavily fortified estate of [Gavin McRae](/gavin-mcrae).

## Battles & Encounters
*   **The [Council of Secrets](/council-of-secrets)**: An emotional, pivotal roleplay encounter where the party shares their tragic pasts and aligns their long-term campaign goals.

## Loot & Rewards

*   **[Cloak of Protection](/cloak-of-protection)**: Salvaged from their previous excursions and officially identified by [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous).
*   **[Tidewater Cutlass](/tidewater-cutlass) Proficiency**: Awarded to [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) as a specialized combat boon.
