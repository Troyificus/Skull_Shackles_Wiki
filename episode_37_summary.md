# [Skull and Shackles Episode 37: Torrin's Secret and Whalebone Pilk](/skull-and-shackles-episode-37-torrins-secret-and-whalebone-pilk)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 20-21 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Following their successful raid of the [True Wind](/true-wind), the party officially transfers the cargo to the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake). Before setting sail, [Lanny](/lanny) shows an honorable streak by handing a healing potion (unbeknownst to him, a questionable custom concoction) to the defeated [Dragonborn Bodyguard](/dragonborn-bodyguard) as a gesture of "just business, no bad feelings." They leave the merchant crew with their lives and a crippled mainmast.

During their travels, the crew meets and recruits a new member—an overly upbeat, cheerful half-elf named [Johnny](/johnny). While the rest of the crew welcomes [Johnny](/johnny), [Desmond](/desmond) remains highly suspicious of her too-good-to-be-true attitude.

That evening, the crew gathers on deck to celebrate their successful raid. [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert) brings up a mini-keg of decent rum provided by [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop). However, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) is deeply dejected, having spent the afternoon failing to copy a new spell into her spellbook, wasting the last of her valuable paper.

A significant roleplay breakthrough occurs on deck between [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous). Slipping out of her Miss Blackheart persona, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) has a rare moment of sincerity, admitting she wears her dark, edgy identity as a protective mask. She confesses to [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) that she has massive personal goals she cannot yet share, before kissing him on the cheek, thanking him for his friendship, and retreating back into her quarters with an insult.

Later that night, during the evening watch, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) hears the distant, ominous toll of a ship's bell echoing across the water, accompanied by a strange green glow on the horizon. He alerts [Desmond](/desmond), and together they wake [Lanny](/lanny), [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous), [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) (aka [Fishguts](/fishguts)), and [Tally](/tally). 

[Fishguts](/fishguts) and [Tally](/tally) immediately identify the signs: it is the approach of [Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk), a legendary undead whaler captain who sails the spectral vessel [Death Knell](/death-knell), hunting down prey. [Fishguts](/fishguts) is terrified, stating that once Pilk selects a target, he hunts them relentlessly until they are dead. 

[Desmond](/desmond) is unfazed and aggressively suggests they sail directly toward the green glow to "chin the fucker." However, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) and the rest of the council decide that they are woefully understaffed and unprepared for a battle against an army of undead. They choose to run for the nearest port—the Taldan colony of [Little Upara](/little-upara)—to seek safety, recruit more crew, and search for clerical assistance.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Johnny](/johnny)**: An overly cheerful half-elf who joins the crew, instantly raising [Desmond](/desmond)'s suspicions.
*   **[Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)**: The cook who shares his rum and panics upon hearing the legend of [Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk).
*   **[Tally](/tally)**: The ship's navigator who recognizes the telltale bell and green glow of the ghost ship.
*   **[Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk)**: The legendary undead captain of the [Death Knell](/death-knell) who has targeted the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake).

## Locations Visited
*   **[Fever Sea](/fever-sea)**: The open waters where the ghostly green glow of the [Death Knell](/death-knell) is spotted.
*   **[Blood Wake](/blood-wake)**: The party's ship, sailing at maximum speed to escape the spectral threat.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[The Chase of the Death Knell](/the-chase-of-the-death-knell)**: An avoided conflict as the party flees the approaching ghost ship.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[A Mini-Keg of Rum](/a-mini-keg-of-rum)**: Provided by [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) to celebrate their victory over the merchant vessel.
