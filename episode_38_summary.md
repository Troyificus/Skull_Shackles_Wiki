# [Skull and Shackles Episode 38: Coconut Moonshine and Punching Dragons](/skull-and-shackles-episode-38-coconut-moonshine-and-punching-dragons)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 22-23 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The episode begins with the crew of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) sailing frantically through the night to outrun the spectral [Death Knell](/death-knell). Inside the captain's quarters, [Desmond](/desmond) confronts [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) about an offhand comment he made about his twin brother, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), which deeply upset her. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) opens up about her past, revealing that she accidentally started a fire that killed her own brother—a heavy guilt she carries to this day.

The next day, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) attempts to copy another spell and fails again, completely running out of paper. Meanwhile, [Desmond](/desmond) attempts to brew a new batch of moonshine but spoils the ingredients. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), with the help of [Cogwood](/cogwood), successfully skins the Sahuagin bodies harvested from their previous encounter.

On the second night of their flight, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) is on watch and hears the raspy bell of the [Death Knell](/death-knell) ringing closer than before, accompanied by the eerie green glow. The crew is thrown into a panic. To restore order, [Lanny](/lanny) delivers a highly inspiring and concise speech to the crew from the stern castle, successfully calming their nerves.

The following afternoon, the exhausted crew finally makes port at [Little Upara](/little-upara), a talden colony in the Shackles. Due to sailing non-stop for two days, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), [Desmond](/desmond), and [Lanny](/lanny) all suffer from a level of exhaustion. After paying the port fees to the [Dock Master](/dock-master), the party splits up:

*   [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn), [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell), and [Johnny](/johnny) head into town to gather rumors and information from the locals on how to defeat [Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk) and his undead crew.
*   [Desmond](/desmond) and [Lanny](/lanny) visit [The Arena](/the-arena) (number 16) to investigate fighting opportunities.
*   On the local notice board, they discover a lucrative bounty: **5,000 gold pieces** offered for the extermination of a winged menace plaguing the island's hunting grounds, known to the locals as [Black Fang](/black-fang).

Realising they need coin and a way to build their infamy to gain an audience with the [Hurricane King Karch Bonefist](/hurricane-king-karch-bonefist), the party decides to take on the quest to hunt down [Black Fang](/black-fang).

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Little Upara Dock Master](/little-upara-dock-master)**: The port official who registers the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) and collects the harbor fees.
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: The cleric who leads the investigation into [Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk)'s weaknesses in town.
*   **[Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)**: Accompanies [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) on her search for lore.
*   **[Black Fang](/black-fang)**: A terrifying winged creature/dragon plaguing [Little Upara](/little-upara), carrying a 5,000 gold piece bounty.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Little Upara](/little-upara)**: A beautifully organized Taldan colony on the eastern edge of the Shackles.
*   **[The Arena](/the-arena)**: A grand gladiatorial amphitheatre in [Little Upara](/little-upara) where champions fight.
*   **[Blood Wake](/blood-wake)**: The party's vessel, currently docked safely in the harbor.

## Battles & Encounters
*   None (this is a transitional session focused on travel, roleplay, and quest acquisition).

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Black Fang Bounty Notice](/black-fang-bounty-notice)**: A lead for a high-paying beast-hunting contract.
