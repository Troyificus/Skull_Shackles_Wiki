# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 39: Hunting Black Fang With Disguised Zombies]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 22 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Having arrived at the Taldean colony of [[Little Upara]] to escape the spectral pursuit of [[Whalebone Pilk]], the crew of the [[Blood Wake]] enjoys their first day of shore leave [38, 663]. [[Torrin Horrendous]], [[Devi McRae]], and [[Desmond]] explore a local tavern while offtaking their cargo of plunder from the [[True Wind]] [688]. Looking to secure funds and build their reputation, they notice a posting on the local bounty board: a massive **[[5,000 gold pieces]]** reward for the extermination of a winged terror known as [[Black Fang]] [688, 689].

To coordinate the hunt, the party visits the local guard house to speak with [[Captain Haley]] of the [[Town Guard]] [688, 689]. Intrigued by the substantial reward, the party decides to set off into the wilderness. To prepare for their absence, they assign the highly experienced [[Tally]] to take charge of the [[Blood Wake]], with the chef [[Ambrose Kroop]] acting as his second [691, 694]. They officially invite the cleric [[Sandara Quinn]] to join their hunting expedition, and she eagerly accepts, wishing to take an active role in their adventures [694, 695, 696].

Meanwhile, [[Torrin Horrendous]] plans a covert operation. She commissions [[Tally]] to purchase two sets of [[Chain Mail]] from a local smithy for [[300 gold pieces]] [698]. When he returns, [[Torrin Horrendous]] outfits her newly raised [[Zombies]] (now four of them) in the heavy metal armor and hooded [[Cloaks]] to disguise them as heavily-armored living mercenaries [698, 699, 702]. This allows her to smuggle her undead thralls through the outskirts of the town without alerting the local authorities [702, 748].

At the same time, [[Lanny]] visits the arena master [[Stephanie Garrison]] to sign the party up for a high-profile exhibition match [705, 706, 747]. They register under the team name [[Blood Wake Crew]] with [[Lanny]] listed as [[Captain Skull Crusher]] [707]. They schedule a high-stakes combat against a dangerous [[Venom Troll]] to take place on the evening of the third day, paying a hefty holding fee to secure the booking [707, 708, 747].
