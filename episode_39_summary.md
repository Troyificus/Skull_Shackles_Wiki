# [Skull and Shackles Episode 39: Hunting Black Fang With Disguised Zombies](/skull-and-shackles-episode-39-hunting-black-fang-with-disguised-zombies)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 22 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Having arrived at the Taldean colony of [Little Upara](/little-upara) to escape the spectral pursuit of [Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk), the crew of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) enjoys their first day of shore leave [38, 663]. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous), [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), and [Desmond](/desmond) explore a local tavern while offtaking their cargo of plunder from the [True Wind](/true-wind) [688]. Looking to secure funds and build their reputation, they notice a posting on the local bounty board: a massive **[5,000 gold pieces](/5000-gold-pieces)** reward for the extermination of a winged terror known as [Black Fang](/black-fang) [688, 689].

To coordinate the hunt, the party visits the local guard house to speak with [Captain Haley](/captain-haley) of the [Town Guard](/town-guard) [688, 689]. Intrigued by the substantial reward, the party decides to set off into the wilderness. To prepare for their absence, they assign the highly experienced [Tally](/tally) to take charge of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake), with the chef [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) acting as his second [691, 694]. They officially invite the cleric [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) to join their hunting expedition, and she eagerly accepts, wishing to take an active role in their adventures [694, 695, 696].

Meanwhile, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) plans a covert operation. She commissions [Tally](/tally) to purchase two sets of [Chain Mail](/chain-mail) from a local smithy for [300 gold pieces](/300-gold-pieces) [698]. When he returns, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) outfits her newly raised [Zombies](/zombies) (now four of them) in the heavy metal armor and hooded [Cloaks](/cloaks) to disguise them as heavily-armored living mercenaries [698, 699, 702]. This allows her to smuggle her undead thralls through the outskirts of the town without alerting the local authorities [702, 748].

At the same time, [Lanny](/lanny) visits the arena master [Stephanie Garrison](/stephanie-garrison) to sign the party up for a high-profile exhibition match [705, 706, 747]. They register under the team name [Blood Wake Crew](/blood-wake-crew) with [Lanny](/lanny) listed as [Captain Skull Crusher](/captain-skull-crusher) [707]. They schedule a high-stakes combat against a dangerous [Venom Troll](/venom-troll) to take place on the evening of the third day, paying a hefty holding fee to secure the booking [707, 708, 747].
