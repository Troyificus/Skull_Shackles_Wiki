# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 40: Chainmail Zombies Wake the Black Dragon]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 23 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The session begins with the party gathering at the [[Hunter's Lodge]] to finalise their expedition details [748]. After consulting with the local hunters, they learn that the creature [[Black Fang]] is an aggressive winged beast that has been terrorising hunting parties on the western side of the island [715, 747]. To facilitate their journey, they purchase two days' worth of [[Rations]] for [[5 gold pieces]] and rent a [[Horse and Cart]] [714, 715, 748]. To accommodate the uncomfortable [[Sandara Quinn]], she is permitted to sit on the front bench of the cart, far away from [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s disguised [[Zombies]] riding in the back [748].

The party travels south along the overgrown forest trail until the foliage becomes too dense for the cart [748, 765, 766]. As they begin to establish their campsite for the evening, [[Devi McRae]]'s keen senses detect rapid movement in the nearby underbrush [748]. Suddenly, a stampede of wild [[Boars]] thunders out of the forest, charging directly at their camp [748, 749]. [[Lanny]], [[Desmond]], [[Devi McRae]], and [[Torrin Horrendous]] spring into action, quickly slaughtering the aggressive beasts [749, 755]. [[Devi McRae]] observes that the boars were not attacking them out of hunger, but rather fleeing in absolute panic from a larger predator [749].

Moments later, their suspicions are confirmed by a deafening roar [749]. High above the forest canopy, a massive, dark, winged shape—revealed to be a black [[Dragon]]—takes flight, carrying a dead boar in its massive talons before soaring south into the night [749].

As the party recovers from the shock, a hooded stranger steps out of the dark forest with his bow drawn [750]. He introduces himself as [[Dark Wolf]], the legendary [[Shadowhunter]] (whose real name is [[Copeland]]), claiming he is also hunting [[Black Fang]] for the bounty [750, 752, 758, 776]. Fearing he will steal their prize, the party takes an instant dislike to him [776]. When [[Desmond]] aggressively steps forward to pull down his hood, [[Dark Wolf]] interprets it as a hostile act and shoots a quick arrow, triggering a brief battle [776]. 

The party easily outnumbers and overpowers the hunter [755, 776]. They disarm, bind, and lock [[Dark Wolf]] in heavy manacles [753, 776]. After a tense interrogation, they realize he is relatively bumbling under his edgy persona, so they graciously release him, leaving him to nurse his bruised ego while they take a long rest to prepare for the morning's hunt [759, 764, 776, 777].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Dark Wolf]]** (aka **[[Copeland]]**): A highly theatrical, self-proclaimed "shadowhunter" who attempts to claim the dragon bounty alone [750, 752, 758, 776].
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: The party's cleric companion who sits up front on the cart to avoid the creepy, disguised undead [748].

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Hunter's Lodge]]**: The outpost in [[Little Upara]] where the party acquires their cart and supplies [748].
*   **[[Wilderness Campsite]]**: The dense, dark forest clearing south of the colony where the party makes camp [748].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[Boars]] Stampede**: A fast-paced skirmish where the party easily slaughters a charging pack of wild forest boars [748, 749].
*   **[[Dark Wolf]] Standoff**: A brief, one-sided altercation triggered when [[Desmond]] tries to unhood the bumbling shadowhunter, ending with the party manacling him [753, 776].

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Horse and Cart]]**: Rented from the lodge to transport the party's equipment and disguised zombies [714, 748].
