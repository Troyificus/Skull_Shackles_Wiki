# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 42: Celebrities, New Crew, and the Druid's Awakening]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 19 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Following their legendary triumph over the two black dragons, the party returns to [[Little Upara]] as renowned slayers. While [[Torrin Horrendous]] and [[Devi McRae]] remain on the outskirts of town to hide their undead servants, [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]], accompanied by [[Sandara Quinn]], parade the massive [[Dragon Heads]] through the streets. The spectacle draws a huge crowd of cheering townsfolk and fascinated children. 

The boys march to [[The Arena]] and meet with the eccentric dwarven proprietor, [[Stephanie Garrison]]. They finalize a contract to participate in the upcoming main event: a high-stakes battle against a captured, highly dangerous [[Venom Troll]] in two days. [[Desmond]] negotiates a deal that if they win, [[Stephanie Garrison]] will treat the entire [[Blood Wake Crew]] to a lavish meal of roast meats at a fine tavern. Outside [[The Arena]], [[Desmond]] addresses the crowd, building hype for the show and urging them to buy tickets.

Upon returning to the docks, they spot [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]] waving from the deck of his grand galleon, the [[Pinion]]. After a brief, cordial reunion where [[Pegsworth]] congratulates them on their dragon slays, the party climbs aboard the [[Blood Wake]]. 

There, they are introduced to their newly recruited crew members, found in the local taverns by [[Rosie Cusswell]] and [[Johnny]]: the [[Fiska]] brothers ([[Crawl Fiska]], [[Frank Fiska]], and [[Dutch Fiska]]) and a talented half-orc bard named [[Benoch]]. To test the grit of the new recruits, [[Lanny]] initiates a round of mild hazing, delivering a solid punch to [[Crawl Fiska]]. [[Desmond]] welcomes them to the crew and shares a batch of his questionable, home-brewed [[Mushroom Beer]]. He lays down the ship's absolute laws: no stealing, no harming crewmates, and complete loyalty, warning them that violating these rules carries a penalty of death.

As night falls, [[Torrin Horrendous]] and [[Devi McRae]] slip back aboard with [[Zombie Villahelm]] and the other undead thralls, successfully stowing them in the bilges. The dragon heads are safely locked in the [[Captain's Quarters]] under zombie guard. The session concludes with a major mechanical breakthrough as [[Lanny]] taps into his primal connection with nature, officially taking a level in [[Druid]], allowing him to [[Wild Shape]] while in a barbarian [[Rage]].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Stephanie Garrison]]**: The dwarven owner of the [[Little Upara]] colosseum who signs the party to fight the [[Venom Troll]].
*   **[[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]]**: The friendly peg-legged pirate captain of the [[Pinion]] who celebrates the party's success.
*   **[[Crawl Fiska]]**: A slender new crew recruit who endures [[Lanny]]'s test of strength.
*   **[[Frank Fiska]]**: A new sailor who joins the [[Blood Wake]].
*   **[[Dutch Fiska]]**: Another of the new sailor brothers.
*   **[[Benoch]]**: A half-orc bard recruited to entertain the crew and bolster morale.
*   **[[Johnny]]**: The peppy half-elf crew member who helped recruit the new sailors.
*   **[[Rosie Cusswell]]**: The halfling rigger who welcomes the new recruits with her fiddle.
*   **[[Zombie Villahelm]]**: [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s raised zombie servant, dressed in chain mail and a hooded cloak.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Little Upara]]**: The dapper Taldan colony where the party is currently docked.
*   **[[The Arena]]**: The bustling colosseum where the upcoming troll fight is scheduled.
*   **[[Blood Wake]]**: The party's swift, newly renamed ship.
*   **[[Captain's Quarters]]**: The secure cabin where the dragon heads are stored.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[New Recruit Hazing]]**: A brief test of grit where [[Lanny]] punches the slender [[Crawl Fiska]] to verify his toughness before officially welcoming him.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Dragon Heads]]**: Two massive heads of the slain black dragons, kept as trophies and potential figureheads.
*   **[[New Crew Recruits]]**: Four capable new sailors ([[Crawl Fiska]], [[Frank Fiska]], [[Dutch Fiska]], and [[Benoch]]) officially join the ship's roster.
