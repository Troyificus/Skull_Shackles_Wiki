# [Skull and Shackles Episode 44: High Society and Serial Killer Secrets](/skull-and-shackles-episode-44-high-society-and-serial-killer-secrets)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 21 (evening) - Day 25

## Plot Summary
Directly following their spectacular victory over the [Venom Troll](/venom-troll), [Stephanie Garrison](/stephanie-garrison) congratulates the party and fulfills his promise, treating the crew to a luxurious feast at the [Candle of Ice](/candle-of-ice) tavern. He also provides the party with three valuable doses of harvested [Troll Venom](/troll-venom) for [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)'s future poison-crafting. To avoid the massive crowd of fans blocking the main gates, [Stephanie Garrison](/stephanie-garrison) smuggles the party out of the colosseum through a secure, subterranean tunnel leading directly back to the docks.

The next day, [Lanny](/lanny) has the [Fiska](/fiska) brothers fetch fresh water so he can thoroughly wash the toxic green slime off his skin. [Lanny](/lanny) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) go shopping in town and purchase a couple of valuable [Spyglasses](/spyglasses) to help the crew spot ships on the horizon. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) visits the spell scribe to finalize copying spells into her spellbook. 

The following evening, the party dresses in their newly purchased fine attire. [Desmond](/desmond) wears black garments and [Sandara Quinn's Tricorn Hat](/sandara-quinns-tricorn-hat), [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) wears a dapper white shirt and coat, and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) wears his flamboyant purple-red coat and feathered hat, looking very much like the captain. They hike up to [Lady Olandra](/lady-olandra)'s grand estate. [Captain Haley](/captain-haley) welcomes them at the gates, guiding them past grazing palomino horses into a grand, candle-lit banquet hall.

[Lady Olandra](/lady-olandra) cordially welcomes them, initially addressing the flashy [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) as the captain. The party proudly corrects her and introduces their true leader, [Captain Skull Crusher](/captain-skull-crusher) ([Lanny](/lanny)), who awkwardly kisses the duchess's hand. She presents them with their [5,000 gold pieces](/5000-gold-pieces) bounty for exterminating [Black Fang](/black-fang) and his sibling. Furthermore, she officially bestows upon them honorary noble titles—making them official Lords and a Lady of the colony.

As the elegant dinner nears its end, the doors fly open. [Lady Olandra](/lady-olandra)'s sister, [Petrina](/petrina), bursts into the hall. Acting completely un-ladylike, she dramatically tears meat off a pheasant bone, puts her feet up on the table, and drinks wine straight from a goblet. Having just returned from a pirate council meeting in [Port Peril](/port-peril), [Petrina](/petrina) shares two ominous pieces of news: a fleet of [Chelaxian Navy](/chelaxian-navy) ships has been spotted encroaching upon the southern borders, and a terrifying, elusive [Serial Killer](/serial-killer) is currently on a murder spree in the streets of [Port Peril](/port-peril). The episode ends as [Lady Olandra](/lady-olandra) rubs her temple in sheer frustration at her sister's antics.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Lady Olandra](/lady-olandra)**: The duchess and ruler of [Little Upara](/little-upara) who awards the party their bounty and noble titles.
*   **[Petrina](/petrina)**: [Lady Olandra](/lady-olandra)'s wild pirate sister who brings news of the [Chelaxian Navy](/chelaxian-navy) and the [Port Peril](/port-peril) murders.
*   **[Captain Haley](/captain-haley)**: The guard captain who escorts the dapper party into the estate.
*   **[Stephanie Garrison](/stephanie-garrison)**: The colosseum proprietor who treats the crew to a celebratory feast.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Candle of Ice](/candle-of-ice)**: A luxurious timber-and-brick tavern with stained glass windows and glowing gemstone lights.
*   **[Lady Olandra's Estate](/lady-olandras-estate)**: A grand, high-security mansion with extensive grounds on the outskirts of town.
*   **[Little Upara](/little-upara)**: The dapper Taldan colony.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[The High Society Dinner](/the-high-society-dinner)**: A social and roleplay-heavy parley with the duchess, culminating in the chaotic arrival of her pirate sister.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[5,000 Gold Pieces](/5000-gold-pieces)**: The official bounty reward for slaying the two black dragons.
*   **[Honorary Noble Titles](/honorary-noble-titles)**: The party is officially granted the titles of Lords and Lady of [Little Upara](/little-upara).
*   **[Troll Venom](/troll-venom)**: Three potent doses of harvested acidic venom, collected by [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae).
*   **[Spyglasses](/spyglasses)**: High-quality spyglasses purchased to improve the ship's long-range vision.
