# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 46: Smuggling Skeletons and Whalebone Pilk]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 29 (evening) - Day 38

## Plot Summary
The party returns to [[Little Upara]] under the cover of dusk to avoid drawing attention to [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s newly raised skeletal companions [899]. With [[Devi McRae]]'s custom dragon-hide armor requiring nine days to complete, the crew settles in for a week and a half of relaxing downtime in the port [894]. During this period, [[Torrin Horrendous]] copies spells into her spellbook, and [[Desmond]] refines his custom alcohol brews [894].

To bolster their ranks, the party hosts a small recruitment trial at [[The Arena]] [894]. They successfully recruit four hardened and experienced pirates: the three [[Fiska]] brothers ([[Crawl Fiska]], [[Frank Fiska]], and [[Dutch Fiska]]) and a half-orc bard named [[Benoch]] [830]. [[Lanny]] tests their resolve with a bit of light hazing, while [[Desmond]] welcomes them aboard the [[Blood Wake]] with a fresh batch of his experimental mushroom beer [824, 825, 830]. Meanwhile, the ship's dwarven carpenter, [[Bailey]], works tirelessly to install the freshly harvested dragon skull onto the bow of the [[Blood Wake]] as a formidable new figurehead [918].

A few days into their stay, the party arranges a dapper dinner aboard [[The Pinion]], the grand galleon belonging to [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]] [901]. Over drinks, [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]] congratulates them on their colosseum victory and shares a dark, legendary tale of the open sea: the terrifying curse of [[Whalebone Pilk]] [901, 902]. Pegsworth warns that [[Whalebone Pilk]] is an undead captain who relentlessly stalks his chosen prey across the open ocean aboard his spectral ghost ship, [[The Death Knell]] [902]. Once Pilk marks a vessel, he hunts them down continuously until they are dead or manage to destroy him for good [902]. 

During the conversation, [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]] also inquires about the party's recent exploits in [[Goat's Head]] [904]. Amused to hear they nearly ignited a gang war with the local safety enforcer [[Gibral]], Pegsworth promises to use his influence to smooth things over upon his next return to the [[Fire Grass Isles]] [904, 906]. The party also discusses the strange, dull gray metal necklace they salvaged from the [[Sahuagin]] fishing boat, deducing that the aquatic creatures are aggressively hunting them down to reclaim their stolen holy relic [905]. The evening ends with [[Desmond]] enthusiastically demonstrating his divine magic by launching an [[Eldritch Blast]] into the night sky, leaving a skeptical [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]] rolling his eyes in bewilderment [908].

Once [[Devi McRae]]'s smoldering dragon-hide armor is finally complete, the crew boards the newly styled [[Blood Wake]] and sets sail south into the shipping lanes [894, 921]. morall is high, but as night approaches, an ominous, icy bank of fog rolls in from the south [921]. An anxious silence grips the crew as the heavy, echoing toll of a spectral ship's bell begins ringing through the dark fog [921]. [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] deliver inspiring speeches from the stern castle to embolden the nervous crew, but the tension is shattered when a ghastly, rotted ghost ship—[[The Death Knell]]—crashes through the fog and violently rams the side of the [[Blood Wake]] [921, 922].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]]**: The peg-legged captain of [[The Pinion]] who warns the party about [[Whalebone Pilk]] and agrees to help them with [[Gibral]] [901, 904, 906].
*   **[[Fiska Brothers]]**: [[Crawl Fiska]], [[Frank Fiska]], and [[Dutch Fiska]], three sturdy brothers recruited as new deckhands [830].
*   **[[Benoch]]**: A half-orc bard recruited during [[The Arena]] trials [830].
*   **[[Bailey]]**: The ship's dwarf carpenter who mounts the dragon skull figurehead [918].
*   **[[Gibral]]**: The Goat's Head crime boss whom the party offended [904, 906].

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Little Upara]]**: The Taldan colony where the crew enjoys their downtime [894].
*   **[[The Pinion]]**: [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]]'s grand, well-appointed galleon [901].
*   **[[The Blood Wake]]**: The party's fast-sailing vessel, newly outfitted with a dragon skull figurehead [918, 921].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[The Ghost Ship Ambush]]**: A terrifying midnight collision as [[The Death Knell]] ambushes the [[Blood Wake]] in a magical fog bank [921, 922].
