# [Skull and Shackles Episode 46: Smuggling Skeletons and Whalebone Pilk](/skull-and-shackles-episode-46-smuggling-skeletons-and-whalebone-pilk)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 29 (evening) - Day 38

## Plot Summary
The party returns to [Little Upara](/little-upara) under the cover of dusk to avoid drawing attention to [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s newly raised skeletal companions [899]. With [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)'s custom dragon-hide armor requiring nine days to complete, the crew settles in for a week and a half of relaxing downtime in the port [894]. During this period, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) copies spells into her spellbook, and [Desmond](/desmond) refines his custom alcohol brews [894].

To bolster their ranks, the party hosts a small recruitment trial at [The Arena](/the-arena) [894]. They successfully recruit four hardened and experienced pirates: the three [Fiska](/fiska) brothers ([Crawl Fiska](/crawl-fiska), [Frank Fiska](/frank-fiska), and [Dutch Fiska](/dutch-fiska)) and a half-orc bard named [Benoch](/benoch) [830]. [Lanny](/lanny) tests their resolve with a bit of light hazing, while [Desmond](/desmond) welcomes them aboard the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) with a fresh batch of his experimental mushroom beer [824, 825, 830]. Meanwhile, the ship's dwarven carpenter, [Bailey](/bailey), works tirelessly to install the freshly harvested dragon skull onto the bow of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) as a formidable new figurehead [918].

A few days into their stay, the party arranges a dapper dinner aboard [The Pinion](/the-pinion), the grand galleon belonging to [Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth) [901]. Over drinks, [Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth) congratulates them on their colosseum victory and shares a dark, legendary tale of the open sea: the terrifying curse of [Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk) [901, 902]. Pegsworth warns that [Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk) is an undead captain who relentlessly stalks his chosen prey across the open ocean aboard his spectral ghost ship, [The Death Knell](/the-death-knell) [902]. Once Pilk marks a vessel, he hunts them down continuously until they are dead or manage to destroy him for good [902]. 

During the conversation, [Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth) also inquires about the party's recent exploits in [Goat's Head](/goats-head) [904]. Amused to hear they nearly ignited a gang war with the local safety enforcer [Gibral](/gibral), Pegsworth promises to use his influence to smooth things over upon his next return to the [Fire Grass Isles](/fire-grass-isles) [904, 906]. The party also discusses the strange, dull gray metal necklace they salvaged from the [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) fishing boat, deducing that the aquatic creatures are aggressively hunting them down to reclaim their stolen holy relic [905]. The evening ends with [Desmond](/desmond) enthusiastically demonstrating his divine magic by launching an [Eldritch Blast](/eldritch-blast) into the night sky, leaving a skeptical [Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth) rolling his eyes in bewilderment [908].

Once [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)'s smoldering dragon-hide armor is finally complete, the crew boards the newly styled [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) and sets sail south into the shipping lanes [894, 921]. morall is high, but as night approaches, an ominous, icy bank of fog rolls in from the south [921]. An anxious silence grips the crew as the heavy, echoing toll of a spectral ship's bell begins ringing through the dark fog [921]. [Desmond](/desmond) and [Lanny](/lanny) deliver inspiring speeches from the stern castle to embolden the nervous crew, but the tension is shattered when a ghastly, rotted ghost ship—[The Death Knell](/the-death-knell)—crashes through the fog and violently rams the side of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) [921, 922].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth)**: The peg-legged captain of [The Pinion](/the-pinion) who warns the party about [Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk) and agrees to help them with [Gibral](/gibral) [901, 904, 906].
*   **[Fiska Brothers](/fiska-brothers)**: [Crawl Fiska](/crawl-fiska), [Frank Fiska](/frank-fiska), and [Dutch Fiska](/dutch-fiska), three sturdy brothers recruited as new deckhands [830].
*   **[Benoch](/benoch)**: A half-orc bard recruited during [The Arena](/the-arena) trials [830].
*   **[Bailey](/bailey)**: The ship's dwarf carpenter who mounts the dragon skull figurehead [918].
*   **[Gibral](/gibral)**: The Goat's Head crime boss whom the party offended [904, 906].

## Locations Visited
*   **[Little Upara](/little-upara)**: The Taldan colony where the crew enjoys their downtime [894].

*   **[The Blood Wake](/the-blood-wake)**: The party's fast-sailing vessel, newly outfitted with a dragon skull figurehead [918, 921].
