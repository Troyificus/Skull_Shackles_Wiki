# [Skull and Shackles Episode 47: Whalebone Pilk and the Bell Clapper](/skull-and-shackles-episode-47-whalebone-pilk-and-the-bell-clapper)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 38 (night) - Day 39

## Plot Summary
The violent impact of [The Death Knell](/the-death-knell) ramming the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) sends several crew members tumbling across the wet deck [922]. As the green spectral fog rolls over the deck, a horde of bloated [Zombies](/zombies) swarms the vessel, initiating a brutal struggle for survival [922, 942].

[Lanny](/lanny) instantly stands up, enters a primal rage, and begins brutally wailing on the nearest zombie with his heavy [Maul](/maul) [923]. Taking point on the stern castle, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) draws his newly acquired magical [Longbow +1](/longbow-1) and fires a precise arrow into [Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk)'s skeletal shoulder for 11 piercing damage [924, 925]. Realizing they are heavily outnumbered, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) steps forward and unleashes a devastating level 4 [Lightning Bolt](/lightning-bolt) directly through the enemy lines [926]. The massive bolt of black lightning crackles across the deck, dealing 28 lightning damage and completely vaporizing several zombies into ash [926].

Sensing a threat, [Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk) focuses his attention on [Desmond](/desmond). He reaches out with a glowing green spectral hand, draining [Desmond](/desmond)'s life essence, dealing heavy necrotic damage, and leaving the monk grappled and suffering a level of exhaustion [928, 930]. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) fires another well-placed arrow to chip away at [Pilk](/pilk)'s spectral form [929]. Seizing the moment, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) uses [Misty Step](/misty-step) to teleport onto [The Death Knell](/the-death-knell)'s deck, casting [Thormaturgy](/thormaturgy) to amplify her voice and command the crew to secure the vessel [929, 934].

Desperate to free himself, [Desmond](/desmond) channels his monk abilities to deliver a flurry of precise strikes [933]. In a dramatic show of martial arts, [Desmond](/desmond) grapples [Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk), pulls him close, and lands a colossal fist strike that punches a head-sized hole directly through [Pilk](/pilk)'s skull [933]. With a loud crack of bone, [Pilk](/pilk)'s spectral form disintegrates into ash [933, 935]. Instantly, the remaining zombies on deck collapse into lifeless heaps, and [The Death Knell](/the-death-knell) begins to rapidly splinter and sink into the ocean [933, 934].

As [The Death Knell](/the-death-knell) goes down, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) notices its prominent, glowing ship's [Bell](/bell) [934]. She shouts for the crew to take their injured and retreat [934, 935]. Before the ship sinks completely, [Lanny](/lanny) rushes forward and uses his raw strength to help [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) rip the heavy magical [Bell](/bell) from its wooden mountings [934, 936, 943]. [Desmond](/desmond) grabs a femur bone from [Pilk](/pilk)'s disintegrating remains as a trophy before leaping back onto the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) [935-936].

With the ghost ship fully submerged, the crew celebrates their historic victory on deck with well-deserved rounds of rum [937, 943]. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) embraces [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) in a tight, grateful hug, relieved that they all survived the encounter [937]. In the privacy of her quarters, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) detaches the [Clapper](/clapper) from the magical bell, wraps both pieces in thick cloth, and locks them away [943]. Realizing the bell is what bound [Pilk](/pilk)'s spirit to the mortal realm, she knows they must keep it secure [943]. The episode closes as [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) kneels before her altar, praying to [Myrkul](/myrkul) for guidance on how to harness and control these strange, ancient death magics [941].
