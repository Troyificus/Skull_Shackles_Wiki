# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 47: Whalebone Pilk and the Bell Clapper]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 38 (night) - Day 39

## Plot Summary
The violent impact of [[The Death Knell]] ramming the [[Blood Wake]] sends several crew members tumbling across the wet deck [922]. As the green spectral fog rolls over the deck, a horde of bloated [[Zombies]] swarms the vessel, initiating a brutal struggle for survival [922, 942].

[[Lanny]] instantly stands up, enters a primal rage, and begins brutally wailing on the nearest zombie with his heavy [[Maul]] [923]. Taking point on the stern castle, [[Devi McRae]] draws his newly acquired magical [[Longbow +1]] and fires a precise arrow into [[Whalebone Pilk]]'s skeletal shoulder for 11 piercing damage [924, 925]. Realizing they are heavily outnumbered, [[Torrin Horrendous]] steps forward and unleashes a devastating level 4 [[Lightning Bolt]] directly through the enemy lines [926]. The massive bolt of black lightning crackles across the deck, dealing 28 lightning damage and completely vaporizing several zombies into ash [926].

Sensing a threat, [[Whalebone Pilk]] focuses his attention on [[Desmond]]. He reaches out with a glowing green spectral hand, draining [[Desmond]]'s life essence, dealing heavy necrotic damage, and leaving the monk grappled and suffering a level of exhaustion [928, 930]. [[Devi McRae]] fires another well-placed arrow to chip away at [[Pilk]]'s spectral form [929]. Seizing the moment, [[Torrin Horrendous]] uses [[Misty Step]] to teleport onto [[The Death Knell]]'s deck, casting [[Thormaturgy]] to amplify her voice and command the crew to secure the vessel [929, 934].

Desperate to free himself, [[Desmond]] channels his monk abilities to deliver a flurry of precise strikes [933]. In a dramatic show of martial arts, [[Desmond]] grapples [[Whalebone Pilk]], pulls him close, and lands a colossal fist strike that punches a head-sized hole directly through [[Pilk]]'s skull [933]. With a loud crack of bone, [[Pilk]]'s spectral form disintegrates into ash [933, 935]. Instantly, the remaining zombies on deck collapse into lifeless heaps, and [[The Death Knell]] begins to rapidly splinter and sink into the ocean [933, 934].

As [[The Death Knell]] goes down, [[Torrin Horrendous]] notices its prominent, glowing ship's [[Bell]] [934]. She shouts for the crew to take their injured and retreat [934, 935]. Before the ship sinks completely, [[Lanny]] rushes forward and uses his raw strength to help [[Torrin Horrendous]] rip the heavy magical [[Bell]] from its wooden mountings [934, 936, 943]. [[Desmond]] grabs a femur bone from [[Pilk]]'s disintegrating remains as a trophy before leaping back onto the [[Blood Wake]] [935-936].

With the ghost ship fully submerged, the crew celebrates their historic victory on deck with well-deserved rounds of rum [937, 943]. [[Devi McRae]] embraces [[Sandara Quinn]] in a tight, grateful hug, relieved that they all survived the encounter [937]. In the privacy of her quarters, [[Torrin Horrendous]] detaches the [[Clapper]] from the magical bell, wraps both pieces in thick cloth, and locks them away [943]. Realizing the bell is what bound [[Pilk]]'s spirit to the mortal realm, she knows they must keep it secure [943]. The episode closes as [[Torrin Horrendous]] kneels before her altar, praying to [[Myrkul]] for guidance on how to harness and control these strange, ancient death magics [941].
