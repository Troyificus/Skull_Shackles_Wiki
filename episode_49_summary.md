# [Skull and Shackles Episode 49: Shattered Masts and a Dead Cabin Boy](/skull-and-shackles-episode-49-shattered-masts-and-a-dead-cabin-boy)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 1 following the previous session's events.

## Plot Summary
The day begins with immediate internal crew drama. The goliath enforcer [Albert](/albert) catches the young cabin boy [Dutch](/dutch) stealing food from [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)'s kitchen store. [Albert](/albert) drags [Dutch](/dutch) onto the deck of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) and throws him before [Lanny](/lanny) (acting as [Captain Skull Crusher](/captain-skull-crusher)) for judgment. After a brief deliberation with [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), [Lanny](/lanny) decides to let the terrified boy off with a stern warning: if [Dutch](/dutch) is caught stealing again, the party will cut off one of his fingers as punishment. [Albert](/albert) is deeply unsatisfied with this lenient sentence, grumbling that on his old pirate ship, the boy would have received three lashes at the mast.

With the internal dispute settled, the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) continues its voyage. In the late afternoon, [Desmond](/desmond) (going by [Fist](/fist)) spots a [Rahadoumi Shooner](/rahadoumi-shooner) on the horizon. Believing it to be a rich merchant vessel, [Lanny](/lanny) orders the crew to give chase. Using a combination of tactical sails coordination, prayers from [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn), and [Desmond](/desmond)'s acrobatic maneuvers in the rigging, the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) swiftly closes the distance to a mere 100 feet.

Sensing the imminent boarding, the [Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi) crew opens a large metal cage on their deck and releases two massive, red-coloured [Manticores](/manticores). The winged beasts take flight and immediately attack the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake). A fierce battle commences. [Desmond](/desmond) unleashes crackling beams of [Eldritch Blast](/eldritch-blast) at the creatures, while the [Manticores](/manticores) retaliate by firing toxic spikes that strike [Lanny](/lanny).

As the ships draw alongside each other, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) uses her magical ability [Misty Step](/misty-step) to teleport directly onto the enemy deck. She materializes behind a guard and casts a critical [Shocking Grasp](/shocking-grasp), instantly cooking him from the inside out. [Lanny](/lanny) follows her, leaping onto the enemy ship and unleashing two devastating swings of his [Maul](/maul) to slaughter one of the [Manticores](/manticores). [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) then channels a powerful [Lightning Bolt](/lightning-bolt) that tears through the deck, vaporizing two more guards and forcing [Torrin](/torrin) to demand that the remaining crew "kneel or die."

Completely overwhelmed, the remaining [Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi) guards and their female captain surrender. The [Blood Wake Crew](/blood-wake-crew) begins boarding the vessel to secure the cargo and prisoners. However, the celebration is cut short when [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) discovers [Dutch](/dutch) sitting lifelessly in the shadows under the stairs with a [Manticore Spike](/manticore-spike) driven directly through his neck. [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) attempts to heal him, but it is too late—[Dutch](/dutch) has already passed away.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Dutch](/dutch)**: The young cabin boy of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) who is pardoned for kitchen theft but is tragically killed by an errant [Manticore Spike](/manticore-spike).
*   **[Albert](/albert)** / **[Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert)**: The imposing goliath enforcer who advocates for harsher discipline but respects the captain's final ruling.
*   **[Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)**: The ship's cook whose kitchen stores were raided by [Dutch](/dutch).
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: The ship's cleric who tragically arrives too late to heal [Dutch](/dutch).
*   **[Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi)**: The faction of merchant sailors and guards defending the targeted shooner.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Blood Wake](/blood-wake)**: The party's main ship.
*   **[Rahadoumi Shooner](/rahadoumi-shooner)**: The captured merchant vessel boarded and occupied by the party.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[The Battle of the Rahadoumi Shooner](/the-battle-of-the-rahadoumi-shooner)**: A brutal high-seas confrontation against two flying [Manticores](/manticores) and [Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi) defenders, ending in a hard-won victory.
