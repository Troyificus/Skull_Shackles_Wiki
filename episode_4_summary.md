# [Skull and Shackles Episode 4: Arming For Mutiny In The Bilges](/skull-and-shackles-episode-4-arming-for-mutiny-in-the-bilges)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 3

## Plot Summary
Following his hard-won victory against [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert), [Desmond](/desmond) collects his prize money from [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) and generously distributes shares of the gold among his companions. Armed with his newfound funds, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) makes a stealthy trip to [Quartermaster Grook](/quartermaster-grook)'s store. There, he successfully bargains to purchase back [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)'s beloved, confiscated [Fiddle](/fiddle). Rosie is absolutely overjoyed by this act of kindness and pledges her steadfast loyalty, giving the party a crucial ally for their future plans.

Meanwhile, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) has a private conversation with [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) in the crew's sleeping quarters. They discuss the worship of the gods, with Torrin revealing her devotion to [Myrkul](/myrkul), a dark and lesser-known deity connected to death and the dead. On deck, [Lanny](/lanny) spends time socializing, having a conversation with [Fedor](/fedor) and the halfling rigger to sound them out about a possible mutiny, building bonds of trust.

The following morning, the ship is hit with foul weather. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) struggles heavily with the choppy seas and becomes severely seasick. The relative morning calm is shattered when [Mahim](/mahim), a crewman wearing a red-and-white bandana, stumbles onto the deck with his arm severely mangled and bleeding. He claims he was working the pumps in the bilges when a "giant red creature" bit him, warning that multiple monsters are lurking below.

Seeing an opportunity to test the party, [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) orders [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), [Desmond](/desmond), [Lanny](/lanny), and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) down into the dank, flooded bilges to clear out the infestation. 

Descending into the waist-deep bilge water, the party navigates the dark, claustrophobic environment. Instead of deep-sea aberrations, they find themselves fighting a horde of aggressive [Giant Rats](/giant-rats). They clear the bilges without serious injury. While sloshing through the water, [Lanny](/lanny) discovers a discarded, slightly dented metal [Buckler](/buckler).

As the party climbs back up the ladder hauling the dead rat carcasses, they emerge onto the deck to find the weather has deteriorated dramatically. The rain is blinding, the waves are crashing violently over the rails, and the ship is in the grip of a ferocious storm. [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) immediately barks orders for them to drop their tools and climb into the rigging to secure the thrashing sails.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)**: First mate of the [Wormwood](/wormwood) who confiscates weapons and orders the party into the dangerous bilges and storm-tossed rigging.
*   **[Quartermaster Grook](/quartermaster-grook)** (aka **[Grok](/grok)**): The superstitious half-orc quartermaster who runs the ship's store and sells [Rosie Cusswell's Fiddle](/rosie-cusswells-fiddle) back to [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae).
*   **[Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)**: The muscular halfling who becomes a loyal ally after [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) returns her fiddle.
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: The cleric of [Besmara](/besmara) who counsels [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) about religion and warns her about the dark nature of her magic.
*   **[Fedor](/fedor)**: A crewman on deck whom [Lanny](/lanny) speaks with to gauge his potential support for a mutiny.
*   **[Mahim](/mahim)**: The crewman who is bitten in the bilges, initiating the party's dungeon-crawl below deck.

## Locations Visited
*   The **[Wormwood](/wormwood)**:
    *   **[Quartermaster Grook's Store](/quartermaster-grooks-store)**: Where [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) conducts his business.
    *   **[The Sleeping Quarters](/the-sleeping-quarters)**: Where [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) and [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) discuss the gods and Torrin performs blood magic.
    *   **[The Bilges](/the-bilges)**: The dark, water-logged bottom of the ship where the party fights the giant rats.
    *   **[The Main Deck / Rigging](/the-main-deck-/-rigging)**: Where the party emerges into the full force of a massive tropical storm.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[The Party](/the-party) vs. [Giant Rats](/giant-rats)**: A soggy skirmish in the flooded bilges of the [Wormwood](/wormwood). The party quickly dispatches the rats, and [Lanny](/lanny) recovers a shield.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Rosie Cusswell's Fiddle](/rosie-cusswells-fiddle)**: Recovered from the ship's store and returned to Rosie to secure her alliance.
*   **[Rusty Metal Buckler](/rusty-metal-buckler)**: A small metal shield found by [Lanny](/lanny) at the bottom of the bilges.
