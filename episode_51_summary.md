# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 51: Blind Rogues and Barbarian Rage]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 26 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The crew of the **[[Blood Wake]]** is ambushed during a foggy night watch when tattered, feral humanoids climb aboard using grappling hooks. **[[Desmond]]** (aka **[[Dez]]** or **[[Fist]]**) and **[[Lanny]]** (aka **[[Captain Skull Crusher]]**) rush to defend the ship as **[[Torrin Horrendous]]** (aka **[[Torrin]]** or **[[Lady Blackheart]]**) emerges from the quarters to command her undead **[[Skeletons]]** to repel the boarders. During the chaotic battle, her skeleton companion **[[Blue Boy]]** is destroyed.

**[[Lanny]]** enters a primal **[[Rage]]** and uses his heavy **[[Maul]]** to crush the invaders. A massive, dirty opponent wielding a magical **[[Spiked Gauntlet]]** thuds onto the deck and relentlessly attacks **[[Lanny]]**. Suddenly, a floating female spellcaster appears over the rails, crackling with green energy, and unleashes a devastating **[[Cone of Cold]]**. The ice shards freeze **[[Torrin]]** and **[[Dez]]** instantly, knocking them unconscious and stable, while destroying another skeleton.

**[[Lanny]]**, still in a furious rage, super-saiyans (his eyes turning pitch black) and completely crushes the spiked-gauntlet leader before pile-driving the female spellcaster into the deck, successfully ending the deadly clash. The rest of the crew finally bursts onto deck, and **[[Sandara Quinn]]** administers healing to **[[Dez]]** and **[[Torrin]]**. Upon investigating the dead spellcaster, **[[Devi McRae]]** (aka **[[Devi]]** or **[[Crimson Cray]]**) discovers a hidden treasure map tattooed across her back, which depicts a fabled cache belonging to the legendary pirate king **[[Cyrus Wolfe]]**. They also salvage six identical glass bottles filled with mysterious liquid from a nearby rowboat.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Feral Pirates]]**: The tattered, aggressive invaders who boarded the ship.
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: Cleric who heals the fallen officers.
*   **[[Cyrus Wolfe]]**: The legendary pirate king whose map was recovered.
*   **[[Myrkul]]**: The dark god of the dead to whom **[[Torrin]]** prays.
*   **[[Besmara]]**: The pirate goddess whose power now flows through **[[Dez]]**.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Blood Wake]]**: The party's flagship, currently sailing the open seas.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[Midnight Fog Ambush]]**: An intense, high-stakes boarding combat against **[[Feral Pirates]]** and their spellcaster leader, featuring a freezing **[[Cone of Cold]]** and Lanny's final crushing blows.

*   **[[Cyrus Wolfe's Skin Map]]**: The treasure map of **[[Mancatcher Cove]]** tattooed on the spellcaster's back.
