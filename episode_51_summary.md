# [Skull and Shackles Episode 51: Blind Rogues and Barbarian Rage](/skull-and-shackles-episode-51-blind-rogues-and-barbarian-rage)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 26 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The crew of the **[Blood Wake](/blood-wake)** is ambushed during a foggy night watch when tattered, feral humanoids climb aboard using grappling hooks. **[Desmond](/desmond)** (aka **[Dez](/dez)** or **[Fist](/fist)**) and **[Lanny](/lanny)** (aka **[Captain Skull Crusher](/captain-skull-crusher)**) rush to defend the ship as **[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)** (aka **[Torrin](/torrin)** or **[Lady Blackheart](/lady-blackheart)**) emerges from the quarters to command her undead **[Skeletons](/skeletons)** to repel the boarders. During the chaotic battle, her skeleton companion **[Blue Boy](/blue-boy)** is destroyed.

**[Lanny](/lanny)** enters a primal **[Rage](/rage)** and uses his heavy **[Maul](/maul)** to crush the invaders. A massive, dirty opponent wielding a magical **[Spiked Gauntlet](/spiked-gauntlet)** thuds onto the deck and relentlessly attacks **[Lanny](/lanny)**. Suddenly, a floating female spellcaster appears over the rails, crackling with green energy, and unleashes a devastating **[Cone of Cold](/cone-of-cold)**. The ice shards freeze **[Torrin](/torrin)** and **[Dez](/dez)** instantly, knocking them unconscious and stable, while destroying another skeleton.

**[Lanny](/lanny)**, still in a furious rage, super-saiyans (his eyes turning pitch black) and completely crushes the spiked-gauntlet leader before pile-driving the female spellcaster into the deck, successfully ending the deadly clash. The rest of the crew finally bursts onto deck, and **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)** administers healing to **[Dez](/dez)** and **[Torrin](/torrin)**. Upon investigating the dead spellcaster, **[Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)** (aka **[Devi](/devi)** or **[Crimson Cray](/crimson-cray)**) discovers a hidden treasure map tattooed across her back, which depicts a fabled cache belonging to the legendary pirate king **[Cyrus Wolfe](/cyrus-wolfe)**. They also salvage six identical glass bottles filled with mysterious liquid from a nearby rowboat.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Feral Pirates](/feral-pirates)**: The tattered, aggressive invaders who boarded the ship.
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: Cleric who heals the fallen officers.
*   **[Cyrus Wolfe](/cyrus-wolfe)**: The legendary pirate king whose map was recovered.
*   **[Myrkul](/myrkul)**: The dark god of the dead to whom **[Torrin](/torrin)** prays.
*   **[Besmara](/besmara)**: The pirate goddess whose power now flows through **[Dez](/dez)**.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Blood Wake](/blood-wake)**: The party's flagship, currently sailing the open seas.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[Midnight Fog Ambush](/midnight-fog-ambush)**: An intense, high-stakes boarding combat against **[Feral Pirates](/feral-pirates)** and their spellcaster leader, featuring a freezing **[Cone of Cold](/cone-of-cold)** and Lanny's final crushing blows.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Spiked Gauntlets](/spiked-gauntlets)**: Magical gauntlets salvaged from the **[Feral Pirates](/feral-pirates)** leader, granting a **[+1 to hit and damage](/1-to-hit-and-damage)**.
*   **[Cyrus Wolfe's Skin Map](/cyrus-wolfes-skin-map)**: The treasure map of **[Mancatcher Cove](/mancatcher-cove)** tattooed on the spellcaster's back.
*   **[Six Glass Bottles](/six-glass-bottles)**: Potion bottles salvaged from the rowboat.
