# [Skull and Shackles Episode 57: Rage Drinking, Fish Sludge, and Underwater Skulls](/skull-and-shackles-episode-57-rage-drinking-fish-sludge-and-underwater-skulls)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Days 31 to 35 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The session opens with [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) recovering from her terrifying near-death experience. Guided by her friends, she officially renounces her dark patron [Myrkul](/myrkul), destroying her skull necklace with [Shocking Grasp](/shocking-grasp), and accepts the divine light and path of [Besmara](/besmara) [1134, 1135, 1155](/1134-1135-1155). As a symbol of her new alignment, her spellcasting lightning changes from an ominous black to a brilliant, pure white [1135, 1155](/1135-1155). She has [Lanny](/lanny) throw the cursed [Golden Twin-Plated Dagger](/golden-twin-plated-dagger) into the sea, where she completely vaporises it with a high-level white [Chromatic Orb](/chromatic-orb) [1135, 1155, 1156](/1135-1155-1156).

The crew sails to [Genzai](/genzai) in the [Ushinawa Isles](/ushinawa-isles) [1135, 1156](/1135-1156). Here, they sell their captured prize ship and accumulated plunder for a massive gold payout [1135, 1156](/1135-1156). [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) (operating under his new moniker, [Crimson Cray](/crimson-cray)) visits the local monastery to safely offload the 20 to 30 rescued slave children, making a generous donation of [5 platinum pieces](/5-platinum-pieces) and [68 gold pieces](/68-gold-pieces), alongside a [70 gold pieces](/70-gold-pieces) bag given to them by the children [1135, 1147, 1148, 1156](/1135-1147-1148-1156). 

Meanwhile, [Desmond](/desmond) (now known as [Fist](/fist)) drinks his completed experimental brew, [Scale Schlager](/scale-schlager) (aka [Fish Scale Gold Schlager](/fish-scale-gold-schlager)), which successfully brews but causes painful internal cramping [1136, 1142](/1136-1142). [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) visit a local magic shop run by a merchant of [Tian](/tian) descent [1137](/1137). They purchase a [Brooch of Shielding](/brooch-of-shielding) (granting force damage resistance and magic missile immunity) and [Dust of Invisibility](/dust-of-invisibility), while [Lanny](/lanny) buys a [Bag of Holding](/bag-of-holding) [1143, 1144](/1143-1144). [Lanny](/lanny) tests the bag's magic, pulling out a wooden ladder, rope, steel mirror, 10-foot pole, and a sack [1145](/1145). He also sells his old leather armor for [5 gold pieces](/5-gold-pieces) [1141](/1141).

[Lanny](/lanny) consults a local cartographer to translate [Cyrus Wolfe's Skin Map](/cyrus-wolfes-skin-map), which identifies the island cluster north of them as the fabled [Mancatcher Cove](/mancatcher-cove) [1135, 1150](/1135-1150). They enjoy a dinner of sake (rice wine) before realizing the next morning that [Janny](/janny) has left the crew [1152, 1153](/1152-1153). [Torrin](/torrin) casts [Sending](/sending) on her; [Janny](/janny) replies she has joined another vessel and wishes them well [1154](/1154). The crew sets sail for [Mancatcher Cove](/mancatcher-cove), anchoring offshore on the West Side by nightfall [1156, 1157, 1158](/1156-1157-1158). 

They decode the map's riddle: *"From Blue Blight's Embrace, spy the grave lady's prized tooth with the dawn flower's first kiss, climb the captain's Wayward orb to claim old King's horde"* [1158, 1169](/1158-1169). [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) uses his spyglass to view beachy outcrops, and [Torrin](/torrin) suggests sailing around the North Edge into the central cove [1159, 1160](/1159-1160). They enter the deep, indigo-hued bay, and [Lanny](/lanny) climbs down a ladder into the water to explore, ending the episode on a cliffhanger [1161, 1162, 1163](/1161-1162-1163).

## Key NPCs & Factions

*   **[Orphanage Headmaster](/orphanage-headmaster)**: The monk of [Tian](/tian) descent who accepts custody of the rescued slave children [1146, 1147](/1146-1147).
*   **[Crawl Fiska](/crawl-fiska) & [Frank Fiska](/frank-fiska)**: Rigger brothers who advise [Lanny](/lanny) on excellent southern fishing grounds [1149](/1149).
*   **[Cyrus Wolfe](/cyrus-wolfe)**: The legendary pirate king whose hidden treasure horde the party seeks [1150, 1158](/1150-1158).
*   **[Pharasma](/pharasma)** (aka the **[Lady of Graves](/lady-of-graves)** or **[Raven Queen](/raven-queen)**): The deity referenced in the skin map's riddle as the "grave lady" [1169](/1169).
*   **[Besmara](/besmara)**: The pirate goddess whose path the party officially aligns with [1135, 1155](/1135-1155).
