# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 58: Shark Cavalry and the Greased Barbarian]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 36

## Plot Summary
While anchored in the cove, the crew is alerted to an underwater threat. [[Torrin Horrendous]] casts [[Thaumaturgy]] to sound a warning bell, and [[Devi McRae]] scales the mast to the [[Crow's Nest]] with his [[Longbow]] [[1165, 1166]]. Three [[Sahuagin]] ("fishy fuckers") are spotted under the water, apparently drawn to the [[Deep Platinum Pendant]] worn by [[Lanny]] [[1167]]. [[Lanny]] walks the rails with his extendable lance to secure the deck [[1168]]. 

They determine that they must wait for the sun to rise to locate the path to the treasure, as indicated by the riddle's reference to the "grave lady" [[Pharasma]] (the Lady of Graves) [[1169, 1170]]. The party plans to explore the smaller islands clockwise and bring [[Owlbear Albert]] and [[Sandara Quinn]] along in a rowboat to keep an eye on [[Albert]]'s behavior [[1171, 1172, 1173, 1174]]. [[Desmond]] uses persuasion to speak with [[Albert]], who reveals his original captain died and was buried outside of the shackles [[1175, 1176]]. Before leaving, [[Torrin Horrendous]] casts [[Animate Dead]] to reassert control over her [[Zombies]] [[1176]].

After a full day of searching the smaller islands clockwise, they find nothing and return to the [[Blood Wake]] for the night [[1177]]. At dawn, the rising sun passes directly between two islands, casting a warm ray of light. [[Devi McRae]] and [[Lanny]] witness a bright glint reflecting off a rock face deep inside the cove [[1178]]. They identify the glint is coming from a sheer, 100-foot cliff. The riddle's instruction to *"climb the captain's Wayward orb"* points them to an eye-like cave opening on the rock face [[1179]].

They pack the [[Steadfast Grapple]] and decide to bring four [[Zombies]] as expendable front-line fodder [[1180]]. They set off in two rowboats, utilizing [[Sandara Quinn]]'s magical [[Tricorn Hat]] boat [[1182, 1183]]. [[Devi McRae]] climbs the cliffside first. Inside a crevice, he discovers a massive, unformed chunk of gold ([[Gold Deposit]]) that had caused the sunrise reflection [[1184]]. At the cliff's peak, they locate two narrow, five-foot-wide tunnel openings in the rock (one left, one right) and a rotten wooden stump where an old beam was once wedged [[1184, 1186]]. Using his spyglass, [[Devi McRae]] checks back on the [[Blood Wake]] anchored offshore [[1187]].

They choose the left tunnel first. Inside, [[Torrin Horrendous]] finds a narrow choke point and a large tree with roots shaped like a bearded, grimacing face [[1188]]. [[Lanny]] uses his massive strength to force his way through the tree roots [[1190]]. Using his [[Maul]], he smashes through a wooden plank floor hidden beneath the dirt. A plank shatters and falls down a dark shaft, followed by the sound of a distant splash below [[1191]].
