# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 59: Sahugin Ambush and the Shackled Prisoner]]
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 36 (continues immediately after Ep 58)

## Plot Summary
Staring down into the dark abyss of the shattered deck, the party prepares to descend [[1194]]. [[Desmond]] dives down the flooded shaft first. While exploring underwater, his [[Eldritch Blast]] illuminates the dark water, revealing a [[Sahuagin]] warrior [[1195]]. [[Lanny]] joins him underwater, entering a [[Rage]] and reckless-attacking a [[Sahuagin]] with his [[Maul]] to deal **29 damage**, instantly pulverising it into a "Red Mist" against the tunnel wall [[1199]]. He then strikes the second one for 13 damage [[1199]].

[[Devi McRae]] slides past [[Lanny]] and uses [[Insightful Fighting]] against the second [[Sahuagin]] [[1200]]. He stabs it with his magical rapier, [[Brine's Sting]] (aka [[Brine Sting]]), for **27 damage**, killing it instantly [[1200]]. A third [[Sahuagin]] attacks [[Lanny]] with a spear and bites him, but [[Lanny]] reckless-attacks again, delivering **13** and **16 damage** to finish off the remaining attackers [[1201, 1202]]. One of the [[Sahuagin]] misses [[Devi]] with a natural 1, wedging its spear in the ceiling before [[Lanny]] squishes it [[1203]].

[[Torrin Horrendous]] writes a warning note on parchment referencing "sea Goblins" and sends her bat familiar [[Zima]] back to the rowboat crew [[1197]]. They explore the flooded tunnels. The left path is a dead end, but the right path slopes up at a 45-degree angle [[1204]]. Breaking the surface, they find a damp, air-filled pocket containing rotted, broken furniture [[1205, 1206]].

Proceeding further, [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] discover a small underwater armory littered with weaponry [[1207, 1208]]. A hidden [[Giant Crab]] (aka [[Grind Crab]]) ambushes them [[1209]]. During the scuffle, [[Desmond]] skillfully parries, forcing the crab's claws to stab itself in its own face until it dies [[1210]]. [[Desmond]] returns to the air pocket to breathe, while [[Lanny]] wonders if they can safely open their [[Bag of Holding]] underwater [[1210]].

[[Devi McRae]] swims ahead and spots a warm, orange-pink glint coming from a deep chamber [[1211]]. Entering the chamber, they discover a bizarre scene: dozens of strange organic filaments running floor-to-ceiling, holding about 50 floating, fist-sized pink egg-like ovoids that twitch with movement [[1211, 1212]]. Looking down a narrow tunnel at the rear of the chamber, they find a fish-like humanoid with crested fins and orange coloration shackled to the far wall. The session ends on a high-stakes cliffhanger as they realize it is *not* a [[Sahuagin]] [[1213]]!

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Sahuagin]]**: The sea-dwelling warriors guarding the flooded tunnels of the cove [[1195, 1198]].
*   **[[Giant Crab]]** (aka the **[[Grind Crab]]**): A massive crab disguised among the armory weaponry that ambushes [[Desmond]] and [[Lanny]] [[1209]].
*   **[[Zima]]**: Torrin's bat familiar used to ferry messages back to the surface party [[1197]].
*   **[[Shackled Prisoner]]**: A mysterious, orange-scaled, crested-finned aquatic humanoid bound to the wall of the back chamber [[1213]].
*   **[[Zombies]]**: Torrin's undead thralls waiting at the entrance [[1207]].

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Flooded Tunnels]]**: The dark, seaweed-choked passages beneath the shattered deck planks [[1195, 1196, 1204]].
*   **[[The Armory]]**: A silted underwater chamber filled with old weapons where the crab lay in wait [[1208, 1209]].
*   **[[The Nesting Chamber]]**: The deep cavern filled with strange organic filaments and floating pink egg ovoids [[1211]].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[[Flooded Tunnel Skirmish]]**: An intense underwater clash against three [[Sahuagin]] warriors, featuring Lanny's reckless maul strikes and Devi's precise thrusts with [[Brine's Sting]] [[1199, 1200]].
*   **[[Grind Crab Ambush]]**: A quick, claustrophobic melee in the armory where [[Desmond]] forces the crab to fatally stab itself [[1210]].

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[[Sahuagin Weapons]]**: Crude spears and tridents recovered from the defeated tunnel guards [[1209]].
