# [Skull and Shackles Episode 5: Hurricane Winds And Deadly Reefclaws](/skull-and-shackles-episode-5-hurricane-winds-and-deadly-reefclaws)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Days 4-5

## Plot Summary
The storm hits the [Wormwood](/wormwood) with catastrophic force, throwing the deck into absolute chaos. While the twins [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) and [Desmond](/desmond) fight to pull in the main sail, [Lanny](/lanny) and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) are ordered by [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) to secure the loose barrels sliding wildly across the deck. Working under intense pressure, [Lanny](/lanny) and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) manage to secure the shifting cargo just as a massive rogue wave crashes over the deck, sweeping [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) clean overboard. 

[Lanny](/lanny) prepares to dive into the raging ocean, but is convinced to throw a rope instead. Working together with [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous), [Lanny](/lanny) manages to pull [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) back on deck, saving her from drowning. 

The storm eventually subsides by evening, leaving the exhausted crew to collapse on deck. The next morning brings glorious sunshine and calm seas. The crew uses the opportunity to set up an impromptu bath on deck using an old sail, washing themselves and their clothes.

The respite is short-lived. [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) arrives to delegate chores, ordering the party to swim out to a nearby [Coral Reef](/coral-reef) to catch crabs in pots for [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan)'s evening feast. 

While filling the pots on the reef, the party is suddenly ambushed by a pair of predatory [Reefclaws](/reefclaws). [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) takes the brunt of the initial attack, being brutally clawed and bitten until he falls bleeding and unconscious into the water. [Desmond](/desmond) rushes to his twin's aid, dragging [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)'s limp body back to the boat. 

The party returns to the ship with their catch, where a very concerned [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) administers divine healing to stabilize [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae). The session ends in a messy muddle—with half of the party on the ship, half on the reef, and the crab pots only partially filled—leaving [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) absolutely furious.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)**: First mate of the [Wormwood](/wormwood) who supervises the storm operations and is left furious by the incomplete crab harvest.
*   **[Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan)**: The captain who demands a feast of fresh crabs, prompting the dangerous reef excursion.
*   **[Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)**: The halfling rigger who is swept overboard in the storm and saved by [Lanny](/lanny) and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous).
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: The cleric of [Besmara](/besmara) who watches over the party and administers critical healing to the wounded [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae).

## Locations Visited
*   The **[Wormwood](/wormwood)**:
    *   **[The Main Deck](/the-main-deck)**: Where [Lanny](/lanny) and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) secure the barrels and rescue [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) from the sea.
    *   **[The Galley](/the-galley)**: Where the crew delivers the captured crabs to [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop).
*   The **[Coral Reef](/coral-reef)**: A nearby shallow reef where the party is ordered to harvest crabs.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[The Party](/the-party) vs. [Reefclaws](/reefclaws)**: An ambush at the [Coral Reef](/coral-reef). The vicious, lobster-like [Reefclaws](/reefclaws) severely wound and knock [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) unconscious. [Desmond](/desmond) and [Lanny](/lanny) successfully fight them off, saving Devi.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Crabs and Oysters](/crabs-and-oysters)**: A partial harvest of crabs and oysters from the reef to satisfy [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan)'s culinary demands.
