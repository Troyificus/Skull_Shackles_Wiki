# [Skull and Shackles Episode 60: Punching Sharks and One-Shotting the Boss](/skull-and-shackles-episode-60-punching-sharks-and-one-shotting-the-boss)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 30 to Day 32 (Season 1 Finale)

## Plot Summary
The epic season finale begins deep within the flooded caverns of **[Mancatcher Cove](/mancatcher-cove)**. The four heroes, accompanied by **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**, examine the **[Shackled Prisoner](/shackled-prisoner)** they discovered at the end of the previous episode. The prisoner is revealed to be a vivid orange-and-blue **[Locathah](/locathah)**—an aquatic humanoid with large round eyes and fat lips, who has been brutally whipped and left for dead by the **[Sahuagin](/sahuagin)** [1226]. To help collect any items, **[Lanny](/lanny)** (aka **[Captain Skull Crusher](/captain-skull-crusher)**) uses his **[Cloak of Patches](/cloak-of-patches)**, detaching the **[Sack Patch](/sack-patch)** which instantly transforms into a real canvas sack [1227].

### The Feeding Frenzy
As the party advances, **[Lanny](/lanny)** leads them into a massive central chamber where a "shiver" of **[Sahuagin](/sahuagin)** are engaged in a wild **[Feeding Frenzy](/feeding-frenzy)** on raw meat [1228]. **[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)** (aka **[Lady Blackheart](/lady-blackheart)**) unleashes a massive, high-level life-draining spell (similar to *Life Transference* or a necrotic area siphon), drawing dark energy from the feeding crowd to deal **[9 necrotic damage](/9-necrotic-damage)** to the combatants while instantly healing **[Lanny](/lanny)** back to full health [1229, 1230, 1231]. 

A chaotic melee breaks out. **[Desmond](/desmond)** (aka **[Dez](/dez)** or **[Fist](/fist)**) leaps forward, punching several Sahuagin with **[Flurry of Blows](/flurry-of-blows)** [1232]. An older, bloated **[Sahuagin](/sahuagin)** priest wielding a **[Coral Staff](/coral-staff)** emerges from behind a seaweed curtain and casts **[Mass Healing Word](/mass-healing-word)** to revive the fallen defenders [1232, 1233], allowing a revived warrior to stab **[Desmond](/desmond)** with a spear for **[10 piercing damage](/10-piercing-damage)** [1234]. Enraged, **[Lanny](/lanny)** enters a primal rage and recklesly slams his **[Maul](/maul)** into the bloated priest, dealing a staggering **[31 points of damage](/31-points-of-damage)** and crushing the spellcaster against the cavern wall [1235]. **[Desmond](/desmond)** and **[Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)** (aka **[Crimson Cray](/crimson-cray)**) quickly clean up the remaining combatants, clearing the chamber [1236, 1237].

### Clearing the Barracks & The Shark Shiver
While **[Desmond](/desmond)** takes a quick breather in a nearby air pocket, **[Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)** uses his rapier to intimidate the cowering non-combatants, ensuring they do not interfere [1238]. Exploring the adjacent private quarters, **[Torrin](/torrin)** finds a **[String of Pearls](/string-of-pearls)** and a valuable **[Soapstone Statuette](/soapstone-statuette)** [1239]. They proceed through a **[Barracks](/barracks)** room containing rusted weapons and set their **[Zombies](/zombies)** to guard the rear tunnels [1240, 1241] before swimming into a deep pool where a "shiver" of three **[Hammerhead Sharks](/hammerhead-sharks)** awaits them [1242].

**[Torrin](/torrin)** kicks off the battle by casting **[Chromatic Orb](/chromatic-orb)** (lightning), dealing **[11 lightning damage](/11-lightning-damage)** [1243]. One of her loyal **[Skeletons](/skeletons)** is torn apart by a shark's teeth [1243, 1244, 1245]. **[Desmond](/desmond)** swims in and lands a stunning blow on one shark [1244], while **[Devi](/devi)** scores a critical strike with his magical rapier, **[Brine's Sting](/brines-sting)**, threading it directly through the eyes of a shark to kill it instantly [1248]. **[Lanny](/lanny)** smashes the final shark to pieces [1247].

### The Final Showdown
Following a long tunnel to the exit of the cave system, **[Lanny](/lanny)** discovers the throne room where the gargantuan **[Sahuagin Baron](/sahuagin-baron)** (the boss) stands on a raised altar wielding a massive double-ended trident [1250]. The Baron activates a lair action, causing thick tendrils of **[Seaweed](/seaweed)** to erupt from the floor to restrain **[Lanny](/lanny)**, but the goliath barbarians easily kicks them away [1250, 1251]. A Sahuagin minion scores a critical spear strike on **[Lanny](/lanny)** for **[13 piercing damage](/13-piercing-damage)** [1251].

**[Desmond](/desmond)** hexes the Sahuagin Baron [1252], and **[Devi](/devi)** follows up with an incredibly spectacular critical hit using **[Brine's Sting](/brines-sting)**, dealing a massive **[49 points of damage](/49-points-of-damage)** in a single thrust [1253]. **[Torrin](/torrin)** summons her **[Storm Sphere](/storm-sphere)** to zap the boss with **[16 lightning damage](/16-lightning-damage)** [1254], and her **[Zombies](/zombies)** slam him for another **[6 bludgeoning damage](/6-bludgeoning-damage)** [1256]. The Baron desperately stabs **[Devi](/devi)** for 19 damage, but **[Desmond](/desmond)** steps in and delivers a final, devastating punch to the back of the Baron's head, crushing his skull and ending the Sahuagin threat once and for all [1257, 1259].

### The Old King's Hoard
Inside the Sahuagin Baron's private chambers behind the throne, the party discovers a **[Stone Chest](/stone-chest)** and a pristine, magical wooden **[Chest of Holding](/chest-of-holding)** (which is far larger on the inside) [1260, 1261]. Inside the chests, they uncover **[Cyrus Wolfe's Old King's Hoard](/cyrus-wolfes-old-kings-hoard)** [1261]:
*   A magical **[Bone Figurehead](/bone-figurehead)** (scrimshawed bones designed to be mounted as a ship figurehead) [1261].
*   **[Magical Footwear](/magical-footwear)** [1261].
*   An ornate **[Deep Platinum Crown](/deep-platinum-crown)** worth **[5,000 gold pieces](/5000-gold-pieces)** [1261, 1262].
*   A small, exquisite **[Music Box](/music-box)** inscribed: *"For my darling Isabella"* [1262].
*   **[10 Points of Plunder](/10-points-of-plunder)** consisting of fine silks, exotic furs, spices, and raw gold/silver ore [1262].
*   Assorted coins worth a massive **[7,000 gold pieces](/7000-gold-pieces)** [1262].

With their hold overflowing with plunder, their pockets loaded with gold, and their names whispered in awe throughout the ports, the crew of the **[Blood Wake](/blood-wake)** sets their course back to **[Goat's Head](/goats-head)** to celebrate their incredible success, concluding **[Season 1](/season-1)** of their legendary high-seas adventures [1262, 1263].
