# [Skull and Shackles Episode 6: Botched Drills and a Bilge Murder](/skull-and-shackles-episode-6-botched-drills-and-a-bilge-murder)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 6 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Following their messy encounter with [Reefclaws](/reefclaws) on the reef, the crew of the [Wormwood](/wormwood) returns to the ship with their catch. [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) remains utterly unimpressed by the chaotic state of the landing party, ordering [Fedor](/fedor) to carry the captured [Reefclaws](/reefclaws) down to the galley. There, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) assists [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) (aka [Fishguts](/fishguts)) in butchering the creatures, noting that [Reefclaw](/reefclaw) meat is exceptionally succulent and highly prized as a delicacy.

In a rare move of positive reinforcement, [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) decides to reward the party for bringing back such a succulent treat. He overrides [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)'s objections and allows each member of the party to reclaim one personal item from [Quartermaster Grook](/quartermaster-grook)'s store. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) happily retrieves his fine [Rapier](/rapier), [Desmond](/desmond) recovers his first set of [Knuckle Dusters](/knuckle-dusters), [Lanny](/lanny) reclaims his [Satchel](/satchel) (containing his [Poncho](/poncho) and personal rations), and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) retrieves her personal [Dagger](/dagger). The party gathers below deck to celebrate with an unusually satisfying meal of boiled crabs and [Reefclaw](/reefclaw) prepared by [Fishguts](/fishguts).

The next morning, the brief respite ends. During daily inspections, [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) introduces [Rhiaris](/rhiaris), a fierce, peg-legged officer sporting a gold septum nose ring, who has been tasked with putting the new recruits through rigorous boarding drills. The party, along with [Conchobhar Shortstone](/conchobhar-shortstone), [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn), and [Jack Scrimshaw](/jack-scrimshaw), is forced to paddle a [Jolly Boat](/jolly-boat) out to a target vessel, throw [Grappling Hooks](/grappling-hooks), and climb up the hull. To simulate combat chaos, the resting crew pelt them with rotten food, garbage, and empty bottles. 

While [Lanny](/lanny) effortlessly scales the rope using raw strength, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) suffer embarrassing failures. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) completely loses her footing on the slick wooden rail and falls directly into the water below, landing in a thick, putrid slick of fresh pig blood thrown overboard to attract sharks. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) is heavily mocked by [Rhiaris](/rhiaris), who deems him "completely useless" after he slips into the sea multiple times.

The boarding drills are cut short when a large ship sits spotted on the horizon. Although [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) attempts to warn the captain of mysterious and unresolved "trouble below" deck (implying the uninvestigated death of [Jape](/jape)), [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) angrily silences him. The captain bellows that there is no time for domestic disputes, ordering [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) to immediately prepare the boarding teams.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan)**: The brutal captain of the [Wormwood](/wormwood) who rewards the party with their gear but prioritizes chasing down the sighted ship over crew problems.
*   **[Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)**: The first mate whose domestic warnings about the bilge are ignored by the captain.
*   **[Rhiaris](/rhiaris)**: A fierce, peg-legged officer with a gold septum ring who oversees the brutal boarding and climbing drills.
*   **[Grook](/grook)**: The half-orc [Quartermaster](/quartermaster) who reluctantly returns the party's confiscated gear.
*   **[Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)** (aka **[Fishguts](/fishguts)**): The ship's cook who enthusiastically prepares the [Reefclaw](/reefclaw) feast.
*   **[Fedor](/fedor)**: [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)'s lackey who carries the crab pots down to the galley.
*   **[Jape](/jape)**: A crewman whose corpse is discovered in the hold, raising unanswered questions.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Wormwood](/wormwood)**: Specifically the [Main Deck](/main-deck), [Galley](/galley), [Quartermaster Store](/quartermaster-store), and [Sleeping Quarters](/sleeping-quarters).
*   **[The Reef](/the-reef)**: The site where the landing party finished securing their crab pots.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[Boarding and Climbing Drills](/boarding-and-climbing-drills)**: A non-lethal but physically exhausting training exercise led by [Rhiaris](/rhiaris). The party faces high climbing DCs while being bombarded with garbage, resulting in [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) falling into bloody, shark-infested waters.
