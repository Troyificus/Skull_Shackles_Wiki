# [Skull and Shackles Episode 7: The Wizard Falls Into Shark Waters](/skull-and-shackles-episode-7-the-wizard-falls-into-shark-waters)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 7 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The [Wormwood](/wormwood) rapidly gains on the merchant ship on the horizon, which sits incredibly heavy in the water. As the crew readies for battle, [Rhiaris](/rhiaris) pulls [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), [Desmond](/desmond), [Lanny](/lanny), and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) aside. She assigns them a high-stakes mission: they are to grapple onto the enemy ship's [Stern Castle](/stern-castle), eliminate the guards on the upper deck, take control of the ship's wheel, and guard the jollyboats to prevent any enemy escape. 

Before the boarding begins, the party scrambles to prepare. [Desmond](/desmond) confronts [Master Scourge](/master-scourge), successfully reclaiming his second pair of [Knuckle Dusters](/knuckle-dusters). [Lanny](/lanny) descends into the dark bilges to retrieve his massive [Maul](/maul) from a hidden barrel, casually stepping around [Jape](/jape), who is slumped, unresponsive, and dead on the ladder. Believing [Jape](/jape) is merely sleeping off a hangover, [Lanny](/lanny) ignores him and heads back up.

Meanwhile, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) takes a calculated risk and approaches [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg). Using her high charisma and persuasion, she strikes a deal with him, convincing him to lend her a valuable [Diamond](/diamond) worth 50 gold pieces to serve as her spellcasting focus for [Chromatic Orb](/chromatic-orb). In return, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) promises to showcase her full magical prowess to aid [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)'s future ambitions. 

To create a distraction and draw away any predatory marine life, [Master Scourge](/master-scourge) orders [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) to fetch a dead pig carcass (the "piggy") from [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)'s galley. The party throws the bloody carcass overboard, creating a massive slick of pig blood in the water.

As they reach the target vessel, the [Wormwood](/wormwood)'s resident spellcaster, [Arita](/arita), unleashes a thick, magical [Fog](/fog) that heavily obscures vision on the deck. The [Wormwood](/wormwood) rams violently into the side of the merchant ship. [Lanny](/lanny) instantly enters a [Rage](/rage) and vaults over the railing onto the enemy deck. 

[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) attempts to follow, but catastrophically slips on the wet wooden rail. She falls 15 feet down into the narrow gap between the two grinding hulls, plunging directly into the putrid pool of pig blood. Clinging to the rope of [Lanny](/lanny)'s grappling hook for dear life, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) narrowly avoids the jaws of a approaching shark before [Lanny](/lanny) uses his immense goliath strength to hoist her back onto the deck with a single, mighty yank.

Once assembled on the [Stern Castle](/stern-castle), the party clashes with the guards. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) casts [Toll the Dead](/toll-the-dead), causing the ears and eyes of a guard to rupture with black blood. Amidst the chaos, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) spots a hidden [Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi) sailor preparing to ambush [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) from the fog. She uses [Message](/message) to alert the captain and casts [Toll the Dead](/toll-the-dead) on the assailant, allowing [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) to run the sailor through with his sword. The captain gives [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) a rare, approving nod.

The initial guards on the [Stern Castle](/stern-castle) are quickly decimated, but [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) spots a new threat through the parting mist: three merchant sailors at the ship's rail are frantically attempting to untie and launch a [Jolly Boat](/jolly-boat) to escape.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan)**: The captain of the [Wormwood](/wormwood), who kills an ambusher thanks to [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s warning.
*   **[Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)**: The first mate who is persuaded by [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) to lend her a precious gem.
*   **[Rhiaris](/rhiaris)**: The peg-legged boarding officer who assigns the party to capture the [Stern Castle](/stern-castle).
*   **[Master Scourge](/master-scourge)**: The boatswain who returns [Desmond](/desmond)'s brass knuckles and orders the pig carcass distraction.
*   **[Arita](/arita)**: The [Wormwood](/wormwood)'s spellcaster who shrouds the battlefield in a magical [Fog](/fog).
*   **[Jape](/jape)**: A crewman whose dead body is found on the bilge ladder by [Lanny](/lanny).
*   **[Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)** (aka **[Fishguts](/fishguts)**): The cook who provides the pig carcass.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Wormwood](/wormwood)**: The ship's deck and bilges.
*   **[Rahadoumi Merchant Ship](/rahadoumi-merchant-ship)**: The targeted vessel, specifically the [Stern Castle](/stern-castle).

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[The Siege of the Stern Castle](/the-siege-of-the-stern-castle)**: The party boards the merchant ship. [Lanny](/lanny) and [Desmond](/desmond) charge the defenders, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) survives a fall into shark-infested bloody water, and they successfully wipe out the first wave of guards.
