# [[Skull and Shackles Episode 9: Officer Blackheart and Plugg's Mutiny]]

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 15 (Two weeks passed aboard the [[Wormwood]])

## Plot Summary
The morning aboard the [[Wormwood]] begins with the daily morning inspection. Before heading up to the deck, [[Lanny]] shares a remarkable revelation with [[Desmond]], [[Devi McRae]], and [[Torrin Horrendous]]. Using a 10-minute ritual of [[Speak with Animals]], [[Lanny]] has established a bond with his rat, [[Mr. Rat]]. Furthermore, by casting [[Beast Sense]], he was able to peer through the eyes of [[Mr. Rat]] and spy on the [[Captain's Quarters]]. [[Lanny]] discovered that [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] slept on the floor, while the ship's mysterious magic-user, [[Arita the Witch]], slept in the captain's bed. 

Upon reaching the deck, the daily routine is interrupted when [[Fedor]] drags the lifeless body of [[Japes Magpie]] (aka [[Jape]]) into the middle of the deck, claiming [[Jape]] died in his hammock from battle wounds. [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] remains completely indifferent to the death, and [[Mr. Plugg]] simply shrugs. [[Jape]] is subsequently sewn into an old sailcloth by [[Rosie Cusswell]] with [[Fedor]]'s assistance, joining [[Badger]] the barber, who also perished.

Shortly after, [[Master Scourge]] brings the eight [[Rahadoumi]] prisoners onto the deck in chains. One of the captives speaks out defiantly, warning the captain of severe diplomatic consequences for attacking a sovereign ship of the [[Rahadumi]] nation. Enraged by the defiance, [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] wraps his massive hand around the spokesman's face like a "facehugger", lifts him off the ground, and hurls him overboard. [[Rhiaris]] fires a heavy crossbow bolt into the water; a brief scream is heard, followed by silence as the sharks consume him. [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] offers the remaining seven prisoners a simple choice: volunteer to join his crew or join their comrade in the shark-infested waters. The captives immediately volunteer, and [[Master Scourge]] unlocks their manacles.

A sea burial is held for [[Japes Magpie]] and [[Badger]]. [[Sandara Quinn]] steps forward to say a few words and tosses a coin into the ocean. The bodies are thrown overboard, and the crew follows suit by tossing coins. [[Devi McRae]] throws a coin; [[Desmond]] tosses a coin, whispering "sorry mate"; [[Torrin Horrendous]] performs a blood ritual, cutting her hand with a [[Dagger]] to smear blood on a coin, touching it to her forehead, dedicating it to her lord [[Myrkul]], and flicking it into the water; and [[Lanny]] tosses two coins—one for [[Badger]] and one for [[Japes Magpie]].

[[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] then announces a division of the crew. The captured merchant vessel, the [[Man's Promise]], is to sail north to [[Port Peril]] with a skeleton crew captained by [[Mr. Plugg]] to be sold. [[Mr. Plugg]] selects his skeleton crew, which consists of [[Aretha]] (the female pirate squab who slept in the officers' quarters), [[Fedor]], [[The Dwarf Spy]], the ship's cook [[Ambrose Kroop]] (aka [[Fishguts]]), the "newbie pirates" ([[Sandara Quinn]], [[Rosie Cusswell]], [[Conchobhar Shortstone]], [[Cogwood]], and the four adventurers), and three of the [[Rahadoumi]] prisoners.

Before boarding, [[Devi McRae]] goes to the galley to inform [[Ambrose Kroop]] of his reassignment. [[Ambrose Kroop]] tearfully hugs [[Grook]] the quartermaster, who gifts him a large [[Cask of Rum]]. [[Devi McRae]] retrieves his magical [[Grappling Hook]] from [[Grook]]'s store, and [[Lanny]] safely recovers [[Mr. Rat]]. On deck, [[Torrin Horrendous]] negotiates with [[Mr. Plugg]] for a [[Diamond]] worth 50 gold pieces to serve as her spellcasting focus, promising to prove her magical value to him.

Once aboard the [[Man's Promise]], [[Mr. Plugg]] warns the crew from the poop deck that duties will be strictly enforced, and any failure will be punished far more severely than on the [[Wormwood]]. Privately in his cabin, [[Mr. Plugg]] offers [[Torrin Horrendous]] an officer position as his personal mage, under the condition that she spies on the twins and reports any insubordination. [[Torrin Horrendous]] immediately informs [[Devi McRae]] and [[Desmond]] of [[Mr. Plugg]]'s scheme, explaining that she will play along to gain his trust and use her position to benefit the group. She heals [[Desmond]]'s wounds by casting [[Cure Wounds]] using her skull necklace, which pulses with black energy.

The party holds a secret meeting to discuss a potential mutiny against [[Mr. Plugg]]. They list potential allies—including [[Ambrose Kroop]], [[Sandara Quinn]], [[Rosie Cusswell]], and [[Cogwood]]—while deciding that [[Conchobhar Shortstone]] could be useful as bait. The session ends with a massive twist: [[Mr. Plugg]] suddenly spins the ship's wheel 90 degrees to the right, steering the vessel away from [[Port Peril]]. He confesses to [[Torrin Horrendous]] that he plans to steal the [[Man's Promise]] for himself, defying [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]], and demands her loyalty.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]]**: The brutal captain of the [[Wormwood]] who executes a [[Rahadoumi]] prisoner and splits his crew.
*   **[[Mr. Plugg]]** (aka **[[Gunter Plug]]**): The treacherous first mate of the [[Wormwood]] who takes command of the [[Man's Promise]] and hatches a plan to steal the ship for himself.
*   **[[Torrin Horrendous]]**: The party's wizard who is promoted to officer/mage under [[Mr. Plugg]] and acts as a double agent for the party.
*   **[[Lanny]]**: The goliath barbarian who uses his unique magical connection to communicate with [[Mr. Rat]].
*   **[[Sandara Quinn]]**: A priestess of [[Besmara]] who leads the sea burial for [[Japes Magpie]] and [[Badger]].
*   **[[Ambrose Kroop]]** (aka **[[Fishguts]]**): The ship's cook who is reassigned to the [[Man's Promise]] and receives a [[Cask of Rum]] from [[Grook]].
*   **[[Fedor]]**: A loyal lackey of [[Mr. Plugg]] who drags [[Japes Magpie]]'s body to the deck.
*   **[[Grook]]**: The quartermaster of the [[Wormwood]] who returns [[Devi McRae]]'s [[Grappling Hook]].
*   **[[The Dwarf Spy]]**: A loitering dwarf selected by [[Fedor]] to join the skeleton crew.
*   **[[Rahadumi]]** / **[[Rahadoumi]]**: The faction of the merchant vessel captured by the [[Wormwood]] crew.

## Locations Visited
*   **[[Wormwood]]**: The pirate ship where the session begins.
*   **[[Man's Promise]]**: The newly captured [[Rahadoumi]] vessel that the party boarding crew sets sail on.
*   **[[Port Peril]]**: The planned destination to sell the [[Man's Promise]], which [[Mr. Plugg]] ultimately decides to bypass.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **Facehugger Execution**: [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] crushes the skull of a defiant [[Rahadoumi]] spokesman with his bare hand and tosses him to the sharks.

*   **[[Diamond]]**: Worth 50 gold pieces, given to [[Torrin Horrendous]] by [[Mr. Plugg]] to serve as her spellcasting focus.
*   **[[Cask of Rum]]**: Gifted to [[Ambrose Kroop]] by [[Grook]].
