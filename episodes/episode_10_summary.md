# [Skull and Shackles Episode 10: Electrocuting Friends and Fighting Grindylows](/skull-and-shackles-episode-10-electrocuting-friends-and-fighting-grindylows)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 16 (During the journey of the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise))

## Plot Summary
The journey of the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise) continues, but the weather quickly deteriorates into rough seas and heavy rain. During a difficult sailing maneuver, [Desmond](/desmond) slips on the rigging, failing to secure a rope, which results in a massive wave broadsiding the vessel. Enraged, [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) orders that both [Desmond](/desmond) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) be immediately disciplined on the deck.

To prevent [Master Scourge](/master-scourge) from brutally whipping the twins, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) boldly intervenes. She proposes administering "shock therapy" using her lightning magic as a more efficient and painful punishment, which [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) permits. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) casts [Shocking Grasp](/shocking-grasp) on both [Desmond](/desmond) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae). The twins play along with theatrical screams, thrashing about the deck in mock agony, which thoroughly delights and satisfies [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg).

As night falls, the weather escalates into a full-on tropical storm. With the ship in danger of breaching, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) runs to the front of the ship and casts [Light](/light) on the figurehead/prow, illuminating the path with 40 feet of light to help [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) navigate through the treacherous reefs. During the frantic maneuvers, [Desmond](/desmond) hobbles to [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) and [Lanny](/lanny), whispering that this chaotic storm might be the perfect opportunity to strike and launch their mutiny. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) strongly advises against a mutiny during a storm, insisting they prioritize keeping the ship afloat.

The argument is cut short when the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise) is suddenly ambushed by a swarm of tentacled, goblin-like octopoid creatures known as [Grindylows](/grindylows). The beasts swarm the deck, attacking the crew. Using [Thormaturgy](/thormaturgy) to amplify her voice, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) shouts a warning across the entire ship for all able men to take up arms.

During the combat, a grindylow uses its tentacles to trip [Lanny](/lanny), knocking him prone. On his turn, [Lanny](/lanny) stands up, enters a rage, and delivers a brutal overhand swipe, driving his weapon through a grindylow and pinning it to the deck for 14 points of damage. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) battles the creatures using [Chromatic Orb](/chromatic-orb) (black lightning) and [Toll the Dead](/toll-the-dead), while [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) stabs and skewers another with his [Rapier](/rapier).

In the middle of the melee, a loud shuddering crash echoes through the ship as the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise) collides with a hidden reef. Despite the collision, the party successfully slays several of the beasts, and the remaining [Grindylows](/grindylows) retreat and flee back into the dark ocean. [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) remains at the wheel, frantically ordering the crew back to their posts to save the ship.

With the battle over, the party counts heads and makes a terrifying discovery: both [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) and [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) are missing. They have been swept overboard during the chaotic struggle and are nowhere to be found.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)**: The furious captain of the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise) who demands the twins be disciplined and struggles to steer the ship through the storm.
*   **[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)**: The party's wizard who uses [Shocking Grasp](/shocking-grasp) to spare the twins a whipping and later uses [Light](/light) to help navigate the storm.
*   **[Desmond](/desmond)**: The monk who slips on the rigging, leading to [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)'s disciplinary action.
*   **[Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)**: The rogue who plays along with [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s mock punishment.
*   **[Lanny](/lanny)**: The barbarian who is tripped by a grindylow but rages and smashes it into the deck.
*   **[Grindylows](/grindylows)**: The tentacled, goblin-like aquatic creatures that ambush the ship.
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: The cleric who is discovered missing and presumed swept overboard.
*   **[Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)**: The halfling who is discovered missing and presumed swept overboard.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Man's Promise](/mans-promise)**: The vessel currently navigating a violent tropical storm and a reef system.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **The Grindylow Ambush**: The crew defends the deck against a sudden swarm of [Grindylows](/grindylows) climbing aboard during the storm.
*   **Reef Collision**: The [Man's Promise](/mans-promise) runs aground on a hidden reef during the combat.
