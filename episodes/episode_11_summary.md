# [Skull and Shackles Episode 11: Leaky Boats, Meaty Wallets, and Ghouls](/skull-and-shackles-episode-11-leaky-boats-meaty-wallets-and-ghouls)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 17 (The morning after the storm)

## Plot Summary
The morning after the violent storm, the crew is forced to face the immediate consequences of their ordeal. Although the storm has cleared, [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) and [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) are still missing. [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) is frantically attempting to keep the damaged ship under control, as a large crack has appeared in the hull of the main hold. 

[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) and [Desmond](/desmond) argue about how to locate the missing girls. [Desmond](/desmond) suggests appealing to a deity, but [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) firmly states that she does not believe in the pirate goddess [Besmara](/besmara) and cannot call upon her for aid. [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) orders [Desmond](/desmond) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) to go below deck and rest, ominously stating that he has "plans" for them in the morning.

[Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) calls [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) into his quarters for a private meeting. He reveals his true, insidious intentions: he plans to send the twins to a nearby island under the guise of foraging for fresh water (since the ship's water barrels were destroyed in the storm). He hopes they will return exhausted, making them easier to handle, as he heavily blames [Desmond](/desmond) for the ship's collision with the reef. [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) tells [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) that the twins must either comply or die.

The following morning, the party sets off in a [Rowboat](/rowboat) toward the nearby island. After landing on the shore, they discover an [Abandoned Village](/abandoned-village) and follow an overgrown path leading into a swampy jungle.

They soon encounter a major obstacle: a boggy mire where only the posts of a long-crumbled bridge remain. As they attempt to cross using the posts, they are ambushed by a pair of [Giant Toads](/giant-toads). [Lanny](/lanny) enters a rage and leaps from a post directly into the bog, sinking up to his shins in the thick mud as he swings his [Maul](/maul). [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) pulls out her [Diamond](/diamond) focus and casts [Chromatic Orb](/chromatic-orb) (black lightning) to blast the creature.

After defeating the toads, the party successfully crosses the mire and continues trekking through the jungle. They eventually emerge into a clearing and discover a large, chaotic tent hung around a massive tree. The interior is smeared with blood and writhing with insects. Believing an inspection is necessary, they step inside the dank abode, only to find it is occupied. Three terrifying [Ghouls](/ghouls) dressed in rotted, fine clothing awake to greet them, ending the session on a chilling cliffhanger.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)**: The treacherous captain of the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise) who reveals his plan to exhaust and eliminate the twins on the nearby island.
*   **[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)**: The wizard who refuses to pray to [Besmara](/besmara) and must lead the twins to the island under [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)'s orders.
*   **[Desmond](/desmond)**: The monk who is blamed for the reef collision and is sent to forage on the island.
*   **[Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)**: The rogue who joins the foraging party to search for fresh water and the missing girls.
*   **[Lanny](/lanny)**: The goliath barbarian who bravely leaps into the bog to battle the [Giant Toads](/giant-toads).
*   **[Giant Toads](/giant-toads)**: A pair of swamp-dwelling predators that ambush the party in the boggy mire.
*   **[Ghouls](/ghouls)**: Three undead ghouls wearing decayed, high-society clothing that awake inside the clearing's tent.
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: The missing cleric who the party hopes to find on the island.
*   **[Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)**: The missing halfling who the party hopes to find on the island.
