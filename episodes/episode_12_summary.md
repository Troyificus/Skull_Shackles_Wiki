# [Skull and Shackles Episode 12: Posh Ghouls and the Hanging Man](/skull-and-shackles-episode-12-posh-ghouls-and-the-hanging-man)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 5 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The morning aboard the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise) begins with the party dealing with the aftermath of a devastating storm. [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) reveals to [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) his ulterior motives for sending the twins, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) and [Desmond](/desmond), to explore a nearby island: he hopes to run them into the ground so they return exhausted and easy to manage, planning to let out his frustrations on [Desmond](/desmond) specifically. 

The party, consisting of [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), [Desmond](/desmond), [Lanny](/lanny), and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous), sets off in a [Rowboat](/rowboat) to search for fresh water. Upon landing, they discover an [Abandoned Village](/abandoned-village) and follow a path into a [Swampy Jungle](/swampy-jungle). They encounter a [Boggy Mire](/boggy-mire) with only the posts of a crumbled bridge to cross. There, they are ambushed by a pair of [Giant Toads](/giant-toads). After defeating the toads, they continue deeper into the jungle until they reach a clearing containing a large, chaotic tent hung around a massive tree.

Entering the tent, they find humanoid remains smeared with blood and swarming with insects. Suddenly, three undead [Ghouls](/ghouls) in rotting, high-society [Courtiers' Outfits](/courtiers-outfits) awaken to attack. A brief, chaotic combat ensues: [Desmond](/desmond) disengages and runs, pursued by a ghoul. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) sneaks around to execute a sneak attack with his [Rapier](/rapier) for major damage, while [Lanny](/lanny) enters a [Rage](/rage) and obliterates a ghoul with his [Maul](/maul). After some close calls, including [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) resisting a paralyzing cold touch from a ghoul, the undead are destroyed.

Upon searching the tent, the party recovers two chests containing valuable [Gems](/gems) worth several hundred gold pieces, along with rotted clothing and skeletal remains. Proceeding cautiously around the coast, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) takes the lead. He senses a rumbling beneath the ground before Lanny is suddenly clamped in the mandibles of a [Large Insect-like Creature](/large-insect-like-creature) (an [Ankheg](/ankheg)). The creature spews a line of acid, coating [Lanny](/lanny) and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) for substantial damage. [Lanny](/lanny) breaks the grapple, raging and retaliating with his [Maul](/maul), while [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) unleashes a [Chromatic Orb](/chromatic-orb) (lightning) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) throws his last [Dagger](/dagger) to finally slay the beast. The party then takes a short rest to recuperate.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)**: The cruel first mate who orchestrates the island expedition to exhaust and weaken the party.
*   **[Ghouls](/ghouls)**: Three flesh-eating undead dressed in rotted high-society garments who ambush the party inside their tent.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Man's Promise](/mans-promise)**: The captured merchant vessel currently anchored off the coast.
*   **[Abandoned Village](/abandoned-village)**: A ghost town on the shore of the island.
*   **[Swampy Jungle](/swampy-jungle)**: The dense, boggy interior of the island.
*   **[Boggy Mire](/boggy-mire)**: A swampy crossing with crumbled bridge posts where the toad ambush occurs.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[Giant Toads](/giant-toads) Ambush**: A combat encounter at the swamp crossing where the party fends off two giant, hungry amphibians.
*   **Tent [Ghouls](/ghouls)**: A fight inside the bloody tent against three surprisingly fast, rotting undead.
*   **[Large Insect-like Creature](/large-insect-like-creature) (An [Ankheg](/ankheg))**: A deadly encounter with an underground insect that clamps [Lanny](/lanny) and spews acid before being slain by the party's combined efforts.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Gems](/gems)**: A collection of valuable gemstones recovered from two chests inside the ghouls' tent, worth at least a couple of hundred gold pieces.
