# [Skull and Shackles Episode 14: Lanny’s Cannonball and the Whale Toad](/skull-and-shackles-episode-14-lannys-cannonball-and-the-whale-toad)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 5 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
As the party rests on the cliff's edge, a lone [Grindylow](/grindylow) emerges from the cave, spots its dead kin, and locks eyes with [Lanny](/lanny). [Lanny](/lanny) immediately launches himself off the 60-foot cliff, falling like a cannonball to smush the creature with his [Maul](/maul) (rolling a natural 20 on initiative). The party re-enters the caves. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) uses his newly discovered [Magic Ring of Swimming](/magic-ring-of-swimming) (which grants him a swim speed and the ability to breathe underwater) to scout ahead. 

[Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) hears chattering and spots two [Grindylows](/grindylows) in a heated argument. He signals back to the group, but as they move into a bottleneck pool, they are ambushed. After a quick skirmish, they push deeper. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) swims ahead and discovers the [Grindylow Queen](/grindylow-queen) (a spellcasting matriarch) alongside her guards and a massive, swollen [Whale-Toad](/whale-toad) (a grotesque, giant grindelow). Stringing from the ceiling are [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) and a pale, unconscious [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell), whose leg has been brutally severed below the knee.

The party coordinates a rescue plan. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) swims stealthily under the water (rolling a 14) and cuts [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)'s binds, handing her a dagger as she floats to the surface. However, the combat spirals out of control: [Lanny](/lanny) is bitten and swallowed whole by the massive [Whale-Toad](/whale-toad), taking acidic stomach damage. [Desmond](/desmond) dives in to punch the beast with disadvantage, breaking off pieces of its rubbery skin. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) protects the swallowed barbarian by casting [Shield of Faith](/shield-of-faith) on [Lanny](/lanny), and repeatedly uses [Shocking Grasp](/shocking-grasp) to shock the grndylows.

The [Grindylow Queen](/grindylow-queen) launches her magical harpoon at [Desmond](/desmond), who deftly deflects it. During the chaos, [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) cuts [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)'s bonds and drags her to safety. The Queen stabs [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), knocking him unconscious at the water's surface. With their spell slots spent and resources depleted, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) steps up. She unleashes a level 2 [Chromatic Orb](/chromatic-orb) (lightning), dealing 21 lightning damage to the Queen. As [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) floats unconsciously, [Lanny](/lanny) slices his way out of the [Whale-Toad](/whale-toad)'s stomach. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) finishes off the [Grindylow Queen](/grindylow-queen) with a final level 1 [Chromatic Orb](/chromatic-orb), electrocuting her from the inside out and laughing maniacally as the matriarch collapses.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Grindylow Queen](/grindylow-queen)**: The spellcasting matriarch of the caves who holds [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) and [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) captive.
*   **[Whale-Toad](/whale-toad)**: A grotesque, giant grindelow of swollen proportions that swallows [Lanny](/lanny) whole during the battle.
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: Captured cleric of [Besmara](/besmara) rescued from the ceiling of the cavern.
*   **[Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)**: Captured halfling rigger rescued by the party, suffering from a severed leg.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Grindylow Cave](/grindylow-cave)**: The flooded, dark cavern system of the sea goblins.
*   **The Queen's Cavern**: The main chamber of the grindylow tribe where prisoners are kept.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **The Cliff Plunge**: [Lanny](/lanny) jumps off the cliff to crush a lone grindylow.
*   **The Queen's Chamber Battle**: A high-stakes, underwater boss fight where [Lanny](/lanny) is swallowed by a [Whale-Toad](/whale-toad), [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) is knocked unconscious, and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) ultimately electrocutes the [Grindylow Queen](/grindylow-queen) to secure a desperate victory.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Magic Ring of Swimming](/magic-ring-of-swimming)**: A ring that grants the wearer aquatic mobility and water breathing (discovered earlier and tested by [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) this session).
