# [Skull and Shackles Episode 15: Tactical Gore and the Hat Boat](/skull-and-shackles-episode-15-tactical-gore-and-the-hat-boat)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 12-13 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Following the chaotic battle in the depths of the [Grindylow Cave](/grindylow-cave), the session begins with the party securing their victory. [Desmond](/desmond) rushes to retrieve the limp body of his twin brother, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), who is sinking beneath the water's surface [18]. [Desmond](/desmond) brings [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) to the surface and calls out for [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)'s aid [18]. Having survived her own ordeal, [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) channels her remaining level 1 spell slot to cast [Cure Wounds](/cure-wounds) on [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), restoring him to consciousness [18].

The party then turns their attention to the cavern, checking on a pale and exhausted [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell), who has tragically lost her leg from the knee down [18, 144]. Under the supervision of [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) and the party, [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) is kept stable [27]. The heroes proceed to loot the [Grindylow Queen](/grindylow-queen)'s cavern, retrieving several valuable items including a [Silver Dagger](/silver-dagger), a [Silver Sickle](/silver-sickle), and an ornate [Bone Blade](/bone-blade) with a scrimshawed handle [20]. They also discover [Sandara's Tricorn Hat](/sandaras-tricorn-hat), a massive [Shark's Jawbone](/sharks-jawbone) engraved with magical runes representing spell scrolls, and four [Magical Crossbow Bolts](/magical-crossbow-bolts) [21, 22].

Recognizing that the afternoon is drawing to a close, [Lanny](/lanny) helps carry the injured [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) while the party collects their substantial spoils [23, 25]. They scale the cliffs using their grappling hook and return to the safety of the fortified [Stockade](/stockade) at the top [25].

To prepare for their eventual return to the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise), the party devises a cunning ruse to deceive [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg). They cut down the rotting corpse of the stockade's former occupant (the hung man) and burn it outside [26]. Before doing so, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), with [Lanny](/lanny)'s assistance, harvests the thick, dark ghoul blood from the corpse to create a gruesome "bloody mary" cocktail mixed with water [26, 27]. The twins plan to smear this thin, visceral liquid over themselves to fake being in a severely weakened and beaten state, claiming that [Desmond](/desmond) was "eaten by a giant toad" or otherwise mauled, to make [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) believe they are too exhausted to mount any resistance [28, 30].

Meanwhile, [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) reveals a critical secret to [Desmond](/desmond): her tricorn hat is actually a magical item passed down from her father [34]. Upon reading [Besmara's Code](/besmaras-code) embroidered along the inside brim, [Desmond](/desmond) learns that the hat can magically transform into a functional rowing boat when placed in water [34, 445]. This provides the party with the perfect vehicle to execute a midnight mutiny. The plan is finalized: [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous), [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), and [Lanny](/lanny) will return to the ship by day with the fresh water, faking the tragic news of [Desmond](/desmond)'s demise [30]. At night, [Desmond](/desmond), [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn), and [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) will use the magical tricorn hat boat to sneak onto the opposite side of the ship, allowing the party to launch a surprise attack on [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) and his loyalist officers [30, 31].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: The devout cleric of [Besmara](/besmara) who heals the party, shares the secret of her magical tricorn hat, and stands ready to support the mutiny against [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) [18, 30, 34].
*   **[Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)**: The tough halfling rigger who is rescued from the [Grindylows](/grindylows), though she is left severely weakened after losing her leg from the knee down [18, 36, 144].
*   **[Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)**: The cruel first mate of the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise) whose impending plot to "thin out" the party's ranks has forced the group to plan an immediate, high-stakes mutiny [28].
*   **[Master Scourge](/master-scourge)**: Mentioned as a loyalist and chief threat aboard the ship who must be dealt with during the mutiny [29, 31].
*   **[Fedor](/fedor)**: Mentioned as a potential wild card; the party wonders if he can be swayed to their side due to his previous interaction with [Lanny](/lanny) [29].
*   **[Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert)**: The chained goliath-sized champion who might be turned to the party's side or used as a chaotic distraction if released [29, 32].

## Locations Visited
*   **[Grindylow Cave](/grindylow-cave)**: The damp, sea-swept cavern where the party rescued their friends, looted the Queen's hoard, and recovered the magical [Shark's Jawbone](/sharks-jawbone) [16, 21].
*   **[Stockade](/stockade)**: The fortified wooden camp at the top of the island cliff where the party holes up to rest, heal their wounds, and coordinate their mutiny plan [25, 33].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **None (Post-Combat Recovery)**: This session focused entirely on healing from the previous battle, looting the cave system, and roleplaying the logistics of the upcoming mutiny [18, 25, 30].

*   **[Ghoul Blood Cocktail](/ghoul-blood-cocktail)**: A thin, viscid mixture of water and congealed undead blood crafted by [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) to simulate severe wounds [26, 27].
