# [Skull and Shackles Episode 16: The Toad Lie and the Lightning Harpoon](/skull-and-shackles-episode-16-the-toad-lie-and-the-lightning-harpoon)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 13-14 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The session begins at the [Stockade](/stockade) at first light, where [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) performs an 11-minute ritual casting of [Identify](/identify) on their newly acquired grappling hook, discovering it is a [Steadfast Grapple](/steadfast-grapple) that requires a colossal strength check to remove once set [37, 38]. 

Before executing their return to the ship, the party decides to venture back down the cliff to the [Grindylow Cave](/grindylow-cave) to retrieve any valuable items they might have missed during their initial, rushed retreat [37, 39]. [Lanny](/lanny) joins the twins, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) and [Desmond](/desmond), while [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) keeps watch on the cliffside [39]. 

Avoiding the chambers inhabited by undead [Ghouls](/ghouls), the party discovers a large, hidden room deep within the cave system [40]. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) casts [Detect Magic](/detect-magic), and the group uncovers three valuable artifacts: an [Ivory Walrus Tusk](/ivory-walrus-tusk) decorated with gold and a detailed scrimshaw map of the [Shackles](/shackles), and a curious [Orca-Jaw Harp](/orca-jaw-harp) set with pearls [40]. Intrigued by the musical instrument, [Lanny](/lanny) tries his hand at playing it, rolling an incredible natural 20 on his [Performance](/performance) check [41]. Despite his massive goliath hands, his fingers pluck the strings with surprising softness, producing a beautiful, resonant tune [41]. 

While in the cavern, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) presents the items recovered from the [Grindylow Queen](/grindylow-queen)'s corpse the previous day [41]. Since none of the Queen's personal items—including the [Silver Sickle](/silver-sickle) and the [Bone Blade](/bone-blade)—are magical, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) happily trades her standard steel [Dagger](/dagger) to [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) in exchange for the unique scrimshawed [Bone Blade](/bone-blade), which she adds to her collection [42].

With the harvest complete, the party crosses back over the treacherous [Boggy Mire](/boggy-mire) as afternoon draws on, making their way back toward the abandoned village on the north side of the island [43]. To avoid being spotted by [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)'s spyglass, they hold up in a ruined hut near the tree line [43]. 

The final stages of the mutiny plan are put into motion. [Desmond](/desmond) is smeared in the ghoul-blood cocktail, faking his "death" and preparing to hide out near the jungle edge alongside [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) and an injured [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) [44]. [Lanny](/lanny) secures their rowboat filled with the fresh water barrels [44]. 

As the sun begins to set on the horizon, [Lanny](/lanny), [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) board their rowboat and row back to the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise) [45]. As they approach, the ship's lookouts spot them and signal the deck [45]. [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) stands on the ship's rails, wearing [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)'s stolen purple coat, which billows in the wind [45]. A look of deep confusion and suspicion washes over the first mate's face as he scans the small boat and realizes that of the four crew members who left, only three have returned [45]. The session ends on a tense cliffhanger as [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) jumps down onto the deck, ready to confront them [45].
