# [Skull and Shackles Episode 20: Weaponized Honesty and the Wizard Rodeo](/skull-and-shackles-episode-20-weaponized-honesty-and-the-wizard-rodeo)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 19 at [Rickety's Squibs](/ricketys-squibs) (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The tense standoff at the shipyard docks is resolved as the party engages in an amicable parley with the newly arrived pirate captain, [Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth). Handing his greatsword to a crewman, [Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth) approaches unarmed with a warm smile. [Lanny](/lanny) (acting as [Captain Skull Crusher](/captain-skull-crusher)) and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) (acting as [Miss Blackheart](/miss-blackheart)) raise their hands in peace. [Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth) reveals that he commands a fleet of four ships, with his flagship being the [Pinion](/pinion). He explains that [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) is "absolutely no friend" of his and is highly amused to learn that the party mutinied against and disposed of [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg) (referred to as [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) or [Gunter Plug](/gunter-plug)) to steal the ship. He invites the party to visit his home port at [Goat's Head](/goats-head) on the [Fire Grass Isles](/fire-grass-isles) and offers to establish mutual trust.

To aid [Rickety Hake](/rickety-hake) and ensure the safety of the shipyard, the party agrees to investigate the nearby flag tower, which has failed to raise its warning flag. [Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth) decides to join them on the hike up the ridge. 

While navigating the dense canopy trail, the group is ambushed by a pair of wild boars. A frantic combat ensues. Amidst the chaos, a boar of immense size—a raging "mama pig"—charges out of the shadows and bombards directly into [Desmond](/desmond), inflicting a deep, gashing chest wound that knocks him unconscious. Showing incredible bravado, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) teleports herself directly onto the thrashing beast's back, holding on for dear life while administering severe electrical shocks using [Shocking Grasp](/shocking-grasp) that blacken and crisp the creature's fur. [Lanny](/lanny) goes into a furious rage and pile-drives his heavy [Maul](/maul) directly onto the giant pig's forehead, crushing its skull and ending the threat. 

After [Desmond](/desmond) is stabilized and healed, the group presses on to the stilt-mounted wooden tower. There, they discover the lifeless body of [Lyle the Lookout](/lyle-the-lookout) (the lookout), whose neck is marked by a grotesque, swollen wound—likely the work of the giant wasps. Among the debris in the tower, they find a poorly treated, plucked [Parrot](/parrot) in a sorry state, which takes a liking to [Desmond](/desmond) and sits on his hand. The party wraps [Lyle the Lookout](/lyle-the-lookout)'s body in a hammock to carry back for a proper burial. As they begin their trek back to the shipyard, dragging the pig carcasses, [Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth) begins to regale the party with the tragic backstory of [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert), ending the session on a cliffhanger.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth)**: A peg-legged pirate captain commanding four ships, including the [Pinion](/pinion). He forms a tentative alliance with the party and joins their excursion.
*   **[Lyle the Lookout](/lyle-the-lookout)**: The shipyard lookout found dead of wasp stings in the flag tower.
*   **[Parrot](/parrot)**: A plucked, mistreated bird found in the tower that bonds with [Desmond](/desmond).
*   **[Rickety Hake](/rickety-hake)**: Shipyard owner who requested the flag tower investigation.
*   **[Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan)**: Mentioned as a common enemy of both [Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth) and the party.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Rickety's Squibs](/ricketys-squibs)**: The main shipyard docks where the party parleys with [Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth).
*   **[The Flag Tower](/the-flag-tower)**: A stilt-mounted lookout hut situated on a high ridge overlooking the sea.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[The Boar Backwoods Fray](/the-boar-backwoods-fray)**: The party defeats a pair of wild boars and an enormous "mama pig" on the ridge trail. [Desmond](/desmond) is knocked unconscious by a charge, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) rides the beast's back while casting [Shocking Grasp](/shocking-grasp), and [Lanny](/lanny) delivers the killing blow.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Plucked Parrot](/plucked-parrot)**: Rescued from the flag tower by [Desmond](/desmond).
*   **[Lyle's Body](/lyles-body)**: Retrieved for a proper burial.
*   **[Boar Carcasses](/boar-carcasses)**: Harvested for meat to feed the shipyard commune.
