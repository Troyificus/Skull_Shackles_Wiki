# [Skull and Shackles Episode 21: Mind Wipes and Mercenary Parrots](/skull-and-shackles-episode-21-mind-wipes-and-mercenary-parrots)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 15 to Day 18 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
On the trek back from the flag tower to the shipyard, [Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth) of the [Pinion](/pinion) shares the tragic backstory of [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert) (originally known simply as [Albert](/albert) or [The Albert](/the-albert)). 

Years ago, [Albert](/albert) and [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) were young rival pirate captains on the docks of [Port Peril](/port-peril). Their rivalry came to a head at [The Slippery Whale](/the-slippery-whale) tavern, culminating in a legendary bar brawl where [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) smashed a bottle over [Albert](/albert)'s face, blinding him in one eye. In retaliation, [Albert](/albert) burned [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan)'s ship. Enraged by the destruction, [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) paid off three other captains to ambush [Albert](/albert), executed his entire crew before his eyes, and had his [Sorceress](/sorceress) cast a powerful mind-wipe spell to turn [Albert](/albert) into the bumbling, simple-minded giant he is today. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) consults with [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) about whether restorative magic could reverse this condition, but both agree it is far beyond their current magical capabilities.

[Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth) also advises the party on how to officially become [Free Captains](/free-captains) of the [Shackles](/shackles), explaining that they must gather enough [Infamy](/infamy) to warrant an audience with the [Hurricane King Karch Bonefist](/hurricane-king-karch-bonefist) at [Port Peril](/port-peril). 

The group returns to [Rickety Hake](/rickety-hake)'s dockyard, delivering the body of the lookout [Lyle the Lookout](/lyle-the-lookout) wrapped in a hammock. [Rickety Hake](/rickety-hake) provides a refund on their rental of the horse and cart and prepares a proper burial for [Lyle the Lookout](/lyle-the-lookout). That evening, a grand celebratory feast is held. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) tells an embellished story of the party's victory in the [Grindylow Cave](/grindylow-cave) (rolling a high [Performance](/performance) check with advantage), successfully recruiting several of [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)'s halfling companions to join their crew. 

Meanwhile, [Lanny](/lanny) casts [Speak with Animals](/speak-with-animals) to converse with [Desmond](/desmond)'s newly acquired, plucked [Parrot](/parrot). The parrot, who is highly aggressive but quickly pacified by food, bonds with the party.

The party sets sail south for [Blood Cove](/blood-cove) on their ship, the [Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze) (formerly the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise)). On the voyage, [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) (aka [Fishguts](/fishguts)) remains remarkably sober and warns the crew that [Blood Cove](/blood-cove) is run strictly on mercantile business; they must behave themselves to avoid drawing the wrath of the local militia. 

Upon arrival, the party negotiates with [Dock Master Tesha Ambertine](/dock-master-tesha-ambertine), paying harbor fees of [8 gold pieces](/8-gold-pieces) per day upfront for a week's stay. They register the vessel under the name [Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze), officially listing [Captain Skull Crusher](/captain-skull-crusher) (Lanny) as captain and [Dev McRae](/dev-mcrae) (Devi McRae) as first mate. To prevent a panic in the streets of [Blood Cove](/blood-cove), they decide to transport the bound and unconscious [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg) below deck inside a wooden [Crate](/crate) carried by the colossal [Lanny](/lanny).

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth)**: The peg-legged captain of the Chelish galleon [Pinion](/pinion), who shares [Albert](/albert)'s tragic backstory and acts as an amicable pirate advisor.
*   **[Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert)**: The party's simple-minded giant companion, revealed to have once been a proud rival pirate captain named [Albert](/albert).
*   **[Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan)**: The cruel captain of the [Wormwood](/wormwood) who executed [Albert](/albert)'s crew and had his mind wiped.
*   **[Rickety Hake](/rickety-hake)**: The master of the squibbing yard at [Rickety's Squibs](/ricketys-squibs) who concludes business with the party.
*   **[Lyle the Lookout](/lyle-the-lookout)**: The deceased lookout of [Rickety's Squibs](/ricketys-squibs), whose body is returned to the yard.
*   **[Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)**: The ship's cook who provides sober and practical advice about [Blood Cove](/blood-cove).
*   **[Dock Master Tesha Ambertine](/dock-master-tesha-ambertine)**: The strict bureaucrat who registers the [Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze) upon arrival in [Blood Cove](/blood-cove).
*   **[Plucked Parrot](/plucked-parrot)**: A highly aggressive, mostly featherless bird rescued from [Lyle the Lookout](/lyle-the-lookout)'s tower who bonds with [Desmond](/desmond).

## Locations Visited
*   **[Rickety's Squibs](/ricketys-squibs)**: The coastal dry dock and estuary where the ship's refitting is completed.
*   **[The Slippery Whale](/the-slippery-whale)**: A tavern in [Port Peril](/port-peril) mentioned as the location of [Harrigan](/harrigan) and [Albert](/albert)'s legendary bar brawl.
*   **[Port Peril](/port-peril)**: The pirate capital of the [Shackles](/shackles).
*   **[Blood Cove](/blood-cove)**: A sprawling, wealthy port town outside the [Shackles](/shackles) built on massive mangrove roots.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **Recruitment Drive**: [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) performs an elaborate retelling of the [Grindylow](/grindylow) battle, converting a crowd of halflings and dockworkers into loyal crew members.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Plucked Parrot](/plucked-parrot)**: Rescued from the watchtower and kept as a pet.
*   **[Pig Carcasses](/pig-carcasses)**: Harvested from the ridge to provide a week of fresh pork feast for the crew.
