# [Skull and Shackles Episode 22: The Silent Howl and the Cursed Greatsword](/skull-and-shackles-episode-22-the-silent-howl-and-the-cursed-greatsword)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 19 and Day 20 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
After docking the [Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze) at [Blood Cove](/blood-cove), the party ventures ashore to explore the sprawling port town. Twin brothers [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) and [Desmond](/desmond) immediately seek out local entertainment and head to a local gambling house owned by [Shadook](/shadook) (or [Shidduch](/shidduch)). 

At the gaming tables, the twins play poker and [Dice](/dice), winning a few silver pieces. They inquire about purchasing a sleeker, faster ship and [Shadook](/shadook) points them in the direction of [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher), a retired sea dog who possesses a highly nimble ship he might be willing to part with. [Shadook](/shadook) also tells them about a bare-knuckle boxing event scheduled for the following evening. Desmond, possessing a keen interest in fighting, secures a test bout for later that night against [Shadook](/shadook)'s cousin, [Tank](/tank).

Meanwhile, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) and [Lanny](/lanny) browse the shops along the elevated wooden gangways of [Blood Cove](/blood-cove). They visit the [Umbertine Arms and Armor](/umbertine-arms-and-armor) store, where [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) identifies some magical armor, and [Lanny](/lanny)'s attention is strangely captivated by an incredibly heavy, cursed [Greatsword](/greatsword).

Later, the party reunites at [The Blood Root](/the-blood-root) tavern to meet with [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher). The limping old captain agrees that he might be willing to sell his ship, [The Silent Howl](/the-silent-howl), but the transaction requires a significant favor: the party must retrieve a buried cache of treasure located at a once-idyllic cove that has mysteriously transformed into a dangerous, haunted [Death Trap](/death-trap) surrounded by shipwrecks. [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher) also promises to use his local contacts to find a buyer for the party's current ship, the [Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze).

That evening, the crew returns to [Shadook](/shadook)'s gambling house for [Desmond](/desmond)'s test fight. Inside a makeshift ring, [Desmond](/desmond) easily beats [Tank](/tank) to a pulp, earning a spot in the main event against the reigning local champion, [The Pugilist](/the-pugilist). [Shadook](/shadook) is so impressed that he agrees to let [Lanny](/lanny) participate in a fight the following night as well. The episode ends as the party returns to the [Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze) to rest before the big fight night.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Shadook](/shadook)**: The proprietor of a local [Blood Cove](/blood-cove) gambling den who organizes the bare-knuckle fights and guides the party to [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher).
*   **[Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher)**: A friendly, limping retired captain who offers to sell [The Silent Howl](/the-silent-howl) in exchange for a treasure retrieval favor.
*   **[Tank](/tank)**: [Shadook](/shadook)'s brawler cousin who is soundly defeated by [Desmond](/desmond) in a test bout.
*   **[The Pugilist](/the-pugilist)**: The local bare-knuckle boxing champion scheduled to fight [Desmond](/desmond).

## Locations Visited
*   **[Blood Cove](/blood-cove)**: The grand trade port built into the side of a colossal mangrove tree.
*   **[Umbertine Arms and Armor](/umbertine-arms-and-armor)**: A local armory containing dwarven iron furnishings and unique armaments.
*   **[The Blood Root](/the-blood-root)**: A local tavern where the party parleys with [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher).

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[Desmond](/desmond) vs. [Tank](/tank)**: A brief test bout in [Shadook](/shadook)'s fighting ring. [Desmond](/desmond) easily outmaneuvers and defeats [Tank](/tank) without taking any significant damage.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **Silver Coins**: Won by [Desmond](/desmond) at the gambling tables.
