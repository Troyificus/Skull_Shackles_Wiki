# [Skull and Shackles Episode 24: Owlbear Handshakes and Unread Vampire Contracts](/skull-and-shackles-episode-24-owlbear-handshakes-and-unread-vampire-contracts)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 16 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Following the epic, hard-fought victory at [Chidux's Gambling Den](/chiduxs-gambling-den) (also known as [Shadook's Gambling Den](/shadooks-gambling-den)), the crowd erupts into deafening cheers. [Desmond](/desmond) stands in the center of the ring, victorious after knocking out the local champion, [The Pugilist](/the-pugilist), with a devastating uppercut. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) steps forward and heals [Desmond](/desmond)'s dislocated shoulder and severely bruised jaw using a Level 3 [Cure Wounds](/cure-wounds) spell.

Carried by the towering goliath brawler [Lanny](/lanny) (who also won his undercard match against the dwarven brawler [Braden the Brawler Brimstone](/braden-the-brawler-brimstone)), a drunken and battered [Desmond](/desmond) is escorted back to their ship, the [Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze). On the way, the party stops by a street vendor's cart to buy hot pies to celebrate with the rest of the crew.

At the ship, [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert) (who has finally been cleaned of his tar and feathers) stands guard alongside [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) and [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell). The next morning, [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher) arrives at the dock with a potential buyer for the [Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze): [Mr. Girdan Danan](/mr-girdan-danan), an elegant representative of the wealthy [House Carthagen](/house-carthagen) (secretly run by vampires).

[Mr. Girdan Danan](/mr-girdan-danan) conducts a thorough inspection of the ship, examining the Captain's quarters (including [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s newly acquired magical [Orca-Jaw Harp](/orca-jaw-harp) and inscribed [Jawbone](/jawbone) spell scrolls), the cargo hold, and the bilges where [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg) had recently been executed. 

[Mr. Girdan Danan](/mr-girdan-danan) offers [4,000 gold pieces](/4000-gold-pieces) (to be paid in [400 platinum pieces](/400-platinum-pieces)) for the vessel. The party successfully barters and coordinates the transfer. [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher) brings his nimble, fast ship, the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl), alongside the [Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze) by lunchtime. The crew immediately begins transferring all their plundered cargo, magical items, and personal gear to their new vessel.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher)**: The friendly captain who arranged the sale of the [Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze) and traded his nimble vessel, the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl).
*   **[Mr. Girdan Danan](/mr-girdan-danan)**: The sophisticated buyer representing [House Carthagen](/house-carthagen).
*   **[House Carthagen](/house-carthagen)**: A highly influential and wealthy merchant house in [Blood Cove](/blood-cove), secretly run by vampires.
*   **[The Pugilist](/the-pugilist)**: The formidable local boxing champion defeated by [Desmond](/desmond).
*   **[Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert)**: The reformed, mind-wiped goliath companion of the party.
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) & [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)**: Handled guard duties on the ship.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Blood Cove](/blood-cove)**: The lawless, thriving trade port where the transaction takes place.
*   **[Chidux's Gambling Den](/chiduxs-gambling-den)** / **[Shadook's Gambling Den](/shadooks-gambling-den)**: The location of the bare-knuckle fight night.
*   **[The Sea Breeze](/the-sea-breeze)**: The party's refitted merchant ship (formerly the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise)), officially sold to [House Carthagen](/house-carthagen).
*   **[The Silent Howl](/the-silent-howl)**: The party's newly acquired, exceptionally fast ship.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[Desmond](/desmond) vs. [The Pugilist](/the-pugilist)**: Concludes with [Desmond](/desmond) landing a glorious uppercut to knock out the champion.

*   **[The Silent Howl](/the-silent-howl)**: Their sleek new ship.
