# [Skull and Shackles Episode 26: Botched Heist Spirals Into Zombie Horror](/skull-and-shackles-episode-26-botched-heist-spirals-into-zombie-horror)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Days 18-20 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The high-stakes chase with [The Dominator](/the-dominator) reaches a boiling point. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) uses [Misty Step](/misty-step) to sneak onto a decorative overhang on the hull of [The Dominator](/the-dominator) to sabotage it, but rolls another **natural 1** on her stealth check, getting immediately spotted.

Cornered by Chelish officers, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) defends herself by casting [Mirror Image](/mirror-image). An officer attacks her, destroying her duplicates. She retaliates by unleashing a devastating Level 3 [Dragon's Breath](/dragons-breath) (fire damage) to burn the officers and set fire to the ship's rigging. She then uses [Misty Step](/misty-step) to leap 20 feet out of a cabin window into the dark ocean below.

The [Silent Howl](/silent-howl) picks up speed and sails directly into the line of fire to rescue [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) (who was hanging over the side). [Lanny](/lanny) and [Desmond](/desmond) struggle to haul them up but manage to pull them safely aboard.

Enraged, the crew of [The Dominator](/the-dominator) unleashes a volley of heavy crossbow bolts and fires their rear catapult. A massive projectile strikes the hull of the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl) above the water line, causing repairable damage. [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher) is struck by two crossbow bolts, including a critical hit to the clavicle, and collapses. [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) bravely rushes to the stern castle to take the wheel and keep the ship on course. Due to the severe fire damage on [The Dominator](/the-dominator)'s rigging and rudder ropes, the Chelish warship is left floundering in flames as the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl) escapes into the night.

Over the next two days of sailing, the party recovers during a long rest. They pass the [Fire Grass Isles](/fire-grass-isles) and [Goat's Head](/goats-head) before reaching the [Ongongta Overlook](/ongongta-overlook) on the mainland, where [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher) has his treasure buried.

To avoid the "graveyard of wrecks" clogging the cove, they trek through a dense, humid jungle to reach the top of the cliff. Everyone except [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) rolls terribly on their Constitution checks and gains a point of [Exhaustion](/exhaustion) from the grueling climb.

Using the [Steadfast Grapple](/steadfast-grapple) and extra rope, they abseil down onto the sandy beach. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) casts [Detect Magic](/detect-magic) and senses a magical aura buried next to a rock. As they begin to dig, the ocean waves roll in, and a shambling horde of bloated, rotting [Brine Zombies](/brine-zombies) carrying rusted blades emerges from the surf to attack them. The session ends on a terrifying cliffhanger.
