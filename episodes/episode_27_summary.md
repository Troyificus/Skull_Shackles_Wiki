# [Skull and Shackles Episode 27: Struck by Lightning for Cursing Besmara](/skull-and-shackles-episode-27-struck-by-lightning-for-cursing-besmara)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 15 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
On the eerie beach of the [Ongongta Overlook](/ongongta-overlook), the party's search for [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher)'s buried [Chest of Gold](/chest-of-gold) is rudely interrupted as three rotting, bloated [Brine Zombies](/brine-zombies) emerge from the ocean surf. A chaotic struggle begins as [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) takes point, stabbing one of the creatures with his rapier and delivering a heavy kick that shatters its bones, though the resilient undead remains standing. 

[Desmond](/desmond) maneuvers to protect [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) as the zombies slowly march forward and swing their rusted cutlasses. [Lanny](/lanny) enters a furious rage, bringing his heavy [Maul](/maul) down in a devastating strike that completely pulverizes the head of one of the zombies. 

To speed up the excavation of [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher)'s treasure, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) quickly hands a kitchen [Shovel](/shovel) to [Lanny](/lanny). Using his colossal goliath strength, [Lanny](/lanny) rolls a natural 20 (a total of 28 on Athletics) to furiously dig a three-foot-deep hole in the wet sand, finally making visual contact with the chest's lid. [Desmond](/desmond) uses [Step of the Wind](/step-of-the-wind) to disengage from the combat, retreats to the hole, and ties a secure knot with a rope around the chest's handle.

As more [Brine Zombies](/brine-zombies) crawl from the waves, [Torrin Horhendous](/torrin-horhendous) steps up and unleashes a massive, crackling bolt of lightning across the beach. During the magical storm and chaotic crossfire, [Desmond](/desmond)—who had recently expressed deep skepticism and irreverence toward the pirate goddess [Besmara](/besmara)—is suddenly struck directly by a natural bolt of lightning from the heavens. The devastating shock knocks [Desmond](/desmond) completely unconscious, leaving him at death's door as the episode ends on a shocking cliffhanger.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher)**: The retired pirate captain whose buried cache on the beach is the object of the party's excavation.
*   **[Besmara](/besmara)**: The pirate goddess of the [Shackles](/shackles), whose name [Desmond](/desmond) cursed, resulting in a sudden deific strike of lightning.
*   **[Myrkul](/myrkul)**: The god of the dead to whom [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) is devoted.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Ongongta Overlook](/ongongta-overlook)**: The sheer, dangerous cliff and haunted beach where [Captain Glade Fletcher](/captain-glade-fletcher)'s treasure is buried amidst a literal graveyard of shipwrecks.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[Brine Zombies](/brine-zombies) Encounter**: A grueling combat on the beach against slow but incredibly hardy undead that emerge continuously from the surf.
*   **The Lightning Strike**: [Desmond](/desmond) is struck by a deific bolt of lightning from [Besmara](/besmara)'s storm.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Chest of Gold](/chest-of-gold)**: Located and partially excavated by [Lanny](/lanny) using a [Shovel](/shovel), then tied off by [Desmond](/desmond).
