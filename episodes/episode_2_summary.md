# [Skull and Shackles Episode 2: Defying Plugg and Regaining the Spellbook](/skull-and-shackles-episode-2-defying-plugg-and-regaining-the-spellbook)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 1 to Day 2 (Morning)

## Plot Summary
Following the brutal [Keelhauling](/keelhauling) and death of crewman [Japes Magpie](/japes-magpie) on the previous night [1], [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) asks [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) if there are any other punishments for the day [1]. [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) reads from his notebook, nominating the wizard [Torrin](/torrin) for failing to adequately perform her swabbing duties [1]. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) attempts to intervene, boldly stepping forward to argue that [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) is being unfair and suggests the punishment is motivated by [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)'s rejected "amorous intentions" toward [Torrin](/torrin) [2]. [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) dismisses the defense, threatening to put [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) on the whipping post himself, forcing [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) to step back [2, 3].

[Torrin](/torrin) is secured to the main mast by [Master Scourge](/master-scourge) using rope loops [3, 4]. During the whipping, [Torrin](/torrin) considers casting a spell but is unable to perform somatic components due to her bound hands [4, 5]. [Master Scourge](/master-scourge) administers three lashes, dealing 12 points of slashing damage total [5, 6]. Undeterred, [Torrin](/torrin) smiles and spits directly into [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)'s face [6]. Enraged, [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) seizes the whip and strikes [Torrin](/torrin) a fourth time for 4 points of slashing damage before [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) halts the punishment [6, 7]. [Torrin](/torrin) is released and silently retrieves her coat [7].

During the subsequent free time on deck, the crew relaxes with drinks, dice, and arm wrestling [8]. [Desmond](/desmond) sits down with [Jack Scrimshaw](/jack-scrimshaw), who teaches him the art of [Scrimshaw](/scrimshaw) [10, 16]. Using his [Mason's Tools](/masons-tools) proficiency, [Desmond](/desmond) successfully carves a square wave pattern into a piece of bone, which [Jack Scrimshaw](/jack-scrimshaw) allows him to keep [11-13]. Meanwhile, [Lanny](/lanny) is challenged to an arm-wrestling match by a mustachioed human named [Fedor](/fedor) [17, 18, 75]. Caught off-guard, [Lanny](/lanny) loses the match, and [Fedor](/fedor) mockingly refuses a rematch due to [Lanny](/lanny)'s lack of coin [19].

[Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) approaches [Lanny](/lanny) at the bow of the ship to discuss plans for a future mutiny [21, 22]. [Lanny](/lanny) eagerly agrees to join the conspiracy, citing his need to escape the vessel [22]. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) informs [Lanny](/lanny) that his twin brother, [Desmond](/desmond), is also part of the plan, and they agree to target [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)'s lackeys first [22, 23]. 

[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) approaches [Torrin](/torrin) and leads her down to the crew's sleeping quarters below the main hold [25]. There, [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) reveals that she retrieved [Torrin](/torrin)'s stolen spellbook from the superstitious quartermaster, [Quartermaster Grook](/quartermaster-grook), by claiming it and her own holy symbol were sacred to [Besmara](/besmara) [27, 28]. [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) then casts a healing spell to restore 10 hit points to [Torrin](/torrin)'s back, and the two establish a pact to watch each other's backs [28, 29]. Left alone, [Torrin](/torrin) conjures her fruit bat familiar, [Zema](/zema), who hides inside her coat [32]. 

On deck, [Desmond](/desmond) plays a game of poker [Dice](/dice) with an elven rigger named [Tally](/tally) [33, 34]. Lacking gold, [Desmond](/desmond) wagers a future favor against [Tally](/tally)'s three silver pieces [34, 37]. [Desmond](/desmond) wins the match with a three-of-a-kind, successfully winning the three silver pieces [38, 39]. Later, the party converges at the bow to pool information [40]. [Lanny](/lanny) reveals he has two [Punching Daggers](/punching-daggers) concealed in his waistband, and [Torrin](/torrin) demonstrates her magical abilities by sparking lightning between her fingers [43]. [Lanny](/lanny) explains that he is from a [Goliath](/goliath) clan on the island of [Bogoponda](/bogoponda) and was captured in chains after drifting in a small boat [47, 48].

At nightfall, [Master Scourge](/master-scourge) orders the crew to the sleeping quarters [54]. [Torrin](/torrin) locks her spellbook in a locker chest and pockets the key [58]. The next morning, the ship's bell rings [58]. As the crew prepares to head up, [Lanny](/lanny) notices [Fedor](/fedor), the half-orc arm wrestler, a dwarf, and a dark-haired female pirate loitering near the stairs [59, 60]. [Fedor](/fedor) approaches [Torrin](/torrin), claiming to have something for her from [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg), and suddenly swings a punch at her face [61, 62].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan)**: The cruel commander of [The Wormwood](/the-wormwood). He oversees the morning's punishments but halts [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)'s excessive whipping of [Torrin](/torrin) [7].
*   **[Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)**: The first mate of [The Wormwood](/the-wormwood). Motivated by spite after [Torrin](/torrin)'s defiance, he takes the whip to lashing her himself [6, 7]. He later orders [Fedor](/fedor) to ambush [Torrin](/torrin) below decks [74].
*   **[Master Scourge](/master-scourge)**: The brutal boatswain who administers the lashes to [Torrin](/torrin) and orders the crew below at night [3, 54].
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: A captive cleric of [Besmara](/besmara) who successfully tricks [Quartermaster Grook](/quartermaster-grook) to recover [Torrin](/torrin)'s spellbook, heals [Torrin](/torrin)'s wounds, and forms an alliance with the party [27-29].
*   **[Jack Scrimshaw](/jack-scrimshaw)**: A friendly member of the crew who carves bone art and teaches [Desmond](/desmond) the basics of [Scrimshaw](/scrimshaw) [10, 16].
*   **[Tally](/tally)**: An elven rigger who plays poker [Dice](/dice) with [Desmond](/desmond) and wagers silver against a favor [33, 34].
*   **[Fedor](/fedor)**: A mustachioed human pirate and lackey of [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) [74, 75]. He defeats [Lanny](/lanny) in arm wrestling, but is later cowed by the party after launching a failed ambush on [Torrin](/torrin) [19, 61, 74, 75].
*   **[Quartermaster Grook](/quartermaster-grook)**: The superstitious half-orc quartermaster of [The Wormwood](/the-wormwood) who returns confiscated goods out of fear of angering [Besmara](/besmara) [27].
*   **[Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)** (aka **[Fishguts](/fishguts)**): The ship's cook, found completely incapacitated and snoring drunk behind a barrel in the galley [80, 81].
*   **[Conchobhar Shortstone](/conchobhar-shortstone)**: A gnome captive who expresses his gratitude to [Lanny](/lanny) with a gift of silver [55, 56].

## Locations Visited
*   **[The Wormwood](/the-wormwood)**: The pirate vessel where the party remains trapped under press-ganged service [1].
    *   **The Main Deck & Mast**: The location of the whipping post where [Torrin](/torrin) receives her lashes [3, 4].
    *   **The Sleeping Quarters**: The hammock-filled lower deck where the crew sleeps, and where [Torrin](/torrin) and [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) form their alliance [25, 26].
    *   **The Galley**: The ship's kitchen where [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) finds [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) passed out drunk [80, 81].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **The Lower Deck Ambush**:
    *   **Opponents**: [Fedor](/fedor) and his dark-haired female pirate accomplice [59, 61].
    *   **Notable Tactical Moments**: [Fedor](/fedor) attempts to sucker-punch [Torrin](/torrin) under the guise of delivering an item [61, 62]. [Torrin](/torrin) reacts instantly by casting [Shield](/shield), blocking the strike [62, 63]. 
    *   **The Skirmish**: 
        *   [Torrin](/torrin) retaliates with [Shocking Grasp](/shocking-grasp), delivering 5 points of lightning damage to [Fedor](/fedor)'s gut [64]. 
        *   [Lanny](/lanny) enters a rage and lands an unarmed strike to [Fedor](/fedor)'s face for 10 damage, knocking him off-balance [66, 68].
        *   [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) attempts to strike [Fedor](/fedor) with a [Stolen Dagger](/stolen-dagger) from the galley but misses [67, 68].
        *   [Desmond](/desmond) intercepts [Fedor](/fedor)'s shadow-like female accomplice, using a key point to execute a [Flurry of Blows](/flurry-of-blows) that completely knocks her unconscious [69, 70].
        *   The half-orc wrestler grapples [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) in a bear hug [71], but [Fedor](/fedor) quickly calls a halt to the combat and disengages [72, 73].
    *   **Consequences**: The half-orc releases [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) [73]. Surrounded by [Lanny](/lanny) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), [Fedor](/fedor) is thoroughly intimidated by [Desmond](/desmond) [74]. [Fedor](/fedor) confesses that [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) ordered the attack as revenge for the spitting incident [74]. [Desmond](/desmond) kicks [Fedor](/fedor) in the groin, cowing him completely, and warns him to tell [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) to fight them himself on deck [75]. [Fedor](/fedor) and his allies flee upstairs [76].

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Torrin's Spellbook](/torrins-spellbook)**: Recovered from storage by [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) and returned to [Torrin](/torrin) [27, 28].
*   **Three Silver Pieces**: Won by [Desmond](/desmond) in his poker [Dice](/dice) game against [Tally](/tally) [39].
*   **Three Silver Pieces**: Gifted to [Lanny](/lanny) by [Conchobhar Shortstone](/conchobhar-shortstone) as a reward for saving him from the rigging fall in the previous episode [55, 56].
*   **[Scrimshaw Bone](/scrimshaw-bone)**: A cylindrical piece of bone given to [Desmond](/desmond) by [Jack Scrimshaw](/jack-scrimshaw) after [Desmond](/desmond) successfully carves it [11-13].
