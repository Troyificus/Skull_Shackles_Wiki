# [Skull and Shackles Episode 31: Boar's Blood and a Zombie Toy](/skull-and-shackles-episode-31-boars-blood-and-a-zombie-toy)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 19 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The morning after the necromantic debate, the party prepares for their second day in [Goat's Head](/goats-head). [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) sleeps on the cabin floor of the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl), keeping [Zombie Plugg](/zombie-plugg) covered under a sheet in the corner, while [Lanny](/lanny) snores in his tight hammock.

[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) coordinate a plan to visit [The Cathedral](/the-cathedral) on the north side of town. Seeking to undo [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert)'s tragic mental condition, they decide to take [Albert](/albert) with them to find a specialist in restorative magic. [Lanny](/lanny) delegates duties for the day, granting shore leave to [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) and [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn), while ordering [Conchobhar Shortstone](/conchobhar-shortstone), [Cogwood](/cogwood), and [Tally](/tally) to guard the ship.

[Desmond](/desmond) and [Lanny](/lanny) head out first, seeking trouble in the lower quarters. They enter [The Fat Man's Feed Bag](/the-fat-mans-feed-bag), a run-down tavern with straw on the floor and mismatched furniture. Hoping to lure out a representative of the local crime syndicate, they act loudly and brashly. While no syndicate member approaches, they draw the ire of two hostile half-orcs. A brawl quickly erupts. [Lanny](/lanny) launches a devastating reckless attack with his [Maul](/maul) (without raging), completely shattering the lower half-orc, while [Desmond](/desmond) punches out the second, leaving both half-orcs bloody and unconscious. The bartender, [Craig](/craig), is delighted by the gold coin [Desmond](/desmond) throws him for the mess and shares local rumors.

Meanwhile, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous), [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn), and [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert) walk north. On the way, they pass [Building 21](/building-21) (a local theater) and [The Cracked Tooth Tavern](/the-cracked-tooth-tavern) (number 3), both of which are closed. They observe the [Red Dog Smithy](/red-dog-smithy) (number 10), an open-walled forge where a tall, red-haired blacksmith works under the guard of three massive, short-haired dogs. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) asks [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) to case the smithy for a future break-in. 

They also discuss [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s necromancy. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) argues that "recycling meat" is bad for business in a pirate haven like [Goat's Head](/goats-head), and they shouldn't zombify local armorers because they still need them to make gear. 

The group reaches [The Cathedral](/the-cathedral) (number 15), a stilted stone courtyard building with an open ceiling. [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) is awed by the grand shrine to [Besmara](/besmara) and leaves an offering. In the central courtyard, they meet [Father Torbin](/father-torbin), a cleric dressed in blue robes, alongside a red-haired female cleric. They explain [Albert](/albert)'s condition, and [Father Torbin](/father-torbin) agrees to visit the [Silent Howl](/silent-howl) later that evening to attempt a restorative ritual.

On their return journey, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) stop at [The Buttered Ram](/the-buttered-ram) (number 23), a busy dwarven forge. They are warmly greeted by a young, clean-shaven dwarf wearing spectacles and a welder's mask, who is highly cooperative. [Lanny](/lanny) and [Desmond](/desmond) join them there, and [Lanny](/lanny) pitches a creative idea to the dwarf about forging a massive sword using the jaws of predatory fish.

To wrap up their shopping, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) visit a basic clothing shop. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) purchases two heavy, hooded waterproof cloaks for 4 silver pieces each to disguise [Zombie Plugg](/zombie-plugg). [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) spends 3 gold pieces on two fresh outfits and boots to wash his old garments. The session ends as they coordinate their plans to meet [Father Torbin](/father-torbin) back at the ship.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Father Torbin](/father-torbin)**: A cooperative cleric of the [Cathedral](/cathedral) who agrees to perform restorative magic on [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert).
*   **[Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert)** (aka **[Albert](/albert)**): The mind-wiped goliath-sized pirate, brought to [The Cathedral](/the-cathedral) for help.
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: The cleric who accompanies the group to [The Cathedral](/the-cathedral) to pray to [Besmara](/besmara).
*   **[Craig](/craig)** (aka **[Greg](/greg)**): The bartender at [The Fat Man's Feed Bag](/the-fat-mans-feed-bag).
*   **Clean-Shaven Dwarf**: The friendly young smith at [The Buttered Ram](/the-buttered-ram).

## Locations Visited
*   **[The Fat Man's Feed Bag](/the-fat-mans-feed-bag)**: A dilapidated, mismatched dive bar in [Goat's Head](/goats-head).
*   **[The Cathedral](/the-cathedral)** (number 15): A generic stone temple housing shrines to multiple deities on the north side of town.
*   **[Red Dog Smithy](/red-dog-smithy)** (number 10): A closed-off forge guarded by three massive dogs.
*   **[The Buttered Ram](/the-buttered-ram)** (number 23): A busy dwarven forge with a ram-headed anvil.
*   **[Goat's Head](/goats-head)**: The pirate haven on the [Fire Grass Isles](/fire-grass-isles).

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[The Fat Man's Feed Bag](/the-fat-mans-feed-bag) Brawl**: [Desmond](/desmond) and [Lanny](/lanny) engage and quickly knock out two aggressive half-orcs inside the tavern.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Disguise Cloaks](/disguise-cloaks)**: Two heavy, hooded waterproof cloaks purchased by [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) to hide [Zombie Plugg](/zombie-plugg).
*   **[Fresh Outfits](/fresh-outfits)**: Two sets of clean clothing and boots purchased by [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae).
