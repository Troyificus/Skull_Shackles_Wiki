# [Skull and Shackles Episode 35: Catapulting Zombies and Punching Sharks](/skull-and-shackles-episode-35-catapulting-zombies-and-punching-sharks)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 21 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The crew of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) enjoys their first full day of sailing southwest into the [Fever Sea](/fever-sea). During the afternoon, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) presents the newly identified [Cloak of Protection](/cloak-of-protection) to [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous), who puts it on. She retreats to the [Captain's Quarters](/captains-quarters) to study the magical [Jawbone](/jawbone) scrolls, attempting to copy [Enlarge/Reduce](/enlarge/reduce) into her spellbook. However, she rolls a failure on her Arcana check, and the complex magical runes fade permanently from the bone, leaving her highly frustrated. Seeking a distraction, she begins conducting anatomical experiments on the fresh corpse [Devi](/devi) brought her, cutting off its fingers to test how the blood drains. 

By evening, the ship anchors in open water. While [Desmond](/desmond) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) sleep below deck and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) rests with her undead servants in the cabin, [Lanny](/lanny) drapes his heavy poncho over his shoulders and sleeps in the open air at the prow of the ship. 

In the dead of night, [Lanny](/lanny) is abruptly woken by a wet, clambering sound. He spots a feral [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) (sea orc) stepping onto the deck, followed by a second scaling the port rail. [Lanny](/lanny) attempts to sneakily grab his [Maul](/maul), but rolls an 11 on his Stealth check, alerting the intruders. Instantly, a third [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) climbs up, followed by a massive, battle-scarred, four-armed [Sahuagin Baron](/sahuagin-baron) wielding a heavy, double-headed trident. 

[Lanny](/lanny) lets out a roaring battlecry, entering a rage to engage them. The [Sahuagin Baron](/sahuagin-baron) strikes [Lanny](/lanny) with a devastating critical hit (natural 20) with his trident, but [Lanny](/lanny) uses [Stone's Endurance](/stones-endurance) and his barbarian rage to mitigate the damage. 

The commotion wakes the rest of the ship. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) exits her quarters with [Zombie Plugg](/zombie-plugg) and [Zombie Villahelm](/zombie-villahelm) in tow, immediately locking the hatch to below deck to keep the rest of the vulnerable crew safe. [Desmond](/desmond) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) sprint up from the lower holds just in time to join the fray. 

[Desmond](/desmond) casts [Hex](/hex) on the [Sahuagin Baron](/sahuagin-baron). [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) channels two powerful casts of [Toll the Dead](/toll-the-dead), felling one of the smaller [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) whose head violently implodes as black blood spews from his ears. Seeing his vanguard decimated, the [Sahuagin Baron](/sahuagin-baron) decides that retreat is wise and leaps overboard into the dark ocean. 

Determined not to let his prey escape, [Desmond](/desmond) leaps overboard into the pitch-black water after him. Under the surface, [Desmond](/desmond)'s eyes adjust to reveal a horrifying sight: the [Sahuagin Baron](/sahuagin-baron) has mounted a massive, predatory [Hammerhead Shark](/hammerhead-shark). The Baron thrusts his coral-tipped trident deep into [Desmond](/desmond)'s chest. The wound is severe, and [Desmond](/desmond)'s skin immediately begins to crackle, stiffen, and turn to stone under the weapon's magical petrifying venom. 

Gravely wounded but stubborn, [Desmond](/desmond) fires two crackling bolts of [Eldritch Blast](/eldritch-blast) into the gloomy depths. Above him, [Lanny](/lanny) wild shapes into a massive, shark-skinned bipedal "shark-bear" (using the statistics of a brown bear with sharkskin) and dives into the ocean. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) activates his magical [Magic Ring of Swimming](/magic-ring-of-swimming) and plunges into the water alongside him. 

The trio swims close together in the deep, inky blackness of the [Fever Sea](/fever-sea), illuminated only by [Lanny](/lanny)'s magically glowing weapon. As they scan the darkness, three fast-moving shapes—a shiver of predatory [Sharks](/sharks)—suddenly emerge from the gloom, swimming directly towards them to feed. The episode concludes on a high-stakes, underwater cliffhanger.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Sahuagin Baron](/sahuagin-baron)**: A massive, four-armed sea orc leader wielding a double-headed coral trident; rides a [Hammerhead Shark](/hammerhead-shark).
*   **[Sahuagin](/sahuagin)**: Ferocious, fish-like sea humanoids (referred to as "sea orcs" by the crew) who launch a coordinated midnight raid on the ship.
*   **[Zombie Plugg](/zombie-plugg)**: [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s one-armed undead servant, used as a bodyguard during the deck brawl.
*   **[Zombie Villahelm](/zombie-villahelm)**: [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s newly raised zombie, dressed in chain mail, who assists in the fight.
*   **[Sharks](/sharks)**: A shiver of three hostile, harnessed sharks that surround the party in the deep water.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Blood Wake](/blood-wake)**: The party's newly christened ship, anchoring in the open sea.
*   **[Fever Sea](/fever-sea)**: The deep, treacherous shipping lanes southwest of [Goat's Head](/goats-head).

## Battles & Encounters
*   **Midnight [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) Boarding**: A frantic deck battle where the party defends the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) against a midnight ambush.
*   **Deep Water Chase**: An underwater skirmish where [Desmond](/desmond) pursues the mounted [Sahuagin Baron](/sahuagin-baron) into the depths, leading to the party being surrounded by sharks.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Cloak of Protection](/cloak-of-protection)**: Formally equipped by [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) after identification.
