# [Skull and Shackles Episode 36: Shark Ambush, Sahuagin Skins, and Lightning Bolts](/skull-and-shackles-episode-36-shark-ambush-sahuagin-skins-and-lightning-bolts)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 19 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The session opens in the freezing, pitch-black depths of the [Fever Sea](/fever-sea), where the submerged trio of [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), [Desmond](/desmond), and [Lanny](/lanny) find themselves immediately surrounded by three hostile [Sharks](/sharks). Realising his brother [Desmond](/desmond) is gravely wounded from his previous encounter with the [Sahuagin Baron](/sahuagin-baron), [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) swims over to hand him two [Potions of Greater Healing](/potions-of-greater-healing) before stealthily hiding behind [Lanny](/lanny) to position himself for a sneak attack. 

[Lanny](/lanny) rages and leads the underwater assault, swinging his glowing [Maul](/maul) through the water. Together, the trio defeats the sharks. [Lanny](/lanny) then points upwards, and the group swims back to the surface of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake).

Upon breaking the surface, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) has [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert) throw down a rope to haul the exhausted fighters back on deck. [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) immediately tends to [Desmond](/desmond)'s wounds. [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert) is highly frustrated at being locked below deck during the combat, but the party reassures him of his value to the crew. 

To turn their victory into a psychological advantage, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) decides to harvest the Sahuagin blood into barrels and skin the dead creatures to display their hides on the ship's rails as a warning. [Cogwood](/cogwood) eagerly assists with the skinning. To assist them in the dark, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) transfers her [Light](/light) spell to her bat familiar, [Zima](/zima), who flies above the deck as a tiny, winged beacon.

The following day, the crew sails into calmer waters. During watch, [Cogwood](/cogwood) spots a ship on the horizon—the [True Wind](/true-wind), a merchant vessel. Realising the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) is significantly faster, [Lanny](/lanny) steers the ship to pursue. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) commands [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) to pray for the ship, boosting their speed and allowing them to close the distance to 100 feet.

A boarding action is initiated. [Lanny](/lanny) leaps across the gap onto the deck of the [True Wind](/true-wind), instantly entering a rage and recklessly smashing his [Maul](/maul) into a [Dragonborn Bodyguard](/dragonborn-bodyguard)'s face, dealing massive damage. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) casts [Misty Step](/misty-step) to teleport onto the deck behind a surrendering guard, holding her action to cast [Shocking Grasp](/shocking-grasp) if anyone resists. 

Seeing their bodyguard fall, the crew of the [True Wind](/true-wind) surrenders. The captain of the vessel begs for mercy and asks [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) to spare a critically wounded female crew member. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) complies, casting [Spare the Dying](/spare-the-dying) on the woman. The crew of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) plunders the ship's cargo, seizing a wealth of tools, farming equipment, textiles, and metals.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[True Wind Crew](/true-wind-crew)**: The merchant sailors aboard the captured vessel who quickly surrender to the party.
*   **[Dragonborn Bodyguard](/dragonborn-bodyguard)**: A formidable defender of the merchant vessel who is brutally beaten down by [Lanny](/lanny).
*   **[Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert)**: The party's powerhouse ally who assists in throwing ropes and guarding the ship.
*   **[Zima](/zima)**: [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s bat familiar used to provide light on deck.
*   **[Cogwood](/cogwood)**: The crew's rigger who assists [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) in skinning the dead Sahuagin.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Fever Sea](/fever-sea)**: The open ocean where the party battles underwater sharks and sails in search of prey.
*   **[True Wind](/true-wind)**: The captured merchant vessel carrying manufactured goods.
*   **[Blood Wake](/blood-wake)**: The party's main ship.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[The Underwater Shark Fray](/the-underwater-shark-fray)**: A tense, murky battle against three trained sharks in the depths of the Fever Sea.
*   **[Boarding of the True Wind](/boarding-of-the-true-wind)**: A fast-paced boarding action that culminates in the complete surrender of the merchant crew.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[True Wind Cargo](/true-wind-cargo)**: A substantial hoard of manufactured tools, copper, salt, clothing, and farming equipment.
*   **[Sahuagin Hides](/sahuagin-hides)**: Harvested by [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) and [Cogwood](/cogwood) to decorate the ship's rails.
