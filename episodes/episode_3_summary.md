# [Skull and Shackles Episode 3: Cheating the Owlbear and Captain Harrigan’s Punch](/skull-and-shackles-episode-3-cheating-the-owlbear-and-captain-harrigans-punch)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 2 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The third day aboard the [Wormwood](/wormwood) begins with the party facing their mandatory morning tasks. Expecting a harsh altercation with [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) following [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s public defiance the previous evening, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) is instead assigned to the galley to assist [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) (aka [Fishguts](/fishguts)), who is hopelessly incapacitated by drink. Tasked with preparing the day's main meal, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) wrangles together a turtle recipe, successfully preparing a decent turtle soup with the help of [Desmond](/desmond), who assists from his high vantage point up in the rigging. 

Meanwhile, [Lanny](/lanny) and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) are assigned to rat-catching duty in the lower holds. They easily manage the chore, and [Lanny](/lanny) spends his time bizarrely trying to "interrogate" a bewildered rat.

After the evening meal, tensions boil over on deck. It is revealed that every threat and insult directed at [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) by the party has been reported to [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan). Rather than lying low, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) flagrantly repeats his insubordinate comments directly in front of the captain and the assembled crew. Enraged by the disrespect, [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) delivers a single, devastating punch that knocks [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) unconscious. [Desmond](/desmond) instinctively steps forward to defend his twin brother, but [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) holds him back, convincing him to show restraint as the officers draw their weapons—including a heavy crossbow. [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) declares that any threat to his officers is a personal threat to him, deeming the matter closed before retiring to his quarters.

Attempting to defuse the palpable tension on deck, [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) suggests some sport and offers a purse of gold to anyone who can defeat his personal champion, the lumbering, simple-minded goliath-sized gerridae [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert) (aka [Albert](/albert)), in a boxing match. Still furious over his brother's injury, [Desmond](/desmond) eagerly accepts the challenge.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan)**: The ferocious captain of the [Wormwood](/wormwood) who demands complete obedience and knocks [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) out for his insubordination.
*   **[Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)**: Cruel first mate of the [Wormwood](/wormwood) who reports insubordination to the captain and wagers a gold purse on a boxing match to mock the party.
*   **[Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert)** (aka **[Albert](/albert)**): [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)'s simple-minded, physically imposing champion who fights [Desmond](/desmond).
*   **[Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)** (aka **[Fishguts](/fishguts)**): The drunken ship's cook whose inebriation forces [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) to take over the galley.
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: Cleric of [Besmara](/besmara) who watches over the party's antics and assists in caring for the wounded.
*   **[Corki](/corki)**: The young cabin girl who arrives to collect and taste-test the captain's food.

## Locations Visited
*   The **[Wormwood](/wormwood)**: The pirate vessel currently sailing the open seas.
    *   **[The Galley](/the-galley)**: Where [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) cooks the turtle meal.
    *   **[The Bilges/Lower Deck](/the-bilges/lower-deck)**: Where [Lanny](/lanny) and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) catch rats.
    *   **[The Main Deck](/the-main-deck)**: The location of the morning roll call, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)'s confrontation, and [Desmond](/desmond)'s boxing match.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) vs. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)**: The captain summarily knocks [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) out with a single punch to enforce discipline.
*   **[Desmond](/desmond) vs. [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert)**: A brutal boxing match on the main deck. [Albert](/albert) strikes first and hard, but [Desmond](/desmond)'s superior speed allows him to counter. During the bout, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) cheats by secretly casting [Blindness/Deafness](/blindness/deafness) on [Albert](/albert). Blinded, the gerridae swings wildly at the air. Just as the spell wears off, [Desmond](/desmond) executes a final, decisive combination, finishing with a heavy blow to the back of [Albert](/albert)'s head to knock the giant out cold.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Purse of Gold](/purse-of-gold)**: A bounty offered by [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg), won by [Desmond](/desmond) after knocking out [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert).
