# [Skull and Shackles Episode 40: Chainmail Zombies Wake the Black Dragon](/skull-and-shackles-episode-40-chainmail-zombies-wake-the-black-dragon)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 23 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The session begins with the party gathering at the [Hunter's Lodge](/hunters-lodge) to finalise their expedition details [748]. After consulting with the local hunters, they learn that the creature [Black Fang](/black-fang) is an aggressive winged beast that has been terrorising hunting parties on the western side of the island [715, 747]. To facilitate their journey, they purchase two days' worth of [Rations](/rations) for [5 gold pieces](/5-gold-pieces) and rent a [Horse and Cart](/horse-and-cart) [714, 715, 748]. To accommodate the uncomfortable [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn), she is permitted to sit on the front bench of the cart, far away from [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s disguised [Zombies](/zombies) riding in the back [748].

The party travels south along the overgrown forest trail until the foliage becomes too dense for the cart [748, 765, 766]. As they begin to establish their campsite for the evening, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)'s keen senses detect rapid movement in the nearby underbrush [748]. Suddenly, a stampede of wild [Boars](/boars) thunders out of the forest, charging directly at their camp [748, 749]. [Lanny](/lanny), [Desmond](/desmond), [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) spring into action, quickly slaughtering the aggressive beasts [749, 755]. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) observes that the boars were not attacking them out of hunger, but rather fleeing in absolute panic from a larger predator [749].

Moments later, their suspicions are confirmed by a deafening roar [749]. High above the forest canopy, a massive, dark, winged shape—revealed to be a black [Dragon](/dragon)—takes flight, carrying a dead boar in its massive talons before soaring south into the night [749].

As the party recovers from the shock, a hooded stranger steps out of the dark forest with his bow drawn [750]. He introduces himself as [Dark Wolf](/dark-wolf), the legendary [Shadowhunter](/shadowhunter) (whose real name is [Copeland](/copeland)), claiming he is also hunting [Black Fang](/black-fang) for the bounty [750, 752, 758, 776]. Fearing he will steal their prize, the party takes an instant dislike to him [776]. When [Desmond](/desmond) aggressively steps forward to pull down his hood, [Dark Wolf](/dark-wolf) interprets it as a hostile act and shoots a quick arrow, triggering a brief battle [776]. 

The party easily outnumbers and overpowers the hunter [755, 776]. They disarm, bind, and lock [Dark Wolf](/dark-wolf) in heavy manacles [753, 776]. After a tense interrogation, they realize he is relatively bumbling under his edgy persona, so they graciously release him, leaving him to nurse his bruised ego while they take a long rest to prepare for the morning's hunt [759, 764, 776, 777].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Dark Wolf](/dark-wolf)** (aka **[Copeland](/copeland)**): A highly theatrical, self-proclaimed "shadowhunter" who attempts to claim the dragon bounty alone [750, 752, 758, 776].
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: The party's cleric companion who sits up front on the cart to avoid the creepy, disguised undead [748].

## Locations Visited
*   **[Hunter's Lodge](/hunters-lodge)**: The outpost in [Little Upara](/little-upara) where the party acquires their cart and supplies [748].
*   **[Wilderness Campsite](/wilderness-campsite)**: The dense, dark forest clearing south of the colony where the party makes camp [748].

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[Boars](/boars) Stampede**: A fast-paced skirmish where the party easily slaughters a charging pack of wild forest boars [748, 749].
*   **[Dark Wolf](/dark-wolf) Standoff**: A brief, one-sided altercation triggered when [Desmond](/desmond) tries to unhood the bumbling shadowhunter, ending with the party manacling him [753, 776].

## Loot & Rewards
