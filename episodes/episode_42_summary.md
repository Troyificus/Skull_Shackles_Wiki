# [Skull and Shackles Episode 42: Celebrities, New Crew, and the Druid's Awakening](/skull-and-shackles-episode-42-celebrities-new-crew-and-the-druids-awakening)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 19 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Following their legendary triumph over the two black dragons, the party returns to [Little Upara](/little-upara) as renowned slayers. While [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) remain on the outskirts of town to hide their undead servants, [Desmond](/desmond) and [Lanny](/lanny), accompanied by [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn), parade the massive [Dragon Heads](/dragon-heads) through the streets. The spectacle draws a huge crowd of cheering townsfolk and fascinated children. 

The boys march to [The Arena](/the-arena) and meet with the eccentric dwarven proprietor, [Stephanie Garrison](/stephanie-garrison). They finalize a contract to participate in the upcoming main event: a high-stakes battle against a captured, highly dangerous [Venom Troll](/venom-troll) in two days. [Desmond](/desmond) negotiates a deal that if they win, [Stephanie Garrison](/stephanie-garrison) will treat the entire [Blood Wake Crew](/blood-wake-crew) to a lavish meal of roast meats at a fine tavern. Outside [The Arena](/the-arena), [Desmond](/desmond) addresses the crowd, building hype for the show and urging them to buy tickets.

Upon returning to the docks, they spot [Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth) waving from the deck of his grand galleon, the [Pinion](/pinion). After a brief, cordial reunion where [Pegsworth](/pegsworth) congratulates them on their dragon slays, the party climbs aboard the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake). 

There, they are introduced to their newly recruited crew members, found in the local taverns by [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) and [Johnny](/johnny): the [Fiska](/fiska) brothers ([Crawl Fiska](/crawl-fiska), [Frank Fiska](/frank-fiska), and [Dutch Fiska](/dutch-fiska)) and a talented half-orc bard named [Benoch](/benoch). To test the grit of the new recruits, [Lanny](/lanny) initiates a round of mild hazing, delivering a solid punch to [Crawl Fiska](/crawl-fiska). [Desmond](/desmond) welcomes them to the crew and shares a batch of his questionable, home-brewed [Mushroom Beer](/mushroom-beer). He lays down the ship's absolute laws: no stealing, no harming crewmates, and complete loyalty, warning them that violating these rules carries a penalty of death.

As night falls, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) slip back aboard with [Zombie Villahelm](/zombie-villahelm) and the other undead thralls, successfully stowing them in the bilges. The dragon heads are safely locked in the [Captain's Quarters](/captains-quarters) under zombie guard. The session concludes with a major mechanical breakthrough as [Lanny](/lanny) taps into his primal connection with nature, officially taking a level in [Druid](/druid), allowing him to [Wild Shape](/wild-shape) while in a barbarian [Rage](/rage).

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Stephanie Garrison](/stephanie-garrison)**: The dwarven owner of the [Little Upara](/little-upara) colosseum who signs the party to fight the [Venom Troll](/venom-troll).
*   **[Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth)**: The friendly peg-legged pirate captain of the [Pinion](/pinion) who celebrates the party's success.
*   **[Crawl Fiska](/crawl-fiska)**: A slender new crew recruit who endures [Lanny](/lanny)'s test of strength.
*   **[Frank Fiska](/frank-fiska)**: A new sailor who joins the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake).
*   **[Dutch Fiska](/dutch-fiska)**: Another of the new sailor brothers.
*   **[Benoch](/benoch)**: A half-orc bard recruited to entertain the crew and bolster morale.
*   **[Johnny](/johnny)**: The peppy half-elf crew member who helped recruit the new sailors.
*   **[Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)**: The halfling rigger who welcomes the new recruits with her fiddle.
*   **[Zombie Villahelm](/zombie-villahelm)**: [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s raised zombie servant, dressed in chain mail and a hooded cloak.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Little Upara](/little-upara)**: The dapper Taldan colony where the party is currently docked.
*   **[The Arena](/the-arena)**: The bustling colosseum where the upcoming troll fight is scheduled.
*   **[Blood Wake](/blood-wake)**: The party's swift, newly renamed ship.
*   **[Captain's Quarters](/captains-quarters)**: The secure cabin where the dragon heads are stored.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[New Recruit Hazing](/new-recruit-hazing)**: A brief test of grit where [Lanny](/lanny) punches the slender [Crawl Fiska](/crawl-fiska) to verify his toughness before officially welcoming him.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Dragon Heads](/dragon-heads)**: Two massive heads of the slain black dragons, kept as trophies and potential figureheads.
*   **[New Crew Recruits](/new-crew-recruits)**: Four capable new sailors ([Crawl Fiska](/crawl-fiska), [Frank Fiska](/frank-fiska), [Dutch Fiska](/dutch-fiska), and [Benoch](/benoch)) officially join the ship's roster.
