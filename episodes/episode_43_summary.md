# [Skull and Shackles Episode 43: Scribing Spells and the Colosseum Clash](/skull-and-shackles-episode-43-scribing-spells-and-the-colosseum-clash)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 20-21

## Plot Summary
The morning after their return, the party plans their next moves. With the looming threat of the spectral captain [Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk) and his ghost ship, the [Death Knell](/death-knell), chasing them, they debate seeking an alliance or researching a way to put him down permanently. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) decides to utilize her downtime to research necromancy and [Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk) at local arcane shops.

Before heading into town, [Desmond](/desmond) and [Lanny](/lanny) deliver the two dragon heads to [White Hall](/white-hall), the headquarters of the [Town Guard](/town-guard). They meet with [Captain Haley](/captain-haley), who is thoroughly impressed by their proof. He coordinates with [Lady Olandra](/lady-olandra)'s estate to secure their payment of [5,000 gold pieces](/5000-gold-pieces). While at [White Hall](/white-hall), the boys chat with the guards about the [Venom Troll](/venom-troll). The guards reveal a vital tactical clue: the troll possesses highly acidic, poisonous blood that sprays violently out of its wounds whenever it is struck in melee. 

Armed with this knowledge, [Desmond](/desmond) and [Lanny](/lanny) visit [The Copper Chalice](/the-copper-chalice) to buy exceptionally fine, aristocratic clothing for their upcoming dinner with the duchess. Meanwhile, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) and [Desmond](/desmond) visit [Chorga's Quill](/chorgas-quill), the local spell scribe. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) sells the massive, engraved [Ivory Walrus Tusk](/ivory-walrus-tusk) / [Whalebone](/whalebone) they salvaged from the grindylow caves, which contains [Alter Self](/alter-self), [Blur](/blur), and [Color Spray](/color-spray) spells, to fund her purchase of spell scrolls. [Lanny](/lanny) and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) also visit [Ellie the Blacksmith](/ellie-the-blacksmith) at the [Red Dog Smithy](/red-dog-smithy) to discuss forging a colossal greatsword out of predatory fish parts.

That evening, the main event arrives. The [Blood Wake Crew](/blood-wake-crew) enters [The Arena](/the-arena) to a deafening roar as the crowd chants "[Skull Crusher](/skull-crusher)!". They face off against the massive, slimy [Venom Troll](/venom-troll). The battle is brutal; every time [Lanny](/lanny) and [Desmond](/desmond) strike the beast, a spray of corrosive [Troll Venom](/troll-venom) bursts from its flesh, burning [Desmond](/desmond) and [Lanny](/lanny) for [7 poison damage](/7-poison-damage). [Desmond](/desmond) strategically casts [Hex](/hex) to weaken the troll's strength. Unleashing his new druidic powers, [Lanny](/lanny) goes into a primal rage, transforming his hands into savage [Claws](/claws) to deliver devastating strikes. The episode ends as the party closes in for the kill amidst the roaring cheers of the colosseum.
