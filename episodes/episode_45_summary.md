# [Skull and Shackles Episode 45: Crimson Cray and Besmara's Warning](/skull-and-shackles-episode-45-crimson-cray-and-besmaras-warning)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 25 - Day 29

## Plot Summary
Following their elegant dinner with [Lady Olandra](/lady-olandra), the party departs her estate carrying a heavy wooden chest containing [5,000 gold pieces](/5000-gold-pieces) [876, 878]. As they exit the gates into the cool night air, [Lanny](/lanny) sheepishly admits to the twins that he never actually wanted the duchess's peacock as a pet—he simply wanted to roast and eat it [878]. The party heads back to the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) to count and divvy up their massive dragon-slaying haul, celebrating their largest financial score to date [879].

With their pockets lined with platinum and gold, the party spends the following day on a lavish shopping spree in [Little Upara](/little-upara) [876, 880]. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) visits [Fox's Armors and Shields](/foxs-armors-and-shields) to place a custom order for a suit of dapper studded leather armor made from their harvested black dragon hide [880]. He leaves a hefty [500 gold pieces](/500-gold-pieces) deposit for the custom gear, which is designed to hold both "smoldering" and "mariner's" magical properties [880]. In a generous gesture of friendship, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) also takes [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) and [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) to [The Copper Chalice](/the-copper-chalice) fine clothing boutique, instructing the shopkeeper to let the ladies pick out any fine outfits they desire on his tab [880]. Meanwhile, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) attempts to purchase a second-level spell scroll of [Knock](/knock) from the magic shop [Gigi's Tinctures](/gigis-tinctures) [883]. She rolls a disappointing 14 (failing the 15 DC check) and is instead sold a useless scroll that disintegrates into a sticky substance resembling "pva glue" and congealed spider webs [883, 884].

Looking ahead, the party plans a return expedition to the northern [Abandoned Temple](/abandoned-temple) where they fought the black dragons to fully harvest their carcasses for bone decorations, scales, and hide [881, 884]. However, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) faces a major necromantic logistical problem: she has two active zombies, [Zombie Plug](/zombie-plug) and [Zombie Villahelm](/zombie-villahelm), who require her to reassert control over them every 24 hours [885]. Unable to leave them unattended on the ship without risk of them going rogue, [Lanny](/lanny) and the twins help [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) pack the manacled zombies into large wooden barrels to smuggle them along in their rented horse-and-cart [885, 886]. 

During the journey north, [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) sits on the front bench of the cart next to [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) [887]. Fascinated and only mildly unsettled by the cargo, [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) strikes up a theological conversation about necromancy, asking [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) about the exact limits of the magic and "how dead" a corpse must be for animate spells to function [887]. They spend the night camped in the wilderness before re-entering the temple the next morning [888].

Inside the cavern lair, [Lanny](/lanny) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) set to work skinning the dragon remains, collecting scales and removing the massive leathery wings to fashion a custom cloak for [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) [891]. Meanwhile, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) explores the spider-infested side tunnels [888]. She discovers the rotted skeleton of a canine-like [Gnoll](/gnoll) and performs a minute-long ritual to raise it as her third skeletal thrall [888, 889]. [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) watches the process and admits that clean skeletons are far less creepy and a much easier sell to the crew than rotting zombies [889, 890]. The party also burns away residual flesh from the dragon bones using acid to prep them as ship trophies [890]. Before departing, the group senses an ominous, powerful vibration emanating from the mysterious closed door in the cavern [892]. Acting on a bad vibe, [Desmond](/desmond) urges the party to leave immediately, and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) reluctantly backs out of the temple with her newly expanded undead entourage in tow [892].

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: The cleric who questions [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) about her magic and finds skeletons more acceptable than zombies [887, 889].
*   **[Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)**: Treated to a fine clothing shopping spree by [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) [880].
*   **[Lady Olandra](/lady-olandra)**: The duchess who paid the party their bounty [876].

*   **[Little Upara](/little-upara)**: The Taldan colony where the party spends their gold [876].
