# [Skull and Shackles Episode 48: Weaponizing Rats and Hiding Skeletons](/skull-and-shackles-episode-48-weaponizing-rats-and-hiding-skeletons)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Not explicitly specified, but takes place shortly after departing the colony of [Little Upara](/little-upara).

## Plot Summary
The session begins in the immediate aftermath of the battle against the ghost ship. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) (also known as [Miss Blackheart](/miss-blackheart)) retires to the captain's quarters on the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) with the magical [Bell of the Death Knell](/bell-of-the-death-knell). To neutralize its unnatural necromantic power, she detaches its [Clapper](/clapper) and wraps the pieces in thick cloth, hiding them in her cabin.

While inside her quarters, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) realizes that the mysterious [Golden Twin-Plated Dagger](/golden-twin-plated-dagger) she had been keeping in a drawer is missing. She immediately confronts her companions, suspecting a theft. The tension is defused when it is revealed that [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) (now calling himself [Crimson Cray](/crimson-cray)) has the dagger safely tucked away in his pocket. They share a brief, serious conversation regarding the nature of [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s dark deity, [Myrkul](/myrkul).

Meanwhile, [Lanny](/lanny) (known to the crew as [Captain Skull Crusher](/captain-skull-crusher)) descends into the cargo hold of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) to check on the ship's resident rats. Using his druidic magical ability [Speak with Animals](/speak-with-animals), [Lanny](/lanny) holds a formal meeting with [Rat Damon](/rat-damon) (also referred to as [Damien](/damien)) and the rest of the rat population. [Lanny](/lanny) successfully negotiates a pact with the rodents: in exchange for protection and food, the rats agree to serve as an onboard security force and defend the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) if the vessel is ever boarded by hostile forces.

After [Lanny](/lanny) feeds his new allies with bread and crackers, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) reassures the rest of her skeleton crew on deck, and the party officially sets sail southwest into the open ocean, steering directly into the shipping lanes.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Rat Damon](/rat-damon)**: The intelligent, leading resident rat of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) who swears eternal loyalty to [Lanny](/lanny)'s crew in exchange for food and safety.
*   **[Myrkul](/myrkul)**: The dark god of the dead whom [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) serves and prays to for guidance.
*   **[Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk)**: The spectral captain of the ghost ship mentioned during the recap.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Blood Wake](/blood-wake)**: The party's primary ship, currently sailing southwest into the shipping lanes.
