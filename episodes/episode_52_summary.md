# [Skull and Shackles Episode 52: Sea Shanties and the Shark Bear](/skull-and-shackles-episode-52-sea-shanties-and-the-shark-bear)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 27

## Plot Summary
As the morning mist parts, a **[Rahadoumi Slavers](/rahadoumi-slavers)** merchant schooner becomes visible in the distance. Eager to pursue, **[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)** casts **[Animate Dead](/animate-dead)** to raise four of the slain feral invaders as **[Zombies](/zombies)**—including **[Zombie Drunk](/zombie-drunk)** and **[Zombie Pirate](/zombie-pirate)**—to help crew the vessel. They begin chasing the fleeing ship.

To prepare for boarding, **[Torrin](/torrin)** uses her **[Driftwood Wand](/driftwood-wand)** to cast **[Mirror Image](/mirror-image)** on herself and commands her **[Skeletons](/skeletons)** to rain down shortbow arrows. **[Lanny](/lanny)** utilizes his new **[Wild Shape](/wild-shape)** ability to transform into a massive, shark-skinned "shark-bear" and leaps across to the enemy deck.

**[Dez](/dez)** casts **[Hex](/hex)** on the defenders and utilizes **[Tipsy Sway](/tipsy-sway)** to make the enemies attack their own allies. **[Torrin](/torrin)** casts a massive **[Tidal Wave](/tidal-wave)** across the enemy deck, knocking multiple defenders prone. Though the shark-bear takes heavy damage and Lanny reverts to his goliath form, the crew successfully subdues the defenders, including the dragonborn bodyguard **[Kiliaks](/kiliaks)** and **[Lady Zyra Viniloa](/lady-zyra-viniloa)**. Searching the vessel, **[Dez](/dez)** descends into the lower hold and is shocked to discover a hidden cargo area packed with young children and teenagers forced to man the ship's oars.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Rahadoumi Slavers](/rahadoumi-slavers)**: Faction of the captured vessel.
*   **[Kiliaks](/kiliaks)**: The noble dragonborn bodyguard who defends **[Lady Zyra Viniloa](/lady-zyra-viniloa)**.
*   **[Lady Zyra Viniloa](/lady-zyra-viniloa)**: The captain/proprietor of the captured schooner.
*   **[Zombies](/zombies)**: Raised by **[Torrin](/torrin)** to bolster the boarding crew.
