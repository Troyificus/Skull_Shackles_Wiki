# [Skull and Shackles Episode 53: Adopting Cultists and Skinning the Captain](/skull-and-shackles-episode-53-adopting-cultists-and-skinning-the-captain)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Days 28 to 30

## Plot Summary
Standing in the dark hold, **[Dez](/dez)** confronts the terrified captives while the injured slaver captain tries to attack him, prompting **[Dez](/dez)** to grapple and non-lethally subdue him. As **[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)** joins them, the teenagers make a hand gesture and call her "**[Mother](/mother)**" in gurgling grunts. Guided by a newly discovered conscience, the party decides to rescue the 20 to 30 children rather than abandoning them. They plan to tow the captured ship to **[Genzai](/genzai)** in the **[Ushinawa Isles](/ushinawa-isles)** to deliver them safely to a local monastery.

To sail both vessels, they split their crew: **[Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)** (aka **[Croup](/croup)**), **[Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell)**, **[Balak](/balak)**, **[Dez](/dez)**, and two of the **[Fiska](/fiska)** brothers remain aboard the ***[Blood Wake](/blood-wake)***, while **[Lanny](/lanny)**, **[Torrin](/torrin)**, **[Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)**, and **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)** pilot the towed prize ship with the help of the **[Zombies](/zombies)** pulling the rigging. Before setting sail, they harvest the tattooed skin map from the dead female spellcaster to locate **[Mancatcher Cove](/mancatcher-cove)**, and **[Sandara](/sandara)** performs a respectful pirate burial for the deceased enemies, throwing gold coins into the sea. **[Torrin](/torrin)** uses **[Detect Magic](/detect-magic)** to sweep the vessel for hidden arcane dangers.
