# [Skull and Shackles Episode 54: The Spinning Wizard and the Ghost Captain](/skull-and-shackles-episode-54-the-spinning-wizard-and-the-ghost-captain)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 31 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
Following their intense encounter in the slave hold, the party deals with the rescued children and teenagers. After learning their former captain had been slain, the children are terrified. However, after some magical display—including a brief exchange of threats, a lightning form, and some healing—the children begin to view [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) as a protective figure, calling her [Mother](/mother) as a form of honorific [1104, 1105](/1104-1105). 

The crew leaves a single sailor to look after them below deck, and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) and [Desmond](/desmond) return topside to discuss their options [1104](/1104). [Lanny](/lanny) (aka [Captain Skull Crusher](/captain-skull-crusher)) helps repair the holes on the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) while [Desmond](/desmond) and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) organize the crew to begin sailing, towing the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) behind their newly captured, larger vessel [1105](/1105). 

Seeking to bolster their ranks to sail both ships, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) prays to [Myrkul](/myrkul) (referred to in-session as [Merkel](/merkel)) and uses [Animate Dead](/animate-dead) to raise several of the slain feral invaders as [Zombies](/zombies) to help pull ropes and crew the vessel [1105](/1105). After a long day, they drop anchor. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) is plagued by insomnia and sleep evades her [1105](/1105). She paces the [Captain's Quarters](/captains-quarters), checking on her undead servants, and investigates the cabin, finding it messy and smelling of decades of rot [1107](/1107). 

Her control over her skeletons and zombies is limited, so she realizes she must permanently destroy several of her zombies (the yellow-marked [Yellow Zombies](/yellow-zombies)) whose 24-hour control limit is about to expire [1108](/1108). She commands them to stand still and has [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) and [Lanny](/lanny) decapitate and shred them [1109, 1110](/1109-1110). 

Up in the crow's nest, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) is on watch through the night, scanning the horizon and the water below for any green spectral glows or fog [1111](/1111). At dawn, [Desmond](/desmond) puts the [Clapper of the Death Knell](/clapper-of-the-death-knell) around his neck next to his [Dull Gray Metal Necklace](/dull-gray-metal-necklace) (the deep platinum pendant). They do not react or spark [1112](/1112). 

[Lanny](/lanny) conducts an investigation of the ship's hold and discovers the decomposing corpse of the ship's former captain, who died from a blunt blow to the back of the skull about six months to a year prior [1113, 1114](/1113-1114). [Lanny](/lanny) drags the body onto deck and dumps it overboard [1114](/1114). 

The party sets sail toward the [Ushinawa Isles](/ushinawa-isles), expecting to reach port by the next evening according to [Tally](/tally)'s navigational calculations [1115, 1123](/1115-1123). [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) remains awake and exhausted, prompting [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) to tell her she looks like shit [1115](/1115). [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) fetches her an experimental potion from [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)'s stock—the [White Cap Nightcap](/white-cap-nightcap)—which Croup warns will keep someone up for 24 hours but eventually knock them out [1116](/1116). Torrin drinks it and also a shot of Croup's nastiest alcohol, eventually collapsing in a drunken sleep [1116, 1124](/1116-1124). 

The twins and [Lanny](/lanny) sneak into [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s cabin and find her fast asleep. They discuss the [Golden Twin-Plated Dagger](/golden-twin-plated-dagger), which has mysteriously reappeared on her belt despite her having wrapped it up and locked it in a drawer [1124](/1124). [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) takes the dagger to keep it safe from further harm [1124](/1124). 

Later, [Desmond](/desmond) investigates the giant skull figurehead mounted on the prow of the ship, realizing it is far too large and oddly shaped to be a normal dragon's skull [1125](/1125). Suddenly, the [Bell of the Death Knell](/bell-of-the-death-knell) begins to vibrate violently and glow green in [Lanny](/lanny)'s hands [1126, 1127](/1126-1127). As [Desmond](/desmond) and [Lanny](/lanny) desperately try to dismantle and detach the bell with a hammer and chisel, the wood splinters, and a massive green spectral tornado erupts [1127, 1128](/1127-1128). Out of the bell, [Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk) materializes in a terrifying, spectral form, ready to exact his ghostly vengeance [1128](/1128).

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)** (aka **[Lady Blackheart](/lady-blackheart)**): The wizard who is suffering from severe sleep deprivation and experimenting with undead and the mysterious golden dagger.
*   **[Lanny](/lanny)** (aka **[Captain Skull Crusher](/captain-skull-crusher)**): The goliath barbarian/druid captain who investigates the ship's hold and holds the vibrating bell.
*   **[Desmond](/desmond)** (aka **[Dez](/dez)**): The monk who attempts to help detach the bell and wears the clapper.
*   **[Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)** (aka **[Devi](/devi)**): The rogue who steals the cursed dagger to keep it safe and watches from the crow's nest.
*   **[Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)**: The ship's cook who provides the [White Cap Nightcap](/white-cap-nightcap) brew.
*   **[Tally](/tally)**: The crew's navigator who directs them toward the [Ushinawa Isles](/ushinawa-isles).
*   **[Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk)**: The legendary undead captain of the [Death Knell](/death-knell) who manifests from the magical bell.
*   **[Zombies](/zombies)** / **[Skeletons](/skeletons)**: The undead thralls raised by [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous).
*   **[Myrkul](/myrkul)**: The god of the dead to whom [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) prays.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Blood Wake](/blood-wake)**: The party's flagship, currently towing their newly captured vessel.
*   **[Fever Sea](/fever-sea)**: The open waters they are currently sailing.
