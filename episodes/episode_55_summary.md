# [Skull and Shackles Episode 55: The Shark Bear and Torrin’s Divine Betrayal](/skull-and-shackles-episode-55-the-shark-bear-and-torrins-divine-betrayal)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 31 - Day 32 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The session picks up directly with the terrifying manifestation of [Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk) on the deck of the ship. Erupting from the cracked [Bell of the Death Knell](/bell-of-the-death-knell), his spectral form expands as a tornado of green ghostly spirits and trapped souls swirls around him [1130](/1130). 

[Pilk](/pilk) unleashes a "Dreadful Visage," a terrifying wave of horror across the deck [1131](/1131). While [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) and [Lanny](/lanny) successfully resist the fear, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) and [Desmond](/desmond) are completely paralyzed with terror [1131](/1131). Because [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) is paralyzed, her undead skeletons and zombies are unable to receive orders and stand motionless on deck [1132](/1132). 

[Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) fires a magical arrow from his longbow at [Pilk](/pilk), which successfully damages the spectral captain despite his ghostly form [1133](/1133). [Lanny](/lanny) casts [Entangle](/entangle) using his newly gained druid magic, sending spectral vines to wrap around the ghost captain, but [Pilk](/pilk) manages to break free [1133](/1133). [Pilk](/pilk) retaliates by throwing his spectral harpoon at [Lanny](/lanny). The harpoon pierces [Lanny](/lanny)'s side, dealing a massive combination of piercing and necrotic damage (34 points total), leaving the goliath heavily wounded [1134](/1134). 

At the end of his turn, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) is finally free of her paralysis [1134, 1135](/1134-1135). [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) emerges from the below-deck hatch, successfully braves the dreadful presence (taking only minor damage), and rushes to [Lanny](/lanny)'s side to cast a healing spell [1138, 1140](/1138-1140). Reinvigorated, [Lanny](/lanny) activates his [Wild Shape](/wild-shape) and transforms into a massive, shark-skinned "shark bear" to claw and tear at the ghost captain [1137, 1140](/1137-1140). 

[Pilk](/pilk) attacks [Lanny](/lanny) again with his spectral harpoon, dealing heavy damage and forcing the goliath out of his shark-bear form, leaving him on the brink of death at only 1 hit point [1140, 1141, 1144](/1140-1141-1144). [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) draws the magical [Diamond](/diamond) from her pocket and channels her arcane power to cast a powerful spell [1140](/1140), while [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) lets loose a critical arrow from his bow, dealing 23 damage and nearly tearing [Pilk](/pilk)'s spectral body apart [1141](/1141). [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) yells at the party to "destroy that fucking Bell!" [1141](/1141) 

[Lanny](/lanny) reaches deep into the chest cavity of [Pilk](/pilk) and rips out his essence, causing a massive, blinding explosion of green light [1142](/1142). With [Pilk](/pilk) destroyed, the tattered souls he had trapped for centuries are finally freed, drifting up into the sky and whispering "thank you" as a soul named "Murray" is released [1143](/1143). The only things left of the ghost captain are tattered ash and the cracked [Bell of the Death Knell](/bell-of-the-death-knell), now split completely into two pieces [1143](/1143). 

[Desmond](/desmond) uses his monk abilities to perform a final banishing rite over the cracked bell, casting [Besmara](/besmara)'s name to banish [Pilk](/pilk)'s soul to the deep bilges of the sea wraith forever [1145](/1145). To break the tension, [Lanny](/lanny) playfully uppercuts [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) for his "good shot with the arrows" and plants a kiss on his bald head [1146](/1146). The crew emerges from the hatch, celebrating their survival [1147](/1147). 

In a massive roleplay breakthrough, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) stands on deck staring at the [Myrkul](/myrkul) skull necklace around her neck. Feeling betrayed by the god of death who did not help her, she casts [Shocking Grasp](/shocking-grasp) on the skull, completely disintegrating it into dust and throwing it into the sea, officially denouncing her devotion to [Myrkul](/myrkul) [1148, 1149](/1148-1149). 

In an act of complete defiance, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) unhooks the cursed [Golden Twin-Plated Dagger](/golden-twin-plated-dagger) from her belt and hurls it into the ocean [1150](/1150). Instantly, she is struck by a wave of excruciating psychic damage (9 points, followed by 6 points), causing her to bleed from her ears and weep blood as the dagger's curse retaliates [1150, 1151, 1152](/1150-1151-1152). [Lanny](/lanny) dives into the ocean with his 40-foot swim speed, retrieves the cursed dagger, and stabs it into the wooden deck in front of [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous), telling her she must keep it for now [1151, 1152](/1151-1152). Torrin takes another 10 points of psychic damage [1153](/1153). 

[Desmond](/desmond) kneels in front of her, urging her to call upon [Besmara](/besmara) for help, promising that the pirate goddess can protect her from the curse [1154](/1154). Supported by her friends, a weeping, bleeding [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) finally whispers: "help me please," requesting [Besmara](/besmara)'s aid before passing out [1155](/1155).

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Whalebone Pilk](/whalebone-pilk)**: The terrifying spectral whaler captain who is finally destroyed for good.
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: The cleric who provides vital healing to [Lanny](/lanny) during the fight.
*   **[Myrkul](/myrkul)**: The god of death whom [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) denounces.
*   **[Besmara](/besmara)**: The pirate goddess whose aid [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) begs for at her lowest moment.
