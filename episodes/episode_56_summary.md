# [Skull and Shackles Episode 56: Divine Divorce and the Rat Jesus Incident](/skull-and-shackles-episode-56-divine-divorce-and-the-rat-jesus-incident)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Days 32 to 35 (approximate)

## Plot Summary
The session opens as [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) lies unconscious on deck [1155, 1158](/1155-1158). In her sleep, she experiences a vivid, terrifying vision of [Myrkul](/myrkul) standing above her in smoking black robes. However, she actively turns her back on the god of death and accepts the warm, guiding light of [Besmara](/besmara) [1158](/1158). [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) wakes up fully recovered and free of the psychic pain [1158](/1158). Wrapping her hand securely in her cloak, she pulls the cursed [Golden Twin-Plated Dagger](/golden-twin-plated-dagger) out of the deck and has [Lanny](/lanny) hurl it as far as he can into the sky over the ocean [1159](/1159). In mid-air, she unleashes a brilliant white orb of lightning, completely vaporizing the cursed dagger [1159, 1176, 1177](/1159-1176-1177). The magical energy has transformed from a creepy, necrotic black into a brilliant, pure white. 

The party holds a massive discussion about their deific experiences. They realize that [Myrkul](/myrkul) may have been the very threat [Besmara](/besmara) sent [Desmond](/desmond) back to fight, or that the pirate queen strategically poached [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) to weaken her divine rival [1160](/1160). [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) formally aligns with [Besmara](/besmara), noting that while she is not her slave, she will happily live by the tenets of piracy and take what she wants [1161, 1162](/1161-1162). The crew jokes about getting matching [Besmara](/besmara) tattoos to commemorate their divine connections [1163](/1163). 

[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) casts [Animate Dead](/animate-dead) to reassert her control over her 4 [Skeletons](/skeletons) and 2 surviving [Zombies](/zombies) (which are safely stowed away in the [Captain's Quarters](/captains-quarters) of the new ship) [1163, 1164](/1163-1164). [Desmond](/desmond) throws one half of the cracked [Bell of the Death Knell](/bell-of-the-death-knell) into the sea to ensure it is lost forever, keeping the other half [1164](/1164). 

[Lanny](/lanny) heads to the hold of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) and ritually casts [Speak with Animals](/speak-with-animals) to commune with his tiny friend [Rat Damon](/rat-damon), feeding the ship's rats cheese and bread [1165, 1166, 1167](/1165-1166-1167). 

They set sail northeast, arriving at [Genzai](/genzai) on the [Ushinawa Isles](/ushinawa-isles) in the late afternoon [1169, 1172](/1169-1172). At [Genzai](/genzai), the party conducts major business: they sell their captured Rahadoumi merchant ship and their accumulated cargo of plunder, giving them a massive payout of gold [1171, 1172](/1171-1172). Accompanied by [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn), [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) visit the local temple/monastery and successfully offload the 20 to 30 rescued slave children [1170, 1177](/1170-1177), paying the monks a generous donation of [5 platinum pieces](/5-platinum-pieces) and [68 gold pieces](/68-gold-pieces) to ensure they receive a safe upbringing and education [1185](/1185). They pay off the [Blood Wake Crew](/blood-wake-crew) and commission full repairs to the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake), choosing to keep the smaller, nimbler vessel over the massive, slow merchant ship [1171, 1172](/1171-1172). 

[Lanny](/lanny) studies [Cyrus Wolfe's Skin Map](/cyrus-wolfes-skin-map) with a local cartographer, who identifies the island cluster as [Mancatcher Cove](/mancatcher-cove), located directly north of their current position [1177, 1187](/1177-1187). 

[Desmond](/desmond) finishes brewing his experimental beverage and eagerly drinks it [1175, 1177, 1178](/1175-1177-1178). The brew has a scaly, glittery taste, and [Desmond](/desmond) wakes up the next day realizing he has grown shiny, protective scales on the inside of his throat [1178](/1178). 

The next morning, [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) runs onto deck and alerts [Lanny](/lanny) that their newest, overly cheerful crew member, [Johnny](/johnny), is missing and hasn't been seen since they docked [1188](/1188). [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) casts a [Sending](/sending) spell to contact [Johnny](/johnny) [1189](/1189). [Johnny](/johnny) replies that she has decided to stay on the island and seek placement on another ship [1189](/1189). [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) runs around the docks for 10 minutes trying to say goodbye, but no local captains have heard of her [1190, 1191](/1190-1191). The party decides she left of her own free will and prepares to set sail for [Mancatcher Cove](/mancatcher-cove) [1191](/1191).

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Johnny](/johnny)** (aka **[Janny](/janny)**): The cheerful crew member who unexpectedly departs the crew at [Genzai](/genzai).
*   **[Rat Damon](/rat-damon)**: [Lanny](/lanny)'s rat friend below deck.
*   **[Myrkul](/myrkul)** & **[Besmara](/besmara)**: The competing deities whose conflict has directly shaped the party's destiny.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Genzai](/genzai)**: The peaceful, wooden-built monastery town in the [Ushinawa Isles](/ushinawa-isles).
*   **[Mancatcher Cove](/mancatcher-cove)**: The northern treasure site identified on [Cyrus Wolfe's Skin Map](/cyrus-wolfes-skin-map).

## Battles & Encounters
*   **[Parley with the Monks](/parley-with-the-monks)**: A social encounter where the party successfully negotiates a safe haven for the rescued children in exchange for a massive gold donation.

## Loot & Rewards
*   **[Cyrus Wolfe's Skin Map](/cyrus-wolfes-skin-map)** Coordinates: The exact location of [Cyrus Wolfe](/cyrus-wolfe)'s treasure cache, identified as [Mancatcher Cove](/mancatcher-cove).
*   **[Scaly Throat Mutation](/scaly-throat-mutation)**: A unique physical mutation gained by [Desmond](/desmond) after drinking his experimental brew, granting him scales inside his throat.
