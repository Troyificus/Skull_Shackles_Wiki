# [Skull and Shackles Episode 58: Shark Cavalry and the Greased Barbarian](/skull-and-shackles-episode-58-shark-cavalry-and-the-greased-barbarian)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 36

## Plot Summary
While anchored in the cove, the crew is alerted to an underwater threat. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) casts [Thaumaturgy](/thaumaturgy) to sound a warning bell, and [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) scales the mast to the [Crow's Nest](/crows-nest) with his [Longbow](/longbow) [1165, 1166](/1165-1166). Three [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) ("fishy fuckers") are spotted under the water, apparently drawn to the [Deep Platinum Pendant](/deep-platinum-pendant) worn by [Lanny](/lanny) [1167](/1167). [Lanny](/lanny) walks the rails with his extendable lance to secure the deck [1168](/1168). 

They determine that they must wait for the sun to rise to locate the path to the treasure, as indicated by the riddle's reference to the "grave lady" [Pharasma](/pharasma) (the Lady of Graves) [1169, 1170](/1169-1170). The party plans to explore the smaller islands clockwise and bring [Owlbear Albert](/owlbear-albert) and [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) along in a rowboat to keep an eye on [Albert](/albert)'s behavior [1171, 1172, 1173, 1174](/1171-1172-1173-1174). [Desmond](/desmond) uses persuasion to speak with [Albert](/albert), who reveals his original captain died and was buried outside of the shackles [1175, 1176](/1175-1176). Before leaving, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) casts [Animate Dead](/animate-dead) to reassert control over her [Zombies](/zombies) [1176](/1176).

After a full day of searching the smaller islands clockwise, they find nothing and return to the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) for the night [1177](/1177). At dawn, the rising sun passes directly between two islands, casting a warm ray of light. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) and [Lanny](/lanny) witness a bright glint reflecting off a rock face deep inside the cove [1178](/1178). They identify the glint is coming from a sheer, 100-foot cliff. The riddle's instruction to *"climb the captain's Wayward orb"* points them to an eye-like cave opening on the rock face [1179](/1179).

They pack the [Steadfast Grapple](/steadfast-grapple) and decide to bring four [Zombies](/zombies) as expendable front-line fodder [1180](/1180). They set off in two rowboats, utilizing [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)'s magical [Tricorn Hat](/tricorn-hat) boat [1182, 1183](/1182-1183). [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) climbs the cliffside first. Inside a crevice, he discovers a massive, unformed chunk of gold ([Gold Deposit](/gold-deposit)) that had caused the sunrise reflection [1184](/1184). At the cliff's peak, they locate two narrow, five-foot-wide tunnel openings in the rock (one left, one right) and a rotten wooden stump where an old beam was once wedged [1184, 1186](/1184-1186). Using his spyglass, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) checks back on the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) anchored offshore [1187](/1187).

They choose the left tunnel first. Inside, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) finds a narrow choke point and a large tree with roots shaped like a bearded, grimacing face [1188](/1188). [Lanny](/lanny) uses his massive strength to force his way through the tree roots [1190](/1190). Using his [Maul](/maul), he smashes through a wooden plank floor hidden beneath the dirt. A plank shatters and falls down a dark shaft, followed by the sound of a distant splash below [1191](/1191).
