# [Skull and Shackles Episode 59: Sahugin Ambush and the Shackled Prisoner](/skull-and-shackles-episode-59-sahugin-ambush-and-the-shackled-prisoner)
**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 36 (continues immediately after Ep 58)

## Plot Summary
Staring down into the dark abyss of the shattered deck, the party prepares to descend [1194](/1194). [Desmond](/desmond) dives down the flooded shaft first. While exploring underwater, his [Eldritch Blast](/eldritch-blast) illuminates the dark water, revealing a [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) warrior [1195](/1195). [Lanny](/lanny) joins him underwater, entering a [Rage](/rage) and reckless-attacking a [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) with his [Maul](/maul) to deal **29 damage**, instantly pulverising it into a "Red Mist" against the tunnel wall [1199](/1199). He then strikes the second one for 13 damage [1199](/1199).

[Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) slides past [Lanny](/lanny) and uses [Insightful Fighting](/insightful-fighting) against the second [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) [1200](/1200). He stabs it with his magical rapier, [Brine's Sting](/brines-sting) (aka [Brine Sting](/brine-sting)), for **27 damage**, killing it instantly [1200](/1200). A third [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) attacks [Lanny](/lanny) with a spear and bites him, but [Lanny](/lanny) reckless-attacks again, delivering **13** and **16 damage** to finish off the remaining attackers [1201, 1202](/1201-1202). One of the [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) misses [Devi](/devi) with a natural 1, wedging its spear in the ceiling before [Lanny](/lanny) squishes it [1203](/1203).

[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) writes a warning note on parchment referencing "sea Goblins" and sends her bat familiar [Zima](/zima) back to the rowboat crew [1197](/1197). They explore the flooded tunnels. The left path is a dead end, but the right path slopes up at a 45-degree angle [1204](/1204). Breaking the surface, they find a damp, air-filled pocket containing rotted, broken furniture [1205, 1206](/1205-1206).

Proceeding further, [Desmond](/desmond) and [Lanny](/lanny) discover a small underwater armory littered with weaponry [1207, 1208](/1207-1208). A hidden [Giant Crab](/giant-crab) (aka [Grind Crab](/grind-crab)) ambushes them [1209](/1209). During the scuffle, [Desmond](/desmond) skillfully parries, forcing the crab's claws to stab itself in its own face until it dies [1210](/1210). [Desmond](/desmond) returns to the air pocket to breathe, while [Lanny](/lanny) wonders if they can safely open their [Bag of Holding](/bag-of-holding) underwater [1210](/1210).

[Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) swims ahead and spots a warm, orange-pink glint coming from a deep chamber [1211](/1211). Entering the chamber, they discover a bizarre scene: dozens of strange organic filaments running floor-to-ceiling, holding about 50 floating, fist-sized pink egg-like ovoids that twitch with movement [1211, 1212](/1211-1212). Looking down a narrow tunnel at the rear of the chamber, they find a fish-like humanoid with crested fins and orange coloration shackled to the far wall. The session ends on a high-stakes cliffhanger as they realize it is *not* a [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) [1213](/1213)!

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Sahuagin](/sahuagin)**: The sea-dwelling warriors guarding the flooded tunnels of the cove [1195, 1198](/1195-1198).
*   **[Giant Crab](/giant-crab)** (aka the **[Grind Crab](/grind-crab)**): A massive crab disguised among the armory weaponry that ambushes [Desmond](/desmond) and [Lanny](/lanny) [1209](/1209).
*   **[Zima](/zima)**: Torrin's bat familiar used to ferry messages back to the surface party [1197](/1197).
*   **[Shackled Prisoner](/shackled-prisoner)**: A mysterious, orange-scaled, crested-finned aquatic humanoid bound to the wall of the back chamber [1213](/1213).
*   **[Zombies](/zombies)**: Torrin's undead thralls waiting at the entrance [1207](/1207).

## Locations Visited

*   **[Grind Crab Ambush](/grind-crab-ambush)**: A quick, claustrophobic melee in the armory where [Desmond](/desmond) forces the crab to fatally stab itself [1210](/1210).
