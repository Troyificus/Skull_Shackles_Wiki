# [Skull and Shackles Episode 9: Officer Blackheart and Plugg's Mutiny](/skull-and-shackles-episode-9-officer-blackheart-and-pluggs-mutiny)

**In-Game Time/Days passed:** Day 15 (Two weeks passed aboard the [Wormwood](/wormwood))

## Plot Summary
The morning aboard the [Wormwood](/wormwood) begins with the daily morning inspection. Before heading up to the deck, [Lanny](/lanny) shares a remarkable revelation with [Desmond](/desmond), [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae), and [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous). Using a 10-minute ritual of [Speak with Animals](/speak-with-animals), [Lanny](/lanny) has established a bond with his rat, [Mr. Rat](/mr-rat). Furthermore, by casting [Beast Sense](/beast-sense), he was able to peer through the eyes of [Mr. Rat](/mr-rat) and spy on the [Captain's Quarters](/captains-quarters). [Lanny](/lanny) discovered that [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) slept on the floor, while the ship's mysterious magic-user, [Arita the Witch](/arita-the-witch), slept in the captain's bed. 

Upon reaching the deck, the daily routine is interrupted when [Fedor](/fedor) drags the lifeless body of [Japes Magpie](/japes-magpie) (aka [Jape](/jape)) into the middle of the deck, claiming [Jape](/jape) died in his hammock from battle wounds. [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) remains completely indifferent to the death, and [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) simply shrugs. [Jape](/jape) is subsequently sewn into an old sailcloth by [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell) with [Fedor](/fedor)'s assistance, joining [Badger](/badger) the barber, who also perished.

Shortly after, [Master Scourge](/master-scourge) brings the eight [Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi) prisoners onto the deck in chains. One of the captives speaks out defiantly, warning the captain of severe diplomatic consequences for attacking a sovereign ship of the [Rahadumi](/rahadumi) nation. Enraged by the defiance, [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) wraps his massive hand around the spokesman's face like a "facehugger", lifts him off the ground, and hurls him overboard. [Rhiaris](/rhiaris) fires a heavy crossbow bolt into the water; a brief scream is heard, followed by silence as the sharks consume him. [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) offers the remaining seven prisoners a simple choice: volunteer to join his crew or join their comrade in the shark-infested waters. The captives immediately volunteer, and [Master Scourge](/master-scourge) unlocks their manacles.

A sea burial is held for [Japes Magpie](/japes-magpie) and [Badger](/badger). [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn) steps forward to say a few words and tosses a coin into the ocean. The bodies are thrown overboard, and the crew follows suit by tossing coins. [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) throws a coin; [Desmond](/desmond) tosses a coin, whispering "sorry mate"; [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) performs a blood ritual, cutting her hand with a [Dagger](/dagger) to smear blood on a coin, touching it to her forehead, dedicating it to her lord [Myrkul](/myrkul), and flicking it into the water; and [Lanny](/lanny) tosses two coins—one for [Badger](/badger) and one for [Japes Magpie](/japes-magpie).

[Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) then announces a division of the crew. The captured merchant vessel, the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise), is to sail north to [Port Peril](/port-peril) with a skeleton crew captained by [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) to be sold. [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) selects his skeleton crew, which consists of [Aretha](/aretha) (the female pirate squab who slept in the officers' quarters), [Fedor](/fedor), [The Dwarf Spy](/the-dwarf-spy), the ship's cook [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) (aka [Fishguts](/fishguts)), the "newbie pirates" ([Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn), [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell), [Conchobhar Shortstone](/conchobhar-shortstone), [Cogwood](/cogwood), and the four adventurers), and three of the [Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi) prisoners.

Before boarding, [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) goes to the galley to inform [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) of his reassignment. [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop) tearfully hugs [Grook](/grook) the quartermaster, who gifts him a large [Cask of Rum](/cask-of-rum). [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) retrieves his magical [Grappling Hook](/grappling-hook) from [Grook](/grook)'s store, and [Lanny](/lanny) safely recovers [Mr. Rat](/mr-rat). On deck, [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) negotiates with [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) for a [Diamond](/diamond) worth 50 gold pieces to serve as her spellcasting focus, promising to prove her magical value to him.

Once aboard the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise), [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) warns the crew from the poop deck that duties will be strictly enforced, and any failure will be punished far more severely than on the [Wormwood](/wormwood). Privately in his cabin, [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) offers [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) an officer position as his personal mage, under the condition that she spies on the twins and reports any insubordination. [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) immediately informs [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) and [Desmond](/desmond) of [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)'s scheme, explaining that she will play along to gain his trust and use her position to benefit the group. She heals [Desmond](/desmond)'s wounds by casting [Cure Wounds](/cure-wounds) using her skull necklace, which pulses with black energy.

The party holds a secret meeting to discuss a potential mutiny against [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg). They list potential allies—including [Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop), [Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn), [Rosie Cusswell](/rosie-cusswell), and [Cogwood](/cogwood)—while deciding that [Conchobhar Shortstone](/conchobhar-shortstone) could be useful as bait. The session ends with a massive twist: [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) suddenly spins the ship's wheel 90 degrees to the right, steering the vessel away from [Port Peril](/port-peril). He confesses to [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) that he plans to steal the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise) for himself, defying [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan), and demands her loyalty.

## Key NPCs & Factions
*   **[Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan)**: The brutal captain of the [Wormwood](/wormwood) who executes a [Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi) prisoner and splits his crew.
*   **[Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg)** (aka **[Gunter Plug](/gunter-plug)**): The treacherous first mate of the [Wormwood](/wormwood) who takes command of the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise) and hatches a plan to steal the ship for himself.
*   **[Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)**: The party's wizard who is promoted to officer/mage under [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) and acts as a double agent for the party.
*   **[Lanny](/lanny)**: The goliath barbarian who uses his unique magical connection to communicate with [Mr. Rat](/mr-rat).
*   **[Sandara Quinn](/sandara-quinn)**: A priestess of [Besmara](/besmara) who leads the sea burial for [Japes Magpie](/japes-magpie) and [Badger](/badger).
*   **[Ambrose Kroop](/ambrose-kroop)** (aka **[Fishguts](/fishguts)**): The ship's cook who is reassigned to the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise) and receives a [Cask of Rum](/cask-of-rum) from [Grook](/grook).
*   **[Fedor](/fedor)**: A loyal lackey of [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) who drags [Japes Magpie](/japes-magpie)'s body to the deck.
*   **[Grook](/grook)**: The quartermaster of the [Wormwood](/wormwood) who returns [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae)'s [Grappling Hook](/grappling-hook).
*   **[The Dwarf Spy](/the-dwarf-spy)**: A loitering dwarf selected by [Fedor](/fedor) to join the skeleton crew.
*   **[Rahadumi](/rahadumi)** / **[Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi)**: The faction of the merchant vessel captured by the [Wormwood](/wormwood) crew.

## Locations Visited
*   **[Wormwood](/wormwood)**: The pirate ship where the session begins.
*   **[Man's Promise](/mans-promise)**: The newly captured [Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi) vessel that the party boarding crew sets sail on.
*   **[Port Peril](/port-peril)**: The planned destination to sell the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise), which [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) ultimately decides to bypass.

## Battles & Encounters
*   **Facehugger Execution**: [Captain Barnabas Harrigan](/captain-barnabas-harrigan) crushes the skull of a defiant [Rahadoumi](/rahadoumi) spokesman with his bare hand and tosses him to the sharks.
