# [[Factions]]

This file documents the major groups, organizations, and crews encountered by the [[Blood Wake]] crew throughout Golarion and the [[Shackles]].

---

## [[Blood Wake Crew]]
*   **Leader:** [[Captain Skull Crusher]] ([[Lanny]]) [18, 21, 42]
*   **Type:** Pirate Crew / Freelance Merchants [38, 42]
*   **Description:** A tight-knit band of mutineers who seized control of the [[Man's Promise]] (renamed the [[Sea Breeze]]) from [[Mr. Plugg]] [17, 18], sold it to [[House Carthagen]] [24], and purchased the sleeker [[Silent Howl]] (renamed the [[Blood Wake]]) [24, 34]. Under [[Lanny]]'s captaincy, they operate on principles of honor, equal pay, and mutual defense [17, 33, 44].

## [[House Carthagen]]
*   **Representative:** [[Mr. Girdan Danan]] [24]
*   **Type:** Noble/Merchant House [24]
*   **Description:** An elegant, elite family based in [[Blood Cove]] known to consist of blood-drinking vampires [24]. They operate a major local sugar press and purchased the newly-refitted [[Sea Breeze]] from the party for [[4,000 gold pieces]] [24].

## [[Chelaxian Navy]] (House of Cheliax)
*   **Notable Vessels:** [[The Dominator]] (Three-decked Man-of-War) [25, 26]
*   **Type:** Imperial Military [26, 38]
*   **Description:** A massive, lawful empire to the north that strictly enforces anti-piracy laws [25, 26, 38]. They are notorious for hunting down Golarion's freelancers with heavy artillery and massive fleets, but their patrol warship, [[The Dominator]], was successfully sabotaged and set on fire by [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s alchemist's fire infiltration [25, 26].

## [[Rahadoumi Slavers]]
*   **Notable Leaders:** [[Lady Zyra Viniloa]], [[Kiliaks]] (Dragonborn Bodyguard) [52]
*   **Type:** Illegal Slaver Syndicate [52, 53]
*   **Description:** Merchants and mercenary forces hailing from Rahadoum who utilize long, fat, rowing-oar merchant schooners to transport captured children and teenagers forced into labor [52, 53]. After their vessel was boarded and captured by the [[Blood Wake]], the cargo of 20 to 30 children was rescued and delivered to a monastic haven in [[Genzai]] [53, 56].

## [[Sahuagin]] (Sea Goblins)
*   **Leader:** [[Sahuagin Baron]] (Four-armed boss) [35, 60]
*   **Type:** Aquatic Hostiles [35, 59, 60]
*   **Description:** Feral, fish-like humanoids with webbed extremities and needle-like teeth who ambush waterlogged ships, raid coastal coves, and worship ancient underwater forces [29, 35, 59]. The party defeated a massive settlement of them inside [[Mancatcher Cove]], slaying their four-armed Baron and harvesting their blood and hides [35, 36, 60].
