# [Factions](/factions)

This file documents the major groups, organizations, and crews encountered by the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake) crew throughout Golarion and the [Shackles](/shackles).

---

## [Blood Wake Crew](/blood-wake-crew)
*   **Leader:** [Captain Skull Crusher](/captain-skull-crusher) ([Lanny](/lanny)) [18, 21, 42]
*   **Type:** Pirate Crew / Freelance Merchants [38, 42]
*   **Description:** A tight-knit band of mutineers who seized control of the [Man's Promise](/mans-promise) (renamed the [Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze)) from [Mr. Plugg](/mr-plugg) [17, 18], sold it to [House Carthagen](/house-carthagen) [24], and purchased the sleeker [Silent Howl](/silent-howl) (renamed the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake)) [24, 34]. Under [Lanny](/lanny)'s captaincy, they operate on principles of honor, equal pay, and mutual defense [17, 33, 44].

## [House Carthagen](/house-carthagen)
*   **Representative:** [Mr. Girdan Danan](/mr-girdan-danan) [24]
*   **Type:** Noble/Merchant House [24]
*   **Description:** An elegant, elite family based in [Blood Cove](/blood-cove) known to consist of blood-drinking vampires [24]. They operate a major local sugar press and purchased the newly-refitted [Sea Breeze](/sea-breeze) from the party for [4,000 gold pieces](/4000-gold-pieces) [24].

## [Chelaxian Navy](/chelaxian-navy) (House of Cheliax)
*   **Notable Vessels:** [The Dominator](/the-dominator) (Three-decked Man-of-War) [25, 26]
*   **Type:** Imperial Military [26, 38]
*   **Description:** A massive, lawful empire to the north that strictly enforces anti-piracy laws [25, 26, 38]. They are notorious for hunting down Golarion's freelancers with heavy artillery and massive fleets, but their patrol warship, [The Dominator](/the-dominator), was successfully sabotaged and set on fire by [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous)'s alchemist's fire infiltration [25, 26].

## [Rahadoumi Slavers](/rahadoumi-slavers)
*   **Notable Leaders:** [Lady Zyra Viniloa](/lady-zyra-viniloa), [Kiliaks](/kiliaks) (Dragonborn Bodyguard) [52]
*   **Type:** Illegal Slaver Syndicate [52, 53]
*   **Description:** Merchants and mercenary forces hailing from Rahadoum who utilize long, fat, rowing-oar merchant schooners to transport captured children and teenagers forced into labor [52, 53]. After their vessel was boarded and captured by the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake), the cargo of 20 to 30 children was rescued and delivered to a monastic haven in [Genzai](/genzai) [53, 56].

## [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) (Sea Goblins)
*   **Leader:** [Sahuagin Baron](/sahuagin-baron) (Four-armed boss) [35, 60]
*   **Type:** Aquatic Hostiles [35, 59, 60]
*   **Description:** Feral, fish-like humanoids with webbed extremities and needle-like teeth who ambush waterlogged ships, raid coastal coves, and worship ancient underwater forces [29, 35, 59]. The party defeated a massive settlement of them inside [Mancatcher Cove](/mancatcher-cove), slaying their four-armed Baron and harvesting their blood and hides [35, 36, 60].
