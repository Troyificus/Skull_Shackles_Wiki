# [[Locations]]

Key ports, islands, and structures charted by the [[Blood Wake]].

---

## [[Port Peril]]
*   **Region:** The [[Shackles]] (Capital) [1]
*   **Description:** The massive, bustling pirate capital of Golarion governed by [[Hurricane King Karch Bonefist]] [1, 21]. It was here that our four heroes were drugged and press-ganged into the crew of the [[Wormwood]] [1, 24].

## [[Rickety's Squibs]]
*   **Proprietor:** [[Rickety Hake]] [18]
*   **Region:** Slithering Coast [18]
*   **Description:** A secretive, heavily camouflaged dry dock situated in an estuary where ships are "squibbed" (given entirely new physical identities and papers to hide them from their former owners) [18]. It contains a camouflaged lookout tower manned by [[Lyle the Lookout]] [20] and is flanked by thick, wasp-infested jungles [19, 20].

## [[Blood Cove]]
*   **Region:** Southern Golarion (Outside the [[Shackles]]) [21]
*   **Description:** A prosperous port city built into the massive roots of Golarion's tallest mangrove tree, operating primarily on trade, commerce, and non-piratical business [21]. It houses [[Chidux's Gambling Den]] [24], the [[Khaveh Exports]] trade house [21], [[The Blood Root]] tavern [22], and [[Umbertine Arms and Armor]] [22].

## [[Goat's Head]]
*   **Region:** [[Fire Grass Isles]] [20, 30]
*   **Description:** A lawless, rowdy pirate sanctuary with no formal guard presence or laws [30]. It is the home port of [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]] [20]. Key establishments include **[[The Rusty Kraken]]** (lodging run by a black-haired barmaid with white streaks) [30], **[[The Fat Man's Feed Bag]]** (a dilapidated dive bar) [31], **[[The Hagfish]]** (famed for [[Nora]]'s slime-drinking challenge) [32], and **[[The Cathedral]]** (stilted courtyard of multiple faiths run by [[Father Torbin]]) [31].

## [[Little Upara]]
*   **Region:** Taldan Colony [37, 38]
*   **Description:** A highly fancy, civilized, and multi-tiered noble settlement constructed upon vertical pillars of rock connected by wooden bridges and rope pulleys [38]. It is ruled by [[Lady Olandra]] from her sprawling cliffside estate [44]. Key spots include [[White Hall]] (town guard HQ) [43], [[The Arena]] (gladiator colosseum) [38], [[Gigi's Tinctures]] [45], [[The Copper Chalice]] fine clothing [43], and [[Fox's Armors and Shields]] [45].

## [[Mancatcher Cove]]
*   **Region:** North of [[Ushinawa Isles]] [56]
*   **Description:** A perfectly round, bowl-shaped island cove with 100-foot sheer cliffs draped in an overgrown vine canopy [58]. It is the fabled burial site of [[Cyrus Wolfe]]'s old king's hoard, guarded by a deep, flooded cavern system and an active tribe of [[Sahuagin]] [58, 59, 60].
