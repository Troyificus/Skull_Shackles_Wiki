# [Locations](/locations)

Key ports, islands, and structures charted by the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake).

---

## [Port Peril](/port-peril)
*   **Region:** The [Shackles](/shackles) (Capital) [1]
*   **Description:** The massive, bustling pirate capital of Golarion governed by [Hurricane King Karch Bonefist](/hurricane-king-karch-bonefist) [1, 21]. It was here that our four heroes were drugged and press-ganged into the crew of the [Wormwood](/wormwood) [1, 24].

## [Rickety's Squibs](/ricketys-squibs)
*   **Proprietor:** [Rickety Hake](/rickety-hake) [18]
*   **Region:** Slithering Coast [18]
*   **Description:** A secretive, heavily camouflaged dry dock situated in an estuary where ships are "squibbed" (given entirely new physical identities and papers to hide them from their former owners) [18]. It contains a camouflaged lookout tower manned by [Lyle the Lookout](/lyle-the-lookout) [20] and is flanked by thick, wasp-infested jungles [19, 20].

## [Blood Cove](/blood-cove)
*   **Region:** Southern Golarion (Outside the [Shackles](/shackles)) [21]
*   **Description:** A prosperous port city built into the massive roots of Golarion's tallest mangrove tree, operating primarily on trade, commerce, and non-piratical business [21]. It houses [Chidux's Gambling Den](/chiduxs-gambling-den) [24], the [Khaveh Exports](/khaveh-exports) trade house [21], [The Blood Root](/the-blood-root) tavern [22], and [Umbertine Arms and Armor](/umbertine-arms-and-armor) [22].

## [Goat's Head](/goats-head)
*   **Region:** [Fire Grass Isles](/fire-grass-isles) [20, 30]
*   **Description:** A lawless, rowdy pirate sanctuary with no formal guard presence or laws [30]. It is the home port of [Captain Merrill Pegsworth](/captain-merrill-pegsworth) [20]. Key establishments include **[The Rusty Kraken](/the-rusty-kraken)** (lodging run by a black-haired barmaid with white streaks) [30], **[The Fat Man's Feed Bag](/the-fat-mans-feed-bag)** (a dilapidated dive bar) [31], **[The Hagfish](/the-hagfish)** (famed for [Nora](/nora)'s slime-drinking challenge) [32], and **[The Cathedral](/the-cathedral)** (stilted courtyard of multiple faiths run by [Father Torbin](/father-torbin)) [31].

## [Little Upara](/little-upara)
*   **Region:** Taldan Colony [37, 38]
*   **Description:** A highly fancy, civilized, and multi-tiered noble settlement constructed upon vertical pillars of rock connected by wooden bridges and rope pulleys [38]. It is ruled by [Lady Olandra](/lady-olandra) from her sprawling cliffside estate [44]. Key spots include [White Hall](/white-hall) (town guard HQ) [43], [The Arena](/the-arena) (gladiator colosseum) [38], [Gigi's Tinctures](/gigis-tinctures) [45], [The Copper Chalice](/the-copper-chalice) fine clothing [43], and [Fox's Armors and Shields](/foxs-armors-and-shields) [45].

## [Mancatcher Cove](/mancatcher-cove)
*   **Region:** North of [Ushinawa Isles](/ushinawa-isles) [56]
*   **Description:** A perfectly round, bowl-shaped island cove with 100-foot sheer cliffs draped in an overgrown vine canopy [58]. It is the fabled burial site of [Cyrus Wolfe](/cyrus-wolfe)'s old king's hoard, guarded by a deep, flooded cavern system and an active tribe of [Sahuagin](/sahuagin) [58, 59, 60].
