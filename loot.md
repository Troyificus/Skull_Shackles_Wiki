# [[Loot and Notable Items]]

Key magical treasures, armaments, and unique items salvaged during the voyage.

---

## [[Brine's Sting]]
*   **Type:** Magical Rapier [59, 60]
*   **Properties:** Highly refined, magical blade designed for swift underwater combat. It deals severe piercing damage and was used by [[Devi McRae]] to deliver a devastating [[49 points of damage]] to the [[Sahuagin Baron]] [59, 60].

## [[Steadfast Grapple]]
*   **Type:** Magical Climbing Gear [16, 18]
*   **Properties:** A magical grappling hook identified by [[Torrin Horrendous]] [18]. It secure-mounts to virtually any surface and was used to abseil down the 50-foot cliffs of [[Ongongta Overlook]] [26].

## [[Shackles of Compliance]]
*   **Type:** Magical Manacles [18]
*   **Properties:** Magical shackles that enforce obedience and submissiveness upon those bound by them. Salvaged from [[Wormwood]] officers [18].

## [[Driftwood Wand]] (Wand of Mirror Image)
*   **Type:** Charged Arcane Wand [16]
*   **Properties:** A magical wand constructed from a bone-inscribed piece of driftwood. It was recovered from the [[Grindylow Cave]] hidden chamber and contains [[23 charges]] of the [[Mirror Image]] spell [16].

## [[Spiked Gauntlets]]
*   **Type:** Magical Combat Wear [51]
*   **Properties:** A pair of leather-and-steel gauntlets tipped with dual spikes, salvaged from a feral pirate leader. They grant a [[+1 bonus to hit and damage]], and deal an additional [[2d6 piercing damage]] on a critical hit [51, 1115]. Equipped by [[Desmond]] [1117].

## [[Cloak of Patches]]
*   **Type:** Utility Magical Garment [60]
*   **Properties:** A patch-covered cloak. Tearing off a patch instantly transforms it into a real-world object. Includes a **[[Sack Patch]]** which Lanny tore off to create a real canvas sack to gather treasure [60].

## [[Troll Venom]]
*   **Type:** Toxic/Acidic Harvest [44]
*   **Properties:** Three highly toxic doses of pressurized acidic blood harvested by [[Devi McRae]] from the slain colosseum [[Venom Troll]] [44].
