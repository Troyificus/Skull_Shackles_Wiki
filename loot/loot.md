# [Loot and Notable Items](/loot-and-notable-items)

Key magical treasures, armaments, and unique items salvaged during the voyage.

---

## [Brine's Sting](/brines-sting)
*   **Type:** Magical Rapier [59, 60]
*   **Properties:** Highly refined, magical blade designed for swift underwater combat. It deals severe piercing damage and was used by [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) to deliver a devastating [49 points of damage](/49-points-of-damage) to the [Sahuagin Baron](/sahuagin-baron) [59, 60].

## [Steadfast Grapple](/steadfast-grapple)
*   **Type:** Magical Climbing Gear [16, 18]
*   **Properties:** A magical grappling hook identified by [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) [18]. It secure-mounts to virtually any surface and was used to abseil down the 50-foot cliffs of [Ongongta Overlook](/ongongta-overlook) [26].

## [Shackles of Compliance](/shackles-of-compliance)
*   **Type:** Magical Manacles [18]
*   **Properties:** Magical shackles that enforce obedience and submissiveness upon those bound by them. Salvaged from [Wormwood](/wormwood) officers [18].

## [Driftwood Wand](/driftwood-wand) (Wand of Mirror Image)
*   **Type:** Charged Arcane Wand [16]
*   **Properties:** A magical wand constructed from a bone-inscribed piece of driftwood. It was recovered from the [Grindylow Cave](/grindylow-cave) hidden chamber and contains [23 charges](/23-charges) of the [Mirror Image](/mirror-image) spell [16].

## [Spiked Gauntlets](/spiked-gauntlets)
*   **Type:** Magical Combat Wear [51]
*   **Properties:** A pair of leather-and-steel gauntlets tipped with dual spikes, salvaged from a feral pirate leader. They grant a [+1 bonus to hit and damage](/1-bonus-to-hit-and-damage), and deal an additional [2d6 piercing damage](/2d6-piercing-damage) on a critical hit [51, 1115]. Equipped by [Desmond](/desmond) [1117].

## [Cloak of Patches](/cloak-of-patches)
*   **Type:** Utility Magical Garment [60]
*   **Properties:** A patch-covered cloak. Tearing off a patch instantly transforms it into a real-world object. Includes a **[Sack Patch](/sack-patch)** which Lanny tore off to create a real canvas sack to gather treasure [60].

## [Troll Venom](/troll-venom)
*   **Type:** Toxic/Acidic Harvest [44]
*   **Properties:** Three highly toxic doses of pressurized acidic blood harvested by [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) from the slain colosseum [Venom Troll](/venom-troll) [44].
