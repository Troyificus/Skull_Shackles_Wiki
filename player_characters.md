# [Player Characters](/player-characters)

The founding officers and legendary lords/lady of the [Blood Wake](/blood-wake).

---

## [Lanny](/lanny) (aka [Captain Skull Crusher](/captain-skull-crusher))
*   **Class/Ancestry:** male Islander Goliath Barbarian / Druid [Things to remember.txt, 18, 42]
*   **Traits:** Stoic, honorable, childlike curiosity, massive physical strength [17, 29, 44].
*   **Key Abilities:** Primal [Rage](/rage) [17, 35], [Wild Shape](/wild-shape) (transforms into a shark-skinned "Shark-Bear") [52, 55], permanent telepathic bond with [Rat Damon](/rat-damon) [34].
*   **Achievements:** Crushed the skull of [Braden the Brawler Brimstone](/braden-the-brawler-brimstone) [23], ripped [Gunter Plugg](/gunter-plugg)'s arm off with raw strength [23], successfully drank putrid fish slime at [The Hagfish](/the-hagfish) [32], awarded the noble title of Lord of [Little Upara](/little-upara) [44].

## [Torrin Horrendous](/torrin-horrendous) (aka [Lady Blackheart](/lady-blackheart))
*   **Class/Ancestry:** Female human Wizard [Things to remember.txt, 1]
*   **Traits:** Pragmatic, ruthless, calculating, traumatized past (survived torture by [Blackheart](/blackheart) and carries guilt over a fire that killed her brother) [33, 34, 38].
*   **Deific Shift:** Formerly dedicated to [Myrkul](/myrkul) (Lord of Bones) [48, 55], she renounced him after feeling abandoned during a spectral attack, disintegrating her skull necklace and accepting [Besmara](/besmara)'s patronage [55, 56]. This shifted her lightning magic from dark black to brilliant white [56, 57].
*   **Key Spells:** [Animate Dead](/animate-dead) (maintains a small cohort of skeletons/zombies) [30, 45, 56], [Lightning Bolt](/lightning-bolt) [47, 55], [Misty Step](/misty-step) [26, 47].
*   **Familiar:** A bat named [Zima](/zima) [29, 36].

## [Devi McRae](/devi-mcrae) (aka [Crimson Cray](/crimson-cray))
*   **Class/Ancestry:** Male human Rogue [Things to remember.txt, 1]
*   **Traits:** Flamboyant, strategic, highly protective of his twin brother [Desmond](/desmond) and the crew's legal standing [17, 34]. Wears a striking feathered hat and custom crimson dragon-hide studded leather [44, 46].
*   **Key Abilities:** [Insightful Fighting](/insightful-fighting), sneak-attack master [36, 59]. Left-handed [54].
*   **Weapons:** Wields the magical rapier [Brine's Sting](/brines-sting) [59, 60].
*   **Family:** Son of estranged smuggler admiral [Gavin McRae](/gavin-mcrae) of [Tidewater Rock](/tidewater-rock) [21, 34].

## [Desmond McRae](/desmond-mcrae) (aka [Fist](/fist))
*   **Class/Ancestry:** Male human Monk / Champion of [Besmara](/besmara) [Things to remember.txt, 1, 28]
*   **Traits:** Bare-knuckle brawler, deeply superstitious, quick-tempered, loyal [1, 23, 27]. Bald with a broken nose [1].
*   **Key Abilities:** [Flurry of Blows](/flurry-of-blows), [Eldritch Blast](/eldritch-blast) (granted by [Besmara](/besmara)'s pact) [28, 29], [Step of the Wind](/step-of-the-wind) [27].
*   **Achievements:** Defeated [The Pugilist](/the-pugilist) in [Blood Cove](/blood-cove) [24], struck by divine lightning and resurrected as the chosen champion of [Besmara](/besmara) [27, 28].
