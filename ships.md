# [[Ships]]

Vessels captured, sailed, or encountered on the high seas.

---

## [[Wormwood]]
*   **Captain:** [[Captain Barnabas Harrigan]] [24]
*   **Type:** Heavy Pirate Galleon [1, 24]
*   **Description:** The brutal, oppressive flagship where our heroes were press-ganged [1, 24]. It is heavily manned by roughly 20-30 loyal crewmen and officers [1].

## [[Man's Promise]] (renamed [[Sea Breeze]] / [[Dog Rose]])
*   **Captains:** [[Mr. Plugg]] (temporary) [10, 201], [[Lanny]] (after mutiny) [17, 18]
*   **Type:** Merchant Vessel [10]
*   **Description:** A large, somewhat bulky merchant ship captured by the Wormwood boarding crew [51]. After the mutiny, it was dry-docked and squibbed at [[Rickety's Squibs]] [18], eventually sold to [[Girdan Danan]] of [[House Carthagen]] for [[4,000 gold pieces]] [24].

## [[Silent Howl]] (renamed [[Blood Wake]])
*   **Captains:** [[Captain Glade Fletcher]] (former) [22, 33], [[Lanny]] (current) [34]
*   **Type:** Sleek, narrow-hulled, fast pirate clipper [24, 409]
*   **Description:** A nimbly sailed, triangular-masted ship purchased from Captain Fletcher for [[5,000 gold pieces]] [33, 34]. It features a customized black dragon-skull figurehead mounted on the bow [46] and is manned by a small, elite crew of living sailors and Torrin's skeletal/zombie sentinels [46, 50].

## [[The Pinion]]
*   **Captain:** [[Captain Merrill Pegsworth]] [19, 20]
*   **Type:** Grand, gilded galleon / military cruiser [19]
*   **Description:** A massive, beautifully maintained vessel displaying military gold-embroidery and featuring a prominent golden eagle figurehead on its bow [20, 701].

## [[The Dominator]]
*   **Faction:** Chelaxian Navy [25, 26]
*   **Type:** Three-decked Man-of-War / War Galleon [25, 415]
*   **Description:** A towering military behemoth armed with 10 ballistas, dual catapults, and a crew of 30 to 40 marines [415]. It was heavily damaged and set ablaze by [[Torrin Horrendous]]'s daring midnight infiltration [26].

## [[The Death Knell]]
*   **Captain:** [[Whalebone Pilk]] [37, 47]
*   **Type:** Spectral Ghost Ship / Magnemarian Whaler [47, 958]
*   **Description:** A rotting, barnacle-encrusted vessel crewed by waterlogged zombies that sails Golarion's seas preceded by a thick fog and the toll of an ominous green-glowing bell [46, 47]. It was rammed into the Blood Wake and subsequently disintegrated after Pilk's defeat [46, 47].
